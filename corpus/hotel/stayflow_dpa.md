# Data Processing Agreement

This Data Processing Agreement (the "**DPA**") is made with effect from {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") between:

(1) **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc**, a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels (the "**Client**"); and

(2) **Stayflow Property Systems Inc** (the "**Supplier**").

## Background

(A) The Client and the Supplier are parties to a SaaS agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} (the "**Parent Agreement**"), under which the Supplier provides software services to the Client for use at the Client's individual hotels (the "**Properties**").

(B) In providing those services the Supplier processes personal data of the Client's customers and employees on behalf of the Client.

(C) This DPA is issued under the Parent Agreement and sets out the terms on which the Supplier shall process that personal data.

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this DPA:

1.1.1 "**Applicable Data Protection Law**" means all laws and regulations relating to privacy, data protection or the security of personal data that apply to the processing of Personal Data under this DPA;

1.1.2 "**Personal Data**" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person that the Supplier processes on behalf of the Client in connection with the Services, including personal data of the Client's customers and employees;

1.1.3 "**Security Incident**" means any breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data;

1.1.4 "**Services**" means the services described in Schedule 1 – Services; and

1.1.5 "**Sub-processor**" means any third party engaged by the Supplier that processes Personal Data.

1.2 Terms defined in the Parent Agreement and not defined here have the meaning given in the Parent Agreement. Clause headings do not affect interpretation.

1.3 If there is a conflict between this DPA and the Parent Agreement in relation to the processing of Personal Data, this DPA prevails.

## 2. Term

2.1 This DPA commences on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}}, being an initial term of 36 months (the "**Initial Term**").

2.2 Any extension of the Initial Term shall be effective only if agreed in writing by both parties.

2.3 The Supplier's obligations under this DPA continue after expiry or termination for as long as the Supplier retains any Personal Data.

## 3. Scope and Roles

3.1 The Client is the controller of the Personal Data and the Supplier is its processor.

3.2 The Supplier shall process Personal Data only to provide the Services at the Properties and in accordance with this DPA and the Client's documented instructions, unless required otherwise by law, in which case the Supplier shall inform the Client before processing unless the law prohibits it.

3.3 The subject matter, nature, purpose, categories of data and data subjects are set out in Schedule 1 – Services.

3.4 The Client warrants that it has a lawful basis to disclose the Personal Data to the Supplier and to instruct its processing.

## 4. Supplier Obligations

4.1 The Supplier shall:

4.1.1 ensure that persons authorized to process Personal Data are bound by appropriate obligations of confidentiality;

4.1.2 implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data against Security Incidents, having regard to the state of the art, the cost of implementation and the nature of the processing;

4.1.3 assist the Client, taking into account the nature of the processing, in responding to requests from data subjects and in meeting its obligations regarding security, impact assessments and consultation with regulators;

4.1.4 promptly notify the Client if it receives a request from a data subject or regulator relating to Personal Data, and not respond except on the Client's instructions; and

4.1.5 not transfer Personal Data outside the jurisdiction in which it is hosted at the Effective Date without the Client's prior written consent, save where the transfer is made under a lawful transfer mechanism and the Client has been notified in advance.

## 5. Sub-processors

5.1 The Client gives general authorization for the Supplier to engage Sub-processors, subject to this clause 5.

5.2 The Supplier shall give the Client reasonable prior written notice of any intended addition or replacement of a Sub-processor, and the Client may object on reasonable data protection grounds. If the parties cannot resolve the objection, the Client may terminate the affected Services under clause 11.

5.3 The Supplier shall impose on each Sub-processor written obligations no less protective than those in this DPA and remains liable for the acts and omissions of its Sub-processors.

## 6. Security Incidents

6.1 The Supplier shall notify the Client without undue delay after becoming aware of a Security Incident and shall provide the information reasonably required for the Client to meet its notification obligations.

6.2 The Supplier shall take reasonable steps to contain and remediate the Security Incident and shall keep the Client informed of progress.

6.3 Notification under this clause is not an admission of fault or liability.

## 7. Audit

7.1 The Supplier shall make available to the Client all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA.

7.2 The Client may, on reasonable notice and no more than once in any twelve-month period (save following a Security Incident or where required by a regulator), audit the Supplier's compliance with this DPA, subject to reasonable confidentiality and security requirements and so as to minimize disruption.

## 8. Return and Deletion

8.1 On expiry or termination of this DPA, or earlier on the Client's written request, the Supplier shall at the Client's choice return or securely delete all Personal Data and delete existing copies, unless law requires continued retention.

8.2 On request the Supplier shall confirm in writing that deletion has been completed.

## 9. Fees and Invoicing

9.1 No separate fees are payable for the Supplier's compliance with this DPA beyond the charges set out in Schedule 2 – Charges and the Parent Agreement.

9.2 Any invoiced amounts shall be paid in accordance with the payment terms of the Parent Agreement. Any additional work requested by the Client that is outside the ordinary scope of this DPA shall be chargeable only if agreed in writing in advance.

## 10. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

10.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other received in connection with this DPA, shall use it only for the purposes of this DPA, and shall not disclose it except to personnel and advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality, or as required by law.

10.2 Personal Data is the Client's confidential information and remains the property of the Client. Nothing in this DPA transfers any intellectual property rights, and the Supplier retains its rights in its software and systems. The Supplier receives only a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use Personal Data to provide the Services.

## 11. Termination

11.1 The Client may terminate this DPA and the affected Services on written notice if the Supplier materially breaches this DPA and, where the breach is capable of remedy, fails to remedy it within a reasonable period after written notice.

11.2 Either party may terminate this DPA on written notice if the other becomes insolvent or ceases to carry on business.

11.3 This DPA terminates automatically on termination or expiry of the Parent Agreement, subject to clauses 2.3 and 8.

11.4 Clauses 2.3, 6, 8, 10, 12, 13 and 15 survive termination or expiry.

## 12. Warranties, Liability and Indemnity

12.1 The Supplier warrants that it will process Personal Data in accordance with this DPA and Applicable Data Protection Law and that it has the authority and capability to perform its obligations.

12.2 The Supplier shall indemnify the Client against losses, fines, penalties, costs and claims reasonably incurred by the Client arising from the Supplier's breach of this DPA or of Applicable Data Protection Law in its role as processor.

12.3 Subject to clause 12.4, the limitations and exclusions of liability in the Parent Agreement apply to this DPA.

12.4 Nothing in this DPA limits or excludes liability for fraud, willful misconduct, or any liability that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded.

## 13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

13.1 This DPA and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it, including non-contractual disputes, are governed by the laws of Delaware.

13.2 The courts of Delaware have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any such dispute or claim.

## 14. Assignment and Notices

14.1 Neither party may assign, transfer or subcontract any of its rights or obligations under this DPA without the prior written consent of the other (not to be unreasonably withheld), save for the engagement of Sub-processors under clause 5.

14.2 Notices under this DPA must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognized courier or by email to the address that each party has notified to the other for that purpose, and are deemed received on delivery or, for email, on the next business day after sending.

## 15. General

15.1 This DPA and the Parent Agreement constitute the entire agreement between the parties relating to the processing of Personal Data and supersede all prior discussions and understandings on that subject.

15.2 No variation of this DPA is effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.

15.3 No failure or delay in exercising a right is a waiver of it. If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remainder is unaffected.

15.4 This DPA may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one agreement.

## Schedule 1 – Services

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Services | Provision of the software-as-a-service platform and related support under the Parent Agreement to the Client for use at each of the Properties |
| Subject matter | Processing of Personal Data in the course of providing the Services |
| Nature and purpose | Hosting, storage, retrieval, analysis, support and maintenance of data solely to enable the Client to operate the Properties |
| Duration | The Initial Term and any period during which the Supplier retains Personal Data |
| Categories of data subjects | Customers (including guests and prospective guests) of the Client and employees of the Client |
| Categories of Personal Data | Contact details, booking and stay records, payment-related references, preferences, and employee identification and work-related data |

## Schedule 2 – Charges

| Item | Charge |
|---|---|
| Supplier's compliance with this DPA | Included in the charges payable under the Parent Agreement; no additional charge |
| Additional work outside the ordinary scope | Only as agreed in writing in advance under clause 9.2 |
