# Statement of Work No. 1 – Security Services at Seabright Midtown Suites

| **Supplier** | Brightstar Security Services Inc |
| **Period** | Effective Date {{EFFECTIVE}} until {{EXPIRY}} (initial term of 12 months) |

This Statement of Work No. 1 (the "SOW") is made as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "Effective Date") between Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc, a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels (the "Customer"), and Brightstar Security Services Inc (the "Supplier"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between the same parties. Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the MSA.

## 1. Background

1.1 The Customer owns and operates six hotels (each a "Property"). Services under this SOW are delivered at one Property: Seabright Midtown Suites (the "Site").

1.2 The Customer wishes to engage the Supplier to provide security services at the Site, and the Supplier has agreed to provide them on the terms of this SOW and the MSA.

1.3 In the event of conflict, the MSA prevails over this SOW unless this SOW expressly states that it overrides a specific provision of the MSA.

## 2. Scope

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the following services at the Site (the "Services"):

(a) staffed security officer coverage of the public areas, entrances, car park and back-of-house areas;
(b) access control, key and keycard-incident monitoring and visitor management support;
(c) routine and incident-driven patrols, including guest-safety and emergency response support;
(d) written incident reporting and escalation to the Site's general manager; and
(e) coordination with local emergency services and with the Customer's operations team.

2.2 The Services exclude any activity not described in clause 2.1. Additional services require a written change request agreed under clause 2.4.

2.3 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with good industry practice, applicable law and licensing requirements, and the Customer's reasonable site policies notified in writing.

2.4 Either party may propose changes to the Services. No change is binding until recorded in a written change order signed by both parties, which shall state any effect on fees and timing.

## 3. Deliverables and Milestones

| No. | Deliverable / Milestone | Target Date | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mobilisation and site risk assessment | {{MS1}} | Site walk-through, risk assessment and agreed post orders for the Site |
| 2 | Staffing and vetting confirmation | {{MS1}} | Written confirmation that all assigned personnel are licensed, vetted and trained |
| 3 | Go-live of security coverage | {{EFFECTIVE}} | Full service commencement at the Site |
| 4 | Monthly incident and activity reports | Monthly during the term | Report of incidents, patrol activity and recommendations |
| 5 | Closing report | On expiry | Summary of the term and handover of Site records |

## 4. Roles

4.1 **Supplier.** The Supplier shall provide appropriately licensed, vetted and trained personnel, supervise them, and remain responsible for their employment, conduct, wages, insurance and statutory obligations. The Supplier shall appoint an account manager as the Customer's primary contact and shall replace any person the Customer reasonably objects to.

4.2 **Customer.** The Customer shall provide timely access to the Site, relevant information, a Site contact, and reasonable facilities for the Supplier's personnel, and shall make decisions and give approvals promptly.

4.3 **Governance.** The account manager and the Customer's Site contact shall meet as reasonably required to review performance and open issues.

## 5. Assumptions

5.1 The Services will be delivered only at the Site and only to the extent described in clause 2.
5.2 The Customer will give the Supplier access to the Site and its relevant systems and areas as needed.
5.3 The Site's operations, layout and risk profile are substantially as disclosed to the Supplier before the Effective Date. Material changes shall be handled under clause 2.4.
5.4 Delay caused by the Customer's failure to meet its responsibilities will extend the affected dates to the extent of the delay.

## 6. Term and Renewal

6.1 This SOW commences on the Effective Date and continues until {{EXPIRY}} (a term of 12 months), unless terminated earlier under this SOW or the MSA.

6.2 This SOW does not renew automatically. Any extension requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

## 7. Fees and Invoicing

7.1 The fees for the Services are USD 240,000 per year. The total contract value under this SOW is USD 240,000.

7.2 The Supplier shall invoice the Customer in accordance with the MSA. Each invoice must be valid, identify this SOW and the Site, and describe the Services performed.

7.3 The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

7.4 The Customer may dispute an invoice in good faith by written notice stating the reasons, and shall pay any undisputed portion on time. The parties shall work promptly to resolve the dispute.

7.5 Fees are the entire consideration for the Services. No additional charges or expenses are payable unless agreed in writing in advance.

## 8. Acceptance

8.1 The Customer may review each Deliverable and shall notify the Supplier in writing of acceptance or of reasonable grounds for rejection.

8.2 If a Deliverable is rejected, the Supplier shall correct it promptly and resubmit it, and the process in clause 8.1 shall repeat.

8.3 Acceptance of a Deliverable does not waive any right or remedy of the Customer in respect of latent defects or non-conformity with this SOW or the MSA.

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other received in connection with this SOW, including guest information, Site security arrangements, access procedures and the terms of this SOW. It shall use that information only to perform or receive the Services and shall disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know and are bound by equivalent obligations, or as required by law. The confidentiality provisions of the MSA also apply.

## 10. Intellectual Property

10.1 Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property.
10.2 Reports and records prepared for the Customer specifically under this SOW belong to the Customer on creation, and the Supplier assigns all rights in them to the Customer.
10.3 The Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive, perpetual license to use any Supplier materials embedded in those reports and records to the extent needed to use them.

## 11. Data Protection

11.1 Each party shall comply with applicable data protection laws in connection with this SOW.
11.2 The Supplier shall process personal data of guests, staff and visitors, including any CCTV footage and access logs, only on the Customer's documented instructions and for the purpose of providing the Services.
11.3 The Supplier shall maintain appropriate technical and organizational security measures, notify the Customer without undue delay on becoming aware of any personal data breach, and assist the Customer in meeting its legal obligations.
11.4 On expiry or termination, the Supplier shall return or securely delete personal data as the Customer directs, subject to legal retention requirements.

## 12. Warranties

The Supplier warrants that: (a) it has the authority, licenses and permits required to provide the Services; (b) the Services will be performed in accordance with clause 2.3; (c) its personnel are suitably vetted, trained and licensed; and (d) the Services will not infringe the rights of any third party. The warranties in the MSA also apply.

## 13. Liability and Indemnity

13.1 Each party's liability under or in connection with this SOW is governed by the liability provisions of the MSA.

13.2 Indemnification obligations in respect of this SOW, including for third-party claims arising from the Supplier's acts or omissions, personal injury, damage to property or breach of data protection obligations, are governed by the indemnity provisions of the MSA.

## 14. Termination

14.1 Either party may terminate this SOW for material breach by the other that is not remedied within a reasonable period after written notice specifying the breach.
14.2 Either party may terminate this SOW immediately by written notice if the other becomes insolvent or ceases to carry on business.
14.3 The Customer may terminate this SOW on the other grounds and in the manner provided in the MSA.
14.4 On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall hand over all Site records, keys, access devices and Customer property, and the Customer shall pay undisputed fees for Services properly performed up to the termination date. Clauses which by their nature are intended to survive shall survive.

## 15. Assignment and Subcontracting

Neither party may assign or transfer this SOW without the prior written consent of the other, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. The Supplier shall not subcontract any part of the Services without the Customer's prior written consent and remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

## 16. Notices

Notices under this SOW must be in writing and delivered by hand, by courier or by email to the address or contact notified by the receiving party for this purpose, with a copy to the account manager or Site contact. Notices take effect on receipt.

## 17. Entire Agreement; Governing Law

17.1 This SOW and the MSA constitute the entire agreement of the parties regarding its subject matter and supersede all prior discussions and understandings. This SOW may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.

17.2 This SOW is governed by the laws of New York. Disputes shall be resolved as provided in the MSA.
