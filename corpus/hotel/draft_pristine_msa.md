# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**DRAFT – NOT EXECUTED – FOR DISCUSSION PURPOSES ONLY**

This Master Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made with effect from {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") between **Pristine Spa & Amenity Supplies Inc** (the "**Supplier**") and **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc** (the "**Customer**"), a hotel group that owns and operates six (6) hotels (each a "**Property**" and together the "**Properties**").

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement: "**Services**" means the supply of spa and amenity products, consumables and related ancillary services described in Section 3 and in any Order; "**Order**" means a written purchase order issued by a Property and accepted by the Supplier; "**Fees**" means the sums payable under Section 5; "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential; "**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 2.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation". No rule of construction shall apply to the disadvantage of a party because that party prepared this Agreement.

## 2. TERM AND RENEWAL

2.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}} (the "**Initial Term**"), being a period of twenty-four (24) months.

2.2 Upon expiry of the Initial Term, and of each Renewal Term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months (each a "**Renewal Term**") unless either party gives the other at least ninety (90) days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term. Notice given later than that period shall be ineffective for the then-current term.

## 3. SERVICES AND SCOPE

3.1 The Supplier shall supply the Services to the Properties in accordance with Orders accepted under this Agreement. The Customer is the contracting party for all Properties and is responsible for the acts, omissions and payment obligations of each Property.

3.2 The Supplier's obligations are limited to the Services expressly described in an accepted Order. Product specifications, delivery windows and lead times are estimates only, and the Supplier may substitute products of equivalent or better quality where supply constraints arise.

3.3 The Customer shall provide timely access to the Properties, accurate forecasts and information, and suitable storage. The Supplier shall not be liable for any delay or failure to the extent caused by the Customer's failure to comply with this Section 3.3.

3.4 Risk in products passes to the Customer on delivery to the relevant Property. Title passes on payment in full.

## 4. CHANGES

4.1 No change to the Services or this Agreement is binding unless recorded in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both parties. The Supplier may decline any requested change and may charge reasonable additional fees for any change it agrees to implement.

## 5. FEES AND INVOICING

5.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier annual Fees of USD 330,000 for each year of the Initial Term, giving a total contract value for the Initial Term of USD 660,000.

5.2 The Supplier shall invoice the Fees in accordance with the schedule agreed between the parties. Each invoice must identify the relevant Property or Properties and the Order concerned.

5.3 The Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within fifteen (15) days of receipt of a valid invoice. The Customer shall notify the Supplier in writing of any disputed amount, with reasons in reasonable detail, before the due date, and shall pay the undisputed portion on time.

5.4 Late payment shall entitle the Supplier, without prejudice to its other rights, to charge interest at the maximum rate permitted by law and to suspend the Services until all overdue sums are paid.

5.5 Fees are exclusive of all sales, use, value added and similar taxes, which the Customer shall bear (other than taxes on the Supplier's income). The Customer shall make all payments without set-off or counterclaim.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential, use it only to perform or receive the benefit of this Agreement, and disclose it only to its personnel, advisers and affiliates who need to know and are bound by equivalent obligations.

6.2 Section 6.1 does not apply to information that is or becomes public other than through breach, was lawfully known to the recipient beforehand, is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information, or is lawfully received from a third party. A party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, giving prompt notice where lawful.

6.3 The obligations in this Section 6 survive for one (1) year after this Agreement ends.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

7.1 All intellectual property rights in the Services, the Supplier's products, formulations, packaging, branding, designs, know-how and any materials created by the Supplier (together, "**Supplier IP**") remain the exclusive property of the Supplier. Nothing in this Agreement assigns any Supplier IP to the Customer.

7.2 The Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the Term to use Supplier IP solely as necessary to receive and use the Services at the Properties.

7.3 The Customer retains ownership of its names, marks and logos. The Customer grants the Supplier a limited license to use them solely to perform the Services, including for any co-branded packaging requested by the Customer.

No express warranty period applies to the Services or to any product, and the Customer's sole remedy for breach of this warranty is, at the Supplier's option, re-performance of the non-conforming Services or a refund of the Fees paid for them, provided the Customer notifies the Supplier in writing promptly after discovering the non-conformity.

8.3 The Customer is responsible for the manner in which products are used, stored, handled and presented to guests at the Properties, including compliance with applicable health, safety and hospitality regulations and the Supplier's written usage instructions.

9.1 SUBJECT TO SECTION 9.4, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR IN CONNECTION WITH THIS AGREEMENT, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT (INCLUDING NEGLIGENCE), BREACH OF STATUTORY DUTY OR OTHERWISE, SHALL NOT EXCEED 0.5 TIMES THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

9.2 SUBJECT TO SECTION 9.4, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, EXEMPLARY OR PUNITIVE LOSS OR DAMAGE, OR FOR ANY LOSS OF PROFIT, REVENUE, BUSINESS, GOODWILL, ANTICIPATED SAVINGS, OR REPUTATION, OR ANY BUSINESS INTERRUPTION OR LOSS OF BOOKINGS, IN EACH CASE HOWEVER ARISING AND EVEN IF FORESEEABLE OR IF THE PARTY HAS BEEN ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH LOSS.

9.3 The Supplier shall have no liability for any loss to the extent caused by the Customer's or any Property's breach of this Agreement, misuse or improper storage of products, failure to follow instructions, or events beyond the Supplier's reasonable control. Liability for the Fees due to the Supplier is not subject to the cap in Section 9.1.

9.4 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded, including liability for fraud or for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

9.5 Any claim must be notified in writing within a reasonable time of the claimant becoming aware of it, and the parties agree that the limitations in this Section 9 are a fundamental basis of the bargain and are reflected in the Fees.

## 10. INDEMNITY

10.1 The Customer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Supplier and its officers, employees and affiliates against all third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages, costs and expenses (including reasonable legal fees), arising from (a) the Customer's or any Property's use, storage, handling or presentation of products other than in accordance with the Supplier's written instructions; (b) any modification of products by or for the Customer; or (c) the Customer's materials, names or marks infringing third-party rights.

10.2 The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against third-party claims alleging that the Supplier IP, as supplied and used in accordance with this Agreement, infringes that third party's intellectual property rights, subject to the Customer giving prompt notice, sole control of the defense to the Supplier and reasonable cooperation. The Supplier has no obligation under this Section 10.2 for claims arising from the matters in Section 10.1. This Section 10.2 states the Supplier's entire liability for infringement claims.

10.3 The indemnities in this Section 10 are subject to Section 9 only to the extent the Supplier's indemnity under Section 10.2 is concerned.

## 11. INSURANCE

11.1 The Supplier shall maintain throughout the Term commercial general liability insurance with cover of at least USD 1,000,000 per claim, with a reputable insurer. On reasonable written request, the Supplier shall provide a certificate evidencing that cover. The maintenance of insurance does not increase or otherwise affect the Supplier's liability under this Agreement.

## 12. TERMINATION

12.1 Neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience. This Agreement may be terminated only for cause in accordance with this Section 12.

12.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of written notice specifying the breach and requiring its remedy, or if the other party becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of bankruptcy or similar proceedings.

12.3 Without limiting Section 5.4, the Supplier may suspend the Services immediately on notice if any undisputed sum remains unpaid after the due date.

12.4 On termination or expiry: (a) the Customer shall pay all Fees accrued or invoiced up to the effective date of termination, and all Fees for Orders accepted and products produced or procured specifically for the Customer; (b) each party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information on request; and (c) termination or expiry does not affect accrued rights and Sections 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 13, 15 and 16 and any other provisions intended to survive shall survive.

## 13. DATA PROTECTION

13.1 The parties acknowledge that the Services do not involve the processing of personal data. Neither party shall be required to process personal data on behalf of the other in performing this Agreement. If the parties later agree that personal data will be processed, they shall first enter into a written data processing addendum.

13.2 Business contact details of each party's personnel exchanged for administering this Agreement shall be used only for that purpose.

## 14. FORCE MAJEURE

14.1 The Supplier shall not be liable for any failure or delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, fire, flood, epidemic, governmental action, labor disputes, supply chain disruption, shortage of raw materials, transportation failure or failure of third-party suppliers. The Supplier's time for performance shall be extended accordingly, and the Customer's payment obligations for Services already delivered shall not be affected.

## 15. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Florida, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

15.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Florida. EACH PARTY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

## 16. GENERAL

16.1 **Assignment.** The Customer shall not assign, novate, subcontract or otherwise transfer any right or obligation under this Agreement without the Supplier's prior written consent. The Supplier may assign this Agreement to an affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its relevant business, and may use subcontractors to perform the Services, remaining responsible for them as for itself.

16.2 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognized courier or by registered mail to the address of the receiving party stated in the most recent Order or otherwise notified in writing, marked for the attention of its General Counsel or equivalent. Notices are deemed received on delivery, or if delivery is refused, on the date of refusal.

16.3 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with accepted Orders, is the entire agreement between the parties concerning its subject matter and supersedes all prior proposals, negotiations, representations and understandings. Each party acknowledges that it has not relied on any statement not expressly set out in this Agreement. In the event of conflict, the body of this Agreement prevails over any Order, and no terms on any Customer purchase order or other document shall apply.

16.4 **Waiver and Severability.** A failure or delay in exercising any right is not a waiver of it. If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted and the remainder of this Agreement shall continue in effect.

16.5 **Relationship.** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, agency or joint venture, and no third party has any right to enforce its terms.

16.6 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

## 17. EXECUTION

This Agreement is a draft and has not been executed. It shall become binding only when signed by authorized representatives of both parties.
