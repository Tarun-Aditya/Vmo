# Statement of Work No. 1 – Laundry and Linen Services at Seabright Harbour Hotel

**Project:** Laundry and Linen Services at Seabright Harbour Hotel
**Parent Agreement:** Master Services Agreement between the parties dated {{PARENT_DATE}}
**Period:** Effective Date {{EFFECTIVE}} to {{EXPIRY}} (initial term of 24 months)
**Fees:** USD 348,000 per year; total contract value USD 696,000
**Status:** Vendor draft – not yet signed

This Statement of Work No. 1 (the "SOW") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "Effective Date") between **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Ltd**, a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels (the "Customer"), and **Crestline Laundry & Linen Services Ltd** (the "Supplier"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the Master Services Agreement between the parties dated {{PARENT_DATE}} (the "Parent Agreement"). Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Parent Agreement. If this SOW conflicts with the Parent Agreement, this SOW prevails solely as to the services described in it.

## 1. Background

1.1 The Customer owns and operates six hotels. Services under this SOW are delivered at individual hotels of the Customer (each a "Property"). For the purposes of this SOW, the Property is **Seabright Harbour Hotel**.

1.2 The Customer wishes to engage the Supplier to collect, launder, finish and return hotel linen for Seabright Harbour Hotel, and the Supplier has agreed to do so on the terms of this SOW.

## 2. Scope

2.1 **Services.** The Supplier shall provide the following services at and for Seabright Harbour Hotel (the "Services"):
(a) scheduled collection of soiled linen from the Property;
(b) sorting, laundering, drying, finishing and inspection of linen to the quality standard in Section 7;
(c) packaging and return of clean linen to the Property on the agreed schedule; and
(d) reporting of volumes processed, losses and damage.

2.2 **Linen.** "Linen" means bed linen, towels, table linen, and such other textile items as the parties agree in writing from time to time.

2.3 **Additional Properties.** The Services may be extended to other Properties only by a written change order signed by both parties.

2.4 **Laundering charge.** The laundering charge rises to USD 1.62 per kilogram of linen. Weight shall be measured at the Supplier's facility on receipt of soiled linen using calibrated scales, and the Supplier shall make weight records available to the Customer on request.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

4.1 **Supplier.** The Supplier shall: (a) perform the Services with reasonable skill and care using suitably trained personnel; (b) comply with applicable laws, including health, safety, environmental and employment laws; (c) maintain its facility, equipment and vehicles in good working order; and (d) appoint a representative with authority to manage the Services day to day.

4.2 **Customer.** The Customer shall: (a) make soiled linen available for collection at the agreed times and locations; (b) segregate linen as reasonably requested; (c) provide reasonable access to the Property; and (d) appoint a representative to liaise with the Supplier.

4.3 **Governance.** Each party's representative shall meet at the intervals set out in the operating procedures to review performance and resolve operational issues.

## 5. Assumptions

5.1 The Customer will supply linen of a type and condition suitable for commercial laundering.
5.2 Volumes will be broadly consistent with those discussed before the Effective Date; material changes will be raised promptly and addressed by change order where needed.
5.3 The Supplier may rely on information and instructions given by the Customer's representative.
5.4 Neither party is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other promptly and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.

## 6. Fees and Invoicing

6.1 **Fees.** The annual fees for the Services are USD 348,000. The total contract value over the initial term of 24 months is USD 696,000. Charges are calculated by reference to the laundering charge in Section 2.4.

6.2 **Invoicing.** The Supplier shall invoice the Customer in accordance with the Parent Agreement. A valid invoice must identify this SOW, the Property, the period covered and the volumes processed, and must be addressed to the Customer.

6.3 **Payment.** The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 75 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

6.4 **Disputes.** The Customer shall notify the Supplier of any disputed amount in good faith and with reasons, and shall pay the undisputed portion on time. The parties shall seek to resolve disputed amounts promptly.

6.5 **Taxes.** Fees are exclusive of applicable sales, use or similar taxes, which shall be shown separately on the invoice.

## 7. Acceptance and Quality

7.1 Linen returned to the Property shall be clean, free of stains and odour, properly finished and undamaged beyond normal wear.

7.2 The Customer may inspect returned linen and shall notify the Supplier of any non-conforming items within a reasonable time after delivery. The Supplier shall re-process non-conforming items promptly at no additional charge.

7.3 Each Deliverable in Section 3 is accepted when the Customer's representative confirms in writing that it meets this SOW, or when it is used in the ordinary operation of the Property without objection after a reasonable period.

7.4 Persistent failure to meet the quality standard is a breach to be addressed under Section 10.

## 8. Term and Renewal

8.1 This SOW commences on the Effective Date and continues until {{EXPIRY}} (the "Initial Term"), a period of 24 months.

8.2 On expiry of the Initial Term, this SOW renews automatically for successive periods of 12 months unless a party gives the other at least 180 days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

## 9. Confidentiality, Intellectual Property and Data Protection

9.1 **Confidentiality.** Each party shall keep the other's non-public information confidential, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know and are bound by equivalent duties, or as required by law. These obligations survive termination.

9.2 **Intellectual property.** Each party retains its pre-existing intellectual property. The Customer retains all rights in its linen, branding and marks. The Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive licence to use any reports delivered under this SOW for its internal business purposes.

9.3 **Data protection.** Each party shall comply with applicable data protection laws. The Supplier shall process any personal data (for example, guest-related items or staff contact details) only to deliver the Services and on the Customer's documented instructions, shall maintain appropriate security measures, and shall notify the Customer without undue delay on becoming aware of a breach affecting that data.

## 10. Warranties, Liability and Indemnity

10.1 **Warranties.** The Supplier warrants that it will perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, in compliance with applicable law, and that it holds the licences and permits needed to perform them.

10.2 **Indemnity.** The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against third-party claims arising from the Supplier's negligence, breach of applicable law or infringement of third-party rights in performing the Services. The Customer shall indemnify the Supplier against third-party claims arising from the Customer's breach of Section 9.3 or its instructions to the Supplier. The indemnified party shall give prompt notice, allow the indemnifying party to control the defence, and reasonably cooperate.

10.3 **Limitation of liability.** Each party's aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this SOW is limited to 0.25 times the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. Nothing in this SOW limits liability that cannot be limited by law, including liability for fraud or wilful misconduct.

10.4 **Indirect loss.** Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profit, arising under this SOW, except as required by law.

## 11. Termination

11.1 **No termination for convenience.** Neither party may terminate this SOW for convenience. This SOW may be terminated only for cause in accordance with this Section.

11.2 **Termination for cause.** A party may terminate this SOW by written notice if the other party: (a) commits a material breach that is incapable of remedy, or that it fails to remedy within a reasonable period specified in a written notice requiring remedy; or (b) becomes insolvent, enters liquidation or administration, or ceases to carry on business.

11.3 **Consequences.** On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall promptly return all Customer linen and the Customer shall pay undisputed charges for Services performed up to the termination date. Accrued rights and provisions intended to survive remain in effect.

## 12. General

12.1 **Governing law.** This SOW is governed by the laws of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

12.2 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign or subcontract its rights or obligations under this SOW without the other's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

12.3 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, courier or email with confirmation to the representative and address each party designates in writing for that purpose.

12.4 **Entire agreement.** This SOW and the Parent Agreement constitute the entire agreement between the parties on their subject matter and supersede all prior understandings. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.

12.5 **Counterparts.** This SOW may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
