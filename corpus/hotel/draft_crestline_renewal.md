This Statement of Work No. 1 (the "SOW") is made between **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc** (the "Customer"), a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels, and **Crestline Laundry & Linen Services Inc** (the "Supplier"). The Customer enters into this SOW on its own behalf and for the benefit of the hotel at which the Services are delivered, namely **Seabright Harbor Hotel** (the "Property"). Where the Customer's other hotels later receive Services, they are also "Properties" only by written agreement of the Parties.

This SOW is issued under, and forms part of, the Parent Agreement. If this SOW conflicts with the Parent Agreement, this SOW prevails solely as to the Services described here and only to the extent of the conflict.

## 1. Background

1.1 The Customer requires reliable commercial laundering, handling and delivery of hotel linen and related textiles for the Property.

1.2 The Supplier carries on the business of commercial laundry and linen services and has agreed to provide the Services to the Customer on the terms of this SOW.

## 2. Scope

2.1 **Services.** The Supplier shall, at the Property, provide the following (the "Services"):

(a) collection of soiled linen and textiles from designated collection points at the Property;
(b) sorting, laundering, drying, finishing and inspection of that linen at the Supplier's facility;
(c) delivery of clean linen to the Property's designated storage areas;
(d) replacement or repair of linen damaged in processing; and
(e) periodic reporting of volumes processed, rejects and losses.

2.2 **Standards.** The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with good industry practice, applicable law, and the Customer's reasonable written hygiene and brand standards notified to the Supplier.

2.3 **Out of scope.** Any service not described in this SOW, including dry-cleaning of guest garments and supply of new linen stock, requires a written change order signed by both Parties.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

4.1 **Supplier.** The Supplier shall appoint an account manager as primary contact, supply suitably trained and vetted personnel, maintain adequate processing capacity, and comply with the Property's site, safety and security rules.

4.2 **Customer.** The Customer shall designate a Property liaison, give the Supplier reasonable access to collection and delivery areas, present linen sorted and bagged as reasonably agreed, and provide timely information and decisions reasonably requested.

4.3 **Governance.** The account manager and the Property liaison shall meet as reasonably required to review performance and resolve operational issues. Unresolved matters shall be escalated to a senior executive of each Party before any formal dispute step.

## 5. Assumptions

5.1 Linen volumes will be broadly consistent with those discussed before signature; material sustained deviations shall be raised promptly, and the Parties shall discuss in good faith any adjustment needed.

5.2 The Property will maintain linen stock sufficient to support the agreed cycle.

5.3 Linen delivered to the Supplier is the Customer's property at all times. The Supplier shall mark, segregate and keep it secure, and shall not use it for any other customer or purpose.

5.4 The Supplier's performance is excused to the extent caused by the Customer's failure to perform its responsibilities under this SOW.

## 6. Fees and Invoicing

6.1 **Fees.** The annual fees for the Services are USD 348,000, giving a total contract value of USD 696,000 over the initial term of 24 months.

6.2 **Laundering charge.** The laundering charge rises to USD 1.62 per kilogram of linen. The Supplier shall apply this charge in calculating the invoiced amounts and shall show the weight of linen processed on each invoice.

6.3 **Invoicing.** The Supplier shall issue invoices in arrears and each invoice must be valid, meaning it identifies the Property, the period covered, the quantities processed, the applicable rate and any taxes separately.

6.4 **Payment.** The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 10 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

6.5 **Disputed amounts.** The Customer may withhold a amount it disputes in good faith, shall notify the Supplier promptly with reasons, and shall pay the undisputed balance. The Parties shall work together to resolve the dispute promptly.

6.6 **Taxes.** Fees exclude applicable sales, use or value-added taxes, which shall be itemized and borne by the Customer where lawfully chargeable.

## 7. Acceptance

7.1 The Customer may review each deliverable and each monthly report and shall notify the Supplier within a reasonable period of any non-conformity with this SOW.

7.2 The Supplier shall promptly re-perform non-conforming Services, including re-laundering of rejected linen, at no additional charge.

7.3 Absent notice of non-conformity within a reasonable period, the relevant deliverable is accepted, without prejudice to the Customer's rights in respect of latent defects or loss of or damage to linen.

7.4 Acceptance does not waive any warranty or indemnity under this SOW or the Parent Agreement.

## 8. Term and Renewal

8.1 This SOW commences on the Effective Date {{EFFECTIVE}} and continues until {{EXPIRY}} (the "Initial Term").

8.2 It then renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless a Party gives at least 180 days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

## 9. Termination

9.1 Neither Party may terminate this SOW for convenience. This SOW may be terminated only for cause as set out below.

9.2 Either Party may terminate this SOW by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within a reasonable period specified in a written notice requiring remedy, or if the breach is incapable of remedy.

9.3 Either Party may terminate by written notice if the other becomes insolvent, enters administration, liquidation or bankruptcy, or ceases to carry on business.

9.4 On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall promptly return all Customer linen, whether clean or soiled, and the Customer shall pay undisputed fees for Services performed up to the termination date.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other received in connection with this SOW, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know and are bound by equivalent duties, or as required by law. This obligation survives termination and does not apply to information that is public through no fault of the recipient, already lawfully known to it, or independently developed.

## 11. Intellectual Property

Each Party retains its pre-existing intellectual property. The Customer's names, marks, logos and linen markings remain the Customer's property, and the Supplier may use them only as necessary to perform the Services. The Supplier's processes and know-how remain the Supplier's.

## 12. Warranties

12.1 The Supplier warrants that it will perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, with suitably qualified personnel, and in compliance with applicable law, including health, safety and environmental requirements.

12.2 Each Party warrants that it has authority to enter into and perform this SOW.

12.3 Except as expressly stated, all implied warranties are excluded to the extent permitted by law.

## 13. Data Protection

Each Party shall comply with applicable data protection law. To the extent the Supplier handles personal data (for example, staff names or room information on linen records), it shall process it only on the Customer's documented instructions, keep it secure, notify the Customer without undue delay of any breach affecting it, and delete or return it on termination.

## 14. Indemnity

14.1 The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against third-party claims arising from the Supplier's negligence or willful misconduct, its breach of applicable law in performing the Services, or personal injury or property damage caused by its personnel.

14.2 The Customer shall indemnify the Supplier against third-party claims arising from the Customer's breach of applicable law in connection with the linen or the Property, or the Customer's negligence or willful misconduct.

14.3 The indemnified Party shall give prompt notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's cost.

## 15. Limitation of Liability

15.1 Each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this SOW is limited to 0.25 times the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

15.2 Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, or loss of profit or goodwill, except to the extent such loss cannot lawfully be excluded.

15.3 Nothing in this SOW limits liability for fraud, death or personal injury caused by negligence, or any liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

## 16. General

16.3 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, recognized courier or email with confirmation of transmission to the address of the receiving Party stated in the Parent Agreement, or as later notified.

16.4 **Entire agreement.** This SOW and the Parent Agreement constitute the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject matter and supersede prior discussions and proposals. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

16.5 **Counterparts.** This SOW may be executed in counterparts, including electronically.
