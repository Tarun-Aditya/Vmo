# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made with effect from {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "Effective Date") between **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Ltd**, a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels (the "Customer"), and **Evergreen Waste & Recycling Ltd** (the "Supplier"). The Supplier will deliver the Services at the Customer's hotel known as Seabright Old Town Boutique (the "Property"). Where the Customer later designates another hotel for the Services by written agreement, that hotel is also a "Property".

## 1. Term and Renewal

1.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until {{EXPIRY}} (the "Initial Term"), being a period of 12 months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

1.2 This Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of 12 months (each a "Renewal Term") unless either party gives the other at least 60 days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term.

## 2. Fees and Payment

2.1 The annual fees for the Services are USD 54,000 (the "Fees"). The total contract value for the Initial Term is USD 54,000. The Fees are the Supplier's entire remuneration for the Services, including all labour, equipment, vehicles, transport and disposal costs, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

2.2 The Supplier shall invoice the Customer for the Fees in the manner and at the intervals agreed in writing between the parties. Each invoice must be valid, itemised and addressed to the Customer, and must identify the Property.

2.3 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice. The Customer may withhold any amount disputed in good faith, shall notify the Supplier of the dispute promptly with reasons, and shall pay any amount later agreed or determined to be due within 30 days of that agreement or determination. The parties shall work together in good faith to resolve disputes.

2.4 Fees are stated exclusive of applicable value added tax or similar sales tax, which the Customer shall pay in addition against a valid tax invoice.

## 3. Services

3.1 The Supplier shall provide waste collection, recycling and related waste management services at the Property (the "Services") as described in any service schedule or written specification agreed by the parties and referring to this Agreement. The Services shall include the supply and maintenance of containers and the lawful transport, treatment and disposal of waste.

3.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using suitably qualified and experienced personnel, in accordance with applicable law and the Customer's reasonable site, safety and security rules notified to the Supplier.

3.3 The Supplier shall hold and maintain all permits, licences and registrations required by law to carry, treat and dispose of waste, and shall provide waste transfer documentation on request.

3.4 The Supplier shall perform the Services so as to minimise disruption to guests and hotel operations. The Customer shall give the Supplier reasonable access to the Property and the information reasonably needed to perform the Services.

## 4. Confidentiality

4.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other party disclosed in connection with this Agreement, shall use it only to perform or receive the Services, and shall not disclose it except to its personnel, advisers and subcontractors who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations, or as required by law or a competent authority.

4.2 Clause 4.1 does not apply to information that is or becomes public other than through breach, was lawfully known to the recipient beforehand, is independently developed, or is lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

4.3 The obligations in this clause 4 survive for 1 year after this Agreement ends.

## 5. Intellectual Property

5.1 Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property rights. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of any such rights.

5.2 The Customer grants the Supplier a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence during the term to use the Customer's name and materials solely as necessary to perform the Services. The Supplier shall not use the Customer's name or marks for publicity without prior written consent.

5.3 Any reports, data or documentation created by the Supplier specifically for the Customer under this Agreement are owned by the Customer, and the Supplier assigns all rights in them to the Customer, save that the Supplier retains its general methods and know-how.

## 6. Warranties

6.1 The Supplier warrants that it will perform the Services in accordance with clauses 3.2 and 3.3 and that it has the authority and capacity to enter into and perform this Agreement.

6.2 No express warranty period applies. The warranty in this clause 6 is a services warranty only. The Supplier shall promptly re-perform any Services that do not comply with it, at no additional charge. Except as expressly stated, all warranties and conditions implied by law are excluded to the extent permitted by law.

## 7. Insurance

7.1 The Supplier shall maintain throughout the term, with reputable insurers, public liability and other insurance appropriate to the Services, with cover of at least USD 2,000,000 per claim, and shall provide evidence of cover on request.

## 8. Indemnity

8.1 The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against losses, damages, fines and reasonable costs arising from third-party claims relating to (a) death or personal injury or damage to property caused by the negligence of the Supplier or its personnel, (b) the Supplier's breach of applicable environmental or waste laws, or (c) infringement of third-party intellectual property rights by the Services.

8.2 The indemnified party shall give prompt notice of any claim, allow the indemnifying party to conduct the defence and settlement (not to settle in a way that imposes liability on the indemnified party without its consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld), and give reasonable assistance at the indemnifying party's cost.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

9.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or any liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

9.2 Subject to clause 9.1, neither party is liable to the other, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, for any indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profit, revenue or goodwill, arising under or in connection with this Agreement.

9.3 Subject to clause 9.1, each party's aggregate liability arising under or in connection with this Agreement is limited to one (1) times the Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

## 10. Termination

10.1 Neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience. This Agreement may be terminated only for cause in accordance with this clause 10, or by expiry following notice of non-renewal under clause 1.2.

10.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party (a) commits a material breach that is incapable of remedy, or that is capable of remedy and is not remedied within 30 days of written notice requiring remedy, or (b) becomes insolvent, enters administration, liquidation or any analogous procedure, or ceases to carry on business.

10.3 On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall remove its containers and equipment from the Property within a reasonable time, and the Customer shall pay all undisputed Fees for Services performed up to the date of termination or expiry. Clauses 4, 5.3, 8, 9, 10.3, 12 and 13 and any other provision intended to survive shall continue in force.

## 11. Data Protection

11.1 The parties acknowledge that the Services do not involve the processing of personal data on behalf of the Customer. If the parties later agree that the Services will involve such processing, they shall first enter into a written data processing agreement complying with applicable data protection law.

## 12. General

12.1 **Assignment and subcontracting.** Neither party may assign, transfer or subcontract any of its rights or obligations without the other's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. The Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

12.2 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recorded delivery or by email to the address the receiving party has notified for that purpose, and are deemed received on delivery or, for email, at the time of transmission if sent on a business day during business hours, and otherwise on the next business day.

12.3 **Force majeure.** Neither party is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it promptly notifies the other and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. This clause does not excuse payment obligations.

12.4 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, with any agreed service schedule, is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions and understandings. Each party acknowledges it has not relied on any statement not set out in it.

12.5 **Variation and waiver.** No variation is effective unless in writing and signed by both parties. A failure or delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of it.

12.6 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect.

12.7 **Relationship.** The Supplier is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, agency or employment relationship.

12.8 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original.

## 13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

13.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it, including non-contractual disputes or claims, are governed by the laws of England and Wales.

13.2 The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any such dispute or claim.
