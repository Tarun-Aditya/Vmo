# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "Effective Date") between:

**Seabright Hotels & Resorts Ltd**, a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels ("we", "us" or the "Customer"); and

**Verdant Pest Control Ltd** ("you" or the "Supplier").

In this Agreement, "we" and "us" mean the Customer, and "you" means the Supplier. The services are delivered at individual hotels (each a "Property"). At the Effective Date, the Property covered by this Agreement is Seabright Lakeside Resort.

## What you are buying

You will provide pest control services at the Property (the "Services"). The Services include:

- inspection, monitoring, prevention and treatment of pests throughout the Property, including guest rooms, public areas, kitchens, food stores and external areas;
- written service reports after every visit, recording findings, treatments applied and recommended follow-up actions.

We may add further Properties or services only by a written variation signed by both of us. Any addition will state its own scope and fees.

## Visits and standards

You will visit the Property every month.

You will also visit the Property within 24 hours of any pest sighting reported by a guest. We will tell you about the report through the contact points we agree in writing.

You will perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using suitably qualified and trained staff, and in line with applicable law and good industry practice.

Your treatments must comply with food-safety standards for kitchens and food stores. You will use only products and methods suitable for food-handling areas, and you will keep records that show this.

While on site, your staff will follow our reasonable site rules, health and safety requirements and security procedures, and will cause as little disruption to guests as possible.

## How long this lasts

This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and runs until {{EXPIRY}} (the "Initial Term"), a period of 12 months.

After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of 12 months, unless either of us gives the other at least 30 days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

## How we charge

The annual fee for the Services is USD 36,000 (the "Fees"). The total contract value for the Initial Term is USD 36,000.

The Fees cover everything needed to perform the Services, including labour, materials, equipment and travel, unless we agree otherwise in writing.

You will send us valid invoices for the Fees. A valid invoice must identify the Property, the period covered and the amount due, and must be addressed to the Customer.

We will pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of receiving a valid invoice. If we dispute part of an invoice in good faith, we will tell you promptly, pay the undisputed part on time, and work with you to resolve the rest quickly.

Fees are stated exclusive of any applicable VAT or similar tax, which we will pay in addition against a valid tax invoice.

## Our promises to each other

You promise that the Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care and in accordance with this Agreement. This is a services warranty only. No express warranty period applies, and nothing in this Agreement limits any right we have to require you to re-perform defective Services.

You also promise that you hold, and will keep, all licences, certifications and approvals needed to perform the Services and to use the products you apply.

Each of us promises that we have authority to enter into this Agreement.

Except as set out in this Agreement, and to the extent the law allows, all other warranties and conditions are excluded.

## Insurance

You will maintain insurance with reputable insurers of at least USD 2,000,000 per claim, including public liability and professional indemnity cover. You will give us evidence of cover on request.

## Keeping things confidential

Each of us will keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other in connection with this Agreement, including pricing, operations, security arrangements and guest-related matters. We will each use it only to perform or receive the Services, and will disclose it only to our staff, advisers and insurers who need to know and who are bound by similar duties.

These duties do not apply to information that is public through no fault of the recipient, was already lawfully known to it, is independently developed, or must be disclosed by law or a regulator.

Our confidentiality obligations survive for 2 years after this Agreement ends.

## Personal data

The Services do not involve processing personal data on our behalf. If that changes, we will first agree in writing any data protection terms the law requires before any such processing starts.

## Who owns what

Each of us keeps ownership of the intellectual property we owned before this Agreement or develop independently of it.

We own the reports and records you produce specifically for the Property under this Agreement. You grant us a non-exclusive, perpetual licence to use any of your materials embedded in them for our own business purposes.

## If something goes wrong

**Liability cap.** Each party's aggregate liability to the other under or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, is limited to one (1) times the Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

**What the cap does not cover.** Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded.

**Indemnity.** You will indemnify us against losses, fines, and third-party claims arising from your breach of applicable law, or from damage to property or injury to people caused by your negligence or that of your staff or subcontractors in performing the Services. We will tell you promptly of any claim, let you control its defence with our reasonable cooperation, and not settle it without your consent (not to be unreasonably withheld). The indemnity is subject to the liability cap above to the extent the law allows.

Each of us will take reasonable steps to limit our losses.

## Ending this Agreement

**Our right to leave.** We may terminate this Agreement without cause on 30 days' written notice to you.

**For serious problems.** Either of us may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other:

- commits a material breach and, where it can be fixed, fails to fix it within a reasonable period after written notice; or
- becomes insolvent, enters administration, liquidation or a similar process, or stops carrying on business.

**What happens next.** On termination or expiry, you will complete or hand over any work in progress, return our property and confidential information, and invoice us for Services properly performed up to the end date. We will pay any undisputed amount in line with the payment terms above. Any Fees paid for periods after termination will be refunded to us on a pro-rata basis.

Clauses that by their nature should continue (including confidentiality, liability, indemnity and governing law) survive termination or expiry.

## Subcontracting and assignment

You may not subcontract any part of the Services or assign or transfer this Agreement without our prior written consent. You remain responsible for any subcontractor as if their acts were your own.

We may assign this Agreement to any member of our group or to a successor to the business of a Property on written notice to you.

## Events beyond our control

Neither of us is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond our reasonable control, provided we tell the other promptly and use reasonable efforts to carry on. This does not excuse a failure to carry out the monthly visit or the 24-hour response where it is reasonably possible to do so, and it does not excuse payment obligations for Services already performed.

## Notices

Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and sent by hand, by courier, or by email to the address or contact each of us gives the other for this purpose. A notice is treated as received when delivered by hand or courier, or when sent by email if sent during business hours (otherwise at the start of the next business day).
