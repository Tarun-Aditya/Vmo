# Statement of Work No. 1 – Security Services at Seabright Harbor Hotel

|---|---|

This Statement of Work No. 1 (the "SOW") is entered into with effect from {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "Effective Date") between **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc**, a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels (the "Customer"), and **Brightstar Security Services Inc** (the "Supplier"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the MSA dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between the same parties. Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meaning given in the MSA.

## 1. Background

1.1 The Customer operates a portfolio of six hotels (each a "Property"). Services under this SOW are delivered at one Property, Seabright Harbor Hotel (the "Harbor Hotel").

1.2 The Customer wishes to engage the Supplier to provide security services at the Harbor Hotel, and the Supplier has agreed to provide them on the terms of this SOW and the MSA.

1.3 In the event of conflict, the body of the MSA prevails over this SOW, except where this SOW expressly states that it varies a specified provision of the MSA.

## 2. Scope

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the following services at the Harbor Hotel (the "Services"):

(a) manned guarding of public areas, entrances, car parks and back-of-house areas;
(b) access control, including key and staff-pass checks and visitor management;
(c) patrols and monitoring, including operation of the Customer's CCTV control room;
(d) incident response, reporting and cooperation with emergency services; and
(e) support for security arrangements at events held at the Harbor Hotel.

2.2 The Services exclude cash-in-transit, close protection and any services at Properties other than the Harbor Hotel. Services at other Properties require a separate SOW.

2.3 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill, care and diligence, in compliance with applicable law and licensing requirements, and in accordance with the Customer's reasonable site rules and health and safety procedures notified to it.

## 3. Deliverables and Milestones

| No. | Deliverable / Milestone | Description | Target Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Site security assessment | Written risk assessment and proposed post orders for the Harbor Hotel | {{MS1}} |
| 2 | Mobilisation | Deployment of vetted and trained personnel and handover of control room | As agreed in writing under the mobilisation plan |
| 3 | Ongoing service | Delivery of the Services in accordance with Section 2 | Throughout the term |
| 4 | Monthly reports | Incident log, attendance records and service-level summary | Monthly in arrears |
| 5 | Annual review | Review of performance and security risks | Annually during the term |

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

4.1 **Supplier.** The Supplier shall appoint a contract manager, supply suitably licensed, vetted and trained personnel, maintain adequate supervision and rostering, and promptly replace any person whom the Customer reasonably requests be removed from the Harbor Hotel.

4.2 **Customer.** The Customer shall appoint a Harbor Hotel representative, provide timely access to premises, systems and information reasonably required, and notify the Supplier of changes to site rules.

4.3 **Governance.** The contract manager and the Customer's representative shall meet as reasonably required to review performance and resolve operational issues. Unresolved matters shall be escalated in accordance with the MSA.

## 5. Assumptions

5.1 The Harbor Hotel remains open and operating at broadly its current scale, and the Customer provides the equipment and systems (including CCTV and radios) listed in the mobilisation plan unless otherwise agreed.

5.2 Any change to the Services or staffing levels materially affecting cost shall be made only by written change order signed by both parties under the MSA's change control procedure.

5.3 If an assumption proves incorrect, the parties shall discuss in good faith any necessary adjustment, which shall take effect only when agreed in writing.

## 6. Fees and Invoicing

6.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier annual fees of USD 420,000 for the Services. The total contract value over the initial term of 24 months is USD 840,000.

6.2 The Supplier shall invoice the Fees in equal installments in arrears at the intervals stated in the mobilisation plan. Each invoice must be valid, setting out the SOW reference, the period covered and the amount due.

6.3 The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice. The Customer may withhold amounts disputed in good faith, shall notify the Supplier of the dispute with reasons, and the parties shall seek to resolve it promptly.

6.4 The Fees are inclusive of all costs of the Services. Taxes shall be dealt with as provided in the MSA.

## 7. Acceptance

7.1 The Supplier shall notify the Customer when a Deliverable is complete. The Customer shall review it within a reasonable period and either accept it in writing or give written reasons for rejection.

7.2 The Supplier shall promptly correct any non-conformity identified and resubmit the Deliverable. Ongoing Services are accepted by the Customer's continued approval of monthly reports, without prejudice to its other rights under the MSA.

## 8. Term and Renewal

8.1 This SOW starts on the Effective Date and continues until {{EXPIRY}}, an initial term of 24 months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

8.2 This SOW does not renew automatically. Any extension requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

## 9. Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this SOW for material breach by the other that is not remedied within a reasonable period after written notice, or on the other grounds provided in the MSA.

9.2 On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall return Customer property, keys, passes and records, cooperate in an orderly handover, and the Customer shall pay undisputed fees for Services properly performed up to the termination date.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other received in connection with this SOW, including security procedures, access arrangements, CCTV footage and guest information, use it only for performing this SOW, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know and are bound by equivalent duties, or as required by law. This obligation survives termination or expiry.

## 11. Intellectual Property

Each party retains its pre-existing intellectual property rights. Reports, logs and other materials produced specifically for the Customer under this SOW belong to the Customer, and the Supplier grants the Customer a perpetual license to use any Supplier materials incorporated in them for its business purposes.

## 12. Data Protection

Each party shall comply with applicable data protection law. The Supplier shall process personal data, including guest, staff and CCTV data, only on the Customer's documented instructions, apply appropriate technical and organizational security measures, ensure personnel are bound by confidentiality, assist the Customer with data subject requests and regulatory obligations, notify the Customer without undue delay of any personal data breach, and delete or return personal data on termination, in each case as further provided in the MSA.

## 13. Warranties

The Supplier warrants that it holds, and will maintain, all licenses and insurances required to perform the Services, that its personnel are suitably vetted, trained and licensed, and that the Services will be performed in accordance with this SOW and applicable law.

## 14. Liability and Indemnity

14.1 Each party's liability under this SOW is governed by the liability provisions of the MSA.

14.2 The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against third-party claims arising from the Supplier's personnel's negligence, willful misconduct or breach of law in performing the Services, subject to the conduct-of-claims and other provisions of the MSA.

## 15. Assignment and Subcontracting

Neither party may assign or transfer this SOW, or subcontract the Services, without the other's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. The Supplier remains responsible for any permitted subcontractor.

## 16. Notices

Notices under this SOW must be in writing and delivered by hand, courier or email to the address of the receiving party stated in the MSA, or any other address notified in writing, and are effective on receipt.

## 17. Governing Law and Disputes

This SOW and any dispute arising from it are governed by the laws of New York. Disputes shall be resolved in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions of the MSA.

## 18. Entire Agreement

This SOW and the MSA constitute the entire agreement of the parties on its subject matter and supersede prior discussions and understandings. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
