# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") by and between Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc, a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels ("**Seabright**" or the "**Customer**"), and Halcyon Elevator & Lift Maintenance Inc ("**Halcyon**" or the "**Supplier**"). Seabright and Halcyon are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## ARTICLE 1 – DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Properties**" means the six hotels owned and operated by Seabright at which the Services are to be delivered, as listed in the applicable Service Schedule, and "**Property**" means any one of them.

1.2 "**Services**" means the elevator and elevator maintenance, inspection, repair and related services described in Article 2 and in any Service Schedule.

1.3 "**Service Schedule**" means a written schedule agreed and signed by both Parties that identifies the Properties, equipment, scope and any specific requirements for the Services.

1.4 "**Elevator Fault**" means any malfunction, breakdown, entrapment or failure of an elevator, elevator or associated equipment at a Property that impairs its safe or normal operation.

1.5 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form, disclosed by or on behalf of a Party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including technical, operational, commercial and security information relating to the Properties.

1.6 "**Initial Term**" and "**Renewal Term**" have the meanings given in Section 3.1 and Section 3.2.

## ARTICLE 2 – SERVICES

2.1 **Scope.** Halcyon shall provide the Services to Seabright at each Property in accordance with this Agreement and each Service Schedule.

2.2 **Fault Attendance.** Halcyon shall attend any Elevator Fault at a Property within 24 hours of being notified of that Elevator Fault by Seabright or Property personnel.

2.3 **Standard of Performance.** Halcyon shall perform the Services with reasonable skill, care and diligence, using suitably qualified and trained personnel, and in compliance with applicable laws, regulations, safety standards and statutory inspection requirements.

2.4 **Site Rules.** While at a Property, Halcyon's personnel shall comply with Seabright's reasonable site, health, safety and security rules notified to Halcyon, and shall use reasonable efforts to minimize disruption to guests and hotel operations.

2.5 **Subcontracting.** Halcyon shall not subcontract any part of the Services without Seabright's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Halcyon remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

## ARTICLE 3 – TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}}, being a period of 36 months (the "**Initial Term**").

3.2 **Automatic Renewal.** Upon expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for successive periods of 12 months (each a "**Renewal Term**") unless a Party gives the other at least 180 days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term.

## ARTICLE 4 – FEES AND INVOICING

4.1 **Fees.** Seabright shall pay Halcyon the fees set out in the applicable Service Schedule (the "**Fees**"). Unless a Service Schedule states otherwise, the Fees are exclusive of applicable value added or sales taxes, which shall be shown separately on the invoice.

4.2 **Invoices.** Halcyon shall submit invoices to Seabright following performance of the relevant Services. A "**Valid Invoice**" is an invoice that is addressed to the correct Seabright entity, identifies the Property and Services concerned, is itemized and accurately calculated, and complies with applicable tax requirements.

4.3 **Payment.** Seabright shall pay all undisputed amounts within 10 days of receipt of a Valid Invoice.

4.4 **Disputed Amounts.** If Seabright disputes any invoiced amount in good faith, it shall notify Halcyon promptly, giving reasons, and pay the undisputed portion in accordance with Section 4.3. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly, and Halcyon shall continue to perform the Services during any dispute.

## ARTICLE 5 – CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall (a) use Confidential Information solely to perform or receive the benefit of this Agreement; (b) not disclose it to any third party except as permitted by this Article; and (c) protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and in any event reasonable care.

5.2 **Permitted Disclosure.** The receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its employees, officers, professional advisers, insurers and approved subcontractors who need to know it and who are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations, and as required by law, court order or regulatory authority, provided that, where lawful, it gives the disclosing Party prompt notice.

5.3 **Exclusions.** This Article does not apply to information that is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement, was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, is lawfully received from a third party without restriction, or is independently developed without use of Confidential Information.

5.4 **Survival.** The obligations in this Article 5 survive for 2 years after this Agreement ends.

## ARTICLE 6 – INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 **Pre-Existing Rights.** Each Party retains all rights in its pre-existing intellectual property and that which it develops independently of this Agreement.

6.2 **Deliverables.** Reports, inspection records, logs and other materials prepared by Halcyon specifically for Seabright in the course of the Services are owned by Seabright upon creation, and Halcyon assigns to Seabright all rights therein. Halcyon retains ownership of its general know-how, tools and methodologies and grants Seabright a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use any Halcyon materials embedded in such deliverables for Seabright's internal business purposes.

## ARTICLE 7 – WARRANTIES

7.1 **Services Warranty.** Halcyon warrants that the Services will be performed in accordance with Section 2.3 and the applicable Service Schedule. If any Services do not conform, Halcyon shall, at its own cost, promptly re-perform them.

7.2 **No Express Warranty Period.** This Agreement does not provide any express warranty period. The only warranty given is the services warranty in Section 7.1.

7.3 **Authority.** Each Party warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

7.4 **Statutory Rights.** Nothing in this Article excludes any warranty, condition or right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## ARTICLE 8 – INSURANCE

8.1 Halcyon shall maintain throughout the term of this Agreement, with reputable insurers, insurance covering its liabilities under this Agreement, including public liability and professional indemnity cover, of at least USD 1,000,000 per claim.

8.2 Halcyon shall provide evidence of such insurance to Seabright on reasonable request.

## ARTICLE 9 – INDEMNITY

9.1 **By Halcyon.** Halcyon shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seabright and its affiliates, officers and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising out of any third-party claim relating to (a) death or personal injury or damage to property caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Halcyon or its personnel or subcontractors, or (b) Halcyon's breach of applicable law in performing the Services.

9.2 **By Seabright.** Seabright shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Halcyon and its officers and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising out of any third-party claim to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Seabright or its personnel.

9.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall promptly notify the indemnifying Party of any claim, allow it to control the defense and settlement (provided no settlement admits fault on the indemnified Party's behalf without consent), and give reasonable assistance at the indemnifying Party's cost.

## ARTICLE 10 – LIABILITY

10.1 The liability of each Party to the other under or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty or otherwise, is unlimited. No cap applies to the liability of either Party.

10.2 Nothing in this Agreement excludes or limits any liability that cannot be excluded or limited by law.

## ARTICLE 11 – TERMINATION

11.1 **No Termination for Convenience.** Neither Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience. This Agreement may be terminated only in accordance with this Article 11.

11.2 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other if the other Party (a) commits a material breach that is incapable of remedy, or commits a material breach that is capable of remedy and fails to remedy it within 30 days of written notice requiring remedy; or (b) becomes insolvent, enters administration, liquidation or receivership, makes an arrangement with its creditors, or ceases to carry on business.

11.3 **Consequences.** On termination or expiry, Halcyon shall promptly return all Seabright property and Confidential Information, Seabright shall pay all undisputed Fees for Services properly performed up to the date of termination, and accrued rights and remedies of the Parties are unaffected.

11.4 **Survival.** Articles 1, 5, 6, 9, 10, 12 and 13 and any other provision intended to survive shall survive termination or expiry, subject to the period stated in Section 5.4.

## ARTICLE 12 – DATA PROTECTION

12.1 The Parties acknowledge that the Services do not involve the processing of personal data on behalf of Seabright. Halcyon shall not access, collect or otherwise process personal data of guests, staff or others in the course of the Services.

12.2 If either Party becomes aware that the Services have come to involve the processing of personal data, it shall notify the other promptly, and the Parties shall agree in writing appropriate data protection terms before any such processing takes place.

## ARTICLE 13 – GENERAL

13.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement, and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it (including non-contractual disputes), is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

13.2 **Jurisdiction.** the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York have exclusive jurisdiction over any such dispute.

13.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign, transfer or subcontract any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld, except as provided in Section 2.5.

13.4 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognized courier or by registered post to the address of the receiving Party stated in the relevant Service Schedule (or as otherwise notified), and are deemed received on delivery. Notices of breach, termination or non-renewal may not be given by email alone.

13.5 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure to perform (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other promptly and uses reasonable efforts to mitigate. Section 2.2 shall continue to apply to the extent performance remains reasonably possible.

13.6 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with its Service Schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, representations and agreements. Each Party acknowledges that it has not relied on any statement not set out in this Agreement.

13.7 **Amendment and Waiver.** No amendment is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of it.

13.8 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remainder of this Agreement continues in full force and effect.

13.9 **Order of Precedence.** If there is a conflict, the body of this Agreement prevails over a Service Schedule unless the Service Schedule expressly states that it overrides a specified provision.

13.10 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
