# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement (this **"Agreement"**) is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the **"Effective Date"**) by and between Seabright Hotels & Resorts Ltd (**"Seabright"** or the **"Customer"**), a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels, and Halcyon Elevator & Lift Maintenance Ltd (**"Halcyon"** or the **"Supplier"**). Seabright and Halcyon are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

## ARTICLE 1 – DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"Properties"** means the six hotels owned and operated by Seabright at which the Services are delivered, as identified in writing by Seabright to Halcyon from time to time.

1.2 **"Services"** means the maintenance, inspection, repair and fault-response services for passenger lifts, elevators and associated equipment at the Properties, as described in Article 2 and in any Order.

1.3 **"Order"** means a written work order or schedule issued by Seabright and accepted by Halcyon that describes Services for one or more Properties.

1.4 **"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including information concerning the Properties, guests, security arrangements and pricing.

1.5 **"Term"** means the Initial Term and each Renewal Term, as defined in Article 3.

## ARTICLE 2 – SERVICES

2.1 **Scope.** Halcyon shall provide the Services at the Properties in accordance with this Agreement, each Order, applicable law and good industry practice.

2.2 **Fault Attendance.** Halcyon shall attend any lift fault at a Property within twenty-four (24) hours of being notified of the fault by Seabright or the relevant Property.

2.3 **Personnel.** Halcyon shall use suitably qualified, trained and, where required by law, licensed personnel, and shall ensure that its personnel comply with the reasonable site, safety and security rules of each Property.

2.4 **Access and Cooperation.** Seabright shall provide Halcyon with reasonable access to the relevant equipment and premises and such information as Halcyon reasonably requires to perform the Services.

2.5 **Guest Operations.** Halcyon shall perform the Services so as to minimize disruption to guests and hotel operations and shall coordinate planned shutdowns of lifts with the relevant Property in advance.

2.6 **Subcontracting.** Halcyon shall not subcontract any part of the Services without Seabright's prior written consent, and shall remain responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

## ARTICLE 3 – TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Article 11, continues until {{EXPIRY}}, being a period of thirty-six (36) months (the **"Initial Term"**).

3.2 **Automatic Renewal.** Upon expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months (each a **"Renewal Term"**) unless a Party gives the other Party at least one hundred eighty (180) days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term.

## ARTICLE 4 – FEES AND INVOICING

4.1 **Fees.** Seabright shall pay Halcyon the fees set out in the applicable Order for the Services performed (the **"Fees"**). Unless an Order states otherwise, the Fees are the entire consideration for the Services, including labor, travel and routine consumables.

4.2 **Invoices.** Halcyon shall submit invoices to Seabright following performance of the Services. A **"valid invoice"** is one that is addressed to Seabright, identifies the Property and Order to which it relates, itemizes the Services and Fees, and is otherwise accurate and complete.

4.3 **Payment.** Seabright shall pay each undisputed invoice within sixty (60) days of receipt of a valid invoice.

4.4 **Disputed Amounts.** Seabright may withhold payment of any amount it disputes in good faith, shall notify Halcyon of the dispute and its basis promptly, and shall pay any undisputed portion in accordance with Section 4.3. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

4.5 **Taxes.** Fees are stated exclusive of applicable sales, value-added or similar taxes, which shall be invoiced separately where properly chargeable.

## ARTICLE 5 – CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall (a) use Confidential Information solely to perform or receive the Services, (b) not disclose it to any third party other than its personnel, advisers and permitted subcontractors who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations, and (c) protect it with at least the degree of care it applies to its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care.

5.2 **Exclusions.** Section 5.1 does not apply to information that is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement, was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, is independently developed without use of Confidential Information, or is lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

5.3 **Compelled Disclosure.** A Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or a court or regulatory authority, provided it gives the disclosing Party prompt notice where lawful and reasonably cooperates in seeking protective treatment.

5.4 **Survival.** The obligations in this Article 5 survive for two (2) years after this Agreement ends.

## ARTICLE 6 – INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 **Ownership.** Each Party retains all right, title and interest in its pre-existing intellectual property. Seabright owns all reports, records and deliverables prepared specifically for it under this Agreement, excluding Halcyon's pre-existing tools, methods and know-how.

6.2 **Licence.** Halcyon grants Seabright a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use any Halcyon materials incorporated in deliverables to the extent necessary to use those deliverables for the operation of the Properties.

## ARTICLE 7 – WARRANTIES

7.1 **Services Warranty.** Halcyon warrants that the Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care, in a professional and workmanlike manner, and in accordance with applicable law and the requirements of this Agreement.

7.2 **Remedy.** If Services fail to conform to Section 7.1, Halcyon shall, at its own cost and promptly after notice, re-perform the non-conforming Services.

7.3 **No Express Warranty Period.** This Agreement provides no express warranty period. The warranty in this Article 7 is a services warranty only.

7.4 **Authority.** Each Party warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 8 – DATA PROTECTION

8.1 **No Personal Data.** The Parties acknowledge that the Services do not involve the processing of personal data. Neither Party shall be required to process personal data on behalf of the other under this Agreement.

8.2 **Incidental Access.** If Halcyon's personnel incidentally encounter personal data at a Property, Halcyon shall not access, copy, retain or use it, and shall notify Seabright promptly. If the Parties later agree that the Services will involve personal data, they shall first enter into a written agreement addressing it.

## ARTICLE 9 – INDEMNITY AND LIABILITY

9.1 **Indemnity by Halcyon.** Halcyon shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seabright and its affiliates, officers and employees from losses, damages, costs and expenses arising from third-party claims for bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property to the extent caused by the negligence or wilful misconduct of Halcyon or its personnel or subcontractors in performing the Services, or by Halcyon's breach of law.

9.2 **Indemnity by Seabright.** Seabright shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Halcyon and its officers and employees from losses, damages, costs and expenses arising from third-party claims to the extent caused by the negligence or wilful misconduct of Seabright or its personnel.

9.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control its defense and settlement (provided no settlement admits fault on the indemnified Party's behalf without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

9.4 **Unlimited Liability.** The liability of each Party under or in connection with this Agreement is unlimited. Nothing in this Agreement caps or excludes the liability of either Party.

## ARTICLE 10 – INSURANCE

10.1 **Cover.** Halcyon shall maintain throughout the Term, with reputable insurers, insurance covering its liabilities under this Agreement, including public and products liability, of at least USD 1,000,000 per claim.

10.2 **Evidence.** Halcyon shall provide evidence of such insurance on Seabright's reasonable request. Maintaining insurance does not limit Halcyon's liability under this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 11 – TERMINATION

11.1 **No Termination for Convenience.** Neither Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience. This Agreement may be terminated only for cause in accordance with this Article 11.

11.2 **Termination for Cause.** A Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party (a) commits a material breach that is incapable of remedy, (b) commits a material breach that is capable of remedy and fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of written notice requiring remedy, or (c) becomes insolvent, enters administration, liquidation or any analogous proceeding, or ceases to carry on business.

11.3 **Effect.** On termination or expiry, Halcyon shall hand over all records relating to the Properties' equipment, Seabright shall pay undisputed Fees for Services properly performed up to the termination date, and each Party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information on request, subject to Section 5.4.

11.4 **Survival.** Provisions that by their nature are intended to survive termination or expiry, including Articles 5, 6, 9 and 13, survive.

## ARTICLE 12 – GENERAL

12.1 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement or any right or obligation under it without the other Party's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

12.2 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognized courier or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the address a Party notifies to the other for this purpose. Notices are effective on receipt.

12.3 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with each Order, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior understandings. In the event of conflict, the body of this Agreement prevails over an Order unless the Order expressly states otherwise.

12.4 **Amendment and Waiver.** No amendment is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver.

12.5 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other promptly and uses reasonable efforts to mitigate. This Section does not excuse payment obligations for Services performed.

12.6 **Independent Contractors; Severability.** The Parties are independent contractors. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect.

12.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original.

## ARTICLE 13 – GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

13.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of England and Wales.

13.2 **Escalation.** The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith discussions between senior representatives before commencing proceedings.

13.3 **Jurisdiction.** Subject to Section 13.2, the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction over any such dispute.
