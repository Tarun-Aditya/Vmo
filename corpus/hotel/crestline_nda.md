# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Agreement is made on {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "Effective Date") between **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Ltd**, a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels (the "Customer"), and **Crestline Laundry & Linen Services Ltd** (the "Supplier"). Each is a "Party" and together the "Parties".

## Term and Fees

1. **Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until {{EXPIRY}} (the "Initial Term", being 36 months), unless terminated earlier under clause 14.
2. **Fees.** No fees are payable by either Party under this Agreement. Each Party bears its own costs of performing it. Any fees for laundry and linen services shall be set out in a separate written agreement, and nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to enter into one.

## Purpose and Definitions

3. **Purpose.** The Parties wish to discuss and may enter into a commercial relationship under which the Supplier provides laundry and linen services to the Customer at its individual hotels (the "Properties") (the "Purpose"). Confidential Information may be disclosed solely for the Purpose.
4. **Confidential Information** means all information, in any form, disclosed by or on behalf of a Party (the "Discloser") to the other Party (the "Recipient") in connection with the Purpose that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would regard as confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. It includes, without limitation, pricing, volumes, service specifications, Property layouts and operating procedures, guest and employee information, supplier and customer lists, business plans, systems and technical information, and the existence and terms of any negotiations between the Parties.
5. **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show: (a) is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to it before disclosure without restriction; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction on disclosure; or (d) is independently developed without use of or reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## Obligations

6. **Duty of confidence.** The Recipient shall: (a) keep the Discloser's Confidential Information confidential; (b) use it only for the Purpose; (c) not disclose it to any person except as permitted by this Agreement; and (d) protect it using at least the degree of care it applies to its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care.
7. **Permitted disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its and its affiliates' directors, employees, professional advisers, insurers and approved subcontractors, and, in the case of the Customer, to the management of the Properties, in each case who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by written or professional obligations of confidence no less protective than this Agreement. The Recipient is liable for any breach of this Agreement by those persons.
8. **Compelled disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, court order or a regulatory authority, provided that, where lawful, it gives the Discloser prompt prior notice, cooperates in any effort to limit the disclosure, and discloses only what is legally required.
9. **Return and destruction.** On written request or on termination or expiry, the Recipient shall promptly return or securely destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information and confirm in writing that it has done so. The Recipient may retain copies to the extent required by law or by bona fide internal backup or compliance procedures, which remain subject to this Agreement for as long as they are retained.
10. **Notification of breach.** The Recipient shall notify the Discloser without undue delay on becoming aware of any unauthorised use or disclosure of Confidential Information and shall take reasonable steps to mitigate its effects.

## Rights, Data and Warranties

11. **Intellectual property.** All Confidential Information and all intellectual property rights in it remain the property of the Discloser. No licence or right is granted other than the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose. Neither Party shall reverse engineer, analyse or copy Confidential Information except as needed for the Purpose.
12. **Data protection.** If Confidential Information includes personal data, each Party shall comply with applicable data protection law, including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, shall process such data only as necessary for the Purpose, and shall implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures. Neither Party shall disclose guest or employee personal data unless necessary for the Purpose and permitted by law. If the Parties later enter into services involving personal data, they shall agree appropriate data processing terms.
13. **Warranties, liability and remedies.** Confidential Information is provided "as is", and the Discloser gives no warranty as to its accuracy or completeness, save that it warrants it has the right to disclose it. Neither Party is liable for loss arising from the other's use of Confidential Information in accordance with this Agreement. Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for fraud, death or personal injury caused by negligence, or any liability that cannot lawfully be limited. Each Party shall indemnify the other against losses, costs and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising from its breach of clauses 6 to 10. The Recipient acknowledges that damages alone may be an inadequate remedy for breach and that the Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief or specific performance without proof of special damage, in addition to any other remedy.

## Termination and Survival

14. **Termination.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time on written notice to the other. Either Party may terminate immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is incapable of remedy or is not remedied within a reasonable period after written notice requiring remedy.
15. **Survival.** The obligations of confidentiality and non-use in this Agreement continue during the term and survive for 3 years after this Agreement ends, whether through expiry or termination. Clauses 9, 11, 13, 16 and 20 survive for as long as necessary to give them effect. Termination does not affect accrued rights or remedies.

## General

16. **No obligation or partnership.** Nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to disclose any information, to proceed with any transaction, or to enter into any further agreement. No Party is the agent, partner or employee of the other. Neither Party acquires any right in respect of the other's Confidential Information except as stated here.
17. **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign, transfer or subcontract any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld, save that the Customer may disclose to its affiliates as permitted by clause 7.
18. **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recorded delivery post, or by email to the address most recently notified by the receiving Party for this purpose, and are deemed received on delivery or, in the case of email, at the time of transmission if sent during business hours on a business day, and otherwise on the next business day.
19. **Entire agreement; variation; waiver.** This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior understandings relating to it. It may be varied only in writing signed by both Parties. A failure or delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of it. If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect.
20. **Governing law and jurisdiction.** This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it (including non-contractual disputes) are governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
21. **Third party rights; counterparts.** A person who is not a Party has no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 to enforce any term of this Agreement. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
