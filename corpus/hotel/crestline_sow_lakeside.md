This Statement of Work ("SOW") is entered into between **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc** (the "Customer"), a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels, and **Crestline Laundry & Linen Services Inc** (the "Supplier"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between the same parties (the "Parent Agreement"). Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Parent Agreement. If this SOW conflicts with the Parent Agreement, the Parent Agreement prevails unless this SOW expressly states that it overrides a specified provision.

## 1. Background

1.1 The Customer requires a reliable laundry and linen service for its hotels. Services are delivered at individual hotels (each a "Property").

1.2 This SOW applies to one Property only: **Seabright Lakeside Resort** (the "Property"). The Customer may agree further statements of work for other hotels in its group under the Parent Agreement.

1.3 The Supplier has agreed to provide the Services described below on the terms of this SOW and the Parent Agreement.

## 2. Scope

2.1 The Supplier shall collect soiled linen from the Property, launder, finish and return it, in each case in accordance with the Parent Agreement, this SOW and good industry practice. "Linen" includes bed linen, towels, bath mats, table linen, napkins and other textile items supplied for laundering by the Property.

2.2 Collection shall take place five days a week, on days and at times agreed in writing between the Property's General Manager (or delegate) and the Supplier. Clean linen shall be returned in accordance with the agreed schedule.

2.3 The Supplier shall launder, inspect, fold or press, count and package linen to the standard of hygiene and presentation reasonably required by the Customer's brand standards notified to the Supplier in writing.

2.4 Services that are not described in this SOW, including linen purchase, uniform services or deep-cleaning of non-linen items, are outside scope unless agreed under the change control procedure in section 8.

## 3. Deliverables and Milestones

| No. | Deliverable / Milestone | Responsible | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Joint onboarding meeting; agreement of collection schedule, linen inventory and brand standards | Both parties | Before service commencement |
| 2 | Baseline count and condition record of Property linen stock | Supplier, with Property staff | Before first collection |
| 3 | Commencement of five-day-a-week collection and return service | Supplier | Effective Date |
| 4 | Monthly service report (volumes in kilograms, turnaround, rejects, losses and replacements) | Supplier | Monthly in arrears |
| 5 | Periodic service review meeting | Both parties | As agreed, at least quarterly |
| 6 | Replacement of lost or damaged linen under section 5.3 | Supplier | Promptly after identification |

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

4.1 **Supplier.** The Supplier shall provide suitably trained and supervised personnel, vehicles and equipment; comply with applicable laws and the Property's reasonable site, health and safety and security rules; and keep accurate weighing and count records.

4.2 **Customer.** The Customer shall procure that the Property provides safe access, a designated collection and return point, linen presented in the agreed manner and segregated where required, and a named contact for day-to-day matters.

4.3 **Relationship managers.** Each party shall nominate a relationship manager by written notice. Operational issues shall first be raised with the relationship managers before escalation under the Parent Agreement.

## 5. Assumptions and Service Terms

5.1 The Customer's linen is presented in reasonable condition, without hazardous items, sharps or non-linen articles.

5.2 Linen weight is determined by the Supplier's calibrated scales at collection, with the Property entitled to witness weighing and to dispute a recorded weight promptly.

5.3 **Loss and damage.** The Supplier shall replace, at its own cost, any linen lost or damaged beyond normal wear while in its custody or as a result of its processing. Replacement items shall be of equivalent or better quality and specification to those replaced.

5.4 Normal wear and tear and items damaged by foreign objects left in linen by the Customer are not the Supplier's responsibility, provided the Supplier notified the Property promptly of the relevant circumstances.

5.5 Title to linen remains with the Customer at all times.

## 6. Fees and Invoicing

6.1 **Laundering charge.** The Customer shall pay a laundering charge of USD 1.58 per kilogram of linen, collected five days a week.

6.2 **Annual fees.** The annual fees for the Services are USD 265,000, and the total contract value for the Initial Term is USD 530,000. These figures reflect the parties' expected volumes and are the basis on which the Services are priced.

6.3 **Invoicing.** The Supplier shall invoice monthly in arrears, showing weights collected, the applicable rate and any agreed credits. An invoice is valid only if it is accurate, itemised and addressed to the Customer in accordance with the Parent Agreement.

6.4 **Payment.** The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 45 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

6.5 **Disputes.** The Customer shall notify the Supplier of any disputed amount with reasons and pay the undisputed balance on time. The parties shall seek to resolve disputes under the Parent Agreement.

6.6 All sums are exclusive of sales tax, which shall be added where applicable at the prevailing rate on receipt of a valid invoice.

## 7. Acceptance

7.1 The Customer may, acting reasonably, reject any linen returned that is not clean, undamaged or finished to the agreed standard. The Supplier shall re-process rejected linen promptly at no additional charge.

7.2 The Customer shall review each monthly service report and notify the Supplier of any objections within a reasonable period after receipt. Absence of objection does not waive the Customer's rights in respect of latent defects or the Supplier's replacement obligations.

7.3 Milestones 1 to 3 are accepted when the Customer confirms in writing that the relevant step has been completed satisfactorily, such confirmation not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed.

## 8. Change Control

Any change to scope, schedule or fees requires a written change request signed by both parties. Neither party is obliged to perform a change until it is so agreed.

## 9. Term, Renewal and Termination

9.1 This SOW takes effect on {{EFFECTIVE}} and continues for the Initial Term until {{EXPIRY}}, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this SOW or the Parent Agreement.

9.2 This SOW does not renew automatically. Any extension requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

9.3 Either party may terminate this SOW in accordance with the termination provisions of the Parent Agreement. On expiry or termination, the Supplier shall promptly return all Customer linen in its possession, and the Customer shall pay undisputed fees for Services performed up to the termination date.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other party received in connection with this SOW, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations, or as required by law, in each case as provided in the Parent Agreement.

## 11. Intellectual Property

Each party retains its pre-existing intellectual property rights. The Supplier shall not use the Customer's names, logos or marks except as necessary to perform the Services. Any reports prepared specifically for the Customer under this SOW may be used by the Customer for its internal business purposes.

## 12. Warranties

The Supplier warrants that it shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using appropriately qualified personnel, in compliance with applicable laws, and that the Services and processes used will meet the standards set out in this SOW. The warranties in the Parent Agreement also apply.

## 13. Liability and Indemnity

The liability of each party under or in connection with this SOW, and any indemnities, are governed by the liability and indemnity provisions of the Parent Agreement. Nothing in this SOW limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

## 14. Data Protection

Each party shall comply with applicable data protection law in connection with this SOW. To the extent the Supplier processes personal data on the Customer's behalf (for example, guest or staff information appearing on laundry tags or in records), it shall do so only on the Customer's documented instructions and in accordance with the data protection provisions of the Parent Agreement.

## 15. Assignment and Subcontracting

Neither party may assign or transfer its rights or obligations under this SOW without the other party's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. The Supplier shall not subcontract any part of the Services except as permitted by the Parent Agreement, and remains responsible for its subcontractors.

## 16. Notices

Notices under this SOW shall be in writing and delivered in accordance with the notice provisions of the Parent Agreement. Operational communications between relationship managers may be made by email and do not constitute formal notices.

## 17. Entire Agreement and Variation

This SOW and the Parent Agreement constitute the entire agreement between the parties in relation to the Services at the Property and supersede all prior discussions and understandings on that subject. No variation is effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.

## 18. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This SOW and any non-contractual obligations arising out of it are governed by the laws of the State of New York. The courts specified in the Parent Agreement shall have jurisdiction over any dispute, subject to any dispute resolution procedure in the Parent Agreement.
