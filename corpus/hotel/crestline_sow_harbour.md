This Statement of Work No. 1 (the "**SOW**") is entered into between Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc (the "**Customer**"), a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels, and Crestline Laundry & Linen Services Inc (the "**Supplier**"). It is issued under the Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between the same parties (the "**Parent Agreement**"). Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Parent Agreement. If this SOW conflicts with the Parent Agreement, the Parent Agreement prevails unless this SOW expressly states otherwise for a specified provision.

## 1. Background

1.1 The Customer owns and operates six hotels (each a "**Property**"). The Customer requires professional laundry and linen services, and the services under this SOW are delivered at Seabright Harbor Hotel (the "**Property**" for the purposes of this SOW).

1.2 The Supplier operates commercial laundry and linen processing facilities and has agreed to provide the Services to the Customer at the Property on the terms of this SOW and the Parent Agreement.

## 2. Scope

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the following services at and for the Property (the "**Services**"):

(a) collection of soiled linen from the Property;
(b) laundering, finishing and inspection of linen to the standards set out in the Parent Agreement and to good industry practice;
(c) delivery of clean linen to the Property in accordance with the agreed schedule;
(d) tracking, counting and reconciliation of linen items on collection and delivery; and
(e) replacement of linen in accordance with clause 2.3.

2.2 **Laundering charge.** The laundering charge is USD 1.42 per kilogram of linen, collected daily. Linen weights shall be recorded at collection and countersigned by a representative of the Property.

2.3 **Replacement of linen.** The Supplier shall replace, at its own cost, any linen that is lost or damaged beyond normal wear while in the Supplier's possession or as a result of the Supplier's processing.

2.4 Services for any hotel other than the Property require a separate statement of work or a written variation to this SOW.

## 3. Deliverables and Milestones

| No. | Deliverable / Milestone | Responsible | Target Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Joint linen inventory and baseline count at the Property | Both parties | {{MS1}} |
| 2 | Agreed collection and delivery schedule | Supplier | Before commencement of Services |
| 3 | Daily collection, laundering and delivery of linen | Supplier | From the Effective Date, throughout the Period |
| 4 | Monthly linen reconciliation and loss/damage report | Supplier | Monthly during the Period |
| 5 | Replacement of lost or damaged linen | Supplier | Promptly upon identification |

Any further dates shall be agreed in writing between the parties. No date is to be inferred where none is stated.

## 4. Roles

4.1 **Supplier.** The Supplier shall provide suitably skilled and trained personnel, appropriate equipment and transport, and shall appoint a named account manager as the primary contact for the Customer.

4.2 **Customer.** The Customer shall procure that the Property provides reasonable access, designated collection and storage areas, and timely information reasonably required by the Supplier. The Customer's contract owner is Helena Cruz, VP Technology Sourcing, or her successor notified in writing.

4.3 **Governance.** The parties shall hold review meetings as reasonably required to discuss performance, volumes and any issues.

## 5. Assumptions

5.1 Linen supplied for processing is in a condition suitable for commercial laundering and is placed in designated containers at the agreed collection point.

5.2 The Property will make linen available for collection daily in accordance with the agreed schedule.

5.3 The Supplier's obligations are dependent on the Customer's performance of its responsibilities in clause 4.2. The Supplier shall promptly notify the Customer of any failure that may affect performance, and shall not be liable for delay to the extent directly caused by such failure.

5.4 The Supplier shall comply with the Property's reasonable site, health and safety and security requirements when attending the Property.

## 6. Fees and Invoicing

6.1 The annual fees under this SOW are USD 310,000. The total contract value over the 24-month Period is USD 620,000. The laundering charge in clause 2.2 is the basis on which the fees are calculated.

6.2 The Supplier shall issue invoices to the Customer in accordance with the Parent Agreement. A valid invoice shall identify this SOW, the Property, the period covered, recorded linen weights and any applicable taxes.

6.3 The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 45 days of receipt of a valid invoice. The Customer may withhold payment of any amount disputed in good faith, shall notify the Supplier of the dispute with reasons, and the parties shall work together promptly to resolve it.

6.4 Fees are exclusive of applicable value added or sales taxes, which shall be shown separately on the invoice.

## 7. Acceptance

7.1 The Customer may inspect the Services and deliverables. The Customer shall notify the Supplier of any non-conformity within a reasonable period after delivery or receipt of a report.

7.2 The Supplier shall promptly re-perform or correct non-conforming Services at no additional charge. Deliverables are deemed accepted when the Customer confirms acceptance in writing or when the Customer does not notify a non-conformity within a reasonable period after delivery.

7.3 Acceptance does not waive the Supplier's obligations under clause 2.3 or its warranties.

## 8. Term and Renewal

8.1 This SOW commences on {{EFFECTIVE}} and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms or the Parent Agreement, continues until {{EXPIRY}}.

8.2 This SOW does not renew automatically. Any extension requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other party received in connection with this SOW, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations, or as required by law. These obligations are in addition to those in the Parent Agreement.

## 10. Intellectual Property

Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property rights. Nothing in this SOW transfers any such rights. The Customer's branding, marks and linen specifications remain the Customer's property, and the Supplier may use them solely to perform the Services.

## 11. Warranties

The Supplier warrants that the Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care, by suitably qualified personnel, in compliance with applicable law and in accordance with this SOW and the Parent Agreement. The Supplier further warrants that it holds, and will maintain, all licenses and permits needed to provide the Services.

## 12. Liability and Indemnity

12.1 Each party's liability under or in connection with this SOW is governed by the liability provisions of the Parent Agreement.

12.2 Indemnities, if any, apply as set out in the Parent Agreement and extend to the Services under this SOW. Nothing in this SOW limits or excludes liability that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded.

## 13. Termination

13.1 Either party may terminate this SOW in the circumstances, and on the notice, provided for in the Parent Agreement.

13.2 On expiry or termination, the Supplier shall promptly return all Customer linen in its possession, complete any outstanding replacement under clause 2.3, and provide a final reconciliation. The Customer shall pay undisputed fees for Services properly performed up to the termination date, in accordance with clause 6.3.

## 14. Data Protection

Each party shall comply with applicable data protection law in relation to any personal data processed in connection with this SOW. The Supplier shall process any such personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions and as required under the Parent Agreement, and shall implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures. Linen records should not include guest personal data. The Supplier shall promptly notify the Customer of any security incident affecting such data.

## 15. Assignment and Subcontracting

Neither party may assign, transfer or subcontract any of its rights or obligations under this SOW without the prior written consent of the other party, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld. The Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of any permitted subcontractor.

## 16. Notices

Notices under this SOW shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by courier or by email to the address or contact notified by each party for the purposes of the Parent Agreement, and take effect on receipt.

## 17. Entire Agreement

This SOW, together with the Parent Agreement, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties relating to its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, understandings and agreements relating to it. Any variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.

## 18. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This SOW and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of the State of New York. The courts determined under the Parent Agreement have jurisdiction over any such dispute.
