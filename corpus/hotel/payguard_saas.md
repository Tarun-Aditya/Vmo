# SaaS Subscription Agreement

This SaaS Subscription Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made with effect from {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") between **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Ltd**, a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels (the "**Customer**"), and **PayGuard Payments Pte Ltd** (the "**Supplier**"). The Customer and the Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

The Agreement consists of these master terms, Schedule 1 (Service Levels and Security), and Order Form No. 1 (the "**Order Form**"). If there is a conflict, the master terms prevail over the Schedules, except that the Order Form prevails on commercial terms (fees, term and service level targets).

## 1. Definitions

In this Agreement:

"**Properties**" means the individual hotels owned and operated by the Customer at which the Services are delivered and used, as listed in the Order Form.

"**Services**" means the hosted payment processing and related software-as-a-service functionality described in the Order Form, together with support and updates.

"**Customer Data**" means all data submitted to or processed by the Services by or for the Customer or the Properties, including Personal Data.

"**Personal Data**" means personal data of the Customer's customers and employees processed through the Services.

"**Security Incident**" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorised access to, or loss, disclosure or alteration of, Customer Data.

## 2. Services and Licence

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Customer at each Property in accordance with this Agreement, the Order Form and Schedule 1.

2.2 The Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the Term for the Customer and the Properties, and their personnel, to access and use the Services for their internal business purposes.

2.3 The Customer shall not sublicense the Services to third parties outside its group, reverse engineer the Services, or use them unlawfully.

2.4 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using suitably qualified personnel, and in compliance with applicable law.

## 3. Term and Renewal

3.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, until {{EXPIRY}} (the "**Initial Term**"), being a period of 36 months.

3.2 This Agreement does not renew automatically. Any extension of the Term requires a written agreement signed by both Parties.

## 4. Fees and Payment

4.1 The Customer shall pay the fees set out in the Order Form. The annual fees are USD 190,000, and the total contract value over the Initial Term is USD 570,000.

4.2 The Supplier shall issue valid invoices addressed to the Customer. The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

4.3 The Customer may withhold payment of any amount it disputes in good faith, shall notify the Supplier of the dispute with reasons, and the Parties shall work together promptly to resolve it. Withholding a disputed amount is not a breach of this Agreement.

4.4 Unless the Order Form states otherwise, fees are exclusive of applicable goods and services tax, which shall be shown separately on the invoice.

## 5. Service Levels

5.1 The Supplier shall meet the service levels in the Order Form and Schedule 1, including availability of at least 99.95% in each month and notification of Security Incidents within 24 hours of the Supplier becoming aware of them.

5.2 If the Supplier fails to meet a service level, it shall promptly investigate, remedy the failure, and report to the Customer on the root cause and corrective action. The service credits (if any) in Schedule 1 are without prejudice to the Customer's other rights and remedies.

## 6. Data Protection and Security

6.1 The Services involve the processing of Personal Data of the Customer's customers and employees. The Parties have executed, or will execute alongside this Agreement, a Data Processing Agreement (the "**DPA**"), which forms part of this Agreement. The Supplier shall process Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions and in accordance with the DPA and applicable data protection law.

6.2 The Supplier shall maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Customer Data.

6.3 The Supplier shall maintain compliance with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) throughout the Term and shall provide its annual attestation of compliance to the Customer.

6.4 The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any Security Incident within 24 hours of becoming aware of it, and shall cooperate with the Customer in investigating, mitigating and, where required, reporting it.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other Party disclosed in connection with this Agreement ("**Confidential Information**"), use it only to perform or receive the benefit of this Agreement, and not disclose it except to personnel and advisers who need to know and are bound by equivalent duties.

7.2 Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes public other than through breach, was lawfully known to the recipient beforehand, is independently developed, or is lawfully received from a third party. A Party may disclose Confidential Information where required by law or a regulator, after giving the other Party notice where lawful.

7.3 The obligations in this clause 7 survive for 3 years after this Agreement ends.

## 8. Intellectual Property

8.1 The Supplier retains all intellectual property rights in the Services and its underlying software and materials. No rights are transferred except the right of use in clause 2.2.

8.2 The Customer retains all rights in Customer Data and grants the Supplier a limited licence to use it solely to provide the Services.

## 9. Warranties

9.1 Each Party warrants that it has authority to enter into this Agreement.

9.2 The Supplier warrants that, for 30 days from the Effective Date (the "**Warranty Period**"), the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Order Form and Schedule 1. If the Services do not so perform, the Supplier shall correct the non-conformity at no additional charge.

9.3 The Supplier warrants that the Services will not knowingly contain malicious code and that its provision of the Services will not infringe third-party rights.

9.4 Except as expressly stated, all other warranties and conditions implied by law are excluded to the extent permitted.

## 10. Indemnity

10.1 The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against losses, damages and reasonable costs arising from a third-party claim that the Customer's use of the Services in accordance with this Agreement infringes intellectual property rights, or from the Supplier's breach of clause 6 or clause 7.

10.2 The indemnified Party shall give prompt notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control its defence and settlement, and provide reasonable assistance at the indemnifying Party's cost.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 Subject to clause 11.3, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement is limited to two (2) times the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

11.2 Subject to clause 11.3, neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, or loss of profit, arising under this Agreement.

11.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or any liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

## 12. Termination

12.1 The Customer may terminate this Agreement without cause on 90 days' written notice to the Supplier.

12.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is incapable of remedy or, if capable of remedy, is not remedied within a reasonable period stated in a written notice requiring remedy, or if the other becomes insolvent.

12.3 On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall return or securely delete Customer Data on the Customer's request, provide reasonable transition assistance, and the Customer shall pay undisputed fees for Services provided up to the termination date. Clauses 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12.3, 14 and 15 survive.

## 13. Assignment and Subcontracting

Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. The Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

## 14. Governing Law and Disputes

This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of Singapore. The Parties submit to the courts of Singapore.

## 15. General

15.1 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, courier or email to the address in the Order Form, or as notified by a Party.

15.2 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior understandings. Variations must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

15.3 **Severance and waiver.** If a provision is unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect. No failure to exercise a right is a waiver.

15.4 **Force majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other promptly and uses reasonable efforts to mitigate. This does not excuse the Supplier's security or data protection obligations.
