# SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Software as a Service Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into with effect from {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") between **BookWave Distribution Inc** ("**BookWave**" or the "**Supplier**") and **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc**, a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels (the "**Customer**"). The Services are delivered for use at the Customer's individual hotels (each a "**Property**" and together the "**Properties**").

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement: "**BookWave Channel**" means the online distribution, booking and reservation platform operated by BookWave through which room bookings for the Properties are made; "**Booking**" means a room reservation made through the BookWave Channel; "**Customer Personal Data**" means personal data relating to the Customer's customers and employees that is processed by BookWave in connection with the Services; "**Fees**" means the fees stated in Clause 4; "**Services**" means the hosted software, platform access, distribution and related support services described in this Agreement, as made available by BookWave from time to time; and "**Term**" means the Initial Term and any Renewal Term.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. "Including" means "including without limitation".

## 2. SERVICES AND SCOPE

2.1 BookWave grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable right during the Term to access and use the Services at the Properties for the Customer's internal business purposes, subject to this Agreement.

2.2 BookWave may modify, update, suspend or discontinue any feature of the Services, provided that it does not materially reduce the core functionality of the Services during the then-current term.

2.3 The Customer shall: (a) provide accurate rates, inventory, availability and Property information; (b) keep its credentials secure and be responsible for all activity under its accounts; (c) ensure its use of the Services complies with applicable law; and (d) not reverse engineer, copy, resell or interfere with the Services or use them to build a competing product.

2.4 BookWave may suspend access to the Services where reasonably necessary to protect the security or integrity of the Services, where the Customer is in breach of this Agreement, or for scheduled or emergency maintenance. Suspension does not relieve the Customer of its payment obligations.

## 3. TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}} (the "**Initial Term**"), being a period of twelve (12) months.

3.2 At the end of the Initial Term and each Renewal Term, this Agreement renews automatically for a successive period of twelve (12) months (each a "**Renewal Term**") unless either party gives the other at least sixty (60) days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

3.3 Neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience. This Agreement may be terminated only in accordance with Clause 14.

## 4. FEES, COMMISSION AND PAYMENT

4.1 The Customer shall pay BookWave annual fees of USD 420,000 for the Initial Term. The total contract value of this Agreement for the Initial Term is USD 420,000.

4.2 In addition, the Customer shall pay BookWave a commission of fifteen percent (15%) of the room revenue of each Booking made through the BookWave Channel. Commission is calculated and invoiced by BookWave on the basis of its booking records, which shall be conclusive absent manifest error.

4.3 BookWave shall invoice the Fees and commission. Undisputed invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of receipt of a valid invoice. The Customer shall notify BookWave in writing of any good-faith dispute, with reasonable supporting detail, before the due date, and shall pay the undisputed portion on time.

4.4 Overdue amounts accrue interest at the maximum rate permitted by law from the due date until payment in full. BookWave may suspend the Services on written notice where undisputed amounts remain unpaid after the due date.

4.5 All amounts are exclusive of taxes, levies and duties, which the Customer shall bear (other than taxes on BookWave's net income). Fees are non-refundable except as expressly stated in this Agreement. Fees for any Renewal Term shall be those notified by BookWave in writing before the end of the then-current term, failing which the Fees then in effect continue to apply.

## 5. SERVICE LEVELS

5.1 BookWave shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Services available at least 99.0% of the time in each calendar month, measured by BookWave's monitoring systems.

5.2 Availability excludes downtime arising from: scheduled or emergency maintenance; the Customer's or any Property's systems, networks or acts or omissions; third-party services, hosting providers, payment processors, channel partners or internet failures; suspension in accordance with this Agreement; and force majeure events.

5.3 BookWave shall notify the Customer of a security incident affecting Customer Personal Data within ninety-six (96) hours after BookWave confirms the incident.

5.4 The remedies in this Clause 5 and Clause 14, and any service credits BookWave may elect to grant, are the Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for any failure to meet the service levels.

6.2 The Customer is responsible for the accuracy and lawfulness of Customer Personal Data supplied to BookWave and warrants that it has all notices, consents and legal bases necessary for BookWave to process it as contemplated by this Agreement.

6.4 Each party shall comply with data protection laws applicable to it. The Customer acknowledges that BookWave may retain and use Customer Personal Data to the extent required for legal, accounting, audit, dispute-resolution and fraud-prevention purposes.
