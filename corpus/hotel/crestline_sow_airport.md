This Statement of Work No. 1 (the "**SOW**") is made between **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Ltd** (the "**Customer**"), a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels, and **Crestline Laundry & Linen Services Ltd** (the "**Supplier**"). It is issued under the Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between the same parties (the "**Parent Agreement**"). Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Parent Agreement. If this SOW conflicts with the Parent Agreement, the Parent Agreement prevails unless this SOW expressly states that it overrides a specified provision.

## 1. Background

1.1 The Customer owns and operates six hotels. Services under this SOW are delivered at individual hotels of the Customer (each a "**Property**"). The Property covered by this SOW is **Seabright Airport Inn** (the "**Property**" for the purposes of this SOW).

1.2 The Customer requires a reliable laundry and linen service for the Property, and the Supplier has agreed to provide that service on the terms of this SOW and the Parent Agreement.

## 2. Scope

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the following services at and for the Property (the "**Services**"):

(a) daily collection of soiled linen from the Property, including on weekends and public holidays;
(b) laundering, finishing, folding and packaging of linen to a standard suitable for a hotel of the Property's category;
(c) return delivery of clean linen to the Property in accordance with the schedule agreed under clause 4; and
(d) management of linen stock records and reporting of losses, damage and condemned items.

2.2 The Services do not include dry cleaning of guest garments, uniform tailoring, or any service at a Property other than Seabright Airport Inn. Services at another Property require a separate statement of work or a written variation.

2.3 Laundering charge: the Supplier's charge is USD 1.71 per kilogram of linen, with collection made daily including weekends.

2.4 Replacement of linen: the Supplier shall replace, at its own cost, linen that is lost or damaged beyond normal wear while in the Supplier's custody or as a result of the Supplier's processing.

## 4. Roles

4.1 **Supplier.** The Supplier shall provide the Services with reasonable skill and care, using suitably trained and supervised personnel, in compliance with applicable law and the Customer's reasonable site and health and safety rules notified to it. The Supplier shall appoint a named account manager as the Customer's primary contact.

4.2 **Customer.** The Customer shall give the Supplier timely access to the Property, make soiled linen available for collection at an agreed point, and nominate a representative at the Property to confirm weights, counts and receipts.

4.3 **Schedule.** The parties shall agree in writing the collection and delivery times at milestone D1. Changes require the written agreement of both parties, not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 5. Assumptions

5.1 The Customer will provide reasonably accurate estimates of linen volumes and will notify the Supplier promptly of material changes.

5.2 The linen processed is hotel linen of a standard type, and the Property provides suitable storage and loading access.

5.3 Linen weights are determined by the Supplier's calibrated scales and recorded on a collection note, which the Customer's representative may witness or verify.

5.4 If an assumption proves incorrect and materially affects the Supplier's cost or ability to perform, the parties shall discuss in good faith an appropriate variation under the Parent Agreement's change procedure. No variation is binding unless agreed in writing.

## 6. Fees and Invoicing

6.1 The annual fees under this SOW are USD 198,000, and the total contract value for the 18-month Period is USD 297,000. The laundering charge in clause 2.3 is the unit rate on which the fees are based.

6.2 The Supplier shall invoice the Customer in arrears and each invoice shall itemise the weight of linen processed and any adjustments. An invoice is valid only if it is accurate, addressed to the Customer, references this SOW, and contains the information required by the Parent Agreement.

6.3 The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 45 days of receipt of a valid invoice. The Customer may withhold a disputed amount in good faith, shall notify the Supplier of the dispute and its reasons promptly, and shall pay any undisputed portion on time. The parties shall seek to resolve disputes promptly under the Parent Agreement's dispute resolution provisions.

6.4 Fees are stated exclusive of applicable sales taxes, which shall be added at the prevailing rate where properly chargeable and shown on the invoice.

## 7. Acceptance

7.1 The Customer shall review each deliverable and each monthly report within a reasonable period after receipt and shall either accept it or notify the Supplier in writing of any material non-conformity with this SOW.

7.2 The Supplier shall correct any notified non-conformity promptly at its own cost and resubmit the deliverable. Linen returned that is unclean, damaged or short in count may be rejected on delivery and shall be reprocessed or replaced by the Supplier without additional charge.

7.3 Payment of an invoice does not constitute acceptance of non-conforming Services or waive any right or remedy.

## 8. Term and Renewal

8.1 This SOW takes effect on {{EFFECTIVE}} and, unless terminated earlier, continues until {{EXPIRY}}, an initial term of 18 months.

8.2 This SOW does not renew automatically. Any extension requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other party received in connection with this SOW, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations, or as required by law. This clause is subject to, and supplements, the confidentiality provisions of the Parent Agreement, and survives expiry or termination.

## 10. Intellectual Property

Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property. The Customer retains all rights in its brands, logos, and any marking on linen. The Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive licence to use any reports and materials delivered under this SOW for its internal business purposes.

## 11. Warranties

The Supplier warrants that it shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with applicable law, using qualified personnel, and that the Services and the processes used, including detergents and chemicals, will be suitable for hotel linen and will not infringe third-party rights. Each party warrants that it has authority to enter into this SOW.

## 12. Liability and Indemnity

12.1 Each party's liability under or in connection with this SOW is governed by the liability provisions of the Parent Agreement.

12.2 Any indemnities in the Parent Agreement apply to this SOW as if set out in full here. In addition, and subject to clause 12.1, the Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against third-party claims arising from the Supplier's negligence or breach of applicable law in performing the Services.

## 13. Data Protection

Where the Supplier processes personal data on behalf of the Customer in connection with the Services (for example, where guest or staff names appear on items or records), it shall do so only on the Customer's documented instructions, shall implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures, shall assist the Customer in meeting its data protection obligations, and shall comply with the data protection provisions of the Parent Agreement and applicable data protection law.

## 14. Termination

14.1 Either party may terminate this SOW by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is incapable of remedy, or is capable of remedy and is not remedied within a reasonable period stated in a written notice requiring remedy.

14.2 Either party may terminate this SOW on written notice if the other becomes insolvent or enters into any arrangement with its creditors.

14.3 On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall return all Customer linen and records at the Property, the Customer shall pay undisputed fees for Services performed up to the termination date, and accrued rights and surviving clauses shall continue. Termination of this SOW does not terminate the Parent Agreement or any other statement of work unless stated in writing.

## 15. Assignment and Subcontracting

Neither party may assign, transfer or subcontract any rights or obligations under this SOW without the prior written consent of the other, not to be unreasonably withheld. The Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its permitted subcontractors.

## 16. Notices

Notices under this SOW shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognised courier, or by email to the addresses notified by each party for the purposes of the Parent Agreement, and are deemed received in accordance with the notice provisions of the Parent Agreement.

## 17. Entire Agreement

This SOW, together with the Parent Agreement, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions and understandings about it. Any variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.
