# Amendment No. 1 to Master Services Agreement

This Amendment No. 1 (the "**Amendment**") is made with effect from {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") between **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Ltd**, a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels (the "**Customer**"), and **Crestline Laundry & Linen Services Ltd** (the "**Supplier**"). The Customer and the Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## Background

A. The Parties are party to a Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} (the "**MSA**") under which the Supplier provides laundry and linen services to the Customer's hotels (the "**Properties**").

B. The Parties wish to amend the MSA on the terms of this Amendment.

## Agreed Terms

1. **Interpretation.** Capitalised terms not defined in this Amendment have the meaning given in the MSA. References to the "Agreement" in the MSA are to the MSA as amended by this Amendment. References to clauses are to clauses of the MSA unless stated otherwise.

2. **Payment Terms.** With effect from the Amendment Effective Date, the payment terms in the MSA are amended so that the Customer shall pay each properly issued and undisputed invoice within 30 days of the date of receipt of that invoice. Any provision of the MSA stating a different period for payment of invoices is deleted and replaced by this paragraph 2.

3. **Application to Invoices.** Paragraph 2 applies to every invoice received by the Customer on or after the Amendment Effective Date. Invoices received before the Amendment Effective Date remain payable in accordance with the MSA as it stood before this Amendment.

4. **Term.** With effect from the Amendment Effective Date, the term of the MSA is extended so that the Agreement shall now expire on {{NEW_EXPIRY}}, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms. Any provision of the MSA stating a different expiry date is deleted and replaced by this paragraph 4.

5. **Fees.** The fees, rates and charges set out in the MSA are not changed by this Amendment and continue to apply throughout the extended term, subject to any adjustment mechanism already provided for in the MSA. Invoicing requirements, including the manner of invoicing and the treatment of disputed amounts, remain as stated in the MSA.

6. **Services and Scope.** The Services, the Properties at which the Services are delivered, and the service standards and procedures set out in the MSA are unchanged. The Supplier shall continue to perform the Services at each Property throughout the extended term.

7. **Confidentiality.** The confidentiality obligations in the MSA continue in full force and apply to this Amendment and its terms. Neither Party shall disclose the terms of this Amendment except as permitted by the MSA or required by law.

8. **Intellectual Property.** Nothing in this Amendment transfers or licenses any intellectual property right except as already provided in the MSA. Each Party retains ownership of its existing intellectual property rights.

9. **Warranties.** Each Party warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Amendment, and that this Amendment constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation. The Supplier's warranties in the MSA continue to apply throughout the extended term.

10. **Liability and Indemnity.** The limitations and exclusions of liability and the indemnities in the MSA are unchanged and apply to this Amendment and to the Agreement as extended. Nothing in this Amendment limits or excludes any liability that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded.

11. **Termination.** The rights of each Party to terminate the Agreement under the MSA are unchanged. The extension of the term under paragraph 4 does not waive any right of termination or any accrued right or remedy of either Party arising before the Amendment Effective Date.

12. **Data Protection.** The data protection provisions of the MSA continue to apply to all personal data processed in connection with the Services during the extended term. Each Party shall continue to comply with applicable data protection laws.

13. **Continuing Effect.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the MSA remains unchanged and in full force and effect. In the event of conflict between this Amendment and the MSA, this Amendment prevails to the extent of the conflict.

14. **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign, transfer or subcontract any of its rights or obligations under this Amendment except as permitted by the assignment provisions of the MSA, which apply to this Amendment.

15. **Notices.** Notices under this Amendment shall be given in the manner and to the addresses provided for in the MSA.

16. **Entire Agreement.** This Amendment and the MSA together constitute the entire agreement between the Parties relating to their subject matter and supersede all prior discussions, representations and understandings relating to it. No variation of this Amendment is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

17. **Counterparts and Electronic Signature.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

18. **Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** This Amendment and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the law governing the MSA, and the courts or other forum specified in the MSA have jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Amendment.
