This Statement of Work No. 1 (the "**SOW**") is entered into between **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Ltd** (the "**Customer**"), a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels, and **Halcyon Elevator & Lift Maintenance Ltd** (the "**Supplier**"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between the same parties (the "**Parent Agreement**"). The Services under this SOW are delivered at Seabright Summit Lodge (the "**Property**"), being one of the Customer's hotels.

## 1. Background

1.1 The Customer operates hotels, including the Property, and requires reliable, safe and compliant lift operation for guests, staff and goods movement.

1.2 The Supplier is a specialist provider of lift and elevator maintenance services and has agreed to provide the Services at the Property on the terms of the Parent Agreement and this SOW.

1.3 Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Parent Agreement. If there is any conflict between this SOW and the Parent Agreement, the Parent Agreement prevails unless this SOW expressly states that it overrides a specific provision.

## 2. Scope

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the following services at the Property (the "**Services**"):

(a) scheduled preventive maintenance of all passenger, service and goods lifts installed at the Property, including lubrication, adjustment, testing of safety devices, and replacement of consumable parts;

(b) statutory thorough examinations and inspections coordinated with the Customer's insurer or competent person, and preparation of the associated reports;

(c) a call-out service for breakdowns and entrapments, including release of trapped passengers, fault diagnosis and repair;

(d) remote or on-site fault reporting and a written service record following each visit; and

(e) recommendations for repair, refurbishment or modernisation works, which shall be quoted separately and carried out only on the Customer's written approval.

2.2 Major component replacement, modernisation and any works outside Clause 2.1 are excluded from the Services and shall be agreed through a written change under the change control procedure in the Parent Agreement.

2.3 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with good industry practice, applicable law, and the Customer's reasonable site, health and safety, and guest-disruption policies notified to the Supplier.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

4.1 **Supplier.** The Supplier shall: (a) provide suitably trained, qualified and competent personnel; (b) comply with the Customer's site access, security and safety rules; (c) keep accurate maintenance records and make them available on request; (d) promptly notify the Customer of any lift condition presenting a safety risk and, where necessary, recommend that the lift be taken out of service; and (e) nominate an account manager as the Customer's primary contact.

4.2 **Customer.** The Customer shall: (a) provide safe and timely access to the lifts, machine rooms and relevant documentation; (b) nominate the Property's Chief Engineer as its day-to-day representative; (c) promptly report faults and incidents; and (d) not permit third parties to carry out maintenance on the lifts during the Period without notifying the Supplier.

## 5. Assumptions

5.1 The Fees are based on the lifts at the Property as identified in the baseline survey and on the Property remaining open to the Supplier for normal scheduled visits.

5.2 The lifts are in a condition capable of being maintained under this SOW. Pre-existing defects identified in the baseline survey are not covered by the Fees for repair and shall be addressed in accordance with Clause 2.1(e) and Clause 2.2.

5.3 The Customer will give reasonable notice of any events or periods during which access may be restricted, and the Supplier will use reasonable endeavours to schedule visits to minimise disruption to guests.

5.4 If an assumption proves incorrect and affects the Services, the parties shall discuss and agree in writing any necessary adjustment before it takes effect.

## 6. Fees and Invoicing

6.1 The annual fee for the Services is **USD 84,000** (the "**Fees**"). Over the initial term of 36 months, the total contract value is **USD 252,000**.

6.2 The Supplier shall invoice the Fees in accordance with the invoicing schedule in the Parent Agreement, or, if none applies, in equal instalments agreed at mobilisation. Each invoice must be valid, addressed to the Customer, identify the Property and this SOW, and itemise any separately approved works.

6.3 The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice. If the Customer disputes any part of an invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Supplier promptly, pay the undisputed portion, and the parties shall seek to resolve the dispute under the dispute resolution provisions of the Parent Agreement.

6.4 Unless stated otherwise, the Fees are exclusive of applicable sales taxes, which shall be added where properly chargeable and shown on a valid tax invoice.

6.5 No additional charges, expenses or works are payable unless approved in writing by the Customer in advance.

## 7. Acceptance

7.1 The Supplier shall submit each Deliverable for review. The Customer shall review it within a reasonable period and either accept it or notify the Supplier in writing of reasons for non-acceptance.

7.2 The Supplier shall promptly correct any non-conformity at no additional charge and resubmit the Deliverable. Acceptance shall not be unreasonably withheld or delayed.

7.3 Acceptance does not waive any right or remedy of the Customer in respect of latent defects or any breach of warranty.

## 8. Term, Renewal and Termination

8.1 This SOW commences on {{EFFECTIVE}} and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}}, being an initial term of 36 months.

8.2 This SOW does not renew automatically. Any extension of the term requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

8.3 Either party may terminate this SOW in accordance with the termination provisions of the Parent Agreement. On expiry or termination the Supplier shall hand over all maintenance records, keys, access credentials and documentation relating to the Property and cooperate reasonably in the transition to any replacement supplier. The Customer shall pay for Services properly performed up to the date of termination.

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other received in connection with this SOW, including security arrangements and layout of the Property, and shall use it only to perform or receive the Services, subject to the confidentiality provisions of the Parent Agreement and disclosure required by law.

## 10. Intellectual Property

Each party retains its pre-existing intellectual property rights. Reports, records and documents prepared specifically for the Customer under this SOW belong to the Customer, and the Supplier retains ownership of its tools, methodologies and know-how, granting the Customer a non-exclusive licence to use them to the extent necessary to receive the benefit of the Services.

## 11. Warranties

The Supplier warrants that: (a) the Services shall be performed with reasonable skill and care by competent personnel; (b) it holds and shall maintain all licences, certifications and insurances required to perform the Services; (c) parts and materials supplied shall be of satisfactory quality and fit for purpose; and (d) the Services shall comply with applicable law and recognised lift safety standards.

## 12. Liability and Indemnity

12.1 The liability of each party under or in connection with this SOW is governed by the liability provisions of the Parent Agreement.

12.2 The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against third-party claims, and related losses and reasonable costs, arising from the Supplier's negligence or breach of applicable law in performing the Services, in accordance with and subject to the indemnity provisions of the Parent Agreement.

## 13. Data Protection

Each party shall comply with applicable data protection law in connection with this SOW. To the extent the Supplier processes personal data on behalf of the Customer (for example, CCTV or access data in machine rooms or lift cars), it shall do so only on the Customer's documented instructions, keep such data secure, and comply with the data protection provisions of the Parent Agreement.

## 14. Assignment and Subcontracting

Neither party may assign or transfer its rights or obligations under this SOW without the other party's prior written consent. The Supplier shall not subcontract any part of the Services without the Customer's prior written consent and remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

## 15. Notices

Notices under this SOW shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by recorded delivery or by email to the address or representative notified by each party for that purpose under the Parent Agreement. A notice is deemed received when delivered, or on the next business day if sent by email outside business hours.

## 16. Entire Agreement and Variation

This SOW, together with the Parent Agreement, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions and understandings. No variation of this SOW is effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.

## 17. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This SOW and any non-contractual obligations arising from it are governed by the laws of England and Wales, and disputes shall be resolved in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions of the Parent Agreement.
