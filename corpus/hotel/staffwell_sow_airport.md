# Statement of Work No. 1 – Staffing Services at Seabright Airport Inn

| | |
|---|---|
| **Project** | Provision of Hospitality Staffing Services at Seabright Airport Inn |
| **Parent Agreement** | Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} |
| **Period** | Effective Date {{EFFECTIVE}} until {{EXPIRY}} (initial term of 24 months) |
| **Fees** | USD 610,000 per year; total contract value USD 1,220,000 |

This Statement of Work No. 1 (the "**SOW**") is made between **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc** (the "**Customer**"), a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels, and **Staffwell Hospitality Staffing Inc** (the "**Supplier**"). It is issued under the Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between the same parties (the "**Parent Agreement**"). The Customer's hotels are each a "**Property**"; the Property at which the Services under this SOW are delivered is **Seabright Airport Inn** (the "**Property**" for the purposes of this SOW).

## 1. Background

1.1 The Customer requires temporary and contract hospitality staff for the operation of Seabright Airport Inn.

1.2 The Supplier has agreed to recruit, vet, supply and manage such staff on the terms of this SOW and the Parent Agreement.

1.3 This SOW forms part of, and is governed by, the Parent Agreement. Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Parent Agreement. If this SOW conflicts with the Parent Agreement, the Parent Agreement prevails unless this SOW expressly states that it overrides a specific provision.

## 2. Scope

2.1 The Supplier shall provide hospitality staffing services at Seabright Airport Inn (the "**Services**"), comprising:

(a) supply of front desk, housekeeping, food and beverage, and porter personnel (the "**Personnel**") in the numbers and at the times reasonably requested by the Customer;

(b) pre-placement screening, including right-to-work checks, identity verification, reference checks and any role-specific vetting required by applicable law;

(c) induction of Personnel in the Customer's service standards, health and safety, and security procedures, using materials supplied by the Customer;

(d) day-to-day administration of Personnel, including payroll, statutory deductions, holiday entitlement and insurance; and

(e) provision of replacement Personnel where an individual is absent, unsuitable or removed at the Customer's reasonable request.

2.2 The Services do not include permanent recruitment, management of the Property, or any services at the Customer's other hotels unless added by a written change under clause 9.

## 3. Deliverables and Milestones

| No. | Deliverable | Description | Target Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mobilisation Plan | Recruitment, vetting and onboarding plan for the Property | {{MS1}} |
| 2 | Initial Personnel Cohort | Vetted and inducted Personnel ready to commence duties | Agreed in the Mobilisation Plan |
| 3 | Monthly Service Report | Hours worked, vacancies, absences, incidents and corrective actions | Within 10 days after each month end |
| 4 | Compliance Certificate | Confirmation that all Personnel have completed required checks and training | Quarterly |

3.1 Dates not stated in the table above shall be agreed in writing between the parties' representatives. The Supplier shall promptly notify the Customer of any anticipated delay and the steps proposed to recover it.

## 4. Roles

4.1 **Supplier.** The Supplier shall appoint a named account manager as the primary point of contact, ensure Personnel are suitably skilled, qualified and presentable, and remain the sole employer (or engager) of the Personnel responsible for all pay, tax, national insurance and statutory benefits.

4.2 **Customer.** The Customer shall appoint a representative at the Property, give reasonable notice of staffing requirements, provide safe working conditions, site access, and relevant policies, and direct the day-to-day tasks of Personnel within the scope of their roles.

4.3 Nothing in this SOW creates an employment relationship between the Customer and any Personnel, nor a partnership or agency between the parties.

## 5. Assumptions

5.1 The Services are priced on the basis that:

(a) the Customer will provide timely access to the Property, systems and information reasonably required;

(b) staffing requirements will remain broadly consistent with those discussed before the Effective Date, with material changes handled under clause 9;

(c) the Customer's policies and procedures provided to the Supplier are accurate and current; and

(d) applicable laws governing agency and temporary workers do not change in a manner that materially increases the Supplier's cost of performance, in which case the parties shall discuss an equitable adjustment in good faith.

5.2 If an assumption proves incorrect, the Supplier shall notify the Customer promptly, and the parties shall agree any resulting adjustment through clause 9.

## 6. Fees and Invoicing

6.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier annual fees of USD 610,000 for each year of the initial term, giving a total contract value of USD 1,220,000 over the initial term.

6.2 The Supplier shall invoice the fees in accordance with the invoicing schedule in the Parent Agreement or, if none, in equal installments agreed between the parties. Each invoice must be valid, addressed to the Customer, identify this SOW and the Property, and contain reasonable supporting detail.

6.3 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 14 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

6.4 The Customer may withhold any amount disputed in good faith, shall notify the Supplier of the dispute and its grounds promptly, and shall pay any amount later agreed or determined to be due within 14 days of resolution. The parties shall seek to resolve disputes under the Parent Agreement's dispute resolution provisions.

6.5 Fees are exclusive of applicable sales tax, which shall be added at the prevailing rate on receipt of a valid invoice. Unless the parties agree otherwise in writing, no expenses are payable.

## 7. Acceptance

7.1 The Customer shall review each Deliverable within a reasonable period after delivery and shall either accept it in writing or notify the Supplier of reasonable grounds for rejection, identifying the non-conformity.

7.2 The Supplier shall correct any non-conformity promptly and resubmit the Deliverable. Acceptance shall not be unreasonably withheld or delayed.

7.3 Acceptance of a Deliverable does not waive the Customer's rights in respect of latent defects, breach of warranty or failure to comply with the Parent Agreement.

7.4 Continuing Services, including the supply of Personnel, shall be assessed through the Monthly Service Report and the Customer's ongoing review of service quality.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1 This SOW commences on {{EFFECTIVE}} and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}}.

8.2 This SOW does not renew automatically. Any extension requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

8.3 Either party may terminate this SOW in the circumstances, and on the notice, set out in the Parent Agreement. Termination of this SOW does not terminate the Parent Agreement or any other statement of work unless expressly stated.

8.4 On expiry or termination, the Supplier shall cooperate in an orderly handover, return Customer property and information, and the Customer shall pay undisputed fees for Services properly performed up to the termination date.

## 9. Change Control

9.1 Either party may propose a change to the scope, Deliverables, numbers of Personnel or fees. No change is effective unless recorded in a written variation signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

## 10. Confidentiality

10.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other party obtained in connection with this SOW, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to those who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations, or as required by law or a regulator. The confidentiality provisions of the Parent Agreement apply to this SOW.

## 11. Intellectual Property

11.1 Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property. The Customer's brand, policies, training materials and guest data remain the Customer's property, and the Supplier is granted a limited, non-exclusive license to use them solely to provide the Services.

11.2 Reports and materials created specifically for the Customer under this SOW shall be owned by the Customer, save that the Supplier retains its general methods, tools and know-how.

## 12. Data Protection

12.1 Each party shall comply with applicable data protection law in connection with this SOW. To the extent the Supplier processes personal data on behalf of the Customer (including guest data to which Personnel have access), it shall do so only on the Customer's documented instructions, apply appropriate technical and organizational security measures, ensure persons processing the data are bound by confidentiality, assist the Customer with data subject requests, and notify the Customer without undue delay on becoming aware of a personal data breach. The data protection terms of the Parent Agreement also apply.

## 13. Warranties

13.1 The Supplier warrants that it shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using suitably qualified and experienced Personnel, and in compliance with applicable law, including laws on agency workers, wage and hour, minimum wage, anti-slavery and right to work.

13.2 Each party warrants that it has the authority to enter into this SOW.

## 14. Liability and Indemnity

14.1 Each party's liability under or in connection with this SOW is governed by the liability provisions of the Parent Agreement.

14.2 Any indemnity given by either party under the Parent Agreement applies to this SOW. In addition, the Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against losses arising from any claim by Personnel or a tax authority relating to employment status, pay, tax or social security contributions in respect of the Personnel, except to the extent caused by the Customer's breach or negligence.

## 15. General

15.1 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign, transfer or subcontract its rights or obligations under this SOW without the prior written consent of the other, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

15.2 **Notices.** Notices shall be in writing and delivered in accordance with the notice provisions of the Parent Agreement.

15.3 **Entire agreement.** This SOW and the Parent Agreement constitute the entire agreement between the parties regarding the Services and supersede all prior discussions and understandings on that subject.

15.4 **Variation and waiver.** Except under clause 9, no variation is effective unless in writing and signed by both parties. A failure or delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of it.

15.5 **Counterparts.** This SOW may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and which together constitute one instrument.

15.6 **Governing law.** This SOW and any non-contractual obligations arising from it are governed by the laws of the State of New York, and the parties submit to the jurisdiction specified in the Parent Agreement.
