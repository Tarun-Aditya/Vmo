# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement (this **"Agreement"**) is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the **"Effective Date"**) by and between Seabright Hotels & Resorts Ltd, a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels (**"Seabright"**), and Brightstar Security Services Inc (**"Brightstar"**). Seabright and Brightstar are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

## ARTICLE 1 – DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"Properties"** means the six hotels owned and operated by Seabright at which the Services are delivered, and **"Property"** means any one of them.

1.2 **"Services"** means the security guarding, patrol, monitoring and alarm-response services described in this Agreement and in any Order, as further described in Article 3.

1.3 **"Order"** means a written order or statement of work signed by both Parties that identifies the Property, the Services, the staffing levels and the applicable Fees.

1.4 **"Fees"** means the charges payable by Seabright for the Services as set out in the applicable Order.

1.5 **"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including Property layouts, access procedures, security protocols, guest-facing operating practices and pricing, that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

1.6 **"Guards"** means the personnel of Brightstar and its permitted subcontractors who perform the Services.

## ARTICLE 2 – TERM AND RENEWAL

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until {{EXPIRY}}, being a period of thirty-six (36) months (the **"Initial Term"**), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Article 11.

2.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, and of each Renewal Term, this Agreement renews automatically for a successive period of twelve (12) months (each a **"Renewal Term"**, and together with the Initial Term, the **"Term"**), unless a Party gives the other at least sixty (60) days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

## ARTICLE 3 – SERVICES

3.1 **Provision of Services.** Brightstar shall provide the Services at each Property in accordance with this Agreement, the applicable Order and Seabright's reasonable site instructions notified in writing.

3.2 **Standard of Performance.** Brightstar shall perform the Services with due skill, care and diligence, using suitably qualified and supervised Guards, and in accordance with good industry practice and applicable law.

3.3 **Alarm Response.** Brightstar shall respond to an alarm activation at a Property within fifteen (15) minutes.

3.4 **Guard Licensing and Vetting.** Brightstar shall ensure that all Guards hold current licences required by applicable law for the Services and have completed background checks before being assigned to any Property.

3.5 **Employment Compliance.** Brightstar shall comply with all applicable minimum-wage and working-time laws in respect of the Guards.

3.6 **Site Conduct.** Guards shall comply with the reasonable health, safety, conduct and guest-service policies of each Property that are notified to Brightstar. Brightstar shall promptly replace any Guard whom Seabright reasonably requests be removed from a Property.

3.7 **Subcontracting.** Brightstar shall not subcontract any Services without Seabright's prior written consent and remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were its own.

## ARTICLE 4 – FEES AND INVOICING

4.1 **Fees.** Seabright shall pay the Fees set out in the applicable Order. Unless an Order states otherwise, the Fees are exclusive of applicable sales and similar taxes, which shall be separately itemised.

4.2 **Invoicing.** Brightstar shall invoice Seabright for the Fees, and each invoice shall identify the relevant Property, the period covered, the Services performed and the applicable Order. An invoice is **"valid"** if it meets these requirements and is addressed as Seabright reasonably directs.

4.3 **Payment.** Seabright shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt of a valid invoice.

4.4 **Disputed Amounts.** If Seabright disputes any invoiced amount in good faith, it shall notify Brightstar promptly, pay the undisputed portion, and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute. Brightstar shall continue to perform the Services during any dispute.

## ARTICLE 5 – CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall hold the other's Confidential Information in confidence, use it solely to perform or receive the Services, and not disclose it except to its personnel, advisers and permitted subcontractors who need to know it and who are bound by equivalent obligations.

5.2 **Exceptions.** Section 5.1 does not apply to information that is or becomes public through no breach of this Agreement, was lawfully known to or independently developed by the recipient, or is lawfully received from a third party without restriction. A Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, after giving the other Party prompt notice where lawful.

5.3 **Survival.** The obligations in this Article 5 survive for three (3) years after this Agreement ends.

## ARTICLE 6 – INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 **Ownership.** Each Party retains all right, title and interest in its pre-existing intellectual property. Seabright retains all rights in its names, marks, Property plans and data, and Brightstar retains all rights in its proprietary methods, tools and training materials.

6.2 **Reports.** Brightstar grants Seabright a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use incident reports and other deliverables produced for Seabright under this Agreement for Seabright's business purposes.

## ARTICLE 7 – DATA PROTECTION

7.1 **No Personal Data.** The Parties acknowledge that the Services do not involve the processing of personal data on behalf of Seabright.

7.2 **Incidental Information.** If, contrary to Section 7.1, either Party anticipates that personal data will be processed, the Parties shall first agree in writing appropriate data protection terms before such processing begins.

## ARTICLE 8 – WARRANTIES

8.1 **Services Warranty.** Brightstar warrants that the Services will be performed in accordance with Article 3 and applicable law.

8.2 **Authority.** Each Party warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

8.3 **No Other Warranty.** No express warranty period applies to the Services, and, except as expressly stated in this Agreement, each Party disclaims all other warranties, express or implied, to the extent permitted by law.

## ARTICLE 9 – INSURANCE

9.1 **Coverage.** Brightstar shall maintain throughout the Term, with reputable insurers, insurance of at least USD 10,000,000 per claim, covering liability arising from the Services, including bodily injury, property damage and professional liability.

9.2 **Evidence.** Brightstar shall provide certificates of insurance on Seabright's reasonable request. Maintenance of insurance does not limit Brightstar's obligations under this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 10 – INDEMNITY AND LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 **Indemnity.** Brightstar shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Seabright and its affiliates, officers and employees from third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages and reasonable costs, arising from (a) bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property caused by the negligence or wilful misconduct of Brightstar or the Guards, or (b) Brightstar's breach of Sections 3.4 or 3.5.

10.2 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defence and settlement (provided no settlement imposes liability on the indemnified Party without its consent, not to be unreasonably withheld), and provide reasonable cooperation.

10.3 **Liability Cap.** Subject to Section 10.4, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed one (1) times the Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

10.4 **Exclusions.** Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for fraud, gross negligence or wilful misconduct, or any liability that cannot be limited under applicable law. Seabright's obligation to pay undisputed Fees is not limited by Section 10.3.

10.5 **Consequential Loss.** Subject to Section 10.4, neither Party is liable for indirect, special or consequential damages, or loss of profit, arising out of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 11 – TERMINATION

11.1 **No Termination for Convenience.** Neither Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience. This Agreement may be terminated only for cause in accordance with this Article 11 or by expiry or non-renewal under Article 2.

11.2 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to cure it within thirty (30) days after written notice describing the breach, or if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to bankruptcy or similar proceedings.

11.3 **Consequences.** On expiry or termination, Brightstar shall promptly return Seabright's keys, access credentials, equipment and Confidential Information, and Seabright shall pay all undisputed Fees for Services performed up to the effective date of termination. Articles 5, 6, 10 and 12 and this Section 11.3 survive expiry or termination.

## ARTICLE 12 – GENERAL

12.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York.

12.2 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the other Party's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, except that either Party may assign it to a successor to all or substantially all of its relevant business.

12.3 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, nationally recognised courier or registered mail to the address a Party designates in writing, with a copy by email where an email address has been provided. Notices are effective on receipt.

12.4 **Independent Contractor.** Brightstar is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship, and the Guards are not employees of Seabright.

12.5 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure in performance (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other Party promptly and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.

12.6 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with each Order, is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior understandings. In the event of conflict, the body of this Agreement prevails over an Order unless the Order expressly states otherwise.

12.7 **Amendment and Waiver.** Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties. A failure to enforce any provision is not a waiver of it.

12.8 **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder of this Agreement continues in full force, and the provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted.

12.9 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
