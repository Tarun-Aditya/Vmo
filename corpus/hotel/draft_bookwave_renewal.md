# SOFTWARE-AS-A-SERVICE SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Software-as-a-Service Subscription Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") between **BookWave Distribution Inc** (the "**Supplier**") and **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Ltd** (the "**Customer**"), a hotel group that owns and operates six (6) hotels (each a "**Property**" and together the "**Properties**"). This Agreement is a Supplier draft that has not been signed and is not binding until executed by both parties.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

"**Services**" means the hosted distribution, booking and related software services made available by the Supplier to the Customer at the Properties, as described in this Agreement. "**Customer Data**" means data entered into or generated by the Customer's use of the Services, including Personal Data. "**Personal Data**" means information relating to an identified or identifiable individual. "**Fees**" means the fees stated in Section 4.

## 2. SERVICES AND SCOPE

2.1 The Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable right during the Term to access and use the Services at the Properties for the Customer's internal business operations.

2.2 The Services are provided on an "as made available" basis in the form and with the functionality determined by the Supplier from time to time. The Supplier may modify, update or discontinue features of the Services provided that it does not materially reduce the core functionality of the Services during the Term.

2.3 The Customer shall be responsible for the acts and omissions of its personnel and the Properties, for the accuracy and legality of Customer Data, for maintaining its own connectivity and equipment, and for the security of its credentials. The Customer shall not reverse engineer, resell, or use the Services in breach of applicable law or to build a competing product.

2.4 The Supplier may suspend access to the Services where reasonably necessary to protect the security or integrity of the Services, or where undisputed Fees are overdue, without liability for such suspension.

## 3. TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}} (the "**Initial Term**"), being a period of twenty-four (24) months.

3.2 On expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months (each a "**Renewal Term**", and together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**") unless either party gives the other at least one hundred and eighty (180) days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

## 4. FEES, COMMISSION AND PAYMENT

4.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier annual Fees of USD 480,000. The total contract value over the Initial Term is USD 960,000.

4.2 In addition to the Fees, the commission rises to 18 percent of room revenue, and the Customer shall pay that commission on the room revenue booked through the Services at the Properties, calculated and reported in accordance with the Supplier's standard reporting procedures.

4.3 The Supplier shall invoice the Customer in accordance with its standard billing cycle. The Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within fifteen (15) days of receipt of a valid invoice.

4.4 The Customer shall notify the Supplier in writing of any invoice dispute, with reasons in reasonable detail, before the payment due date, and shall pay the undisputed portion on time. Amounts not disputed in this way are deemed undisputed.

4.5 Late payments accrue interest at the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, from the due date until paid. All Fees and commission are exclusive of taxes, which the Customer shall bear, other than taxes on the Supplier's net income. Fees are non-refundable except as expressly stated in this Agreement.

## 5. SERVICE LEVELS

5.1 The Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Services available at least 98.0% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance, emergency maintenance, force majeure events, failures of third-party networks or services, the Customer's equipment or acts, and suspension under Section 2.4.

5.2 The Supplier shall notify the Customer of a Security Incident affecting Customer Data within one hundred and twenty (120) hours after the Supplier confirms the Security Incident. "**Security Incident**" means a confirmed breach of security leading to the unauthorised access to or disclosure of Customer Data in the Supplier's custody.

5.3 Availability shall be measured by the Supplier's monitoring systems, which are conclusive absent manifest error. The remedies in this Section 5 are, to the extent permitted by law, the Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for any failure to meet the service level.

## 6. DATA PROTECTION

6.1 The Services involve the processing of Personal Data of the Customer's customers and employees. The Customer is responsible for ensuring it has all necessary notices, consents and lawful bases for the Supplier to process Personal Data for the purpose of providing the Services.

6.2 The Supplier shall process Personal Data only to provide, secure and support the Services and as otherwise permitted by this Agreement, and shall maintain reasonable technical and organisational security measures appropriate to the nature of the Personal Data.

6.3 The Supplier may use sub-contractors and hosting providers in providing the Services and remains responsible for their performance of its obligations to the extent required by law. The Supplier may use aggregated and de-identified data derived from the Services for analytics and service improvement.

6.4 On expiry or termination, the Supplier shall make Customer Data available for retrieval for a reasonable period, after which it may delete it in accordance with its retention practices.

7.2 The Customer retains ownership of Customer Data and grants the Supplier a non-exclusive licence during the Term to host, copy, process and use Customer Data to provide the Services and exercise its rights under this Agreement.
