# Master Services Agreement

This Agreement is made on {{EFFECTIVE}} between:

(1) **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales, which owns and operates six hotels (the **Client**); and

(2) **Staffwell Hospitality Staffing Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales (the **Supplier**).

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement:

1.1.1 **Agency Workers** means the individuals supplied by the Supplier to perform the Services at the Properties;

1.1.2 **Charges** means the charges for the Services set out in Schedule 2 – Charges;

1.1.3 **Commencement Date** means {{EFFECTIVE}};

1.1.4 **Confidential Information** means all information of a confidential nature disclosed by or on behalf of one party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including the Client's guest, employee and commercial information;

1.1.5 **Personal Data** has the meaning given in the UK GDPR;

1.1.6 **Properties** means the six hotels owned and operated by the Client at which the Services are delivered; and

1.1.7 **Services** means the services described in Schedule 1 – Services.

1.2 Headings do not affect interpretation. References to clauses and Schedules are to those of this Agreement, and the Schedules form part of it.

## 2. Term and Renewal

2.1 This Agreement commences on the Commencement Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}} (the **Initial Term**), being a period of 24 months.

2.2 On expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of 12 months (each a **Renewal Term**) unless either party gives the other at least 60 days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term.

## 3. The Services

3.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Client at the Properties from the Commencement Date in accordance with this Agreement.

3.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using suitably qualified, vetted and trained Agency Workers, and in accordance with the Client's reasonable site rules and health and safety requirements notified to it.

3.3 Where an Agency Worker engaged in housekeeping is absent, the Supplier shall supply a replacement housekeeping worker within 4 hours of the absence being notified or becoming known to the Supplier.

3.4 The Supplier shall pay all Agency Workers at least the applicable minimum wage and shall comply with all applicable working-time rules, and shall on request provide the Client with reasonable evidence of such compliance.

3.5 The Supplier remains the employer or engager of the Agency Workers and is solely responsible for their pay, tax, national insurance, holiday entitlement and other statutory obligations. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship between the Client and any Agency Worker.

3.6 The Client shall give the Supplier timely access to the Properties and such information and co-operation as the Supplier reasonably requires.

## 4. Charges and Payment

4.1 The Client shall pay the Charges in accordance with Schedule 2 – Charges.

4.2 The Supplier shall submit valid invoices addressed to the Client, identifying the Property, the period and the Services to which they relate. The Client shall pay each undisputed invoice within 14 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

4.3 If the Client disputes any invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Supplier promptly, pay any undisputed part, and the parties shall seek to resolve the dispute in good faith. Payment of an invoice does not waive the Client's right to dispute it later.

4.4 Charges are exclusive of VAT, which shall be payable by the Client at the applicable rate on receipt of a valid VAT invoice.

## 5. Warranties

5.1 The Supplier warrants that the Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care and in accordance with this Agreement and applicable law.

5.2 No express warranty period applies to the Services, and the warranty in clause 5.1 is the only warranty given in respect of the Services. Subject to clause 9.1, all other warranties, conditions and terms implied by law are excluded to the extent permitted by law.

5.3 Each party warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

## 6. Data Protection

6.1 The parties acknowledge that the Services involve the processing of Personal Data of the Client's customers and employees.

6.2 Each party shall comply with its obligations under applicable data protection law, including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

6.3 The Supplier shall process such Personal Data only to the extent necessary to perform the Services and in accordance with the Client's lawful written instructions, and shall:

6.3.1 keep the Personal Data confidential and ensure that persons authorised to process it are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations;

6.3.2 implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised or unlawful processing and accidental loss, destruction or damage;

6.3.3 not transfer it outside the United Kingdom except in compliance with applicable data protection law;

6.3.4 notify the Client without undue delay on becoming aware of any personal data breach affecting it; and

6.3.5 on termination or expiry, and at the Client's choice, return or securely delete the Personal Data, save where retention is required by law.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and not disclose it except to its employees, Agency Workers, professional advisers and subcontractors who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations, or as required by law or a competent authority.

7.2 Clause 7.1 does not apply to information that is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement, was lawfully known to the recipient beforehand, or is independently developed or lawfully received from a third party.

7.3 The obligations in this clause 7 survive for 2 years after this Agreement ends.

## 8. Intellectual Property

8.1 Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property rights.

8.2 The Client grants the Supplier a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free licence during the term to use the Client's materials, procedures and trade marks solely to perform the Services.

8.3 Any intellectual property rights in materials created specifically for the Client in the course of the Services vest in the Client, and the Supplier assigns them to the Client with full title guarantee.

## 9. Liability

9.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or any liability that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded.

9.2 Subject to clause 9.1, neither party is liable to the other, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, for any indirect or consequential loss arising under or in connection with this Agreement.

9.3 Subject to clauses 9.1 and 9.4, each party's aggregate liability arising under or in connection with this Agreement is limited to one (1) times the Charges paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

9.4 The limit in clause 9.3 does not apply to the Client's obligation to pay undisputed Charges.

## 10. Indemnity

10.1 The Supplier shall indemnify the Client against all losses, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising from any third-party claim, including any claim by an Agency Worker or a taxing or regulatory authority, relating to the Supplier's failure to comply with clause 3.4 or clause 3.5 or its breach of applicable employment law.

10.2 The indemnified party shall notify the indemnifying party promptly of any claim, allow it to conduct the defence and settlement, and give reasonable assistance at the indemnifying party's cost. This clause is subject to clause 9.

## 11. Insurance

11.1 The Supplier shall maintain throughout the term, with a reputable insurer, employer's liability, public liability and professional indemnity insurance with cover of at least USD 5,000,000 per claim.

11.2 The Supplier shall provide evidence of such insurance on reasonable request.

## 12. Termination

12.1 The Client may terminate this Agreement without cause on 30 days' written notice to the Supplier.

12.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other:

12.2.1 commits a material breach that is irremediable or, if remediable, is not remedied within a reasonable period specified in a written notice requiring remedy; or

12.2.2 becomes insolvent, enters administration, liquidation or any arrangement with its creditors, or ceases to carry on business.

12.3 On termination or expiry, the Client shall pay all undisputed Charges for Services properly performed up to the termination date, and each party shall return or delete the other's Confidential Information on request.

12.4 Termination or expiry does not affect accrued rights, and clauses 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12.3 and 15 survive in accordance with their terms.

## 13. Assignment and Subcontracting

13.1 Neither party may assign, transfer or subcontract any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

13.2 The Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of any permitted subcontractor as if they were its own.

## 14. Notices

14.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, sent by pre-paid first-class post or sent by email to the address most recently notified by the recipient for that purpose.

14.2 A notice is deemed received: if delivered by hand, on delivery; if posted, at 9.00 am on the second business day after posting; and if emailed, at the time of transmission or, if outside business hours, at 9.00 am on the next business day.

## 15. General

15.1 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties relating to its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings and representations. Each party acknowledges that it has not relied on any statement not set out in this Agreement.

15.2 **Variation.** No variation is effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.

15.3 **Waiver.** A failure or delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of it.

15.4 **Severance.** If any provision is found invalid or unenforceable, the remainder is unaffected.

15.5 **Force majeure.** Neither party is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other promptly and uses reasonable endeavours to mitigate the effect. This clause does not excuse the Client's payment obligations.

15.6 **Third party rights.** A person who is not a party has no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 to enforce any term of this Agreement.

15.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and which together constitute one agreement.

15.8 **Governing law and jurisdiction.** This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it (including non-contractual disputes) are governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.

## Schedule 1 – Services

1. The Supplier shall supply Agency Workers to the Properties, principally in housekeeping, but also in such other hospitality roles (including food and beverage service, laundry and front-of-house support) as the parties agree in writing from time to time.

2. The Supplier shall supply Agency Workers who are suitably skilled, right-to-work verified, appropriately vetted and trained, and who arrive punctually in the uniform and with the equipment required by the relevant Property.

3. The Supplier shall supply replacement housekeeping staff within 4 hours when a worker is absent, in accordance with clause 3.3.

4. The Supplier shall pay all Agency Workers at least the applicable minimum wage and comply with working-time rules, in accordance with clause 3.4.

5. The Supplier shall nominate an account manager who shall act as the Client's point of contact for the Services and shall attend service review meetings reasonably requested by the Client.

## Schedule 2 – Charges

| Item | Basis of Charge |
|---|---|
| Housekeeping Agency Workers | Hourly rate per Agency Worker as agreed in writing between the parties for each Property |
| Other hospitality roles | Hourly rate per Agency Worker as agreed in writing for the relevant role and Property |
| Replacement staff under clause 3.3 | Charged at the rate applicable to the role replaced, with no additional premium |
| Expenses | Only where approved in advance in writing by the Client, charged at cost |

Invoices shall be issued in accordance with clause 4 and are payable within 14 days of receipt of a valid invoice, where undisputed.
