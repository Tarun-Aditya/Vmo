# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Ltd**, a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels (the "Customer"), and **Marlowe Hospitality Consulting LLP** (the "Supplier"). The Customer and the Supplier are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

**WHEREAS**, the Customer owns and operates six hotels (each a "Property" and together the "Properties") and purchases consulting services, supplies and related support for the Properties;

**WHEREAS**, the Supplier provides hospitality consulting and related services and wishes to propose and, if engaged, deliver such services at the Properties;

**WHEREAS**, in connection with that proposed relationship, each Party expects to disclose to the other certain non-public information, and the Parties wish to set out the terms on which that information will be protected;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## Section 1. Definitions

1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party.

1.2 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party (the "Discloser") to the other Party (the "Recipient"), in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. It includes, without limitation, pricing, supplier and vendor terms, guest and loyalty data, occupancy and revenue information, operating procedures, business plans, and the terms of any proposal.

1.3 "Purpose" means evaluating, negotiating and, if the Parties so agree, performing a commercial relationship under which the Supplier provides services or supplies to the Customer at one or more Properties.

1.4 "Representatives" means a Party's and its Affiliates' directors, officers, partners, employees, professional advisers and subcontractors who need to know Confidential Information for the Purpose and are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement.

## Section 2. Term

2.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until {{EXPIRY}}, a period of twenty-four (24) months (the "Initial Term"), unless terminated earlier under Section 10.

2.2 The Parties may extend the term only by written amendment signed by both Parties.

## Section 3. Scope and Purpose

3.1 Each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other solely for the Purpose. Nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to disclose any information or to enter into any further agreement.

3.2 Any services or supplies to be delivered at the Properties will be governed by a separate written agreement or statement of work. This Agreement does not itself commit the Customer to purchase, or the Supplier to provide, any services or supplies.

## Section 4. Fees and Invoicing

4.1 No fee is payable by either Party for the disclosure or receipt of Confidential Information under this Agreement.

4.2 Each Party bears its own costs of performing this Agreement. Fees, invoicing and payment terms for any services or supplies will be set out exclusively in the separate agreement or statement of work contemplated by Section 3.2.

## Section 5. Confidentiality Obligations

5.1 The Recipient shall: (a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose; (b) not disclose it to any person other than its Representatives; (c) protect it using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of a similar nature, and no less than reasonable care; and (d) promptly notify the Discloser of any unauthorized use or disclosure of which it becomes aware.

5.2 The Recipient is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

5.3 Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can demonstrate: (a) is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to it before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information.

5.4 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation or court order, provided that, where lawful, it gives the Discloser prompt written notice and reasonable cooperation so the Discloser may seek protective treatment, and discloses only the portion legally required.

5.5 On the Discloser's written request, or on termination or expiry of this Agreement, the Recipient shall promptly return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information and confirm it has done so in writing, except that the Recipient may retain copies required by law or by bona fide backup and compliance procedures, which remain subject to this Agreement.

5.6 The Recipient's obligations under this Section 5 survive for three (3) years after this Agreement ends.

## Section 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. No license or right in any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret or other intellectual property is granted by this Agreement, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

6.2 The Supplier acquires no right in the names, marks or branding of the Customer or any Property. Any feedback voluntarily provided by one Party to the other regarding the other's products or services may be used freely by the receiving Party without obligation.

## Section 7. Warranties

7.1 Each Party represents and warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement and that doing so does not breach any obligation binding on it.

7.2 Except as stated in Section 7.1, all Confidential Information is provided "as is," and the Discloser makes no warranty as to its accuracy or completeness. Any such warranties will be given only in a definitive written agreement.

## Section 8. Limitation of Liability

8.1 Neither Party is liable to the other under this Agreement for indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for loss of profits, except to the extent arising from a breach of Section 5 or from willful misconduct.

8.2 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability that cannot be limited under applicable law.

## Section 9. Indemnity; Remedies

9.1 Each Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other from third-party claims, and resulting losses and reasonable costs, to the extent arising from the indemnifying Party's or its Representatives' unauthorized use or disclosure of the other Party's Confidential Information in breach of this Agreement.

9.2 The indemnified Party shall give prompt notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control its defense and settlement (provided no settlement may impose liability on the indemnified Party without its consent, not to be unreasonably withheld), and provide reasonable cooperation.

9.3 Each Party acknowledges that a breach of Section 5 may cause irreparable harm for which damages are an inadequate remedy, and that the Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief without proof of actual damages, in addition to other available remedies.

## Section 10. Termination

10.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for any reason on written notice to the other.

10.2 Termination or expiry does not relieve either Party of obligations accrued before that date. Sections 5, 6, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13 and 14, and any other provision that by its nature is intended to survive, survive termination or expiry as stated in Section 5.6 and the terms of those Sections.

## Section 11. Data Protection

11.1 If Confidential Information includes personal data (including guest, loyalty-program or employee data), the Discloser shall disclose it only to the extent necessary for the Purpose, and the Recipient shall process it only for the Purpose and in accordance with applicable data protection and privacy laws.

11.2 The Recipient shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss or disclosure, and shall notify the Discloser without undue delay after becoming aware of any security incident affecting that data.

11.3 The Parties will enter into any further data processing terms required by applicable law before any personal data is processed on the Customer's behalf in connection with services at the Properties.

## Section 12. Governing Law and Disputes

12.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require application of another jurisdiction's law.

12.2 The Parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through discussions between senior representatives. Nothing in this Section prevents a Party from seeking urgent injunctive relief under Section 9.3.

## Section 13. Assignment and Notices

13.1 Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the other Party, not to be unreasonably withheld, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business to which this Agreement relates, on written notice.

13.2 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognized courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the address of the receiving Party most recently notified in writing to the other Party. A notice is effective on receipt.

## Section 14. Miscellaneous

14.1 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions and understandings relating to it.

14.2 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only in a writing signed by both Parties. A failure or delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of it.

14.3 **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permitted and the remaining provisions will continue in effect.

14.4 **Relationship.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

14.5 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

*This document is a vendor draft and has not been signed. It becomes binding only when executed by both Parties.*
