# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc**, a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels (the "Customer"), and **Northgate Facilities & HVAC Inc** (the "Supplier"). The Customer and the Supplier are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Customer owns and operates six hotels and requires heating, ventilation, air-conditioning and related facilities maintenance services at those hotels;

WHEREAS, the Supplier is engaged in the business of providing facilities and HVAC services and represents that it has the skill, personnel and resources to perform such services; and

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to set out the terms on which the Supplier will provide such services to the Customer at the Properties.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## Section 1. Definitions

"Affiliate" means any entity controlling, controlled by or under common control with a Party.

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party in connection with this Agreement that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

"Fees" means the amounts payable by the Customer for the Services as set out in an Order.

"Order" means a written statement of work or service order signed by both Parties that references this Agreement.

"Properties" means the six hotels owned and operated by the Customer, as listed in an Order or otherwise notified in writing by the Customer, and "Property" means any one of them.

"Services" means the facilities, heating, ventilation and air-conditioning services described in Section 3 and in any Order.

"Term" has the meaning given in Section 2.

## Section 2. Term and Renewal

2.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}}, a period of thirty-six (36) months (the "Initial Term").

2.2 This Agreement does not renew automatically. Any extension of the Term requires a written agreement signed by both Parties before the Initial Term expires.

## Section 3. Services

3.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services at the Properties in accordance with this Agreement and each Order, with due skill and care, using suitably qualified and supervised personnel, and in compliance with applicable law and the reasonable site rules of each Property.

3.2 Guest Room Response. The Supplier shall restore heating or cooling in guest rooms within four (4) hours of a fault report at any Property.

3.3 Planned Maintenance Reporting. The Supplier shall deliver a monthly planned-maintenance report for each Property, describing the maintenance performed, defects identified and recommended actions.

3.4 The Supplier shall perform the Services so as to minimize disruption to guests and Property operations and shall coordinate access with the relevant Property manager.

3.5 Services may be added or varied only by a written Order or change agreed by both Parties.

## Section 4. Fees and Invoicing

4.1 The Customer shall pay the Fees set out in the applicable Order. Unless an Order states otherwise, the Fees are inclusive of all labor, travel, equipment and incidental costs of the Supplier.

4.2 The Supplier shall submit valid invoices addressed to the Customer, identifying the relevant Property and Order and including reasonable supporting detail.

4.3 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days of receipt of a valid invoice.

4.4 The Customer may withhold payment of any amount disputed in good faith by notifying the Supplier with reasons, and the Parties shall work promptly to resolve the dispute. The Customer shall pay any amount subsequently agreed or determined to be due in accordance with Section 4.3 counted from resolution.

## Section 5. Confidentiality

5.1 The receiving Party shall use the other Party's Confidential Information only to perform or exercise rights under this Agreement, shall protect it with at least reasonable care, and shall not disclose it except to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations.

5.2 These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public without breach, was lawfully known to or independently developed by the receiving Party, or is lawfully received from a third party without restriction. A Party may disclose Confidential Information where required by law, after giving the other Party prompt notice where lawful.

5.3 The obligations in this Section 5 survive for three (3) years after this Agreement ends.

## Section 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property. The Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive, perpetual license to use any reports and deliverables provided under this Agreement for the Customer's business purposes.

6.2 Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of either Party's intellectual property to the other, except as expressly agreed in an Order.

## Section 7. Warranties

7.1 The Supplier warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner, that it holds all licenses and permits required to perform the Services, and that the Services and any materials supplied will comply with applicable law.

7.2 The Supplier warrants all workmanship and installed parts for a period of three hundred sixty-five (365) days from completion of the relevant Services (the "Warranty Period"). During the Warranty Period the Supplier shall, at its own cost, promptly correct any defect notified by the Customer.

7.3 Each Party warrants that it has full authority to enter into this Agreement.

## Section 8. Insurance

The Supplier shall maintain throughout the Term, with reputable insurers, liability insurance cover of at least USD 5,000,000 per claim, together with workers' compensation insurance as required by law, and shall provide certificates of insurance on the Customer's request.

## Section 9. Indemnity

9.1 The Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless the Customer, its Affiliates and the Properties from third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages and reasonable costs, arising from (a) bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of the Supplier or its personnel, or (b) the Supplier's breach of applicable law.

9.2 The indemnified Party shall give prompt notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement (provided that no settlement imposing liability on the indemnified Party may be made without its consent, not to be unreasonably withheld), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## Section 10. Limitation of Liability

10.1 Subject to Section 10.3, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement is limited to one (1) times the Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

10.2 Subject to Section 10.3, neither Party is liable for indirect, consequential or special damages, or for loss of profit, arising out of this Agreement.

10.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for death or bodily injury caused by negligence, fraud, willful misconduct, breach of Section 5, or any liability that cannot be limited under applicable law.

## Section 11. Termination

11.1 The Customer may terminate this Agreement or any Order without cause on thirty (30) days' written notice to the Supplier.

11.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of written notice requiring remedy, or becomes insolvent or subject to bankruptcy or similar proceedings.

11.3 On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall return Customer property and Confidential Information, and the Customer shall pay undisputed Fees for Services properly performed up to the termination date. Sections 5, 7, 9, 10, 12 and 14 and any other provisions intended to survive shall survive.

11.4 **Sale of a Property.** If a Property is sold, or ceases to be owned or operated by the Customer, the Customer may terminate the Services at that Property on thirty (30) days' written notice, without penalty or early-termination charge.

## Section 12. Data Protection

The Parties acknowledge that the Services do not involve the processing of personal data. If the Parties later agree to Services that involve personal data, they shall first agree in writing appropriate data protection terms. The Supplier shall not access guest or employee personal data except incidentally and shall keep any such data confidential, and shall not use it.

## Section 13. Governing Law and Disputes

13.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of Illinois, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

13.2 The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation between senior representatives. If not resolved within a reasonable period, either Party may pursue remedies before the state or federal courts located in Illinois, to whose jurisdiction the Parties submit.

## Section 14. Miscellaneous

14.1 Assignment. Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. The Supplier shall not subcontract the Services without the Customer's prior written consent and remains responsible for its subcontractors.

14.2 Notices. Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, recognized courier or email with confirmation, to the address a Party designates in writing for that purpose, and are effective on receipt.

14.3 Independent Contractor. The Supplier is an independent contractor, and nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, agency or employment relationship.

14.4 Force Majeure. Neither Party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other Party promptly and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. This does not excuse payment obligations for Services performed.

14.5 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, with its Orders, is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior understandings. In case of conflict, the body of this Agreement prevails over an Order unless the Order expressly states otherwise.

14.6 Amendment and Waiver. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no failure to enforce is a waiver.

14.7 Severability. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect.

14.8 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their authorized representatives.
