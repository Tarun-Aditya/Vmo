# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement (this **"Agreement"**) is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the **"Effective Date"**) by and between Arcadia Retail Inc., a corporation with its headquarters in the United States (**"Arcadia"**), and Helix Digital Consulting Inc (**"Helix"**). Arcadia and Helix are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

## ARTICLE 1 – DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"Affiliate"** means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party.

1.2 **"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party (the **"Discloser"**) to the other Party (the **"Recipient"**) in connection with this Agreement that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including Arcadia Data.

1.3 **"Arcadia Data"** means all data, including Personal Data, that Helix accesses, receives or processes on behalf of Arcadia in connection with the Services.

1.4 **"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, including personal data of Arcadia's customers and employees.

1.5 **"Services"** means the information technology consulting, implementation and related services described in a Statement of Work.

1.6 **"Statement of Work"** or **"SOW"** means a written document executed by both Parties that describes Services, deliverables, schedules and fees and that references this Agreement.

1.7 **"Deliverables"** means all work product that Helix creates for Arcadia under a SOW.

## ARTICLE 2 – TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until {{EXPIRY}}, a period of thirty-six (36) months (the **"Initial Term"**), unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

2.2 **No Automatic Renewal.** This Agreement does not renew automatically. Any extension of the Initial Term requires a written agreement signed by both Parties.

2.3 **Effect on SOWs.** Unless otherwise stated in a SOW, no SOW shall continue beyond expiry or termination of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 3 – SERVICES

3.1 **Performance.** Helix shall perform the Services in accordance with this Agreement and each SOW, with due skill and care, using appropriately qualified personnel, and in accordance with generally accepted industry standards.

3.2 **Statements of Work.** Each SOW shall form part of this Agreement. In the event of conflict, the body of this Agreement prevails over a SOW unless the SOW expressly identifies the provision of this Agreement it intends to override.

3.3 **Change Control.** Changes to a SOW are effective only if documented in a written change order signed by both Parties.

3.4 **Monthly Service Report.** Helix shall deliver to Arcadia a written service report covering the preceding month by the fifth (5th) business day of each month, describing performance against the SOW, issues, risks and remedial actions.

3.5 **Arcadia Responsibilities.** Arcadia shall provide timely access to information, systems and personnel reasonably required by Helix to perform the Services.

3.6 **Subcontractors.** Helix shall not subcontract any Services without Arcadia's prior written consent and remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

## ARTICLE 4 – FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Fees.** Arcadia shall pay the fees set out in the applicable SOW. Unless a SOW states otherwise, fees are inclusive of all expenses.

4.2 **Invoices.** Helix shall submit invoices in arrears, each identifying the SOW, the Services performed and the amounts charged (a **"Valid Invoice"**).

4.3 **Payment Terms.** Arcadia shall pay all undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days of receipt of a Valid Invoice.

4.4 **Disputed Amounts.** Arcadia may withhold amounts it disputes in good faith by notifying Helix in writing of the basis for the dispute. The Parties shall work promptly and in good faith to resolve the dispute, and Helix shall continue to perform the Services during the dispute.

4.5 **Taxes.** Fees are exclusive of applicable sales, use and similar taxes, which Arcadia shall pay where properly invoiced, other than taxes on Helix's income, property or employees.

## ARTICLE 5 – CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 **Obligations.** The Recipient shall (a) use the Discloser's Confidential Information solely to perform or receive the Services; (b) not disclose it to any third party other than its personnel, Affiliates and advisors who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than this Article; and (c) protect it with at least reasonable care.

5.2 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes public through no breach by the Recipient; (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

5.3 **Compelled Disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, provided it gives the Discloser prompt notice, where lawful, and reasonable cooperation to seek protective treatment.

5.4 **Survival.** The obligations in this Article survive for three (3) years after the expiry or termination of this Agreement.

5.5 **Return.** Upon request or on termination, the Recipient shall return or securely destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information, subject to legally required retention.

## ARTICLE 6 – DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY

6.1 **Personal Data.** The Parties acknowledge that the Services involve the processing of Personal Data of Arcadia's customers and employees. Helix shall process Personal Data only on Arcadia's documented instructions and only as necessary to perform the Services.

6.2 **Compliance.** Helix shall comply with all data protection and privacy laws applicable to its processing of Personal Data.

6.3 **Information Security Programme.** Helix shall maintain a written information security programme containing administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of Arcadia Data and designed to protect it against unauthorised access, use, loss or disclosure.

6.4 **SOC 2 Report.** Helix shall provide Arcadia, annually during the term, with a SOC 2 Type II report covering the systems used to deliver the Services, and shall promptly remediate any material exceptions identified in it.

6.5 **Security Incidents.** Helix shall notify Arcadia without undue delay after becoming aware of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorised access to or disclosure of Arcadia Data, and shall cooperate in investigation, mitigation and any required notifications.

6.6 **Return and Deletion.** On expiry or termination, or earlier on request, Helix shall return or securely delete Arcadia Data, subject to legally required retention.

## ARTICLE 7 – INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

7.1 **Arcadia Property.** As between the Parties, Arcadia retains all rights in Arcadia Data and its pre-existing materials.

7.2 **Deliverables.** Upon payment, Helix assigns to Arcadia all right, title and interest in the Deliverables, excluding Helix Materials.

7.3 **Helix Materials.** Helix retains all rights in its pre-existing tools, methodologies and materials, and those developed independently of the Services (**"Helix Materials"**). To the extent incorporated in a Deliverable, Helix grants Arcadia a non-exclusive, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use them as part of the Deliverable.

## ARTICLE 8 – WARRANTIES

8.1 **Mutual.** Each Party warrants that it has the authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

8.2 **Services Warranty.** Helix warrants that, for ninety (90) days after delivery or acceptance of each Deliverable (the **"Warranty Period"**), the Deliverable will conform in all material respects to its SOW specifications, and that the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner.

8.3 **Remedy.** If a Deliverable breaches the warranty during the Warranty Period, Helix shall, at its own cost, promptly correct or re-perform the non-conforming work. This remedy does not limit Arcadia's other rights under this Agreement where correction is not achieved within a reasonable time.

8.4 **Disclaimer.** Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, neither Party makes any other warranty, express or implied.

## ARTICLE 9 – INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 **By Helix.** Helix shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Arcadia, its Affiliates and their personnel from third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages and reasonable costs, arising from (a) allegations that the Services or Deliverables infringe or misappropriate any intellectual property right; (b) Helix's breach of Article 5 or Article 6; or (c) Helix's gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

9.2 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defence and settlement (provided no settlement admits fault of the indemnified Party without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## ARTICLE 10 – LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 **Cap.** Subject to Section 10.3, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement is limited to one (1) times the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

10.2 **Exclusion of Indirect Loss.** Subject to Section 10.3, neither Party is liable for indirect, special, incidental, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, however arising.

10.3 **Exceptions.** Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for fraud, gross negligence, wilful misconduct, or any liability that cannot be limited under applicable law.

## ARTICLE 11 – INSURANCE

11.1 Throughout the term and for so long thereafter as claims may reasonably arise, Helix shall maintain, with reputable insurers, insurance covering its liabilities under this Agreement, including professional liability and cyber liability, of at least USD 5,000,000 per claim.

11.2 Helix shall provide certificates of insurance upon Arcadia's reasonable request.

## ARTICLE 12 – TERMINATION

12.1 **For Convenience.** Arcadia may terminate this Agreement or any SOW without cause on thirty (30) days' written notice to Helix.

12.2 **For Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any affected SOW by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within a reasonable period specified in a notice describing the breach, or if the other Party becomes insolvent or subject to bankruptcy proceedings.

12.3 **Effect.** On termination, Arcadia shall pay undisputed fees for Services properly performed up to the termination date, and Helix shall deliver all completed and in-progress Deliverables and provide reasonable transition assistance.

12.4 **Survival.** Articles 5, 7, 9 and 10, this Section 12.4, Article 13 and any other provisions that by their nature are intended to survive shall survive expiry or termination.

## ARTICLE 13 – GENERAL

13.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

13.2 **Dispute Resolution.** The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith escalation to senior executives. Failing resolution, the state and federal courts located in New York shall have exclusive jurisdiction, and each Party submits to that jurisdiction.

13.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, except that either Party may assign it to a successor in a merger or sale of substantially all of its relevant business upon written notice.

13.4 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognised courier or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the address a Party designates in writing for that purpose, and are effective on receipt.

13.5 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, agency or employment relationship.

13.6 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other Party promptly and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. Payment obligations are not excused except as to delays caused by the event.

13.7 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with all SOWs, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions and agreements. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

13.8 **Waiver and Severability.** Failure to enforce any provision is not a waiver. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect.

13.9 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original.
