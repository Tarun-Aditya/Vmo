# SaaS Subscription Agreement

This SaaS Subscription Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into with effect from {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer with its headquarters in the United States (the "**Customer**"), and **CoreLedger Payments Pte Ltd**, a company incorporated in Singapore (the "**Supplier**"). The Customer and the Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

The Agreement comprises these master terms, Schedule 1 (Service Levels and Security) and Order Form No. 1. If there is a conflict, the master terms prevail over the Schedules, and the Order Form prevails over the master terms only as to the commercial particulars it expressly sets out.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Customer Data**" means all data, including Personal Data, that the Customer or its users submit to or generate through the Services, or that the Supplier otherwise processes on the Customer's behalf.

"**Fees**" means the fees set out in the Order Form.

"**Personal Data**" has the meaning given in the DPA.

"**DPA**" means the data processing agreement between the Parties relating to the Services.

"**Services**" means the hosted payment and ledger software-as-a-service platform described in the Order Form, together with support, hosting, maintenance and updates.

"**Security Incident**" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorised access to, or loss, disclosure, alteration or destruction of, Customer Data or the Supplier systems used to provide the Services.

## 2. Term and Renewal

2.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}} (the "**Initial Term**"), being a period of 36 months.

2.2 This Agreement does not renew automatically. Any extension of the Initial Term requires a written agreement signed by both Parties before the Initial Term ends.

## 3. The Services

3.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with this Agreement, the Order Form and Schedule 1, with due skill and care and in accordance with good industry practice.

3.2 The Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the term to access and use the Services for the Customer's and its affiliates' internal business purposes, including use by their employees and contractors.

3.3 The Customer shall not sublicense the Services to third parties outside its group, reverse engineer the Services except as permitted by law, or use the Services in breach of applicable law.

3.4 The Supplier may update the Services from time to time, provided that no update materially reduces the functionality, security or performance of the Services.

## 4. Service Levels and Security

4.1 The Supplier shall meet the service levels in the Order Form and Schedule 1, including availability of at least 99.9% in each calendar month.

4.2 The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any Security Incident within 24 hours of becoming aware of it, and shall promptly provide information reasonably requested to investigate, contain and remediate it.

4.3 The Supplier shall deliver to the Customer, once in each year of the term, a summary of the results of an independent penetration test of the Services, together with a summary of remediation actions for material findings.

4.4 If the Supplier fails to meet a service level, the Customer is entitled to the service credits (if any) set out in Schedule 1, without prejudice to its other rights and remedies.

## 5. Fees, Invoicing and Payment

5.1 The Customer shall pay the Fees set out in the Order Form. The annual Fees are USD 780,000 and the total contract value over the Initial Term is USD 2,340,000.

5.2 The Supplier shall issue valid invoices in accordance with the Order Form. Undisputed invoices are payable within 30 days of the Customer's receipt of a valid invoice.

5.3 The Customer may withhold payment of any amount it disputes in good faith, shall notify the Supplier of the dispute with reasons, and the Parties shall seek to resolve it promptly. The Customer shall pay any portion not in dispute in accordance with clause 5.2.

5.4 Unless the Order Form states otherwise, the Fees are exclusive of applicable indirect taxes, which shall be shown separately on the invoice.

## 6. Data Protection

6.1 The Services involve the processing of Personal Data of the Customer's customers and employees. The Parties have executed, or will execute alongside this Agreement, a DPA, which forms part of this Agreement. The Supplier shall process Personal Data only in accordance with the DPA and the Customer's documented instructions.

6.2 The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach affecting Personal Data within 24 hours of becoming aware of it, and shall cooperate with the Customer in meeting its notification and remediation obligations under applicable law.

6.3 The Customer retains all rights in Customer Data. On expiry or termination, the Supplier shall make Customer Data available for export in a commonly used format and thereafter securely delete it, subject to the DPA and any legal retention requirement.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other Party that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential ("**Confidential Information**"), use it only to perform or exercise rights under this Agreement, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations.

7.2 Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes public other than through breach, was lawfully known to the recipient without restriction, is independently developed, or is lawfully received from a third party. A Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or a competent authority, giving prior notice where lawful.

7.3 The obligations in this clause 7 survive for 5 years after this Agreement ends.

## 8. Intellectual Property

8.1 The Supplier and its licensors retain all intellectual property rights in the Services. The Customer retains all intellectual property rights in the Customer Data and its pre-existing materials.

8.2 The Customer grants the Supplier a limited licence to use Customer Data solely to provide the Services to the Customer.

## 9. Warranties

9.1 Each Party warrants that it has full authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

9.2 The Supplier warrants that, for a period of 90 days from the Effective Date (the "**Warranty Period**"), the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Order Form and Schedule 1, and that it will provide the Services in compliance with applicable law. During the Warranty Period the Supplier shall, at its own cost, promptly correct any non-conformity notified by the Customer.

9.3 The Supplier warrants that the Services will not knowingly contain malicious code and that it shall use reasonable measures to prevent its introduction.

9.4 Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, all other warranties are excluded to the extent permitted by law.

## 10. Indemnity

10.1 The Supplier shall defend and indemnify the Customer against all losses, damages, costs and expenses arising from any third-party claim that the Services or their use in accordance with this Agreement infringe that third party's intellectual property rights.

10.2 The Supplier shall also indemnify the Customer against third-party claims and regulatory penalties to the extent arising from the Supplier's breach of clause 6 or the DPA.

10.3 The indemnified Party shall notify the other promptly of any claim, allow it to control the defence and settlement (provided no settlement admits fault on behalf of the indemnified Party without its consent), and give reasonable assistance at the indemnifying Party's cost. The Supplier has no liability under clause 10.1 to the extent a claim arises from the Customer's unauthorised modification or use of the Services in breach of this Agreement.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 Subject to clauses 11.2 and 11.3, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, is limited to one (1) times the Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

11.2 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, wilful misconduct, or any liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

11.3 Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss or loss of anticipated profit, except to the extent such loss is recoverable under an indemnity in clause 10. The Customer's obligation to pay the Fees is not limited by this clause.

## 12. Insurance

12.1 The Supplier shall maintain throughout the term, with reputable insurers, insurance cover of at least USD 5,000,000 per claim, covering professional indemnity and cyber risks, and shall provide evidence of cover on reasonable request.

## 13. Termination

13.1 The Customer may terminate this Agreement without cause on 60 days' written notice to the Supplier.

13.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is incapable of remedy, or is capable of remedy and is not remedied within a reasonable period specified in a written notice requiring remedy, or becomes insolvent or subject to winding-up or similar proceedings.

13.3 The Customer may terminate by written notice if the Supplier suffers repeated or serious failures to meet the service levels, as described in Schedule 1.

13.4 On termination by the Customer under clause 13.1 the Customer shall pay Fees for Services provided up to the termination date, and on termination by the Customer under clause 13.2 or 13.3 the Supplier shall refund any Fees paid in advance for the period after termination.

13.5 Termination or expiry does not affect accrued rights. Clauses 1, 5 (as to accrued sums), 6.3, 7, 8, 10, 11, 14, 15 and 16, and any other provision intended to survive, continue in force.

## 14. Governing Law and Disputes

14.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising from it are governed by the laws of Singapore.

14.2 The Parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through senior executives. If a dispute is not resolved within a reasonable period, either Party may refer it to the courts of Singapore, which shall have exclusive jurisdiction, save that either Party may seek urgent injunctive relief in any competent court.

## 15. General

15.1 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

15.2 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, courier or email to the address of the receiving Party stated in the Order Form (or as later notified), and are deemed received on delivery, or if by email, when sent without a delivery failure message.

15.3 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, with its Schedules, Order Form and the DPA, is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior understandings. No variation is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

15.4 **Force majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other promptly and uses reasonable efforts to mitigate. This clause does not excuse the Supplier's obligations under clauses 4.2 and 6.2 or any disaster recovery obligation in Schedule 1.

15.5 **Waiver and severability.** A failure to exercise a right is not a waiver. If a provision is held invalid, the remainder continues in effect.

15.6 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
