This Statement of Work No. 1 ("**SOW**") is made as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer with its headquarters in the United States ("**Customer**"), and **Orion Cybersecurity Partners LLP** ("**Supplier**"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between the same parties (the "**Parent Agreement**"). Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meaning given in the Parent Agreement.

## 1. Background

1.1 Customer operates retail businesses and wishes to obtain an independent assessment of the security posture of its information systems, a prioritised plan to remediate identified weaknesses, and verification that agreed remediation has been effective.

1.2 Supplier provides cybersecurity advisory and testing services and has agreed to perform the Services described in this SOW on the terms of this SOW and the Parent Agreement.

1.3 In the event of conflict, the body of the Parent Agreement prevails over this SOW, except where this SOW expressly states that it departs from a specific provision of the Parent Agreement.

## 2. Scope

2.1 **Services.** Supplier shall provide the following services (the "**Services**"):

(a) *Baseline assessment:* a review of Customer's in-scope networks, applications, cloud environments, endpoints and security controls, including configuration review, vulnerability scanning and manual penetration testing, against recognised industry frameworks agreed with Customer;

(b) *Remediation roadmap:* analysis of the findings and preparation of a prioritised, risk-ranked remediation plan with recommended actions, owners and sequencing; and

(c) *Retest and closure:* retesting of findings that Customer has remediated and reporting on their closure status.

2.2 **In-scope systems.** The systems, locations and environments in scope are those identified in writing by Customer and confirmed by Supplier before testing begins (the "**Scoping Document**"). Any extension of scope requires a change under clause 2.4.

2.3 **Critical findings.** Supplier shall give Customer 24 hours' notice of any critical finding. Notice shall be given to Customer's designated security contact and shall describe the finding, the affected systems and any recommended immediate mitigation. For this purpose a "critical finding" is a vulnerability or condition that Supplier reasonably considers to present an immediate and serious risk of compromise of Customer systems or data.

2.4 **Change control.** Changes to the Services, Deliverables, Milestones or Fees take effect only when set out in a written change request signed by authorised representatives of both parties.

2.5 **Exclusions.** Remediation work itself, other than the recommendations and verification described above, is outside the scope of this SOW unless agreed under clause 2.4.

## 3. Deliverables and Milestones

3.1 Supplier shall deliver the following Deliverables by the dates stated:

| Milestone | Deliverable | Description | Due Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Milestone 1 | Baseline assessment report | Findings of the baseline assessment, with severity ratings, evidence and technical detail | {{MS1}} |
| Milestone 2 | Remediation roadmap delivered | Prioritised remediation plan with recommended actions, owners and sequencing | {{MS2}} |
| Milestone 3 | Retest and closure report | Results of retesting and closure status of each finding | {{MS3}} |

3.2 Each Deliverable shall be provided in electronic form in a format reasonably agreed by the parties.

3.3 If Supplier anticipates that a Milestone will be missed, it shall promptly notify Customer, explain the cause and propose a recovery plan. A Milestone date shall be extended to the extent that delay is caused by Customer's failure to perform its responsibilities under this SOW or by a change agreed under clause 2.4.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

4.1 **Supplier** shall: (a) perform the Services with reasonable skill and care using suitably qualified and experienced personnel; (b) appoint an engagement lead as principal point of contact; (c) comply with Customer's reasonable site and security policies notified in advance; and (d) conduct testing only within the agreed scope and testing windows.

4.2 **Customer** shall: (a) appoint a project sponsor and a security contact available to receive notices under clause 2.3; (b) provide timely access to systems, facilities, documentation and personnel reasonably required; (c) obtain all consents and authorisations necessary for Supplier to test in-scope systems, including from third-party hosting providers; and (d) review Deliverables promptly.

4.3 **Governance.** The parties' engagement leads shall meet at regular intervals to review progress, risks and issues.

## 5. Assumptions

5.1 The parties have agreed the Fees and Milestone dates on the following assumptions:

(a) the Scoping Document is complete and accurate and in-scope systems are available during agreed testing windows;

(b) Customer will provide the access, information and authorisations described in clause 4.2 in a timely manner;

(c) Customer will remediate findings in sufficient time before retesting to allow Milestone 3 to be met; and

(d) testing will be performed remotely or at Customer sites as reasonably agreed, with no material interruption to Customer's retail operations.

5.2 If an assumption proves incorrect, either party may request a change under clause 2.4.

## 6. Fees and Invoicing

6.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay Supplier annual fees of USD 950,000 for the Services. The total contract value under this SOW is USD 950,000.

6.2 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall issue invoices in accordance with the invoicing schedule agreed between the parties, each referencing this SOW and addressed to Customer's accounts payable contact. A valid invoice must state the amount due, the relevant Milestone or period and any purchase order number notified by Customer.

6.3 **Payment.** Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

6.4 **Disputed amounts.** Customer shall notify Supplier promptly of any amount disputed in good faith and the reasons. The parties shall seek to resolve the dispute promptly, and Customer shall pay any undisputed portion in accordance with clause 6.3.

6.5 **Taxes and expenses.** Unless the Parent Agreement provides otherwise, the Fees are exclusive of applicable sales, use, value added and similar taxes, which shall be separately stated, and of expenses, which are payable only if approved in advance in writing by Customer.

## 7. Acceptance

7.1 Upon delivery of each Deliverable, Customer shall review it against the description in clause 3.1 and this SOW and shall, within a reasonable period, either accept it in writing or notify Supplier of any material non-conformity with reasons.

7.2 Supplier shall promptly correct any non-conformity at no additional charge and resubmit the Deliverable, whereupon the process in clause 7.1 shall repeat.

7.3 A Deliverable is accepted on the earliest of Customer's written acceptance and Customer's use of the Deliverable for its intended purpose other than for evaluation. Acceptance does not waive any right in respect of defects not reasonably discoverable on review.

7.4 If a Deliverable remains non-conforming after reasonable opportunities to correct it, Customer may exercise its remedies under the Parent Agreement.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1 This SOW commences on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier, continues until {{EXPIRY}} (the initial term of 12 months). Any extension requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

8.2 Either party may terminate this SOW on the grounds and in the manner set out in the Parent Agreement. Termination of this SOW does not terminate the Parent Agreement or any other statement of work unless expressly stated.

8.3 On expiry or termination, Supplier shall deliver all completed and in-progress Deliverables and Customer shall pay undisputed Fees properly due for Services performed up to the date of termination, in accordance with clause 6.3.

9.3 This clause survives expiry or termination of this SOW, in accordance with the Parent Agreement.
