# Statement of Work No. 1

| | |
|---|---|
| **Project** | IT Managed Services and Knowledge Transition |
| **Customer** | Arcadia Retail Inc., a retailer with its headquarters in the United States ("Customer") |
| **Supplier** | Tatva Infosystems Ltd ("Supplier") |
| **Parent Agreement** | Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between the Customer and the Supplier (the "MSA") |
| **Effective Date** | {{EFFECTIVE}} |
| **Period** | Initial term from the Effective Date until {{EXPIRY}} (24 months) |
| **Annual Fees** | USD 4,200,000 |
| **Total Contract Value** | USD 8,400,000 |

This Statement of Work No. 1 (the "SOW") is entered into between Arcadia Retail Inc. and Tatva Infosystems Ltd and is issued under the MSA. Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the MSA.

## 1. Background

1.1 The Customer operates retail businesses and requires dependable management and support of its information technology services.

1.2 The Supplier provides IT services of the kind described in this SOW and has agreed to provide them to the Customer on the terms of the MSA and this SOW.

1.3 This SOW forms part of the MSA. If there is a conflict between this SOW and the MSA, the MSA prevails unless this SOW expressly states that it overrides a specific provision of the MSA.

## 2. Scope

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the following services (the "Services"):

(a) transition of knowledge, documentation, and operational responsibility for the Customer's in-scope IT environment from the Customer and its incumbent providers to the Supplier;

(b) ongoing operation, monitoring, maintenance, and support of the in-scope applications and infrastructure following transition;

(c) incident, problem, and change management in accordance with the processes agreed under the MSA; and

(d) periodic reporting and an annual benchmarking review of the Services.

2.2 Anything not described in this SOW is outside its scope. Any additional work shall be agreed through the change control procedure in the MSA before it begins.

2.3 **Service Level.** The Supplier shall ensure that the in-scope production services are available for at least 99.0% of each calendar month, measured and reported in accordance with the MSA. Remedies for failure to meet this service level are those set out in the MSA.

## 3. Deliverables and Milestones

3.1 The Supplier shall achieve the following milestones by the dates stated:

| No. | Milestone | Deliverable | Due Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Knowledge transition complete | Completed transition of knowledge and documentation, with transition report | {{MS1}} |
| 2 | Steady-state operations begin | Services operating under agreed processes and service level reporting | {{MS2}} |
| 3 | First annual benchmarking review | Written benchmarking report and review meeting | {{MS3}} |

3.2 The Supplier shall promptly notify the Customer in writing if it becomes aware that a milestone is likely to be missed, stating the cause and its proposed recovery plan.

3.3 If a delay is caused by the Customer's failure to perform its responsibilities under this SOW, the Supplier shall be entitled to a reasonable extension of the affected due date, provided it has notified the Customer of the failure promptly.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

4.1 **Supplier.** The Supplier shall: (a) perform the Services with reasonable skill and care using suitably qualified personnel; (b) appoint a service delivery manager as the Customer's principal point of contact; (c) comply with the Customer's reasonable site and security policies notified to it; and (d) report regularly on progress, performance against the service level, and risks.

4.2 **Customer.** The Customer shall: (a) appoint a contract manager with authority to make day-to-day decisions; (b) give the Supplier timely access to information, systems, premises, and personnel reasonably required; (c) make decisions and provide approvals promptly; and (d) review deliverables in accordance with section 8.

4.3 **Governance.** The parties' representatives shall meet at regular intervals to review progress, performance, and risks. Unresolved matters shall be escalated in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions of the MSA.

## 5. Assumptions

5.1 This SOW and the fees are based on the following assumptions:

(a) the in-scope environment is substantially as described to the Supplier before the Effective Date;

(b) the Customer will make its personnel and incumbent providers reasonably available during transition;

(c) the Customer holds, or will obtain, all licences and consents needed for the Supplier to use third-party software and systems for the Services; and

(d) the Customer will not materially change the in-scope environment without following change control.

5.2 If an assumption proves incorrect and affects the Services, the parties shall address the effect through the change control procedure in the MSA.

## 6. Fees and Invoicing

6.1 The annual fees for the Services are USD 4,200,000, giving a total contract value of USD 8,400,000 over the initial term. The fees are the Supplier's full remuneration for the Services, except for any additional work agreed under change control.

6.2 The Supplier shall invoice in the manner and at the intervals set out in the MSA. Each invoice must be valid, showing the SOW reference, the period covered, the amount due, and any applicable taxes.

6.3 The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

6.4 If the Customer disputes any part of an invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Supplier promptly, pay the undisputed part, and the parties shall resolve the disputed part under the MSA dispute resolution provisions.

6.5 Fees are stated exclusive of applicable sales, value added, or similar taxes, which shall be dealt with as provided in the MSA.

## 7. Term and Termination

7.1 This SOW starts on the Effective Date and continues for the initial term ending on {{EXPIRY}}, unless extended by written agreement or terminated earlier in accordance with this SOW or the MSA.

7.2 Either party may terminate this SOW in accordance with the termination rights in the MSA, including for material breach that remains unremedied after notice and a reasonable opportunity to remedy.

7.3 On expiry or termination, the Supplier shall provide reasonable exit assistance as set out in the MSA, and the Customer shall pay undisputed fees properly due for Services performed up to the date of termination.

## 8. Acceptance

8.1 On completing each milestone, the Supplier shall notify the Customer in writing and submit the relevant deliverables.

8.2 The Customer shall review the deliverables promptly and within a reasonable period, and shall either accept them in writing or notify the Supplier of the respects in which they do not materially conform to this SOW.

8.3 The Supplier shall correct any non-conformity promptly and resubmit the deliverables, and this process shall repeat until the deliverables are accepted or the Customer exercises its rights under the MSA.

8.4 Acceptance does not waive the Customer's rights in respect of defects that were not reasonably discoverable at the time of review, nor the Supplier's continuing obligations under this SOW and the MSA.

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other party received in connection with this SOW, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only as permitted by the confidentiality provisions of the MSA, which apply to this SOW.

## 10. Intellectual Property

10.1 Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property rights.

10.2 Ownership of deliverables and other materials created for the Customer under this SOW, and the licences granted to each party, are as set out in the intellectual property provisions of the MSA.

## 11. Warranties

The Supplier warrants that it will perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with good industry practice, and in compliance with applicable law. Each party warrants that it has full authority to enter into this SOW. The warranties in the MSA also apply to this SOW.

## 12. Data Protection

Each party shall comply with applicable data protection law in connection with this SOW. To the extent the Supplier processes personal data on the Customer's behalf, it shall do so only on the Customer's documented instructions, maintain appropriate technical and organisational security measures, and comply with the data processing terms of the MSA.

## 13. Liability and Indemnity

13.1 Liability under this SOW is governed by the liability provisions of the MSA.

13.2 The indemnities in the MSA apply to this SOW in accordance with their terms.

## 14. General

14.1 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign or transfer its rights or obligations under this SOW without the prior written consent of the other, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld, except as permitted by the MSA.

14.2 **Notices.** Notices under this SOW shall be given in writing and delivered in accordance with the notices provision of the MSA.

14.3 **Entire agreement.** This SOW and the MSA constitute the entire agreement between the parties on their subject matter and supersede all prior discussions, representations, and understandings relating to it. Variations are effective only if in writing and signed by both parties.

14.4 **Governing law.** This SOW and any non-contractual obligations arising from it are governed by the laws of England and Wales, and disputes shall be resolved in accordance with the jurisdiction and dispute resolution provisions of the MSA.

14.5 **Counterparts.** This SOW may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
