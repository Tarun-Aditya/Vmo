# Statement of Work No. 1

| | |
|---|---|
| **Project** | Network Connectivity Deployment: Site Survey and Circuit Installation |
| **Parent Agreement** | Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between Arcadia Retail Inc. and Vantage Telecom Services Inc |
| **Period** | Effective Date {{EFFECTIVE}} until {{EXPIRY}} (initial term of 10 months) |
| **Fees** | USD 540,000 (total contract value) |

This Statement of Work No. 1 (the "**SOW**") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer with its headquarters in the United States (the "**Customer**"), and **Vantage Telecom Services Inc** (the "**Supplier**"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between the Customer and the Supplier (the "**Parent Agreement**").

## 1. Background

1.1 The Customer operates retail locations and requires reliable, managed network connectivity to support its business operations.

1.2 The Supplier provides telecommunications and network services and has agreed to perform a site survey and install network circuits at the Customer's locations on the terms of this SOW and the Parent Agreement.

1.3 Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Parent Agreement.

## 2. Interpretation and Order of Precedence

2.1 This SOW is governed by the Parent Agreement. If there is a conflict between this SOW and the Parent Agreement, the Parent Agreement prevails, unless this SOW expressly identifies the clause of the Parent Agreement it is intended to override and the parties have agreed in writing to that override.

2.2 References to clauses are to clauses of this SOW unless stated otherwise.

## 3. Scope

3.1 **Services.** The Supplier shall provide the following services (the "**Services**"):

(a) **Site survey:** inspect each Customer location identified in writing by the Customer, assess physical access, wiring, power, environmental and carrier-handoff conditions, and deliver a written survey report with recommended installation designs and any identified remediation items;

(b) **Circuit installation:** procure, install, terminate, configure, test and hand over the circuits and associated demarcation equipment at each surveyed location in accordance with the approved designs; and

(c) **Project management:** coordinate scheduling, carrier orders, site access and status reporting for the duration of the SOW.

3.2 **Out of scope.** Any work not described in clause 3.1, including structural building works, remediation of third-party premises, and ongoing managed services after handover, is excluded unless agreed through the change control procedure in clause 9.

3.3 **Standard of performance.** The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using suitably qualified personnel, in accordance with good industry practice and applicable law.

## 4. Deliverables and Milestones

4.1 The Supplier shall deliver the following Deliverables by the dates shown:

| Milestone | Deliverable | Description | Due Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Milestone 1 | Site survey complete | Survey of all identified locations completed and written survey report with proposed designs delivered to the Customer | {{MS1}} |
| Milestone 2 | Circuit installation complete | Circuits installed, tested and handed over at all surveyed locations, with test results and as-built documentation | {{MS2}} |

4.2 The Supplier shall promptly notify the Customer in writing if it becomes aware that a Milestone is likely to be delayed, giving the reasons and a recovery plan.

4.3 A Milestone date shall be extended to the extent that delay is caused by the Customer's failure to perform its responsibilities under this SOW, provided the Supplier notifies the Customer of the delay promptly after becoming aware of it.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

5.1 **Supplier.** The Supplier is responsible for performing the Services and delivering the Deliverables, providing appropriately skilled personnel, complying with the Customer's reasonable site rules and safety requirements, and nominating a project manager as the Customer's primary contact.

5.2 **Customer.** The Customer is responsible for providing timely access to its locations and relevant personnel, supplying accurate information on its premises and existing network environment, obtaining any landlord or third-party consents within its control, making decisions and giving approvals without undue delay, and nominating a project sponsor as the Supplier's primary contact.

5.3 **Governance.** The project manager and project sponsor shall meet or confer at reasonable intervals to review progress, risks and issues. Unresolved issues may be escalated in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions of the Parent Agreement.

## 6. Assumptions

6.1 The parties have agreed the Fees and Milestone dates on the basis that:

(a) the Customer will provide access to each location during normal business hours, or at other agreed times, on reasonable notice;

(b) suitable power, space and environmental conditions exist at each location or will be provided by the Customer;

(c) third-party carriers will deliver their circuits and services within their standard provisioning processes, and the Supplier will not be responsible for carrier delays outside its reasonable control where it has placed orders and followed up diligently;

(d) the list of locations will not change materially after completion of the site survey, except through change control; and

(e) the Customer's information supplied to the Supplier is accurate and complete in all material respects.

6.2 If an assumption proves incorrect, the Supplier shall promptly notify the Customer and the parties shall address the impact through the change control procedure in clause 9.

## 7. Fees and Invoicing

7.1 **Fees.** The total contract value for the Services under this SOW is USD 540,000 (the "**Fees**"). The Fees are inclusive of all Supplier costs of performing the Services unless this SOW states otherwise.

7.2 **Invoicing.** The Supplier shall invoice the Fees in accordance with the Milestones, or as otherwise agreed in writing by the parties, and each invoice must be valid, referencing this SOW, and itemise the Services or Milestone to which it relates. Applicable taxes shall be shown separately.

7.3 **Payment.** The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 60 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

7.4 **Disputed amounts.** If the Customer disputes an invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Supplier promptly, pay any undisputed portion in accordance with clause 7.3, and the parties shall work together to resolve the dispute in accordance with the Parent Agreement.

7.5 **Expenses.** The Supplier shall not charge expenses in addition to the Fees unless the Customer has approved them in writing in advance.

## 8. Acceptance

8.1 **Delivery for acceptance.** On completing each Milestone, the Supplier shall notify the Customer in writing and submit the relevant Deliverables for review.

8.2 **Review.** The Customer shall review the Deliverables promptly and in a reasonable period, and shall either accept them in writing or notify the Supplier of any material non-conformity with the requirements of this SOW.

8.3 **Remediation.** The Supplier shall correct any material non-conformity promptly at its own cost and resubmit the Deliverables for review under this clause 8.

8.4 **Acceptance.** A Milestone is accepted when the Customer gives written acceptance. The Customer shall not unreasonably withhold or delay acceptance of Deliverables that conform in all material respects to this SOW.

## 9. Change Control

9.1 Either party may propose a change to the scope, Deliverables, Milestone dates or Fees. No change is effective unless set out in a written change request signed by authorised representatives of both parties.

9.2 The Supplier shall provide a written assessment of the impact of any requested change on the Fees, timetable and Services within a reasonable time.

## 10. Term and Termination

10.1 This SOW commences on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this clause or the Parent Agreement, continues until {{EXPIRY}}, being an initial term of 10 months.

10.2 Either party may terminate this SOW in accordance with the termination provisions of the Parent Agreement, including for uncured material breach.

10.3 On expiry or termination, the Customer shall pay for Services properly performed and accepted, or properly performed and not reasonably rejected, up to the date of termination, and each party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information in accordance with the Parent Agreement.

10.4 Termination or expiry of this SOW does not affect the Parent Agreement or any other statement of work, nor any rights accrued before termination.

## 11. Confidentiality, Intellectual Property and Data Protection

11.1 **Confidentiality.** Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential in accordance with the Parent Agreement. Network designs, site layouts and security configurations produced under this SOW are the Customer's Confidential Information.

11.2 **Intellectual property.** Ownership of intellectual property is governed by the Parent Agreement. Unless the Parent Agreement provides otherwise, the Customer owns the as-built documentation and survey reports delivered to it, and the Supplier retains its pre-existing materials, tools and know-how, granting the Customer a non-exclusive licence to use them as necessary to receive the benefit of the Services.

11.3 **Data protection.** Each party shall comply with data protection and privacy laws applicable to it. The Supplier shall process any personal data of the Customer solely to perform the Services and in accordance with the Customer's documented instructions and the data protection terms of the Parent Agreement, and shall maintain appropriate technical and organisational security measures.

## 12. Warranties, Indemnity and Liability

12.1 The Supplier warrants that it has the right and authority to perform the Services, that it will perform them in accordance with clause 3.3, and that the Deliverables will conform in all material respects to the requirements of this SOW.

12.2 Each party's indemnification obligations in relation to this SOW are as set out in the Parent Agreement.

12.3 Liability under this SOW is governed by the liability provisions of the Parent Agreement.

## 13. General

13.1 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign or subcontract its rights or obligations under this SOW except as permitted by the Parent Agreement.

13.2 **Notices.** Notices under this SOW shall be given in writing in the manner provided in the Parent Agreement.

13.3 **Entire agreement.** This SOW and the Parent Agreement constitute the entire agreement of the parties regarding the Services and supersede all prior discussions and understandings regarding them. This SOW may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.

13.4 **Governing law.** This SOW and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of Texas, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

13.5 **Counterparts.** This SOW may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
