# Work Order No. 1

This Work Order is issued under the Statement of Work dated {{PARENT_DATE}} (the "Parent Agreement") between Arcadia Retail Inc., a retailer with its headquarters in the United States (the "Customer"), and Helix Digital Consulting Inc (the "Supplier"). It takes effect on {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "Effective Date"). Capitalised terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Parent Agreement.

1. **Term.** This Work Order commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of six (6) months, expiring on {{EXPIRY}}, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Work Order or extended by written agreement signed by both parties.

2. **Fees.** The total contract value for this Work Order is USD 420,000, which is a fixed fee for the Services and Deliverables described in this Work Order. The fixed fee is inclusive of all labour, tooling and overhead of the Supplier. No additional fees or expenses are payable unless approved in advance in writing by the Customer.

3. **Invoicing and Payment.** The Supplier shall invoice the fixed fee in accordance with the milestones in clause 5 or as otherwise agreed in writing. Each invoice must be valid, addressed to the Customer, reference this Work Order, and itemise the amount claimed. The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 45 days of receipt of a valid invoice. The Customer shall notify the Supplier promptly of any disputed amount, giving reasons, and the parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute. Payment of an invoice does not constitute acceptance of any Deliverable.

4. **Services and Scope.** The Supplier shall provide the following services (the "Services"): design and documentation of the interfaces between the Customer's systems and the target platform, and preparation, execution and completion of a cutover rehearsal for the Customer's migration, in each case as further described in the Parent Agreement. Any change to scope, Deliverables or timetable requires a written change request signed by both parties. Work outside the agreed scope is not authorised and is not payable.

5. **Milestones and Acceptance.** The Supplier shall achieve the following milestones:

| Milestone | Description | Due Date |
|---|---|---|
| Milestone 1 | Interface specification delivered | {{MS1}} |
| Milestone 2 | Cutover rehearsal complete | {{MS2}} |

   The Customer shall review each Deliverable within a reasonable period after delivery and shall either accept it or notify the Supplier in writing of any material non-conformity. The Supplier shall correct any non-conformity promptly at no additional charge and resubmit the Deliverable. The Supplier shall notify the Customer without delay if it anticipates a delay to any milestone.

6. **Customer Responsibilities.** The Customer shall provide timely access to relevant personnel, information, environments and decisions reasonably required for the Services. The Supplier is not responsible for delay to the extent caused by the Customer's failure to do so, provided the Supplier has promptly notified the Customer of the failure.

7. **Personnel.** The Supplier shall perform the Services with suitably skilled and experienced personnel, remains responsible for their acts and omissions, and shall comply with the Customer's reasonable site, security and conduct policies notified to it.

8. **Confidentiality.** Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other in connection with this Work Order, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and not disclose it except to its personnel, advisers and subcontractors who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations, or as required by law or a court or regulator. This clause does not apply to information that is or becomes public other than through breach, was already lawfully known to the recipient, or is independently developed. These obligations survive termination or expiry of this Work Order. Where the Parent Agreement contains confidentiality provisions, those provisions also apply and, in case of conflict, the more protective provision prevails.

9. **Intellectual Property.** Each party retains its pre-existing intellectual property rights. Upon payment of the fees due for them, the Customer shall own the Deliverables created specifically for it under this Work Order, excluding the Supplier's pre-existing materials, tools and know-how, for which the Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use as part of the Deliverables for its internal business purposes. The Customer's data and materials remain the property of the Customer.

10. **Warranties.** The Supplier warrants that it shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care in accordance with good industry practice, that the Deliverables will materially conform to the agreed specification, and that the Services and Deliverables will not knowingly infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party. Each party warrants that it has authority to enter into this Work Order.

11. **Data Protection.** Each party shall comply with data protection and privacy laws applicable to its processing of personal data under this Work Order. The Supplier shall process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions, implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures, ensure that personnel with access are bound by confidentiality, assist the Customer in responding to data subject requests and regulatory enquiries, notify the Customer without undue delay on becoming aware of a personal data breach, and on termination or expiry return or delete personal data at the Customer's election, save as required by law.

12. **Indemnity.** The Supplier shall defend and indemnify the Customer against third-party claims alleging that the Deliverables, as supplied by the Supplier, infringe that third party's intellectual property rights, subject to the Customer giving prompt notice of the claim, allowing the Supplier reasonable control of its defence and settlement, and providing reasonable assistance at the Supplier's cost. The Supplier has no liability under this clause to the extent the claim arises from the Customer's modifications, instructions or combination of the Deliverables with items not supplied by the Supplier. Any other indemnities are as set out in the Parent Agreement.

13. **Liability.** Each party's liability arising under or in connection with this Work Order is governed by the liability provisions of the Parent Agreement, which apply to this Work Order as if set out in full.

14. **Termination.** Either party may terminate this Work Order by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within a reasonable period after written notice requiring remedy, or becomes insolvent or subject to bankruptcy or similar proceedings. The Customer may also terminate for convenience on written notice, in which case it shall pay for Services properly performed and Deliverables accepted up to the termination date, together with reasonable, demonstrable and unavoidable costs committed in reliance on this Work Order. Clauses intended by their nature to survive, including clauses 8, 9, 12, 13 and 17, survive termination or expiry.

15. **Assignment and Subcontracting.** Neither party may assign or transfer this Work Order without the prior written consent of the other, not to be unreasonably withheld. The Supplier may not subcontract any part of the Services without the Customer's prior written consent and remains liable for its subcontractors.

16. **Notices.** Notices under this Work Order must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognised courier or by email to the address of the receiving party last notified in writing (or as specified in the Parent Agreement). A notice is deemed received on delivery by hand or courier, or, if sent by email, at the time of transmission if sent on a business day during business hours and otherwise on the next business day.

17. **Governing Law and Disputes.** This Work Order and any dispute arising from it are governed by the laws of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. Any dispute shall be resolved in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions of the Parent Agreement and, where none apply, by the courts of competent jurisdiction in New York, to whose jurisdiction the parties submit.

18. **Order of Precedence and Entire Agreement.** This Work Order, together with the Parent Agreement, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions and understandings relating to it. In case of conflict, this Work Order prevails over the Parent Agreement solely as to the Services, fees, term and milestones specified here; in all other respects the Parent Agreement prevails. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect. A failure to enforce a right is not a waiver of it.

19. **Counterparts.** This Work Order may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. This Work Order becomes binding on the parties when countersigned by the Customer.
