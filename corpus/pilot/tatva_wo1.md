# Work Order

This Work Order is issued under the Statement of Work dated {{PARENT_DATE}} (the "Parent Agreement") between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer with its headquarters in the United States (the "Customer"), and **Tatva Infosystems Ltd** (the "Supplier"). It takes effect on {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "Effective Date") and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}}, being an initial term of five (5) months (the "Term"). The Supplier shall perform the Services for a fixed fee of USD 260,000 (the "Fee"), payable in accordance with paragraph 3.

## 1. Interpretation and Precedence

1.1 Capitalised terms not defined in this Work Order have the meanings given in the Parent Agreement.

1.2 This Work Order forms part of, and is to be read together with, the Parent Agreement. If there is a conflict, the terms of this Work Order prevail only as to the Services and Deliverables described in it and only to the extent expressly stated; in all other respects the Parent Agreement prevails.

1.3 References to paragraphs are to paragraphs of this Work Order.

## 2. Services and Deliverables

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the following services to the Customer (the "Services"): an assessment of the Customer's mainframe environment, including its applications, interfaces, batch workloads, data stores and operational dependencies, and the preparation of the reports and recommendations described in this paragraph 2.

2.2 The Supplier shall deliver the milestone set out below (each a "Milestone", and the output of each a "Deliverable"):

| Milestone | Deliverable | Due date |
|---|---|---|
| Milestone 1 | Mainframe assessment report | {{MS1}} |

2.3 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using suitably qualified and experienced personnel, and in accordance with the Customer's reasonable site, security and change-control policies notified to the Supplier in writing.

2.4 The Customer shall provide timely access to relevant personnel, systems, documentation and information reasonably required by the Supplier. The Supplier shall promptly notify the Customer if any delay in such access is likely to affect a Milestone. The due date of a Milestone shall be extended by a period equal to any delay caused by the Customer's failure to comply with this paragraph, provided the Supplier has given such notice.

2.5 Each Deliverable is subject to review by the Customer. The Customer shall notify the Supplier of acceptance or of any material non-conformity with the description in paragraph 2.1 within a reasonable period after delivery. The Supplier shall correct any notified non-conformity promptly and at no additional charge and resubmit the Deliverable. A Deliverable is accepted when the Customer notifies acceptance in writing or uses it for its intended purpose other than for evaluation.

2.6 Any change to the scope, Milestones or Fee shall be effective only if recorded in a written change request signed by an authorised representative of each party.

## 3. Fee and Invoicing

3.1 The Fee of USD 260,000 is a fixed fee for the Services and Deliverables under this Work Order. It is the Supplier's entire remuneration for them and includes all of the Supplier's costs, overheads and expenses unless this Work Order expressly states otherwise.

3.2 The Supplier shall invoice the Fee in the manner agreed in writing between the parties or, if no manner is agreed, in accordance with the invoicing provisions of the Parent Agreement. Each invoice shall be valid only if it quotes this Work Order, identifies the Services or Milestone to which it relates and is addressed to the Customer's accounts payable function.

3.3 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt of a valid invoice.

3.4 If the Customer disputes any part of an invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Supplier promptly, stating its reasons, and shall pay the undisputed part in accordance with paragraph 3.3. The parties shall work together to resolve the dispute promptly.

3.5 All sums are stated exclusive of applicable sales, use, value added or similar taxes, which shall be payable in addition where properly chargeable and shown on a valid tax invoice. Each party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

## 4. Confidentiality

4.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other party that is disclosed in connection with this Work Order or that would reasonably be regarded as confidential ("Confidential Information"), shall use it only to perform or receive the Services, and shall not disclose it except to its employees, contractors and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations.

4.2 Paragraph 4.1 does not apply to information that is or becomes public other than through breach, was lawfully known to the recipient before disclosure, is independently developed, or is lawfully received from a third party without restriction. A party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or a competent authority, giving the other party prompt notice where lawful.

4.3 This paragraph 4 is in addition to any confidentiality provisions of the Parent Agreement and survives expiry or termination.

## 5. Intellectual Property

5.1 Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property rights and those it develops independently of this Work Order.

5.2 Subject to payment of the Fee, the Supplier assigns to the Customer all intellectual property rights in the Deliverables, excluding the Supplier's pre-existing materials, tools, methodologies and know-how ("Supplier Materials").

5.3 To the extent Supplier Materials are incorporated in a Deliverable, the Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use and reproduce them as part of the Deliverable for the Customer's and its affiliates' internal business purposes.

5.4 The Customer grants the Supplier a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence during the Term to use the Customer's materials and systems solely to perform the Services.

## 6. Warranties

6.1 The Supplier warrants that: (a) it has the right and authority to enter into and perform this Work Order; (b) the Services will be performed in accordance with paragraph 2.3 and in compliance with applicable law; and (c) the Deliverables, when delivered, will not infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party.

6.2 The Customer warrants that it has the right and authority to enter into this Work Order and to provide the materials and access described in paragraph 2.4.

6.3 Except as expressly stated in this Work Order or the Parent Agreement, all warranties and conditions implied by law are excluded to the fullest extent permitted by law.

## 7. Liability and Indemnity

7.1 Each party's liability arising under or in connection with this Work Order is governed by the liability provisions of the Parent Agreement, which apply to this Work Order as if set out in full.

7.2 Nothing in this Work Order limits or excludes liability that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded, including liability for fraud or for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

7.3 The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against losses, damages and reasonable costs arising from any third-party claim that the Deliverables or the Services infringe that third party's intellectual property rights, provided the Customer promptly notifies the Supplier of the claim, allows the Supplier to conduct its defence and settlement, and provides reasonable assistance at the Supplier's cost. The indemnity does not apply to the extent the claim arises from the Customer's materials or from modifications made other than by or for the Supplier. This indemnity is subject to paragraph 7.1.

## 8. Data Protection

8.1 Each party shall comply with the data protection and privacy laws applicable to it in connection with this Work Order.

8.2 To the extent the Supplier processes personal data on behalf of the Customer, it shall do so only on the Customer's documented instructions, shall apply appropriate technical and organisational security measures, shall ensure that persons authorised to process the data are bound by confidentiality, shall assist the Customer in responding to data subject requests and regulatory enquiries, and shall notify the Customer without undue delay on becoming aware of a personal data breach.

8.3 The Supplier shall not transfer such personal data across borders except in compliance with applicable law and the Parent Agreement, and on termination or expiry shall return or securely delete it at the Customer's election, save where retention is required by law.

## 9. Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this Work Order by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is incapable of remedy or, if capable of remedy, is not remedied within a reasonable period stated in a written notice requiring remedy.

9.2 Either party may terminate this Work Order by written notice if the other becomes insolvent, enters into administration, liquidation or any analogous procedure, or ceases to carry on business.

9.3 On expiry or termination, the Supplier shall promptly deliver to the Customer all completed and work-in-progress Deliverables, and the Customer shall pay the undisputed part of the Fee properly attributable to Services performed and Deliverables supplied up to the date of termination, calculated on a fair and proportionate basis, subject to paragraph 3.4.

9.4 Termination or expiry does not affect accrued rights or any provision intended to survive, including paragraphs 4, 5, 7, 8, 10 and 11.

9.5 Termination of this Work Order does not of itself terminate the Parent Agreement.

## 10. General

10.1 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign, transfer or subcontract any of its rights or obligations under this Work Order without the prior written consent of the other, not to be unreasonably withheld. The Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

10.2 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognised courier or by email to the address of the receiving party stated in the Parent Agreement (or as later notified in writing). A notice is deemed received on delivery or, for email, at the time of transmission if sent during business hours at the place of receipt and otherwise at the start of the next business day.

10.3 **Entire agreement.** This Work Order and the Parent Agreement constitute the entire agreement between the parties regarding the Services and supersede all prior discussions, representations and understandings on that subject. Each party acknowledges that it has not relied on any statement not set out in them, without limiting liability for fraud.

10.4 **Variation and waiver.** No variation is effective unless in writing and signed by both parties. A failure or delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of it.

10.5 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remainder continues in full force.

10.6 **Counterparts.** This Work Order may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

## 11. Governing Law and Disputes

11.1 This Work Order and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it (including non-contractual disputes or claims) are governed by the laws of England and Wales.

11.2 The parties shall first seek to resolve any dispute through good-faith discussion between senior representatives, and otherwise in accordance with the dispute resolution and jurisdiction provisions of the Parent Agreement.
