# Software-as-a-Service Subscription Agreement

This Software-as-a-Service Subscription Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer headquartered in the United States ("**Customer**"), and **Atlas Facilities Tech Inc** ("**Supplier**").

1. **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) months until {{EXPIRY}} (the "**Initial Term**"). It renews automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months (each a "**Renewal Term**") unless either party gives the other at least 30 days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term. The Initial Term and any Renewal Term are together the "**Term**".

2. **Fees.** Customer shall pay Supplier annual subscription fees of USD 96,000. The total contract value for the Initial Term is USD 96,000. The fees are exclusive of applicable sales and use taxes, which Supplier shall itemise on the invoice. Supplier may not increase the fees during the Initial Term. Fees for any Renewal Term remain those stated in this clause unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

3. **Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice Customer for the fees in accordance with this Agreement. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice. A valid invoice identifies this Agreement, the Services and the amount due, and is addressed to Customer's accounts payable contact. Customer shall notify Supplier promptly in writing of any good-faith dispute, giving reasons, and shall pay the undisputed portion on time. The parties shall work together promptly to resolve any dispute. Supplier shall not suspend the Services because of an amount disputed in good faith.

4. **Services.** Supplier shall provide Customer and its authorised users with access to its hosted facilities-management software platform, together with related support and maintenance (the "**Services**"), as described in Supplier's then-current documentation and any order form agreed in writing by the parties. Supplier grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the Term to access and use the Services for its internal business purposes, including use by its affiliates and contractors acting on its behalf. Customer shall not (a) sublicense or resell the Services, (b) reverse engineer the Services except as permitted by law, or (c) use the Services in violation of applicable law. Customer is responsible for its users' compliance with this Agreement and for the security of its access credentials.

5. **Service Levels.** Supplier shall make the Services available at least 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance notified to Customer in advance and outages caused by Customer or by events beyond Supplier's reasonable control. Supplier shall notify Customer of any security incident affecting the Services or Customer Data within 48 hours of becoming aware of it, and shall provide reasonable information and cooperation to help Customer assess and respond. If Supplier fails to meet the availability commitment, Supplier shall promptly investigate, remedy the cause and provide Customer a written root-cause report on request. Repeated failure to meet the availability commitment is a material breach for purposes of clause 14.

6. **Customer Data.** As between the parties, Customer owns all data and content that Customer or its users submit to the Services ("**Customer Data**"). Customer grants Supplier a limited licence to use Customer Data solely to provide the Services to Customer. Supplier shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards that are appropriate to the nature of the Customer Data and consistent with recognised industry practice. On termination or expiry, Supplier shall make Customer Data available for export for a reasonable period and shall then delete it on Customer's written request, except where retention is required by law.

7. **Personal Data.** The parties acknowledge that the Services do not involve the processing of personal data. Customer shall not submit personal data to the Services, and Supplier shall have no obligation to process it. If the parties later agree that personal data will be processed, they shall first enter into a written data processing agreement that complies with applicable law.

8. **Confidentiality.** Each party (the "**Recipient**") shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other party (the "**Discloser**") that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential, and shall use it only to perform or receive the benefit of this Agreement. The Recipient may disclose it to its employees, contractors and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations, or where required by law, after giving the Discloser prompt notice where lawful. These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient, was lawfully known to or independently developed by the Recipient, or was lawfully received from a third party without restriction. The obligations in this clause survive for 2 years after this Agreement ends.

9. **Intellectual Property.** Supplier and its licensors retain all intellectual property rights in the Services and in any improvements to them. Customer retains all rights in Customer Data and its own materials. Except for the rights expressly granted in this Agreement, no rights are transferred by either party. If Customer provides feedback about the Services, Supplier may use it without restriction or obligation, provided it does not disclose Customer's confidential information.

10. **Warranties.** Supplier warrants that (a) it has the right and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement; (b) it shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care and in accordance with applicable law; and (c) for a period of 30 days from the Effective Date, the Services will perform materially in accordance with their documentation. If the Services breach the warranty in (c), Supplier shall, at its cost, correct the non-conformity promptly or, if it cannot do so within a reasonable time, Customer may terminate and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the period after termination. Except as stated in this Agreement, and to the extent permitted by law, all other warranties, express or implied, are excluded.

11. **Indemnity.** Supplier shall defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that Customer's use of the Services in accordance with this Agreement infringes that third party's intellectual property rights, and shall pay the damages and costs finally awarded or agreed in settlement. Customer shall defend Supplier against any third-party claim arising from Customer Data or Customer's use of the Services in breach of this Agreement, and shall pay the damages and costs finally awarded or agreed in settlement. The indemnified party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying party sole control of the defence and settlement (provided no settlement admits fault on behalf of the indemnified party without its consent), and give reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense. Supplier has no obligation for claims arising from modifications not made by Supplier or from use in combination with items not supplied or approved by Supplier. If the Services become subject to such a claim, Supplier may procure the right for Customer to continue using them or modify them to be non-infringing.

12. **Limitation of Liability.** Subject to the final sentence of this clause, each party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, is limited to one (1) times the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for loss of profits, revenue or goodwill, arising out of this Agreement. Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, gross negligence, breach of clause 8 (Confidentiality), a party's indemnification obligations under clause 11, or any liability that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded.

13. **Termination for Convenience.** Customer may terminate this Agreement without cause on 30 days' written notice to Supplier. On such termination Supplier shall refund any fees prepaid for the period after the termination date, and Customer shall pay undisputed fees for Services provided up to that date.

14. **Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and, where the breach is capable of remedy, fails to remedy it within 30 days of written notice requiring it to do so. Either party may terminate immediately by written notice if the other becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to bankruptcy or similar proceedings. Where Customer terminates under this clause, Supplier shall refund prepaid fees for the period after termination.

15. **Effect of Termination.** On termination or expiry, Customer's right to use the Services ends, each party shall return or destroy the other's confidential information on request, and clause 6 (as to data export and deletion) applies. Clauses 8, 9, 11, 12, 15, 17 and 18, and any other provisions intended by their nature to survive, survive termination or expiry. Termination or expiry does not affect accrued rights or liabilities.

16. **Assignment and Subcontracting.** Neither party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, except that either party may assign it to a successor in a merger or sale of all or substantially all of its relevant business on written notice. Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

17. **Governing Law and Disputes.** This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of Illinois, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Illinois. Before commencing proceedings (other than for urgent injunctive relief), the parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through escalation to senior executives.

18. **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognised courier or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the address or email most recently notified by the receiving party. A notice is deemed received on delivery if by hand or courier, or on the next business day after sending if by email. Notices of breach or termination sent by email must also be sent by courier.

19. **General.** This Agreement, together with any order form signed by both parties, is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions and understandings. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties. If a provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect. A failure to enforce a right is not a waiver of it. Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, other than payment obligations. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, agency or employment relationship. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original.
