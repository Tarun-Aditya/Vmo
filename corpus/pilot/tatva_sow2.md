# Statement of Work No. 1 – QA Centre of Excellence and Test Automation Services

|---|---|

This Statement of Work No. 1 (the "**SOW**") is made between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer with its headquarters in the United States (the "**Customer**"), and **Tatva Infosystems Ltd** (the "**Supplier**"), with effect from {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**").

## 1. Background

1.1 The Customer and the Supplier are parties to a Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} (the "**Parent Agreement**").

1.2 The Customer wishes to establish a centralised quality assurance function and to increase the proportion of its software testing that is automated. The Supplier has agreed to provide the services described in this SOW on the terms of the Parent Agreement and this SOW.

1.3 This SOW is issued under, and forms part of, the Parent Agreement. Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Parent Agreement. If there is a conflict, the Parent Agreement prevails over this SOW unless this SOW expressly states that it overrides a specified provision of the Parent Agreement.

## 2. Scope

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the following services (the "**Services**") to the Customer:

(a) design, staffing and establishment of a QA centre of excellence, including test strategy, governance, standards, tooling selection and reporting;

(b) assessment of the Customer's existing test assets and the development of a prioritised automation roadmap;

(c) design, build and maintenance of automated test suites across the Customer's agreed e-commerce, point-of-sale and supply-chain applications; and

(d) ongoing test execution, defect reporting and knowledge transfer to the Customer's personnel.

2.2 Any activity not described in clause 2.1 is outside the scope of this SOW and may be added only through the change control procedure in the Parent Agreement.

2.3 **Service level.** The Supplier shall ensure that the test automation platform and reporting environment operated by the Supplier under this SOW are available for at least 99.5% of each month. Availability shall be measured and reported monthly in accordance with the Parent Agreement. Remedies for failure to meet this service level are as set out in the Parent Agreement.

## 3. Deliverables and Milestones

3.1 The Supplier shall deliver the following by the dates stated:

| No. | Milestone / Deliverable | Description | Due Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Milestone 1 | QA centre of excellence established | Operating model, governance framework, standards, tooling and core team in place and operational | {{MS1}} |
| Milestone 2 | Automation coverage reaches 60 percent | Automated tests covering 60 percent of the agreed regression test inventory, as measured under the baseline in clause 5 | {{MS2}} |

3.2 The Supplier shall provide monthly status reports covering progress against milestones, risks, issues, service level performance and defect metrics.

3.3 The Supplier shall notify the Customer promptly if it anticipates that a milestone will not be achieved by its due date, giving reasons and a proposed recovery plan.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

4.1 **Supplier.** The Supplier shall appoint a delivery manager as the single point of accountability, provide suitably skilled and experienced personnel, and perform the Services with reasonable skill and care in accordance with good industry practice.

4.2 **Customer.** The Customer shall appoint a programme sponsor and a product owner for each in-scope application, make decisions in a timely manner, and provide the access, information and environments reasonably required by the Supplier.

4.3 **Governance.** The parties shall hold a monthly steering meeting attended by the delivery manager and the Customer's programme sponsor to review progress, risks and any proposed changes.

| Role | Party | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Executive signatory | Customer: Michael Brennan, Chief Procurement Officer | Commercial oversight and approval of this SOW |
| Authorised signatory | Supplier: Daniel Okafor | Commitment of the Supplier to this SOW |
| Programme sponsor | Customer | Prioritisation, approvals and acceptance |
| Delivery manager | Supplier | Day-to-day delivery, reporting and resource management |

## 5. Assumptions

5.1 The Services and the milestone dates are based on the following assumptions:

(a) the Customer will provide timely access to systems, test environments, test data and subject-matter experts;

(b) the agreed regression test inventory will be baselined jointly by the parties within the first weeks following the Effective Date and will not change materially without agreement under change control;

(c) the in-scope applications are stable enough to support automation and are not subject to unannounced major re-platforming; and

(d) the Customer's third-party vendors will cooperate reasonably with the Supplier.

5.2 If an assumption proves incorrect, the Supplier shall promptly notify the Customer and the parties shall agree, through change control, any necessary adjustment to the milestone dates, Services or fees. The Supplier is relieved of failure to meet a milestone only to the extent caused by the Customer's failure to meet these assumptions or its responsibilities.

## 6. Fees and Invoicing

6.1 The annual fees for the Services are USD 1,850,000. The total contract value of this SOW is USD 1,850,000.

6.2 The Supplier shall invoice the fees in the instalments agreed in writing by the parties' representatives. Each invoice must be valid, meaning it identifies this SOW, the Customer's purchase order reference, the Services or milestone to which it relates, and any applicable taxes shown separately.

6.3 The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

6.4 If the Customer disputes any part of an invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Supplier promptly, pay the undisputed portion, and the parties shall work to resolve the dispute under the dispute resolution provisions of the Parent Agreement.

6.5 Fees are exclusive of applicable sales, use, value added or similar taxes, which shall be borne as provided in the Parent Agreement. Expenses are payable only if approved in advance in writing by the Customer.

## 7. Acceptance

7.1 On completing a milestone, the Supplier shall give the Customer written notice together with evidence that the milestone criteria in clause 3 have been met.

7.2 The Customer shall review the deliverables and either accept them or give written notice of any material non-conformity with reasonable detail, within a reasonable period after receipt of the Supplier's notice.

7.3 The Supplier shall promptly correct any non-conformity at its own cost and resubmit the deliverable for review. This process shall repeat until the deliverable is accepted or the Customer exercises its rights under the Parent Agreement.

7.4 A milestone is accepted on the Customer's written acceptance. The Customer shall not unreasonably withhold or delay acceptance of a deliverable that conforms to the criteria in this SOW.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1 This SOW begins on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier, continues until {{EXPIRY}}, being an initial term of 12 months. Any extension must be agreed in writing by both parties.

8.2 Either party may terminate this SOW on the grounds, and in accordance with the procedures, set out in the Parent Agreement. Termination of the Parent Agreement terminates this SOW unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

8.3 On expiry or termination, the Supplier shall deliver all completed and in-progress deliverables, return Customer materials, and provide reasonable transition assistance. The Customer shall pay undisputed fees for Services properly performed up to the date of termination.

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential the other party's Confidential Information received in connection with this SOW, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only as permitted by the confidentiality provisions of the Parent Agreement, which apply to this SOW.

## 10. Intellectual Property

10.1 Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property rights.

10.2 Test scripts, test data, reports and other deliverables created specifically for the Customer under this SOW (the "**Customer Deliverables**") shall be owned by the Customer on payment of the applicable fees, and the Supplier assigns its rights in them accordingly.

10.3 The Supplier retains its proprietary frameworks, tools and know-how of general application. To the extent they are incorporated in the Customer Deliverables, the Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use them as part of those deliverables.

## 11. Warranties

11.1 The Supplier warrants that it will perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using appropriately qualified personnel, and in compliance with applicable law.

11.2 The Supplier warrants that the Customer Deliverables will not, to the Supplier's knowledge, infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party.

11.3 Further warranties are as set out in the Parent Agreement.

## 12. Liability and Indemnity

12.1 Each party's liability under or in connection with this SOW is governed by the liability provisions of the Parent Agreement.

12.2 Indemnities given by either party are as set out in the Parent Agreement and apply to this SOW as if set out in full.

## 13. Data Protection

13.1 Each party shall comply with applicable data protection laws in performing its obligations under this SOW.

13.2 To the extent the Supplier processes personal data on behalf of the Customer, it shall do so only on the Customer's documented instructions, implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures, ensure that personnel with access are bound by confidentiality, assist the Customer in responding to data subject requests and notify the Customer without undue delay on becoming aware of a personal data breach. The data processing terms of the Parent Agreement apply.

13.3 The Customer shall use test data that is anonymised or masked wherever reasonably practicable.

## 14. Assignment and Subcontracting

Neither party may assign or transfer its rights or obligations under this SOW without the prior written consent of the other, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld. The Supplier may subcontract any part of the Services only as permitted by the Parent Agreement and remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

## 15. Notices

Notices under this SOW must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognised courier or by email to the addresses for notices specified in the Parent Agreement, or to such other address as a party notifies in writing. Notices are deemed received in accordance with the Parent Agreement.

## 16. Entire Agreement and Variation

16.1 This SOW and the Parent Agreement constitute the entire agreement between the parties in relation to the Services and supersede all prior discussions, understandings and representations relating to them.

16.2 No variation of this SOW is effective unless in writing and signed by an authorised representative of each party.

## 17. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This SOW and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it, including non-contractual disputes, are governed by the laws of England and Wales. Disputes shall be resolved in accordance with the dispute resolution and jurisdiction provisions of the Parent Agreement.

## 18. Counterparts

This SOW may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
