# Amendment No. 1 to Master Services Agreement

This Amendment No. 1 (the "**Amendment**") is made with effect from {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer with its headquarters in the United States (the "**Customer**"), and **Helix Digital Consulting Inc** (the "**Supplier**"). The Customer and the Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## Background

A. The Parties entered into a Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} (the "**MSA**") under which the Supplier provides IT services to the Customer.

B. The Parties wish to amend the payment terms and the term of the MSA on the terms of this Amendment.

## Agreed Terms

1. **Definitions and interpretation.** Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the MSA. References in the MSA to "this Agreement" are to the MSA as amended by this Amendment. Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. **Payment terms.** With effect from the Amendment Effective Date, the payment term in the MSA is deleted and replaced with the following: the Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within sixty (60) days after the date on which the Customer receives a correct and valid invoice. All references in the MSA to any other period for payment of invoices are amended accordingly.

3. **Invoices issued before the Amendment Effective Date.** Invoices received by the Customer before the Amendment Effective Date remain payable under the payment term that applied when they were received. Invoices received on or after the Amendment Effective Date are subject to paragraph 2.

4. **Fees.** The fees, rates, expense provisions and invoicing requirements set out in the MSA are not changed by this Amendment. Nothing in this Amendment alters the Customer's right to dispute an invoice in good faith in accordance with the MSA, and the Customer shall notify the Supplier promptly of any disputed amount and the reasons for the dispute. The Parties shall work together in good faith to resolve any dispute. The Customer shall pay any undisputed portion of a disputed invoice in accordance with paragraph 2.

5. **Term.** The term of the MSA is extended so that the MSA, as amended, now expires on {{NEW_EXPIRY}}, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms. Any provision of the MSA stating a different expiry date, or any renewal mechanism that conflicts with this paragraph, is amended to give effect to this paragraph.

6. **Services and scope.** The Services, service levels, acceptance procedures and any Statements of Work in force under the MSA continue during the extended term on their existing terms. Any Statement of Work that, by its own terms, would expire after {{NEW_EXPIRY}} shall expire on {{NEW_EXPIRY}} unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

7. **Confidentiality.** The confidentiality obligations in the MSA continue to apply to all Confidential Information, including the terms of this Amendment, and survive for the period stated in the MSA.

8. **Intellectual property.** The intellectual property provisions of the MSA are unchanged and apply to all Services and Deliverables provided during the extended term. Nothing in this Amendment transfers or licenses any intellectual property right except as already provided in the MSA.

9. **Warranties.** Each Party confirms that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Amendment, and that doing so does not breach any obligation binding on it. The Supplier's warranties in the MSA continue to apply to the Services performed during the extended term.

10. **Liability and indemnity.** The limitations and exclusions of liability and the indemnities in the MSA are unchanged and apply to this Amendment and to the MSA as amended. Nothing in this Amendment limits or excludes any liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

11. **Termination.** The Parties' rights to terminate the MSA, including for breach or insolvency, and the consequences of termination, are unchanged. The extension of the term in paragraph 5 does not waive any right or remedy that has accrued before the Amendment Effective Date.

12. **Data protection.** The data protection, security and data processing provisions of the MSA, including any data processing terms incorporated into it, continue to apply in full during the extended term. Each Party shall comply with the data protection laws applicable to it in connection with the MSA.

13. **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign, transfer or subcontract any of its rights or obligations under the MSA or this Amendment except as permitted by the MSA. This Amendment binds and benefits the Parties and their respective permitted successors and assigns.

14. **Notices.** Notices under this Amendment must be given in writing and delivered in the manner, and to the addresses, specified in the MSA, unless a Party has notified the other of a change of address in accordance with the MSA.

15. **Continuing effect.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the MSA remains unchanged and in full force and effect. This Amendment is to be read together with the MSA, and the MSA and this Amendment shall be read as one agreement.

16. **Entire agreement and precedence.** This Amendment and the MSA together constitute the entire agreement between the Parties relating to their subject matter and supersede all prior discussions, representations and understandings relating to it. If there is a conflict between this Amendment and the MSA, this Amendment prevails to the extent of the conflict with respect to the matters it addresses.

17. **Variation and waiver.** No further variation of the MSA or this Amendment is effective unless in writing and signed by an authorised representative of each Party. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of it.

18. **Severability.** If any provision of this Amendment is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect, and the Parties shall negotiate in good faith a valid replacement that most closely achieves the original intent.

19. **Governing law and jurisdiction.** This Amendment, and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it, is governed by the law that governs the MSA, and the courts or dispute resolution forum specified in the MSA have jurisdiction over any such dispute or claim.

20. **Counterparts and electronic signature.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
