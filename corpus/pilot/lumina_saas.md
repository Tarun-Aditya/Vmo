# SOFTWARE-AS-A-SERVICE SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Software-as-a-Service Subscription Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Lumina Learning Systems Inc** (the "**Supplier**") and **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer headquartered in the United States (the "**Customer**"). The Supplier and the Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## 1. DEFINITIONS

"**Customer Data**" means all data, content and information submitted to the Services by or on behalf of the Customer, including Personal Data. "**Personal Data**" means information relating to an identified or identifiable individual that is processed through the Services. "**Services**" means the Supplier's hosted learning-management software platform, as made available to the Customer under this Agreement, together with related standard support. "**Documentation**" means the Supplier's then-current user guides for the Services. "**Fees**" means the fees payable under Section 4. "**Security Incident**" means a confirmed breach of the Supplier's security leading to unauthorised access to or disclosure of Customer Data in the Supplier's possession.

## 2. SERVICES AND LICENCE

2.1 Subject to this Agreement, the Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable right during the Term to access and use the Services for the Customer's internal business purposes, in accordance with the Documentation.

2.2 The Customer shall not, and shall not permit others to: (a) copy, modify, reverse engineer or create derivative works of the Services; (b) resell, sublicense or provide the Services to third parties; (c) use the Services to build a competing product; (d) interfere with the integrity or performance of the Services; or (e) use the Services in violation of applicable law.

2.3 The Customer is responsible for its users' compliance with this Agreement, for the accuracy and lawfulness of Customer Data, and for maintaining the confidentiality of access credentials. The Supplier may modify the Services from time to time, provided that it does not materially reduce their core functionality.

2.4 The Supplier may suspend access to the Services where reasonably necessary to prevent a security threat, to perform emergency maintenance, or where the Customer is in material breach, and shall restore access promptly once the cause is resolved.

## 3. TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}} (the "**Initial Term**"), being a period of twenty-four (24) months.

3.2 Following the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months (each a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**") unless either Party gives the other at least sixty (60) days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier annual Fees of USD 275,000, giving a total contract value for the Initial Term of USD 550,000. Fees for any Renewal Term shall be those then in effect unless otherwise agreed in writing.

4.2 The Supplier shall invoice the Fees in accordance with the commercial schedule agreed by the Parties. The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt of a valid invoice.

4.3 The Customer shall notify the Supplier in writing of any good-faith dispute regarding an invoice, with reasonable detail, before the due date, and shall pay all undisputed amounts on time. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve disputes promptly.

4.4 Overdue undisputed amounts may bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law, and the Supplier may suspend the Services on written notice if undisputed amounts remain unpaid after the due date. Fees are non-cancellable and non-refundable except as expressly stated in this Agreement.

4.5 Fees are exclusive of sales, use, value-added and similar taxes, which the Customer shall bear, excluding taxes on the Supplier's net income.

## 5. SERVICE LEVELS

5.1 The Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Services available at least 99.0% of the time in each calendar month, excluding downtime resulting from scheduled maintenance, force majeure, the Customer's or its users' acts or omissions, third-party networks or services outside the Supplier's control, and suspension permitted by this Agreement.

5.2 The Supplier shall notify the Customer of a Security Incident within ninety-six (96) hours after the Supplier confirms the Security Incident, and shall take reasonable steps to investigate and mitigate it.

5.3 The Customer's sole and exclusive remedy, and the Supplier's entire liability, for any failure to meet the service levels in this Section is the Customer's right to request a reasonable service remedy and, for repeated material failure, to terminate under Section 12.

## 6. CUSTOMER DATA AND DATA PROTECTION

6.1 The Customer acknowledges that the Services involve the processing of Personal Data of the Customer's customers and employees. As between the Parties, the Customer retains all rights in Customer Data and grants the Supplier a non-exclusive licence during the Term to host, process and use Customer Data solely to provide, secure and support the Services.

6.2 The Customer is responsible for establishing a lawful basis for, and providing any notices and obtaining any consents required in respect of, its collection and provision of Personal Data to the Supplier.

6.3 The Supplier shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the Customer Data and shall process Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions as given through the Customer's use and configuration of the Services, except as required by law. The Supplier may use subcontractors in providing the Services and remains responsible for their performance as provided in this Agreement.

6.4 The Supplier may use aggregated and de-identified data derived from the use of the Services to operate and improve its products, provided that such data does not identify the Customer or any individual.

6.5 Upon expiry or termination, the Supplier shall, on the Customer's written request made within a reasonable period, make Customer Data available for export, after which the Supplier may delete it in accordance with its standard retention practices.
