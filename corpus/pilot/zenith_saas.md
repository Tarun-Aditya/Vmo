# SaaS Subscription Agreement

This Software-as-a-Service Subscription Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "Effective Date") between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer headquartered in the United States ("you" or the "Customer"), and **Zenith Software Ltd** ("we" or the "Supplier").

## What you are buying

We will provide you with access to our hosted software platform and related support (the "Services") for your internal business purposes, for the Term of this Agreement. You may let your employees and contractors use the Services on your behalf, and you are responsible for their compliance with this Agreement.

You will not resell the Services, reverse engineer them (except as the law allows), or use them in a way that breaks the law or harms the security or performance of the platform.

We may improve or update the Services from time to time, but we will not make changes that materially reduce their functionality or security during the Term.

## How long this lasts

This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and the initial term runs until {{EXPIRY}} (12 months) (the "Initial Term").

After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive 12-month periods (each a "Renewal Term") unless either of us gives the other at least 60 days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term. The Initial Term and any Renewal Term together are the "Term".

## How we charge

The annual fee for the Services is USD 360,000. The total contract value for the Initial Term is USD 360,000. The fees cover everything described in this Agreement unless we both agree otherwise in writing.

We will send you a valid invoice for the fees. You will pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receiving a valid invoice. A valid invoice states the amount due, the period it covers and any purchase order reference you have given us.

If you dispute an invoice in good faith, tell us promptly in writing with your reasons and pay the undisputed part. We will work together quickly to resolve the dispute. Fees are exclusive of applicable sales, use and similar taxes, which you will pay where properly chargeable, other than taxes on our income.

## Service levels and reporting

We will make the Services available at least 99.9% of the time each month, excluding downtime for scheduled maintenance that we notify to you in advance and downtime caused by you or by events outside our reasonable control.

We will report availability to you each month. If we miss the availability target, we will investigate, tell you the cause and take reasonable steps to prevent it happening again. Repeated or serious failures may amount to a material breach under the section "If something goes wrong".

We will notify you of any security incident affecting the Services or your data within 48 hours of becoming aware of it, and keep you reasonably informed as we investigate and fix it.

## Your data and privacy

You own your data, including everything you or your users put into the Services. You give us a limited right to use it only to provide the Services to you and as this Agreement requires.

The Services involve processing personal data of your customers and your employees. We will process that personal data only on your documented instructions and for the purpose of providing the Services. We will keep it secure using appropriate technical and organisational measures, make sure anyone with access is bound by confidentiality, and comply with the data protection laws that apply to us.

We will give you reasonable help with requests from individuals and with regulators' enquiries relating to the personal data. We will not transfer the personal data to another country or engage a subprocessor except where that is necessary to provide the Services, we remain responsible for our subprocessors, and the transfer or engagement complies with applicable law. When this Agreement ends, we will return or delete your data on request, except where the law requires us to keep it.

## Keeping things confidential

Each of us may receive confidential information from the other. The receiving party will use it only for this Agreement, keep it secret with at least reasonable care, and share it only with people who need to know and are bound by similar duties.

This does not apply to information that is public through no fault of the recipient, was already lawfully known to the recipient, was independently developed, or was lawfully received from someone else. A party may disclose confidential information where the law requires, giving the other party prompt notice where permitted.

These confidentiality obligations survive for 3 years after this Agreement ends.

## Who owns what

We keep all rights in the Services, our software and our underlying technology. You keep all rights in your data and your own materials. We give you a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the Services during the Term, and you give us no other rights in your intellectual property except as needed to provide the Services.

If you give us feedback about the Services, we may use it freely without obligation to you, provided we do not identify you or disclose your confidential information.

## Our promises

We promise that we have the right to enter this Agreement, that we will provide the Services with reasonable skill and care, and that the Services will perform materially as described in our documentation for a warranty period of 30 days from the Effective Date. If the Services do not meet this warranty, we will correct the problem at no extra charge within a reasonable time.

Except as stated in this Agreement, and to the extent the law allows, we give no other warranties, whether express or implied.

We will maintain insurance cover of at least USD 2,000,000 per claim throughout the Term, with a reputable insurer, and give you evidence of it on reasonable request.

## Protecting each other from claims

We will defend you and pay damages finally awarded (or agreed in settlement) arising from a third-party claim that the Services infringe that party's intellectual property rights, or from our breach of the data protection section. You will defend us and pay damages finally awarded (or agreed in settlement) arising from a third-party claim that your data, or your use of the Services in breach of this Agreement, infringes or violates the rights of that party.

The indemnified party must promptly notify the other of the claim, let it control the defence and settlement (without settling in a way that admits fault of the indemnified party without consent), and give reasonable cooperation. If the Services become subject to an infringement claim, we may modify them, obtain the right for you to continue using them, or, if neither is reasonably possible, end the affected Services and refund prepaid fees for the period after they end.

## Limits on what we owe each other

Each party's aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement is limited to one (1) times the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

Neither of us is liable for indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profit, revenue or goodwill, however it arises.

Nothing in this Agreement limits liability that cannot be limited by law, including liability for fraud, death or personal injury caused by negligence, or wilful misconduct.

## If something goes wrong

Neither of us may terminate this Agreement for convenience. This Agreement can be ended early only for cause, as set out below.

Either of us may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other:

- commits a material breach and does not fix it within a reasonable period after receiving written notice describing the breach; or
- becomes insolvent, enters bankruptcy or similar proceedings, or stops carrying on business.

If this Agreement ends, you will pay undisputed fees for Services provided up to the end date, and we will refund any fees prepaid for periods after termination that you terminated for our uncured breach. Ending the Agreement does not affect rights that have already arisen, or sections that by their nature are meant to continue, including confidentiality, intellectual property, liability, and governing law.
