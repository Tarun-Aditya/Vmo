# Statement of Work No. 1

**Project:** Retail Applications Development and Support Services
**Parent Agreement:** Master Services Agreement between Arcadia Retail Inc. and Cobalt Applications Group dated {{PARENT_DATE}}
**Period:** Effective Date of {{EFFECTIVE}} until {{EXPIRY}} (initial term of twelve (12) months)
**Fees:** USD 1,100,000 per annum; total contract value USD 1,100,000

*Vendor draft – not yet signed. Subject to review and approval by Arcadia Retail Inc.*

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer with its headquarters in the United States ("**Customer**"), and **Cobalt Applications Group** ("**Supplier**"). Customer and Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## 1. Background

1.1 Customer operates retail businesses and requires software development, integration and support services for its commerce, inventory and customer-facing applications.

1.2 Supplier provides information technology services and has agreed to perform the services described in this SOW.

1.3 This SOW is issued under, and incorporates the terms of, the Master Services Agreement identified in the header block (the "**Parent Agreement**"). Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Parent Agreement. If this SOW conflicts with the Parent Agreement, the Parent Agreement prevails unless this SOW expressly states that it overrides a specific provision.

## 2. Scope

2.1 Supplier shall provide the following services (the "**Services**"):

(a) design, development and configuration of enhancements to Customer's e-commerce platform and in-store point-of-sale integrations;
(b) integration of Customer's inventory management systems with order fulfilment and supplier systems;
(c) application maintenance and support for the in-scope applications, including defect correction and routine updates; and
(d) project management, reporting and knowledge transfer to Customer personnel.

2.2 Services not described in this SOW are out of scope. Any change to scope, Deliverables, schedule or Fees shall be made only by a written change request signed by both Parties. Supplier shall not perform out-of-scope work, or be entitled to additional payment for it, without such signed change request.

2.3 Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with good industry practice, and in compliance with applicable law and Customer's reasonable site and security policies notified to Supplier.

## 3. Deliverables and Milestones

3.1 Supplier shall deliver the following Deliverables by the dates shown. Milestone 1 is due on {{MS1}}. Subsequent dates shall be set out in the project plan agreed in writing by the Parties.

| No. | Deliverable | Description | Target Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Project Plan and Design | Detailed project plan, solution architecture and test strategy | {{MS1}} |
| 2 | Platform Enhancements | Configured and tested e-commerce and point-of-sale enhancements | Per agreed project plan |
| 3 | Inventory Integration | Working integration between inventory, fulfilment and supplier systems | Per agreed project plan |
| 4 | Support Services | Ongoing maintenance and support throughout the term | Throughout the term |
| 5 | Documentation and Handover | Technical documentation and knowledge transfer materials | Per agreed project plan |

3.2 Supplier shall notify Customer promptly in writing of any anticipated delay, its cause and its proposed remedial steps.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

4.1 **Supplier** shall: (a) assign suitably qualified personnel and a named engagement manager; (b) provide regular progress reports; (c) comply with the agreed project plan; and (d) remain responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

4.2 **Customer** shall: (a) nominate a project sponsor and a day-to-day contact; (b) provide timely access to information, systems and personnel reasonably required; and (c) review Deliverables and give decisions and feedback within a reasonable time.

4.3 Each Party's project contact shall have authority to make day-to-day decisions but not to vary this SOW.

## 5. Assumptions

5.1 The Fees are based on the following assumptions:
(a) Customer will provide timely access to environments, data and personnel;
(b) Customer's existing systems are as described to Supplier before the Effective Date;
(c) required third-party licences and hosting are procured and paid for by Customer unless this SOW states otherwise; and
(d) Customer decisions and approvals will be given within a reasonable time.

5.2 If an assumption proves incorrect and this materially affects the Services, the Parties shall discuss the effect in good faith and any resulting adjustment shall be made only through a change request under clause 2.2. Supplier shall notify Customer promptly on becoming aware of any such failure.

## 6. Fees and Invoicing

6.1 Customer shall pay Supplier annual fees of USD 1,100,000 for the initial term (the "**Fees**"). The total contract value under this SOW is USD 1,100,000.

6.2 Supplier shall invoice the Fees in the instalments and at the times agreed in the project plan, and the instalments shall not in aggregate exceed the Fees. Each invoice must be valid, meaning it identifies this SOW, describes the Services or Deliverables invoiced, and is addressed to Customer's accounts payable contact.

6.3 Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 15 days of receipt of a valid invoice. Customer may withhold payment of any amount disputed in good faith, shall notify Supplier of the dispute and its grounds, and shall pay any undisputed portion. The Parties shall resolve disputes promptly in good faith.

6.4 The Fees are exclusive of applicable sales and use taxes, which shall be shown separately on the invoice. Pre-approved, reasonable expenses are reimbursable only where agreed in writing in advance.

## 7. Acceptance

7.1 Supplier shall submit each Deliverable to Customer for review upon completion, together with any relevant test results.

7.2 Customer shall review each Deliverable against the description in this SOW and the agreed acceptance criteria and shall, within a reasonable period, either accept it in writing or notify Supplier of non-conformities in reasonable detail.

7.3 Supplier shall correct any non-conformity promptly at no additional charge and resubmit the Deliverable. If a Deliverable still fails to conform after reasonable resubmission, Customer may, at its option, require further correction, accept the Deliverable subject to an equitable adjustment of the Fees, or terminate the affected part of the Services.

7.4 Use of a Deliverable in a production environment shall not of itself constitute acceptance.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1 This SOW commences on the Effective Date and continues for the initial term until {{EXPIRY}}, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this SOW or the Parent Agreement. Any extension requires written agreement of both Parties.

8.2 Either Party may terminate this SOW by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within a reasonable period after written notice requiring remedy, or if the other Party becomes insolvent or subject to bankruptcy proceedings.

8.3 On termination or expiry, Supplier shall deliver all completed and in-progress Deliverables and Customer Data, cooperate in an orderly transition, and Customer shall pay undisputed Fees properly due for Services performed up to the termination date.

## 9. Confidentiality

9.1 Each Party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know and are bound by equivalent obligations.

9.2 These obligations do not apply to information that is public through no fault of the recipient, was lawfully known to or independently developed by the recipient, or is lawfully received from a third party. A recipient may disclose information to the extent required by law, after giving prompt notice where lawful. These obligations survive termination or expiry of this SOW.

## 10. Intellectual Property

10.1 Each Party retains its pre-existing intellectual property rights. Customer owns all Customer Data and Customer materials.

10.2 Upon payment of the applicable Fees, Customer shall own the Deliverables created specifically for Customer under this SOW, and Supplier assigns to Customer all rights in them. Supplier retains its pre-existing tools, methodologies and general know-how and grants Customer a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use any such materials embedded in the Deliverables as necessary to use the Deliverables.

## 11. Warranties

11.1 Supplier warrants that: (a) it has the authority and expertise to perform the Services; (b) the Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care by suitably qualified personnel; (c) the Deliverables will conform in all material respects to their descriptions; and (d) the Deliverables will not, to its knowledge, infringe third-party intellectual property rights.

11.2 Supplier shall re-perform any non-conforming Services notified to it within a reasonable period after delivery. Except as stated in this SOW, neither Party gives any other warranty to the extent permitted by law.

## 12. Data Protection and Security

12.1 Supplier shall process personal data only on Customer's documented instructions and for the purpose of performing the Services, and shall comply with applicable data protection laws.

12.2 Supplier shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Customer Data against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure, shall ensure personnel with access are bound by confidentiality, and shall notify Customer without undue delay on becoming aware of any security incident affecting Customer Data.

12.3 Supplier shall not engage a subprocessor of personal data without Customer's prior written consent, and on termination shall return or securely delete Customer Data at Customer's election.

## 13. Indemnity

13.1 Supplier shall defend and indemnify Customer against third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages and reasonable costs, arising from (a) an allegation that the Deliverables infringe third-party intellectual property rights, or (b) Supplier's breach of clause 12 or gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

13.2 Customer shall defend and indemnify Supplier against third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages and reasonable costs, arising from Customer's use of Customer Data or materials in breach of law or third-party rights.

13.3 The indemnified Party shall give prompt notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control its defence and settlement (provided no settlement admits fault on behalf of the indemnified Party without its consent), and give reasonable assistance.

## 14. Liability

14.1 Each Party's liability to the other under or in connection with this SOW is unlimited. No cap or monetary limit applies to the liability of either Party.

14.2 Nothing in this SOW excludes liability that cannot be excluded by law.

## 15. General

15.1 **Governing law.** This SOW and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of Florida, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The Parties submit to the courts of Florida for the resolution of disputes, subject to any dispute resolution procedure in the Parent Agreement.

15.2 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or subcontract its rights or obligations under this SOW without the other's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

15.3 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, recognised courier or email with confirmation to the address of the receiving Party stated in the Parent Agreement, or as later notified in writing.

15.4 **Entire agreement.** This SOW and the Parent Agreement constitute the entire agreement between the Parties on their subject matter and supersede all prior discussions and understandings. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

15.5 **Counterparts.** This SOW may be executed in counterparts, including electronically.

## 16. Signatures

This SOW is a draft and has not been signed. It becomes binding only when executed by authorised representatives of both Parties.
