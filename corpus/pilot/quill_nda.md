# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer headquartered in the United States ("Arcadia", "the Customer", "we" or "us" where the context requires), and **Quill Staffing Group Ltd** ("Quill", "the Supplier", "you" or "your" where the context requires). Each of us is a "party" and together we are the "parties".

It takes effect on {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "Effective Date").

## Why we are signing this

We want to explore, and possibly carry out, a staffing and IT-services relationship. To do that, each of us may need to share information that is private or commercially sensitive. This Agreement sets out how each of us will look after what the other shares. Either of us may be a "Discloser" (the party sharing information) or a "Recipient" (the party receiving it).

## What this Agreement covers

"Confidential Information" means any information, in any form, that a Discloser or its affiliates share with the Recipient in connection with the Purpose and that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. It includes business plans, pricing, supplier and customer details, store and sales data, candidate and personnel information, technical specifications, systems, source code, security information, and the terms of any discussions between us.

The "Purpose" means evaluating, negotiating and, if agreed, performing a services relationship between us.

## What is not Confidential Information

Information is not Confidential Information if the Recipient can show that it:

- is or becomes public other than through the Recipient's breach;
- was already lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure, without a duty of confidence;
- was lawfully received from a third party who was free to share it; or
- was developed independently without using the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## How we each look after Confidential Information

The Recipient will:

1. use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
2. keep it secret and protect it with at least the same care it uses for its own sensitive information, and never less than reasonable care;
3. share it only with its employees, officers, contractors, advisers and affiliates who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality duties at least as protective as these;
4. not copy it except as needed for the Purpose; and
5. tell the Discloser promptly if it becomes aware of any unauthorised use or disclosure, and help to limit the harm.

The Recipient is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by people to whom it gives access.

## When disclosure is required by law

If the Recipient is required by law, court order or a regulator to disclose Confidential Information, it may do so, provided that (where lawful) it gives the Discloser prompt notice so the Discloser can seek protection, discloses only what is legally required, and cooperates reasonably with any such effort.

## Personal data and security

If either of us shares personal data, the Recipient will process it only for the Purpose, in line with applicable data protection and privacy laws, and with appropriate technical and organisational security measures. We will not share personal data unless it is genuinely needed. If we agree that personal data will be processed on an ongoing basis, we will first sign any additional data processing terms that the law requires.

## Ownership and no licence

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. Nothing in this Agreement gives the Recipient any licence or right in the Discloser's intellectual property, other than the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose. Feedback or suggestions the Recipient offers voluntarily may be used by the Discloser freely, but this does not give the Discloser any right to the Recipient's own Confidential Information.

## Returning or destroying information

At any time on the Discloser's written request, and in any event when this Agreement ends, the Recipient will promptly return or securely destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information and confirm that it has done so in writing. The Recipient may keep copies that it must retain by law or regulation, or that sit in routine backup systems, as long as they stay protected under this Agreement and are not accessed for any other purpose.

## No promises about the information, and no commitment to deal

Confidential Information is provided "as is". Neither of us gives any warranty about its accuracy or completeness, except as may be set out in a later signed agreement. This Agreement does not oblige either of us to disclose any information, to enter into any further agreement, or to buy or supply anything. Any services relationship will only exist under a separate written agreement signed by both of us. No fees are payable under this Agreement.

## How long this Agreement lasts

This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and runs until {{EXPIRY}} (an initial term of 24 months), unless ended earlier. Either of us may end it earlier by giving the other written notice. Ending the Agreement does not affect rights or obligations that have already arisen.

Our confidentiality obligations continue to apply to all Confidential Information disclosed during the term, and survive for 2 years after this Agreement ends. Obligations about ownership, and any liability for breaches that happened before the end date, also survive.

## If something goes wrong

If the Recipient breaches this Agreement, the Discloser may suffer harm that money alone cannot fix. The Discloser may therefore ask a court for an injunction or other equitable relief, in addition to any other remedy, without having to prove actual damage to the extent the law allows.

Each of us is responsible for losses that the other suffers because of our breach of this Agreement, subject to the following. Neither of us is liable to the other for indirect or consequential losses, or for loss of profit, arising from this Agreement, except where the loss results from a breach of confidentiality, fraud, wilful misconduct, or anything else that the law does not allow us to exclude. Nothing in this Agreement limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

Each of us will indemnify the other against third-party claims, and the reasonable costs of defending them, to the extent caused by our unauthorised use or disclosure of the other's Confidential Information in breach of this Agreement.

## Rules for working together

- **Assignment.** Neither of us may transfer this Agreement or any rights under it without the other's prior written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld. Any attempted transfer without consent is void.
- **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognised courier, or by email with confirmation of sending, to the address that each of us gives the other in writing for this purpose. A notice takes effect when it is received.
- **Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the whole agreement between us about its subject matter and replaces earlier discussions on that subject. It can only be changed in writing signed by both of us.
- **Waiver.** If one of us delays in enforcing a right, that is not a waiver of it.
- **Severability.** If any part of this Agreement is found unenforceable, the rest stays in force and the affected part will be applied to the fullest extent allowed.
- **No partnership.** Nothing here makes us partners, agents or joint venturers.
- **Counterparts.** We may sign in separate counterparts, including electronically, and together they form one agreement.

## Governing law and disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. We will try to resolve any dispute in good faith through discussion between senior representatives first. If that does not work, either of us may bring the dispute before the courts of the State of New York, and each of us submits to the jurisdiction of those courts, without limiting the right to seek urgent injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.
