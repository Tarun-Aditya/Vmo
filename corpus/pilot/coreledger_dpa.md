# Data Processing Agreement

This Agreement is made on {{EFFECTIVE}} (the **Effective Date**)

## Parties

1. **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer whose headquarters are in the United States (the **Client**); and
2. **CoreLedger Payments Pte Ltd**, a company incorporated in Singapore (the **Supplier**).

## Background

- (A) The Client and the Supplier are parties to a SaaS agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} (the **Parent Agreement**).
- (B) In providing the Services under the Parent Agreement, the Supplier processes personal data of the Client's customers and employees on behalf of the Client.
- (C) This Agreement is issued under the Parent Agreement and sets out the terms on which the Supplier will process that personal data.

## 1. Definitions and interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Applicable Data Protection Law** | the laws of Singapore relating to the protection of personal data, including the Personal Data Protection Act 2012, and any other data protection legislation that applies to the Client Personal Data |
| **Client Personal Data** | personal data of the Client's customers and employees that the Supplier processes on behalf of the Client in connection with the Services |
| **Data Subject** | an individual to whom Client Personal Data relates |
| **Personal Data Breach** | any breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, Client Personal Data |
| **Services** | the services described in Schedule 1 – Services |
| **Sub-processor** | any third party appointed by the Supplier to process Client Personal Data |
| **Term** | the period described in clause 2 |

1.2 Expressions such as **personal data**, **processing**, **controller** and **processor** have the meanings given in Applicable Data Protection Law. The Supplier acts as processor (or data intermediary) and the Client acts as controller (or organisation).

1.3 The Schedules form part of this Agreement. Clause headings do not affect interpretation. If this Agreement conflicts with the Parent Agreement in relation to the processing of Client Personal Data, this Agreement prevails. Terms defined in the Parent Agreement and not defined here have the same meaning in this Agreement.

## 2. Term

2.1 This Agreement takes effect on {{EFFECTIVE}} and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}}, being an initial term of 36 months.

2.2 Any extension of the term must be agreed in writing and signed by both parties.

2.3 The Supplier's obligations in respect of Client Personal Data continue for as long as the Supplier or any Sub-processor retains it, even after this Agreement ends.

## 3. Scope and instructions

3.1 The Supplier shall process Client Personal Data only:

3.1.1 to provide the Services;

3.1.2 on the Client's documented instructions, including those in this Agreement and the Parent Agreement; and

3.1.3 as required by applicable law, in which case the Supplier shall inform the Client of the requirement before processing, unless the law prohibits it from doing so.

3.2 The subject matter, nature, purpose, and categories of data and Data Subjects are set out in Schedule 1 – Services.

3.3 The Supplier shall tell the Client promptly if it believes an instruction infringes Applicable Data Protection Law.

## 4. Supplier obligations

4.1 The Supplier shall:

4.1.1 ensure that persons authorised to process Client Personal Data are bound by appropriate duties of confidentiality;

4.1.2 implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Client Personal Data against unauthorised or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction, or damage, having regard to the nature of the data and the risks involved;

4.1.3 assist the Client, taking into account the nature of the processing, in responding to requests from Data Subjects exercising their rights, and in meeting the Client's obligations on security, breach notification, and impact assessments;

4.1.4 not sell Client Personal Data or use it for its own marketing or any purpose not permitted by this Agreement; and

4.1.5 maintain records of its processing activities as required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

4.2 If the Supplier receives a request or complaint from a Data Subject or a regulator concerning Client Personal Data, it shall notify the Client without undue delay and shall not respond except on the Client's instructions or as required by law.

## 5. Sub-processors

5.1 The Client gives general authorisation for the Supplier to engage Sub-processors, subject to clauses 5.2 and 5.3.

5.2 The Supplier shall give the Client prior written notice of any intended addition or replacement of a Sub-processor, allowing the Client a reasonable opportunity to object on reasonable data protection grounds. If the parties cannot resolve an objection in good faith, the Client may terminate the affected Services without penalty.

5.3 The Supplier shall impose on each Sub-processor written obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement and remains liable for the acts and omissions of its Sub-processors as if they were its own.

## 6. International transfers

6.1 The Supplier shall not transfer Client Personal Data outside Singapore, or permit access to it from outside Singapore, except in accordance with the Client's instructions and Applicable Data Protection Law.

6.2 Where a transfer is permitted, the Supplier shall ensure that the recipient is bound by legally enforceable obligations or other safeguards providing a standard of protection comparable to that required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

## 7. Personal Data Breach

7.1 The Supplier shall notify the Client without undue delay after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach.

7.2 The notification shall describe, so far as known, the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate volume of data and Data Subjects affected, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address it.

7.3 The Supplier shall take reasonable steps to contain and remediate the breach and shall cooperate with the Client's investigation. The Client is responsible for deciding whether to notify regulators or Data Subjects, save where the Supplier is required by law to do so.

## 8. Audit

8.1 The Supplier shall make available to the Client all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement.

8.2 The Supplier shall allow and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Client or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality, on reasonable notice, during normal business hours, and in a manner that does not unreasonably disrupt the Supplier's business or compromise the data of its other customers.

8.3 Each party bears its own costs of an audit, unless the audit reveals a material breach of this Agreement by the Supplier, in which case the Supplier shall bear the Client's reasonable costs of the audit.

## 9. Return and deletion

9.1 On expiry or termination of this Agreement, or earlier on the Client's written request, the Supplier shall, at the Client's choice, return or securely delete all Client Personal Data and delete existing copies, unless applicable law requires retention.

9.2 The Supplier shall confirm deletion in writing on request. Where retention is required by law, the Supplier shall continue to protect the data in accordance with this Agreement and process it only for the purpose required by that law.

## 10. Charges and invoicing

10.1 The Charges for the Services are set out in Schedule 2 – Charges.

10.2 The Supplier shall invoice, and the Client shall pay, in accordance with the invoicing and payment provisions of the Parent Agreement. Amounts are exclusive of applicable taxes, which are payable in addition on receipt of a valid tax invoice.

10.3 The Supplier shall not charge for compliance with its core obligations under this Agreement. Reasonable additional assistance requested by the Client beyond those obligations may be charged at rates agreed in writing in advance.

## 11. Confidentiality

11.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information of a confidential nature disclosed by the other in connection with this Agreement, including all Client Personal Data, and shall use it only to perform or receive the benefit of this Agreement.

11.2 Clause 11.1 does not apply to information that is or becomes public other than through breach, was lawfully known to the recipient without restriction, or is independently developed. A party may disclose confidential information to the extent required by law, a court, or a regulator, after giving the other party notice where lawful.

11.3 This clause survives termination or expiry of this Agreement.

## 12. Intellectual property

12.1 As between the parties, all rights in the Client Personal Data remain with the Client. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of any intellectual property rights.

12.2 The Client grants the Supplier a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence during the Term to use the Client Personal Data solely to provide the Services in accordance with this Agreement.

12.3 The Supplier retains all rights in its software, systems, and materials, and the Client's rights to use them are governed by the Parent Agreement.

## 13. Warranties

13.1 The Supplier warrants that it will process Client Personal Data in compliance with Applicable Data Protection Law and this Agreement, and that it has the authority and capability to perform its obligations.

13.2 The Client warrants that it has the right to disclose the Client Personal Data to the Supplier for processing under this Agreement, and that it has provided all notices and obtained all consents required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

## 14. Liability and indemnity

14.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

14.2 The Supplier shall indemnify the Client against losses, penalties, damages, and reasonable costs arising from a breach by the Supplier or its Sub-processors of this Agreement or Applicable Data Protection Law, provided that the Client promptly notifies the Supplier of the claim, allows the Supplier to conduct its defence, and gives reasonable assistance at the Supplier's cost.

14.3 Subject to clause 14.1, the limitations and exclusions of liability in the Parent Agreement apply to liability arising under this Agreement, and liability under this Agreement and the Parent Agreement is aggregated for that purpose.

## 15. Termination

15.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is incapable of remedy, or, if capable of remedy, is not remedied within a reasonable period stated in a written notice requiring remedy.

15.2 The Client may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the Supplier breaches clause 6 or clause 7, or if Applicable Data Protection Law no longer permits the processing contemplated by this Agreement.

15.3 This Agreement terminates automatically if the Parent Agreement ends, subject to clause 9.

15.4 Termination does not affect accrued rights or the clauses that are intended to survive, including clauses 2.3, 9, 11, 14, and 17.

## 16. General

16.1 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign, transfer, or subcontract its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, save for Sub-processors appointed under clause 5.

16.2 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement and the Parent Agreement constitute the entire agreement between the parties relating to the processing of Client Personal Data and supersede all prior understandings on that subject.

16.3 **Variation.** No variation is effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.

16.4 **Waiver and severance.** A failure or delay in exercising a right is not a waiver. If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remainder is unaffected.

16.5 **Third party rights.** A person who is not a party has no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 2001 of Singapore to enforce any term of this Agreement.

16.6 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by prepaid courier, or by email to the address most recently notified by the receiving party for that purpose. A notice is deemed received when delivered by hand or courier, or at the time of transmission by email if sent during business hours at the place of receipt, otherwise at the start of the next business day.

16.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is an original.

## 17. Governing law and jurisdiction

17.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of Singapore.

17.2 The courts of Singapore have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, save that either party may seek urgent injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.
