# Data Processing Agreement

This Data Processing Agreement (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer with its United States headquarters in the United States (the "**Customer**"), and **Meridian Cloud Services LLC** (the "**Supplier**"). The Customer and the Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

**WHEREAS**, the Customer and the Supplier are parties to a Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} (the "**MSA**"), under which the Supplier provides certain IT services to the Customer;

**WHEREAS**, in providing those services the Supplier will process personal data of the Customer's customers and employees on behalf of the Customer; and

**WHEREAS**, the Parties wish to set out their respective rights and obligations in relation to that processing and to issue this DPA under the MSA;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual promises in this DPA and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## Section 1. Definitions

1.1 Capitalized terms not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the MSA.

1.2 In this DPA:

(a) "**Applicable Data Protection Law**" means all laws and regulations relating to privacy, data protection or data security that apply to the processing of Personal Data under this DPA.

(b) "**Personal Data**" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual that the Supplier processes on behalf of the Customer in connection with the Services, including personal data of the Customer's customers and employees.

(c) "**Personal Data Breach**" means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data.

(d) "**Services**" means the services provided by the Supplier to the Customer under the MSA.

(e) "**Sub-processor**" means any third party engaged by the Supplier that processes Personal Data on behalf of the Customer.

## Section 2. Scope and Relationship to the MSA

2.1 This DPA is issued under, and forms part of, the MSA. It applies to all processing of Personal Data by the Supplier in the course of providing the Services.

2.2 If there is a conflict between this DPA and the MSA regarding the processing or protection of Personal Data, this DPA prevails. In all other respects the MSA governs.

2.3 The Customer is the controller (or, where applicable, a processor acting for its own controllers) of the Personal Data, and the Supplier is the processor.

## Section 3. Term

3.1 This DPA takes effect on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of forty-eight (48) months, expiring on {{EXPIRY}} (the "**Initial Term**"), unless earlier terminated under Section 12.

3.2 This DPA will continue to apply after the Initial Term for as long as the Supplier retains or processes any Personal Data, and for any Services continuing under the MSA.

## Section 4. Services and Processing Instructions

4.1 The Supplier will process Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions, including as set out in the MSA and this DPA, and only for the purpose of providing the Services, unless required otherwise by law. In that case the Supplier will inform the Customer of the legal requirement before processing, unless the law prohibits such notice.

4.2 The Supplier will promptly notify the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Applicable Data Protection Law.

4.3 The subject matter, nature, purpose and duration of the processing, the types of Personal Data and the categories of data subjects (being the Customer's customers and employees) are as described in the MSA and any applicable statement of work, and as otherwise reasonably specified in writing by the Customer.

4.4 The Supplier will ensure that personnel authorized to process Personal Data are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations and receive suitable training.

## Section 5. Security and Personal Data Breach

5.1 The Supplier will implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data against Personal Data Breaches, taking into account the nature of the data, the risks of the processing and generally accepted industry standards.

5.2 The Supplier will notify the Customer of a Personal Data Breach without undue delay and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of it.

5.3 The notice will, to the extent known, describe the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate volume of Personal Data affected, the likely consequences and the measures taken or proposed to address it. The Supplier will supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

5.4 The Supplier will take reasonable steps to contain and remediate the Personal Data Breach and will cooperate with the Customer in meeting any legal notification obligations to regulators and individuals. The Supplier will not make public statements concerning a Personal Data Breach naming the Customer without the Customer's prior written consent, except as required by law.

## Section 6. Sub-processors

6.1 The Supplier will maintain a current written list of all Sub-processors, including their identity, location and the processing they perform, and will provide that list to the Customer on request and on the Effective Date.

6.2 The Supplier will give the Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any intended addition or replacement of a Sub-processor. The Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period, and the Parties will discuss the objection in good faith. If it cannot be resolved, the Customer may terminate the affected Services without penalty.

6.3 The Supplier will bind each Sub-processor to written obligations no less protective than those in this DPA and remains liable for each Sub-processor's performance of those obligations.

## Section 7. Assistance, Audit and Return of Data

7.1 The Supplier will provide reasonable assistance to the Customer in responding to requests from individuals exercising their rights under Applicable Data Protection Law, and in carrying out data protection assessments and consultations with regulators. The Supplier will promptly forward to the Customer any such request it receives directly.

7.2 The Supplier will make available to the Customer information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and will allow and contribute to audits by the Customer or its appointed auditor, on reasonable notice, during normal business hours and subject to reasonable confidentiality and security requirements.

7.3 On expiry or termination of this DPA, or earlier on the Customer's written request, the Supplier will return or securely delete all Personal Data and certify deletion in writing, unless law requires continued retention, in which case the Supplier will continue to protect that data in accordance with this DPA.

## Section 8. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

8.1 Each Party will keep the other's Confidential Information confidential in accordance with the MSA. Personal Data is the Customer's Confidential Information.

8.2 As between the Parties, the Customer retains all right, title and interest in the Personal Data. The Supplier acquires no rights in it other than the limited right to process it as permitted by this DPA. Each Party retains its pre-existing intellectual property rights.

## Section 9. Fees and Invoicing

9.1 The Supplier's compliance with this DPA forms part of the Services, and no separate fee is payable for it unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

9.2 Any agreed additional charges, such as for assistance beyond that reasonably expected under Section 7, will be invoiced and paid in accordance with the invoicing and payment terms of the MSA.

## Section 10. Warranties

10.1 Each Party warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this DPA.

10.2 The Supplier warrants that it will process Personal Data in compliance with Applicable Data Protection Law and this DPA, and that it has no reason to believe any law applicable to it prevents it from complying with the Customer's instructions.

## Section 11. Liability and Indemnity

11.1 The Supplier will indemnify and hold harmless the Customer against third-party claims, regulatory fines and penalties to the extent permitted by law, and reasonable costs arising from the Supplier's breach of this DPA or of Applicable Data Protection Law.

11.2 The Customer will indemnify the Supplier against third-party claims to the extent arising from the Customer's instructions where those instructions violate Applicable Data Protection Law.

11.3 Except as provided in the MSA, neither Party excludes or limits liability that cannot be excluded or limited by law. The limitations and exclusions of liability in the MSA apply to this DPA in accordance with their terms.

## Section 12. Termination

12.1 Either Party may terminate this DPA by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to cure it within a reasonable period after written notice requiring cure.

12.2 The Customer may terminate this DPA immediately on written notice if the Supplier commits a material breach of Section 4, 5 or 6 that is not capable of remedy.

12.3 This DPA terminates automatically on termination or expiry of the MSA, subject to Sections 3.2 and 7.3. Sections 5, 7.3, 8, 11, 13 and 14 survive termination.

## Section 13. Governing Law and Disputes

13.1 This DPA, and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it, is governed by the laws of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

13.2 The Parties will first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through escalation to senior management. Any dispute not so resolved will be subject to the forum provisions of the MSA.

## Section 14. Miscellaneous

14.1 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this DPA without the other Party's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, except that either Party may assign it to a successor to the whole of its business that assumes its obligations in writing.

14.2 **Notices.** Notices under this DPA must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognized courier or by email with confirmation to the addresses designated in the MSA (or other address notified in writing), and are effective on receipt. Notices of a Personal Data Breach under Section 5 may be given by email to the Customer's designated security or privacy contact.

14.3 **Entire Agreement.** This DPA and the MSA constitute the entire agreement of the Parties on the processing of Personal Data and supersede all prior discussions and understandings on that subject.

14.4 **Amendment and Waiver.** This DPA may be amended only in a writing signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising a right is a waiver of it.

14.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this DPA is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force, and the Parties will replace the affected provision with a valid one that most closely reflects their intent.

14.6 **Counterparts.** This DPA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have caused this DPA to be executed by their duly authorized representatives.
