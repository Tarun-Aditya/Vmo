# Statement of Work No. 1

| | |
|---|---|
| **Project** | Retail Technology Transformation Programme |
| **Parent Agreement** | Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between Arcadia Retail Inc. and Helix Digital Consulting Inc |
| **Period** | Effective Date {{EFFECTIVE}} until {{EXPIRY}} (initial term of 18 months) |
| **Fees** | USD 6,400,000 per year; total contract value USD 9,600,000 |

This Statement of Work No. 1 (the "**SOW**") is made between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer with its headquarters in the United States (the "**Customer**"), and **Helix Digital Consulting Inc** (the "**Supplier**"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between the Customer and the Supplier (the "**Parent Agreement**"). Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Parent Agreement.

## 1. Background

1.1 The Customer operates retail businesses and wishes to modernise selected commercial and operational technology platforms.

1.2 The Supplier has represented that it has the skills, personnel and experience to deliver the Services, and the Customer has relied on that representation in entering into this SOW.

1.3 This SOW sets out the Services, Deliverables, Milestones, responsibilities and commercial terms for that work.

## 2. Term

2.1 This SOW takes effect on {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}} (the "**Initial Term**"), being 18 months.

2.2 This SOW does not renew automatically. Any extension of the Initial Term requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

## 3. Scope

3.1 The Supplier shall provide the following services (the "**Services**"):

(a) mobilisation, including programme set-up, resourcing and preparation of a delivery plan;

(b) solution design covering architecture, integration, data migration and testing approach;

(c) build, configuration, testing and deployment of the solution in two waves (Wave 1 and Wave 2); and

(d) cutover support, knowledge transfer and handover to the Customer's operational teams.

3.2 Services not described in this SOW are out of scope. Any change to the scope, Deliverables, Milestones or Fees shall be made only through the change control procedure in the Parent Agreement and a written change signed by both parties.

3.3 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill, care and diligence, in accordance with good industry practice, the delivery plan once accepted, and applicable law.

## 4. Deliverables and Milestones

4.1 The Supplier shall achieve each Milestone below by the date stated.

| No. | Milestone | Key Deliverables | Due Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mobilisation and delivery plan accepted | Mobilisation report; delivery plan; resourcing and governance plan | {{MS1}} |
| 2 | Design complete and signed off | Solution architecture; integration and data migration designs; test strategy | {{MS2}} |
| 3 | Wave 1 go-live | Configured and tested Wave 1 solution; cutover report; hypercare plan | {{MS3}} |
| 4 | Wave 2 go-live and handover | Configured and tested Wave 2 solution; operational documentation; handover and training materials | {{MS4}} |

4.2 If the Supplier becomes aware that a Milestone is likely to be missed, it shall promptly notify the Customer in writing, explain the cause and propose a recovery plan. Such notice does not waive the Customer's rights under this SOW or the Parent Agreement.

4.3 A Milestone date shall be extended only to the extent that the delay is caused by the Customer's failure to perform its responsibilities under this SOW or by a change agreed under clause 3.2.

## 5. Roles and Governance

5.1 **Supplier.** The Supplier shall appoint a programme manager with authority to act for it in day-to-day matters, provide suitably qualified personnel, and remain responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors and personnel.

5.2 **Customer.** The Customer shall appoint a programme sponsor and a business lead, provide timely decisions and feedback, and give the Supplier reasonable access to relevant personnel, systems, premises and information.

5.3 **Governance.** The Supplier shall hold a weekly steering committee and a monthly governance review, shall circulate agendas in advance and minutes and action logs afterwards, and shall report on progress against Milestones, risks, issues and change requests.

## 6. Assumptions

6.1 The Services and Milestone dates are based on the following assumptions:

(a) the Customer will make its nominated personnel and subject-matter experts reasonably available;

(b) the Customer will provide access to environments, systems and data reasonably required, in a timely manner;

(c) third-party products and services required by the Customer will be available when needed; and

(d) the Customer will give feedback on draft Deliverables within the period agreed in the delivery plan.

6.2 If an assumption proves incorrect, the Supplier shall notify the Customer promptly and the parties shall address any effect on the Services through change control. The Supplier shall use reasonable efforts to mitigate the effect.

## 7. Fees and Invoicing

7.1 The annual fees for the Services are USD 6,400,000. The total contract value of this SOW over the Initial Term is USD 9,600,000.

7.2 The Supplier shall invoice the Fees in accordance with the invoicing schedule agreed in the delivery plan. Each invoice must be valid, addressed to the Customer, and identify this SOW, the relevant Milestone or period, and the amount due. Fees are exclusive of applicable sales or similar taxes, which shall be shown separately.

7.3 The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 45 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

7.4 If the Customer disputes an invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Supplier promptly, pay any undisputed portion, and the parties shall work to resolve the dispute under the dispute resolution procedure in the Parent Agreement. The Supplier shall continue to perform the Services during any dispute.

7.5 No expenses are payable unless approved in writing by the Customer in advance.

## 8. Acceptance

8.1 On completing a Deliverable or Milestone, the Supplier shall notify the Customer in writing and submit the Deliverable with supporting evidence.

8.2 The Customer shall review and test the Deliverable against the acceptance criteria in the delivery plan and this SOW, and shall within a reasonable period notify the Supplier that it is accepted or rejected, giving reasons if rejected.

8.3 If a Deliverable is rejected, the Supplier shall correct the non-conformity at its own cost and resubmit it promptly, and the process in this clause 8 shall be repeated.

8.4 Acceptance of a Milestone occurs on the Customer's written confirmation. Acceptance does not release the Supplier from its warranty obligations or from any latent defect.

8.5 If the Customer remains unsatisfied after repeated resubmission, it may exercise the remedies available under the Parent Agreement.

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the purposes of this SOW, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations, or as required by law. These obligations are in addition to the confidentiality provisions of the Parent Agreement and survive termination or expiry of this SOW.

## 10. Intellectual Property

10.1 Each party retains its pre-existing intellectual property rights.

10.2 Subject to payment of the applicable Fees, intellectual property rights in the Deliverables created specifically for the Customer under this SOW vest in the Customer, and the Supplier assigns them to the Customer on creation, in accordance with the Parent Agreement.

10.3 The Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use any Supplier pre-existing materials incorporated in the Deliverables to the extent necessary to use the Deliverables.

## 11. Warranties

11.1 The Supplier warrants that it will perform the Services in accordance with clause 3.3, that the Deliverables will conform in all material respects to their specifications, and that the Deliverables and Services will not knowingly infringe third-party intellectual property rights.

11.2 The Supplier shall promptly correct, at its own cost, any non-conformity notified to it during the warranty period set out in the Parent Agreement.

## 12. Data Protection

12.1 Each party shall comply with data protection and privacy laws applicable to it in connection with this SOW.

12.2 To the extent the Supplier processes personal data on behalf of the Customer, it shall do so only on the Customer's documented instructions, apply appropriate technical and organisational security measures, ensure personnel are bound by confidentiality, assist the Customer in responding to data subject requests, notify the Customer without undue delay on becoming aware of a personal data breach, and return or delete the data on completion of the Services, in each case in accordance with the data protection terms of the Parent Agreement.

## 13. Liability and Indemnity

13.1 Each party's liability under or in connection with this SOW is governed by the liability provisions of the Parent Agreement.

13.2 Indemnities under this SOW are those set out in the Parent Agreement, which apply to this SOW as if set out in full here.

## 14. Termination

14.1 Either party may terminate this SOW for material breach by the other that is not remedied within a reasonable period after written notice requiring remedy, or immediately where the breach is incapable of remedy, or on the other's insolvency.

14.2 The Customer may terminate this SOW for convenience on written notice in accordance with the Parent Agreement.

14.3 On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall deliver all completed and work-in-progress Deliverables, return Customer materials and data, provide reasonable transition assistance, and the Customer shall pay undisputed Fees properly due for Services performed up to the termination date.

14.4 Termination or expiry does not affect accrued rights or provisions intended to survive.

## 15. Assignment and Subcontracting

Neither party may assign, novate or transfer its rights or obligations under this SOW without the prior written consent of the other, not to be unreasonably withheld. The Supplier shall not subcontract any material part of the Services without the Customer's prior written consent.

## 16. Notices

Notices under this SOW must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognised courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the address of the receiving party notified for that purpose under the Parent Agreement, and are effective on receipt.

## 17. Order of Precedence and Entire Agreement

17.1 This SOW and the Parent Agreement constitute the entire agreement between the parties on the subject matter of this SOW and supersede all prior understandings relating to it.

17.2 If there is a conflict, the Parent Agreement prevails over this SOW, except where this SOW expressly states that it overrides a specific provision of the Parent Agreement.

17.3 Amendments to this SOW are effective only if in writing and signed by both parties.

## 18. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This SOW and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of New York. Disputes shall be resolved in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions of the Parent Agreement.

## 19. General

This SOW may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of it.
