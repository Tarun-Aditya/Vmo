# Master Services Agreement

**THIS AGREEMENT** is made on {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "Effective Date")

**BETWEEN:**

1. **ARCADIA RETAIL INC.**, a retailer whose principal place of business is in the United States of America (the "Client"); and
2. **ORION CYBERSECURITY PARTNERS LLP**, a limited liability partnership (the "Supplier").

(each a "Party" and together the "Parties")

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Client carries on business as a retailer and wishes to receive cybersecurity services.

(B) The Supplier has the skills, personnel and experience to provide such services and has agreed to do so on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement the following terms have the following meanings:

"Agreement" means this master services agreement, including its Schedules and any Order;

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England;

"Charges" means the charges for the Services set out in Schedule 2 – Charges;

"Confidential Information" means all information of a confidential nature, in any form, disclosed by or on behalf of a Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including the Client's systems, security architecture, vulnerabilities, findings and business information, and the terms of this Agreement;

"Deliverables" means all reports, documents, materials and outputs produced by the Supplier for the Client as part of the Services;

"Initial Term" has the meaning given in clause 2.1;

"Intellectual Property Rights" means patents, copyright, rights in designs, database rights, trade marks, rights in confidential information and all other intellectual property rights, whether registered or unregistered, anywhere in the world;

"Services" means the services described in Schedule 1 – Services; and

"Warranty Period" has the meaning given in clause 7.2.

1.2 Headings do not affect interpretation. References to clauses and Schedules are to those of this Agreement. The Schedules form part of this Agreement. In the event of conflict, the clauses prevail over the Schedules.

1.3 The words "including" and "includes" are illustrative and do not limit the words preceding them.

## 2. Term

2.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}}, being a period of 36 months (the "Initial Term").

2.2 This Agreement does not renew automatically. Any extension of the term beyond the Initial Term shall be valid only if agreed in writing and signed by an authorised representative of each Party.

## 3. The Services

3.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Client from the Effective Date in accordance with this Agreement and Schedule 1 – Services.

3.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services:

3.2.1 with reasonable skill, care and diligence;

3.2.2 in accordance with good industry practice and applicable law; and

3.2.3 using appropriately qualified and experienced personnel.

3.3 The Client shall provide the Supplier with such information, access and co-operation as the Supplier reasonably requires to perform the Services, and shall be responsible for the accuracy of the information it provides.

3.4 Any change to the Services or the Charges shall be effective only if recorded in writing and signed by both Parties.

3.5 Where the Supplier requires access to the Client's premises or systems, it shall comply with the Client's reasonable security and site policies notified to it in advance.

## 4. Charges and Payment

4.1 The Client shall pay the Charges to the Supplier in accordance with Schedule 2 – Charges.

4.2 The Supplier shall submit valid invoices addressed to the Client. A valid invoice shall identify the Services performed, the applicable Charges and any purchase order reference notified by the Client.

4.3 The Client shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

4.4 If the Client disputes any part of an invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Supplier promptly, pay the undisputed portion in accordance with clause 4.3, and the Parties shall work together to resolve the dispute without delay.

4.5 Unless stated otherwise, the Charges are exclusive of applicable sales, value added or similar taxes, which shall be payable in addition by the Client against a valid tax invoice.

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each Party shall keep the other Party's Confidential Information confidential, shall use it only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement, and shall not disclose it except as permitted by this clause 5.

5.2 A Party may disclose Confidential Information:

5.2.1 to its employees, partners, officers, professional advisers, insurers and subcontractors who need to know it and who are bound by equivalent obligations of confidence; and

5.2.2 to the extent required by law, court order or a regulatory authority, provided that (where lawful) it gives the other Party prompt notice.

5.3 This clause 5 does not apply to information that is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement, was lawfully known to the recipient before disclosure, is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information, or is lawfully received from a third party free of any duty of confidence.

5.4 The obligations in this clause 5 survive termination or expiry of this Agreement and continue for 7 years after this Agreement ends.

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 Each Party retains ownership of all Intellectual Property Rights that it owned before the Effective Date or develops independently of this Agreement.

6.2 Subject to payment of the applicable Charges, the Supplier assigns to the Client all Intellectual Property Rights in the Deliverables, excluding any Supplier pre-existing materials, tools and methodologies incorporated in them ("Supplier Materials").

6.3 The Supplier grants the Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use the Supplier Materials to the extent necessary to receive and use the Deliverables for the Client's internal business purposes.

6.4 The Client grants the Supplier a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence during the term to use the Client's materials and systems solely to provide the Services.

## 7. Warranties

7.1 The Supplier warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement and that the Services and Deliverables will not knowingly infringe the Intellectual Property Rights of any third party.

7.2 The Supplier warrants that, for a period of 60 days following delivery or performance of the relevant Service or Deliverable (the "Warranty Period"), the Service or Deliverable will conform in all material respects to Schedule 1 – Services.

7.3 If the Client notifies the Supplier of a breach of clause 7.2 during the Warranty Period, the Supplier shall promptly re-perform the Service or correct the Deliverable at no additional charge, without prejudice to the Client's other rights and remedies.

7.4 Each Party warrants that it will comply with all laws applicable to it in connection with this Agreement, including anti-bribery and sanctions laws.

## 8. Data Protection

8.1 The Parties acknowledge and agree that the Services do not involve the processing of personal data by the Supplier on behalf of the Client.

8.2 The Client shall not knowingly provide personal data to the Supplier in connection with the Services. If either Party becomes aware that personal data is or may be processed in connection with the Services, it shall notify the other promptly and the Parties shall agree in writing the data protection terms required by applicable law before such processing continues.

## 9. Insurance

9.1 The Supplier shall maintain throughout the term of this Agreement, with a reputable insurer, professional indemnity and cyber liability insurance with cover of at least USD 10,000,000 per claim.

9.2 The Supplier shall, on reasonable request, provide the Client with evidence that the insurance required by clause 9.1 is in force.

9.3 Nothing in this clause 9 limits or reduces the Supplier's liability under this Agreement.

## 10. Indemnity

10.1 The Supplier shall indemnify the Client against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising out of any third-party claim that the Client's receipt or use of the Services or Deliverables in accordance with this Agreement infringes that third party's Intellectual Property Rights.

10.2 The indemnity in clause 10.1 is conditional on the Client:

10.2.1 promptly notifying the Supplier of the claim;

10.2.2 not making any admission without the Supplier's consent (not to be unreasonably withheld); and

10.2.3 allowing the Supplier, at the Supplier's cost, to conduct the defence and settlement of the claim, with reasonable assistance from the Client.

10.3 The Supplier shall have no liability under clause 10.1 to the extent a claim arises from the Client's modification of the Deliverables or use otherwise than in accordance with this Agreement.

## 11. Liability

11.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes either Party's liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or any liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

11.2 Subject to clause 11.1, the liability of each Party to the other under or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty or otherwise, is unlimited, and no cap on liability applies to either Party.

11.3 Each Party shall take reasonable steps to mitigate any loss for which it claims against the other.

## 12. Termination

12.1 Neither Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience. This Agreement may be terminated only in accordance with this clause 12.

12.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice to the other if the other Party:

12.2.1 commits a material breach which is irremediable, or which is remediable but is not remedied within 30 days of written notice requiring remedy; or

12.2.2 becomes insolvent, is unable to pay its debts as they fall due, enters into any arrangement with its creditors, has a receiver, administrator or liquidator appointed over it or any of its assets, or ceases to carry on business.

12.3 On termination or expiry:

12.3.1 the Client shall pay all undisputed Charges for Services properly performed up to the date of termination;

12.3.2 each Party shall return or securely destroy the other's Confidential Information on request, save as required by law to be retained; and

12.3.3 accrued rights and remedies of the Parties shall not be affected.

12.4 Clauses 1, 5, 6, 7 (in respect of the Warranty Period), 10, 11, 12.3, 13, 14 and 15 and any other provision intended to survive shall continue in force after termination or expiry.

## 13. General

13.1 **Assignment and subcontracting.** Neither Party may assign, transfer or subcontract any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other (not to be unreasonably withheld). The Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

13.2 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties relating to its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, understandings and agreements. Each Party acknowledges that it has not relied on any statement not set out in this Agreement, but nothing in this clause limits liability for fraud.

13.3 **Variation and waiver.** No variation of this Agreement is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of it.

13.4 **Severance.** If any provision is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force and the Parties shall negotiate in good faith a valid replacement that most closely achieves the original intention.

13.5 **Force majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other promptly and uses reasonable endeavours to mitigate the effect. This clause does not excuse any obligation to pay undisputed sums.

13.6 **Third party rights.** A person who is not a Party has no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 to enforce any term of this Agreement.

13.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one agreement.

## 14. Notices

14.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognised international courier or by email to the address notified by the receiving Party for that purpose.

14.2 A notice is deemed received: if delivered by hand or courier, at the time of delivery; and if sent by email, at the time of transmission or, if outside business hours in the place of receipt, at the start of the next Business Day.

14.3 Notices of breach or termination sent by email shall also be sent by hand or courier.

## 15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim (including non-contractual disputes or claims) arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of England and Wales.

15.2 The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any such dispute or claim.

15.3 Before commencing proceedings (other than for urgent injunctive relief), the Parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute by escalation to senior executives.
