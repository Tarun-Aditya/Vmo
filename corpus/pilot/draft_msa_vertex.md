# Master Services Agreement

**VENDOR DRAFT – NOT YET SIGNED – SUBJECT TO CONTRACT**

This Master Services Agreement (this **"Agreement"**) is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the **"Effective Date"**) by and between Arcadia Retail Inc., a corporation with its headquarters in the United States (**"Arcadia"**), and Vertex Managed Services Inc (**"Vertex"**). Arcadia and Vertex are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

## ARTICLE 1 – DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"Affiliate"** means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party.

1.2 **"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party (the **"Discloser"**) to the other Party (the **"Recipient"**), in any form, that is designated as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.

1.3 **"Deliverables"** means all work product, reports, code, documentation and other materials that Vertex creates for Arcadia in performing the Services.

1.4 **"Fees"** means the fees set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

1.5 **"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual that Vertex processes on behalf of Arcadia in connection with the Services, including personal data of Arcadia's customers and employees.

1.6 **"Services"** means the managed IT services described in each Statement of Work.

1.7 **"Statement of Work"** or **"SOW"** means a written statement of work signed by both Parties that references this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 2 – SERVICES AND STATEMENTS OF WORK

2.1 Vertex shall provide the Services described in each SOW in accordance with this Agreement and the applicable SOW.

2.2 Each SOW shall describe the scope of Services, Deliverables, service levels, schedule, Fees and any Arcadia dependencies. In the event of conflict, this Agreement prevails over an SOW unless the SOW expressly identifies the provision of this Agreement it overrides.

2.3 Changes to an SOW are effective only if set out in a written change order signed by both Parties.

2.4 Vertex shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using suitably qualified personnel, and in compliance with applicable law and Arcadia's reasonable site and security policies provided to Vertex in writing. Vertex is responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were its own.

## ARTICLE 3 – TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}}, a period of thirty-six (36) months (the **"Initial Term"**).

3.2 This Agreement does not renew automatically. Any extension of the Initial Term requires a written agreement signed by both Parties before the Initial Term expires.

## ARTICLE 4 – FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Arcadia shall pay Vertex the Fees set out in the applicable SOW. Except as stated in an SOW, the Fees are inclusive of all expenses, and any expense reimbursable by Arcadia must be pre-approved in writing.

4.2 Vertex shall submit valid invoices that identify the SOW, the Services performed and the amounts charged. Arcadia shall pay all undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days of receipt of a valid invoice.

4.3 If Arcadia disputes an invoice in good faith, it shall notify Vertex promptly, pay the undisputed portion, and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute. Vertex shall continue to perform the Services during the dispute.

4.4 Fees are exclusive of sales, use and similar taxes, which Arcadia shall pay where properly invoiced, other than taxes based on Vertex's income, property or employees.

## ARTICLE 5 – CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 The Recipient shall (a) use the Discloser's Confidential Information only to perform or receive the Services and exercise its rights under this Agreement; (b) not disclose it to any third party except to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than this Article; and (c) protect it using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care.

5.2 These obligations do not apply to information that the Recipient can show (a) is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to it before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information.

5.3 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, provided it gives the Discloser prompt prior notice (where lawful) and reasonable cooperation to seek protective treatment.

5.4 The obligations in this Article survive for three (3) years after this Agreement ends. Nothing in this Section shortens any obligation under applicable law to protect trade secrets or Personal Data for longer.

## ARTICLE 6 – INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 Each Party retains all rights in its pre-existing intellectual property and in any materials developed independently of this Agreement. Arcadia retains all rights in its data and Confidential Information.

6.2 Upon payment of the applicable Fees, Vertex assigns to Arcadia all right, title and interest in the Deliverables, excluding Vertex Tools (defined below).

6.3 **"Vertex Tools"** means Vertex's pre-existing or independently developed tools, methodologies and software, and generally applicable know-how. Vertex grants Arcadia a non-exclusive, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use any Vertex Tools incorporated into the Deliverables as necessary to use the Deliverables for Arcadia's and its Affiliates' business purposes.

## ARTICLE 7 – DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY

7.1 The Services involve the processing of Personal Data of Arcadia's customers and employees. The Parties have executed, or will execute alongside this Agreement, a Data Processing Agreement (the **"DPA"**), which is incorporated by reference. In the event of conflict regarding Personal Data, the DPA prevails.

7.2 Vertex shall process Personal Data only on Arcadia's documented instructions and as permitted by the DPA and applicable law.

7.3 Vertex shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the risk and consistent with recognized industry standards, and shall notify Arcadia without undue delay after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to, or loss or disclosure of, Arcadia's data or Personal Data.

## ARTICLE 8 – WARRANTIES

8.1 Each Party represents and warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

8.2 Vertex warrants that for a period of ninety (90) days after performance or delivery (the **"Warranty Period"**), the Services and Deliverables will conform in all material respects to the applicable SOW and will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner.

8.3 If Vertex breaches the warranty in Section 8.2, Vertex shall, at its expense and as Arcadia's first remedy, promptly re-perform the Services or correct the Deliverables. If Vertex cannot do so within a reasonable time, Arcadia may recover the Fees paid for the non-conforming Services or Deliverables, without limiting its other rights under Article 11.

8.4 Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, neither Party makes any other warranty, and all implied warranties are disclaimed to the extent permitted by law.

## ARTICLE 9 – INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 Vertex shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Arcadia, its Affiliates and their personnel from third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from (a) an allegation that the Services or Deliverables infringe or misappropriate a third party's intellectual property rights; (b) Vertex's breach of Article 7; or (c) Vertex's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

9.2 Arcadia shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Vertex and its personnel from third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from Arcadia's provision of materials or data in violation of law or third-party rights.

9.3 The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement (provided no settlement may impose liability or admissions on the indemnified Party without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## ARTICLE 10 – INSURANCE

10.1 During the term of this Agreement, Vertex shall maintain, with reputable insurers, commercial general liability, technology errors and omissions, and cyber liability insurance, with coverage of at least USD 5,000,000 per claim.

10.2 Vertex shall provide certificates of insurance upon Arcadia's reasonable request.

## ARTICLE 11 – LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 Subject to Section 11.3, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement is limited to one (1) times the Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

11.2 Subject to Section 11.3, neither Party is liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement, even if advised of their possibility.

11.3 The limitations in this Article do not apply to liability for fraud, gross negligence or willful misconduct, or to any liability that cannot be limited under applicable law.

## ARTICLE 12 – TERMINATION

12.1 Arcadia may terminate this Agreement or any SOW without cause on thirty (30) days' written notice to Vertex.

12.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any affected SOW by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within a reasonable period stated in a written notice describing the breach, or if the other Party becomes insolvent or subject to bankruptcy proceedings.

12.3 On termination or expiry, Arcadia shall pay undisputed Fees for Services properly performed through the termination date, and Vertex shall promptly return or, at Arcadia's election, securely delete Arcadia's data and Confidential Information and provide reasonable transition assistance on terms to be agreed in good faith.

12.4 Articles 1, 5, 6, 9 and 11, this Article 12 and Article 13, and any other provisions which by their nature are intended to survive, survive termination or expiry.

## ARTICLE 13 – GENERAL

13.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. The state and federal courts located in New York County, New York have exclusive jurisdiction, and each Party submits to their jurisdiction.

13.2 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, except that either Party may assign it to a successor in a merger or sale of all or substantially all of its relevant business upon written notice. Any purported assignment in breach of this Section is void.

13.3 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, nationally recognized courier or email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses each Party designates in writing, and are effective upon receipt.

13.4 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

13.5 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it promptly notifies the other Party and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. This Section does not excuse payment obligations or Vertex's data security obligations.

13.6 **Entire Agreement; Amendment.** This Agreement, together with each SOW and the DPA, is the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions and agreements. It may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

13.7 **Severability; Waiver.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect. A failure to enforce a provision is not a waiver.

13.8 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original.
