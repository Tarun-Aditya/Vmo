# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made with effect from {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") between **Granite Network Integrators Inc** (the "**Supplier**") and **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer with its headquarters in the United States (the "**Customer**"). The Supplier and the Customer are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**".

*This document is a vendor draft issued for review. It is not binding on either party until signed by both.*

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Business Day**" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in the State of Georgia.

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form, disclosed by or on behalf of a party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including the terms of this Agreement.

"**Customer Data**" means data, including Personal Data, supplied by or on behalf of the Customer to the Supplier for the purposes of the Services.

"**Deliverables**" means the materials expressly identified as deliverables in a Statement of Work.

"**Fees**" means the fees payable for the Services as set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

"**Personal Data**" means information relating to an identified or identifiable individual, including customers and employees of the Customer, that the Supplier processes in providing the Services.

"**Services**" means the IT and network integration services described in a Statement of Work.

"**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a document describing Services, Deliverables, Fees and timetable, signed by both parties and expressed to be governed by this Agreement.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation". If a SOW conflicts with these terms, these terms prevail unless the SOW expressly identifies the clause it overrides.

## 2. TERM AND RENEWAL

2.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until {{EXPIRY}} (the "**Initial Term**"), being a period of 24 months.

2.2 On expiry of the Initial Term, and of each Renewal Term, this Agreement renews automatically for a successive period of 12 months (each a "**Renewal Term**") unless either party gives the other at least 30 days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

2.3 Neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience. This Agreement may be terminated before the end of the then-current term only in accordance with Section 13.

## 3. SERVICES AND SCOPE

3.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services described in each SOW in accordance with this Agreement. The Supplier may determine the means, methods, personnel and tools used to perform the Services.

3.2 Any change to the scope, Deliverables, Fees or timetable of a SOW is effective only if recorded in a written change order signed by both parties. The Supplier is not obliged to perform any out-of-scope work unless and until a change order is signed.

3.3 The Customer shall provide, in a timely manner, the access, information, facilities, decisions, approvals and cooperation that the Supplier reasonably requires. The Supplier is not responsible for any delay or failure in performance to the extent caused by the Customer's failure to do so, and any timetable shall be extended accordingly.

3.4 Any dates in a SOW are estimates unless expressly stated to be fixed. The Supplier is not responsible for delay caused by third-party products or services, by Customer-supplied systems or by events described in Section 17.

## 4. FEES AND INVOICING

4.1 The Customer shall pay the Fees set out in the applicable SOW. Unless a SOW states otherwise, Fees are exclusive of all sales, use, value-added and similar taxes, which the Customer shall bear (other than taxes on the Supplier's net income).

4.2 The Supplier shall invoice in accordance with the SOW. Each invoice must be valid, meaning that it identifies the SOW, the Services performed and the amount due.

4.3 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 15 days of receipt of a valid invoice, by electronic transfer to the account nominated by the Supplier.

4.4 If the Customer disputes any part of an invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Supplier in writing with reasonable detail before the due date and shall pay the undisputed portion on time. The parties shall seek to resolve the dispute promptly.

4.5 Without prejudice to its other rights, the Supplier may charge interest on overdue undisputed amounts at the maximum rate permitted by applicable law from the due date until payment, and may suspend the Services on written notice while any undisputed amount remains overdue.

4.6 The Customer shall reimburse reasonable, properly incurred and pre-approved expenses.

7.4 The obligations in this Section 7 survive for 1 years after this Agreement ends.

8.5 The Customer authorises the Supplier to engage subcontractors to process Personal Data, provided that the Supplier remains responsible for their performance of the Services. On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall, on the Customer's written request, delete or return Personal Data, except where retention is required by law or is held in routine backups.
