# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer headquartered in the United States ("Arcadia", "the Customer", "we" or "us" when the context is Arcadia), and **Sable Legal Tech Inc** ("Sable", "the Supplier", "we" or "us" when the context is Sable). Each of us is a "party" and together we are "the parties". "You" means whichever party is receiving Confidential Information at the time.

It takes effect on {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "Effective Date").

## Why we are signing this

We want to explore and, if we agree, carry out an IT-services relationship (the "Purpose"). To do that, each of us will need to share information that is private. This Agreement sets the ground rules for how that information is handled.

## What this Agreement covers

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information that one party (the "Discloser") shares with the other (the "Recipient") in connection with the Purpose, in any form, whether written, oral, electronic or visual. It includes business plans, pricing, customer and supplier details, software, source code, system designs, security information, legal advice and the terms of any discussions between us. It also covers information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature or the circumstances of disclosure, whether or not it is marked.

Confidential Information does not include information that you can show:

- is or becomes public through no fault of yours;
- you already knew, free of any duty of confidence, before receiving it;
- you received lawfully from a third party who was free to share it; or
- you developed independently without using or referring to the Discloser's information.

## How long this lasts

The initial term runs from the Effective Date until {{EXPIRY}}, a period of 24 months (the "Initial Term"). Either of us may end this Agreement earlier by giving the other written notice. We may extend the term only in writing signed by both of us.

## What you must do

You agree to:

1. use the Discloser's Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
2. keep it confidential and not disclose it to anyone except as this Agreement allows;
3. protect it with at least the same care you use for your own confidential information, and never less than reasonable care;
4. limit access to your employees, officers, contractors, affiliates and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality duties at least as protective as these; and
5. tell the Discloser promptly if you become aware of any unauthorised use or disclosure, and help to limit the damage.

You remain responsible for any breach of this Agreement by anyone you share Confidential Information with.

## When disclosure is required by law

If a court, regulator or other authority legally requires you to disclose Confidential Information, you may do so, provided that (where the law allows) you give the Discloser prompt written notice so it can seek protection, disclose only what is legally required, and cooperate reasonably with any effort to limit the disclosure.

## Who owns what

All Confidential Information stays the property of the Discloser. Nothing in this Agreement gives you any licence or right in the Discloser's patents, copyrights, trade secrets, trademarks or other intellectual property, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose. If you give feedback or suggestions, the Discloser may use them freely, but this does not transfer ownership of the Discloser's own Confidential Information.

## Personal data

If Confidential Information includes personal data, you will handle it in line with applicable data protection laws, use it only for the Purpose, keep it secure, and not transfer it in breach of those laws. Neither of us intends to share personal data unless needed for the Purpose. If we do, we will agree any further terms the law requires, such as a separate data processing agreement, before the sharing begins.

## Fees and services

This Agreement is not a services contract. No fees are payable under it, no invoices will be raised, and neither of us is obliged to buy or supply any services. Each of us bears its own costs. Any services will be covered by a separate written agreement signed by both of us.

## Returning or destroying information

When the Discloser asks, or when this Agreement ends, you will promptly return or securely destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information and confirm in writing that you have done so. You may keep copies that the law or your genuine compliance rules require, or that sit in routine electronic backups, but those copies stay confidential and protected under this Agreement for as long as you hold them.

## Promises and what we do not promise

Each of us confirms that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement. Confidential Information is provided "as is". The Discloser makes no promise about its accuracy or completeness, and gives no warranty of any kind, except as we may agree in a later written agreement.

## If something goes wrong

If you breach this Agreement, you are responsible for the losses the Discloser suffers as a result, subject to the paragraph below. You will also indemnify the Discloser against third-party claims arising from your unauthorised use or disclosure of its Confidential Information.

Neither of us is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss, or loss of profit, arising from this Agreement, except where the loss results from a breach of the confidentiality obligations or from fraud. Nothing in this Agreement limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

Because damage from misuse of Confidential Information may not be fixable with money alone, the Discloser may seek an injunction or other urgent relief from a court to stop or prevent a breach, without needing to prove actual loss.

## How long your duties last

Your confidentiality obligations continue to apply after this Agreement ends and survive for 1 years after the agreement ends. Obligations that by their nature are meant to continue, such as those on returning information, governing law and dispute resolution, also continue after that time as needed to be effective. Rights and liabilities that arose before the end of this Agreement are not affected by it ending.

## Ending early

Either of us may end this Agreement by written notice at any time. Ending it does not release you from your duties for Confidential Information already received, which remain in force for the period stated above.

## Governing law and disputes

This Agreement, and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it, is governed by the laws of New York. We will first try in good faith to resolve any dispute through discussions between senior representatives. If we cannot do so within a reasonable time, either of us may bring proceedings in the courts of New York, and each of us submits to the jurisdiction of those courts.

## Giving notices

Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognised courier, or by email with confirmation of sending, to the address or email the receiving party has most recently given the other in writing. A notice takes effect when it is received or, if received outside business hours, at the start of the next business day.

## The usual legal housekeeping

**Assignment.** Neither of us may transfer or assign this Agreement, or any right under it, without the other's prior written consent, which must not be unreasonably withheld.

**Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the whole agreement between us about its subject matter and replaces all earlier discussions and understandings on it. It does not replace any separate written agreement for services, which will have its own terms.

**Changes and waiver.** Any change must be in writing and signed by both of us. If either of us delays or chooses not to enforce a right, that does not waive the right.

**Severability.** If a court finds any part of this Agreement unenforceable, the rest stays in force, and we will replace the unenforceable part with a valid one that is as close as possible to our original intent.

**No partnership.** Nothing in this Agreement makes us partners, joint venturers or agents of each other.

**Counterparts.** We may sign in separate counterparts, including electronically, and together they form one agreement.
