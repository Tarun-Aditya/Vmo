# Work Order

This Work Order is issued on {{EFFECTIVE}} between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer with its US headquarters in the United States ("Customer"), and **Helix Digital Consulting Inc** ("Supplier"), under the Statement of Work dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between the same parties (the "Parent Agreement").

1. **Term.** This Work Order takes effect on {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "Effective Date") and continues for an initial term of four (4) months, ending on {{EXPIRY}}, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Work Order or extended by written agreement signed by both parties.

2. **Fees.** The total fee for the Services is a fixed fee of USD 185,000 (the "Fee"). The Fee is the entire consideration for the Services and includes all labour, tools, travel and other expenses of the Supplier unless the parties agree otherwise in writing before the expense is incurred. No additional amounts are payable without a written change order signed by both parties.

3. **Invoicing and Payment.** The Supplier shall invoice the Fee in accordance with the milestone and payment schedule agreed by the parties in writing, and each invoice shall be valid only if it identifies this Work Order, describes the Services or Deliverable invoiced, and is addressed to the Customer in accordance with clause 18. The Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within 45 days of receipt of a valid invoice. The Customer may withhold any amount disputed in good faith, shall notify the Supplier of the dispute and its reasons promptly, and the parties shall work together to resolve it. Payment does not constitute acceptance of any Deliverable or waiver of any right.

4. **Services and Scope.** The Supplier shall design, build and deliver a test automation framework for the Customer's retail technology environment, together with related documentation and knowledge transfer to the Customer's personnel (the "Services"). The Services shall be performed in accordance with this Work Order and the Parent Agreement. The Supplier shall provide suitably skilled and experienced personnel and shall remain responsible for their acts and omissions.

5. **Milestone.** The Supplier shall deliver the following milestone (the "Deliverable"):

| Milestone | Description | Due Date |
|---|---|---|
| Milestone 1 | Test automation framework delivered | {{MS1}} |

6. **Acceptance.** The Customer shall review each Deliverable within a reasonable period after delivery and shall either accept it in writing or notify the Supplier of any material non-conformity with the agreed specification. The Supplier shall correct any non-conformity promptly at no additional charge and resubmit the Deliverable for review. A Deliverable is not accepted until the Customer confirms acceptance in writing.

7. **Customer Responsibilities.** The Customer shall provide timely access to relevant systems, information, environments and personnel reasonably required for the Services. The Supplier shall promptly notify the Customer if any delay in the Customer's performance is likely to affect the Services, and the affected dates shall be adjusted fairly by written agreement.

8. **Change Control.** Either party may request a change to the Services. No change is effective unless documented in a written change order signed by authorised representatives of both parties, which shall state any effect on the Fee, the timetable and the Deliverable.

9. **Confidentiality.** Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other in connection with this Work Order that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, shall use it only to perform or receive the Services, and shall not disclose it except to its personnel, advisers and subcontractors who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations. These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the recipient, was lawfully known to or independently developed by the recipient, or is lawfully received from a third party. A party may disclose information to the extent required by law or court order, after giving the other party reasonable notice where lawful. This clause survives expiry or termination of this Work Order. Any confidentiality provisions of the Parent Agreement apply in addition.

10. **Intellectual Property.** As between the parties, the Customer owns all right, title and interest in the Deliverable and all materials created specifically for the Customer under this Work Order, upon payment of the Fee. The Supplier retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, tools and know-how, and grants the Customer a non-exclusive, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use, copy and modify them as incorporated in or necessary to use the Deliverable. The Customer retains ownership of its own data and materials. The Supplier shall not incorporate third-party or open-source components in the Deliverable in a manner that would impose obligations on the Customer without the Customer's prior written consent.

11. **Warranties.** The Supplier warrants that: (a) the Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care in accordance with good industry practice; (b) the Deliverable will conform in all material respects to the agreed specification; (c) the Deliverable and the Services will not infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party; and (d) the Supplier has the right and authority to enter into this Work Order. The Supplier shall re-perform or correct any non-conforming Services notified to it promptly at no additional charge. Each party warrants that it will comply with applicable laws in performing this Work Order.

12. **Indemnity.** The Supplier shall defend and indemnify the Customer against third-party claims alleging that the Deliverable or the Services infringe any intellectual property right, and against losses arising from the Supplier's gross negligence or wilful misconduct, in each case subject to the Customer giving prompt notice, reasonable cooperation, and sole control of the defence and settlement to the Supplier (provided that no settlement imposing liability on the Customer may be made without its consent). Any further indemnities in the Parent Agreement continue to apply.

13. **Liability.** Each party's liability under or in connection with this Work Order is governed by the liability provisions of the Parent Agreement, which apply to this Work Order as if set out in full in it.

14. **Data Protection.** Where the Supplier processes personal data on behalf of the Customer, it shall do so only on the Customer's documented instructions, shall implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures, shall ensure that personnel with access are bound by confidentiality, shall assist the Customer in responding to data subject requests and regulatory enquiries, shall not transfer such data except as permitted by the Customer and applicable law, and shall notify the Customer without undue delay on becoming aware of any unauthorised access to or loss of such data. On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall return or securely delete such personal data at the Customer's election, unless law requires retention. The parties shall comply with data protection laws applicable to them.

15. **Termination.** Either party may terminate this Work Order by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within a reasonable period after written notice requiring remedy, or if the other party becomes insolvent or ceases to carry on business. The Customer may also terminate this Work Order for convenience on written notice, in which case it shall pay for Services properly performed and Deliverables accepted up to the termination date, calculated fairly by reference to the Fee. On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall promptly deliver to the Customer all work in progress and Customer materials, and accrued rights and clauses intended to survive shall continue.

16. **Assignment and Subcontracting.** Neither party may assign or transfer this Work Order, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of the other, not to be unreasonably withheld. The Supplier shall not subcontract any part of the Services without the Customer's prior written consent and shall remain liable for the performance of its subcontractors.

17. **Governing Law and Disputes.** This Work Order and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute by good-faith discussion between senior representatives before commencing proceedings, without prejudice to either party's right to seek urgent injunctive relief.

18. **Notices.** Notices under this Work Order must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognised courier or by email with confirmation of transmission to the address that each party has notified to the other for this purpose, and are deemed received on delivery or, for email, on the next business day after transmission.

19. **Order of Precedence.** This Work Order is subject to the Parent Agreement. In the event of conflict, the terms of this Work Order prevail only as to the Services, Fee, term and Deliverable described in it, and otherwise the Parent Agreement prevails. Capitalised terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Parent Agreement.

20. **Entire Agreement.** This Work Order, together with the Parent Agreement, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, representations and understandings concerning it. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver. This Work Order may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
