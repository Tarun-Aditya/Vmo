# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made with effect from {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "Effective Date") between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer with its United States headquarters at the address notified in writing to the other party (the "Customer"), and **Atlas Facilities Tech Inc**, a corporation with its registered office at the address notified in writing to the other party (the "Supplier"). Each is a "Party" and together the "Parties".

1. **Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until {{EXPIRY}}, being an initial term of 24 months, unless terminated earlier under clause 12. The Parties may extend the term only by written agreement signed by both of them.

2. **Fees.** No fees, charges or other consideration are payable by either Party to the other under this Agreement. Each Party bears its own costs of performing it. Any services the Parties may later agree will be governed by a separate written agreement, and no invoice may be issued under this Agreement.

3. **Purpose and Scope.** The Parties wish to discuss and evaluate a possible commercial relationship in which the Supplier may provide facilities-related technology and IT services to the Customer (the "Purpose"). Nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to disclose any information, to enter into any further agreement, or to continue discussions.

4. **Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means all non-public information, in any form, disclosed or made available by or on behalf of a Party (the "Discloser") to the other Party (the "Recipient") in connection with the Purpose, whether before or after the Effective Date, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature or the circumstances of disclosure. It includes business plans, pricing, store and facilities data, systems architecture, source code, security information, product and supplier information, and the terms and existence of the Parties' discussions.

5. **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show by written records: (a) is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient without restriction before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party not under a duty of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed without use of or reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information.

6. **Obligations.** The Recipient shall: (a) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (b) keep it confidential and protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of a similar nature, and no less than reasonable care; (c) not disclose it to any person other than its employees, officers, affiliates, professional advisers and subcontractors who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement; and (d) promptly notify the Discloser on becoming aware of any unauthorised use or disclosure and cooperate to remedy it. The Recipient is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by persons to whom it discloses Confidential Information.

7. **Compelled Disclosure.** If the Recipient is required by law, regulation, court order or governmental authority to disclose Confidential Information, it shall, where lawful, give the Discloser prompt written notice so that the Discloser may seek a protective order, and shall disclose only that portion which it is legally required to disclose.

8. **Return and Destruction.** On the Discloser's written request, or on expiry or termination of this Agreement, the Recipient shall promptly return or securely destroy all Confidential Information in its possession or control and, on request, confirm in writing that it has done so. The Recipient may retain copies required by law or held in routine backup systems, provided they remain subject to this Agreement and are not accessed for any other purpose.

9. **Intellectual Property.** All Confidential Information and all intellectual property rights in it remain the property of the Discloser or its licensors. No licence or other right is granted to the Recipient except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose. Feedback or suggestions voluntarily provided by one Party may be used by the other without obligation, provided that this does not permit disclosure of the providing Party's Confidential Information.

10. **Warranties and Disclaimer.** Each Party warrants that it has authority to enter into this Agreement and to make the disclosures it makes under it. Except as stated in this clause, all Confidential Information is provided "as is", and the Discloser makes no warranty as to its accuracy, completeness or fitness for any purpose.

11. **Data Protection.** The Parties do not intend to exchange personal data under this Agreement. If personal data is nevertheless disclosed, the Recipient shall process it only for the Purpose, in accordance with applicable data protection and privacy laws, shall apply appropriate technical and organisational security measures, and shall notify the Discloser without undue delay of any security incident affecting it. Before any material transfer of personal data, the Parties shall agree appropriate additional terms.

12. **Termination.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time on written notice to the other. Termination does not affect accrued rights or liabilities. The obligations of confidentiality and restricted use in this Agreement survive expiry or termination and continue for 3 years after this Agreement ends, except that obligations in respect of any trade secret continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

13. **Liability.** Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law. Subject to that, neither Party is liable to the other under this Agreement for indirect, special or consequential loss, or for loss of profit, revenue or goodwill, except in respect of a breach of clauses 6 to 8.

14. **Indemnity.** Each Party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages and reasonable legal costs, to the extent arising from the indemnifying Party's wilful or grossly negligent unauthorised disclosure of the other Party's Confidential Information, provided that the indemnified Party gives prompt notice of the claim, reasonable assistance, and control of its defence and settlement.

15. **Remedies.** Each Party acknowledges that unauthorised use or disclosure of Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which damages alone would be an inadequate remedy, and that the Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief without proof of actual damage, in addition to any other remedy available.

16. **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign, novate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any right or obligation under it without the prior written consent of the other, not to be unreasonably withheld. Any purported transfer in breach of this clause is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

17. **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognised courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the address or email address that each Party has notified to the other for this purpose. A notice is deemed received on delivery if delivered by hand or courier, and at the time of transmission if sent by email during business hours on a business day, and otherwise on the next business day.

18. **Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of Illinois, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. The state and federal courts located in Illinois have exclusive jurisdiction, and each Party submits to their jurisdiction, save that either Party may seek injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

19. **General.** This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior understandings relating to it. It may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver. If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect. Neither Party acquires any right to publicise the discussions or use the other's name or marks without prior written consent. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
