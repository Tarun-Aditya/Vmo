# Statement of Work No. 1

| | |
|---|---|
| **Project** | Cloud Landing Zone Build and Workload Migration |
| **Parent Agreement** | Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between Arcadia Retail Inc. and Meridian Cloud Services LLC |
| **Period** | Effective Date {{EFFECTIVE}} until {{EXPIRY}} (initial term of nine (9) months) |
| **Annual Fees** | USD 900,000 |
| **Total Contract Value** | USD 675,000 |

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer with its headquarters in the United States ("**Customer**"), and **Meridian Cloud Services LLC** ("**Supplier**"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between the same parties (the "**Parent Agreement**"). Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Parent Agreement.

## 1. Background

1.1 Customer operates retail businesses and wishes to move selected applications and data from its existing hosting environments to a secure, well-governed cloud environment.

1.2 Supplier provides cloud engineering and migration services and has agreed to perform the Services described in this SOW on the terms of the Parent Agreement and this SOW.

1.3 If there is a conflict between this SOW and the Parent Agreement, the Parent Agreement prevails unless this SOW expressly identifies the clause of the Parent Agreement that it intends to vary.

## 2. Scope

2.1 **Services.** Supplier shall provide the following services (the "**Services**"):

(a) design, build and configure a cloud landing zone, including account structure, network connectivity, identity and access management, logging, monitoring, security baselines and cost-governance controls (the "**Landing Zone**");

(b) assess, plan and migrate the in-scope workloads identified by Customer and agreed in the migration plan to the Landing Zone in two waves ("**Wave 1**" and "**Wave 2**");

(c) carry out cutover testing, rollback planning and post-migration stabilisation support for each wave; and

(d) provide documentation and knowledge transfer to Customer personnel sufficient to operate the migrated environment.

2.2 **Out of scope.** Application re-architecture or code changes beyond those necessary for migration, ongoing managed services after stabilisation, and the procurement of third-party licences and cloud consumption charges are outside the scope of this SOW unless agreed in a change order.

2.3 **Change control.** Any change to the scope, Deliverables, Milestones or Fees shall be made only by a written change order signed by authorised representatives of both parties.

## 3. Deliverables and Milestones

3.1 Supplier shall deliver the following Deliverables by the dates shown (each a "**Milestone**"):

| Milestone | Deliverable | Description | Due Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Milestone 1 | Landing zone ready | Landing Zone built, configured, security-reviewed and handed over, with design and operations documentation | {{MS1}} |
| Milestone 2 | Workload migration wave 1 | Wave 1 workloads migrated, tested and in production, with cutover report | {{MS2}} |
| Milestone 3 | Workload migration wave 2 | Wave 2 workloads migrated, tested and in production, with final migration report and knowledge transfer completed | {{MS3}} |

3.2 Supplier shall promptly notify Customer in writing if it becomes aware that a Milestone is likely to be missed, stating the cause and its proposed recovery plan.

3.3 A Milestone date shall be extended to the extent that Supplier's delay is caused by Customer's failure to perform its responsibilities under this SOW, provided that Supplier notifies Customer of the delay promptly and uses reasonable efforts to mitigate it.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

4.1 **Supplier** shall: (a) perform the Services with reasonable skill, care and diligence using suitably qualified personnel; (b) appoint a project manager as the primary point of contact; (c) provide regular status reports; and (d) comply with Customer's reasonable security and site policies notified to it.

4.2 **Customer** shall: (a) appoint a project sponsor and a technical lead with authority to make day-to-day decisions; (b) provide timely access to systems, information, environments and personnel reasonably required; (c) make decisions and give approvals promptly; and (d) be responsible for its cloud consumption charges and third-party licences.

4.3 The parties shall hold governance meetings at a frequency agreed by the project managers to review progress, risks and issues.

## 5. Assumptions

5.1 The Fees and Milestone dates are based on the following assumptions:

(a) Customer will provide an accurate inventory of in-scope workloads and their dependencies before the planning of each wave begins;

(b) Customer's existing environments will remain available and supported until the relevant wave is accepted;

(c) Customer will make its personnel available for testing and decision-making when reasonably requested; and

(d) the in-scope workloads are compatible with the target cloud platform without material re-architecture.

5.2 If an assumption proves incorrect, the parties shall discuss the impact in good faith and, where it affects the Services, the Fees or the Milestones, address it under the change control procedure in clause 2.3.

## 6. Fees and Invoicing

6.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay Supplier the annual fees of USD 900,000, which for the initial term of nine (9) months of this SOW result in a total contract value of USD 675,000 (the "**Fees**"). The Fees are the entire charge for the Services unless a change order provides otherwise.

6.2 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice the Fees in the manner set out in the Parent Agreement or, if it is silent, upon achievement of each Milestone in the instalments agreed in writing by the parties. Each invoice shall be valid only if it identifies this SOW, the relevant Milestone, the amount due and any applicable taxes, and is sent to Customer's designated accounts payable contact.

6.3 **Payment.** Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days of receipt of a valid invoice.

6.4 **Disputes.** Customer shall notify Supplier promptly of any amount disputed in good faith, giving reasons, and shall pay the undisputed portion. The parties shall work to resolve the dispute under the dispute resolution provisions of the Parent Agreement.

6.5 **Taxes and expenses.** The Fees exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes, which shall be shown separately on the invoice. Expenses are payable only if approved in advance in writing by Customer.

## 7. Acceptance

7.1 Supplier shall notify Customer in writing when a Deliverable is ready for acceptance. Customer shall review and test the Deliverable against the description in clause 3.1 and the agreed acceptance criteria within a reasonable period and shall either accept it in writing or give written notice of material non-conformities with reasonable detail.

7.2 Supplier shall correct any non-conformity promptly and resubmit the Deliverable, and the process in clause 7.1 shall be repeated.

7.3 A Deliverable is accepted when Customer gives written acceptance, or when Customer uses the Deliverable in production for its intended purpose other than for testing, whichever occurs first. Customer shall not unreasonably withhold or delay acceptance of a Deliverable that conforms in all material respects to its description.

7.4 If a Deliverable remains non-conforming after reasonable opportunities to correct it, Customer may exercise the remedies available under the Parent Agreement.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1 This SOW commences on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier, continues until {{EXPIRY}}. It may be extended only by written agreement of the parties.

8.2 Either party may terminate this SOW in accordance with the termination provisions of the Parent Agreement. Termination of the Parent Agreement terminates this SOW unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

8.3 On expiry or termination, Supplier shall deliver to Customer all completed and in-progress Deliverables, return Customer Data and Customer Confidential Information, and Customer shall pay undisputed Fees for Services properly performed up to the date of termination, in accordance with clause 6.

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party shall hold the other's Confidential Information in confidence and use it only for the purposes of this SOW, in accordance with the confidentiality provisions of the Parent Agreement, which apply to this SOW.

## 10. Intellectual Property

10.1 Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property. Customer retains all rights in Customer Data and in its applications and content.

10.2 Ownership of Deliverables and licences to Supplier's pre-existing materials incorporated in them are governed by the intellectual property provisions of the Parent Agreement. Where those provisions are silent, Customer shall own the Deliverables created specifically for it on payment, and Supplier grants Customer a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use any Supplier pre-existing materials embedded in the Deliverables as necessary to use the Deliverables.

## 11. Warranties

11.1 Supplier warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards, and that the Deliverables will conform in all material respects to their descriptions in this SOW.

11.2 Supplier shall re-perform any Services that do not meet this warranty, at no additional charge, where Customer notifies Supplier within a reasonable period after discovery. This is without prejudice to Customer's other rights under the Parent Agreement.

11.3 Each party warrants that it has full authority to enter into this SOW.

## 12. Data Protection and Security

12.1 Each party shall comply with data protection and privacy laws applicable to it in connection with the Services. To the extent Supplier processes personal data on Customer's behalf, it shall do so only on Customer's documented instructions and in accordance with the data protection terms of the Parent Agreement or any data processing addendum between the parties.

12.2 Supplier shall maintain appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect Customer Data against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure, and shall notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a security incident affecting Customer Data.

## 13. Liability and Indemnity

13.1 Each party's liability arising out of or in connection with this SOW is governed by the liability provisions of the Parent Agreement, which apply to this SOW as if set out in full.

13.2 Each party's indemnification obligations in respect of this SOW are as set out in the indemnity provisions of the Parent Agreement, which apply to the Services and Deliverables.

## 14. General

14.1 **Governing law.** This SOW and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

14.2 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign or transfer this SOW or any rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of the other, not to be unreasonably withheld, except as permitted by the Parent Agreement.

14.3 **Notices.** Notices under this SOW shall be in writing and given in accordance with the notice provisions of the Parent Agreement.

14.4 **Entire agreement.** This SOW, together with the Parent Agreement, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions and understandings relating to it. No variation is effective unless made in writing and signed by both parties.

14.5 **Counterparts.** This SOW may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
