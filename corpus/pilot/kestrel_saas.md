# SOFTWARE-AS-A-SERVICE SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

The Supplier and the Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Customer Data**" means all data, content and information submitted to or processed through the Services by or on behalf of the Customer, including Personal Data.

"**Personal Data**" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person that forms part of Customer Data, being personal data of the Customer's customers and employees.

"**Services**" means the Supplier's hosted marketing cloud platform and related support, as made available to the Customer under this Agreement and described in the Supplier's then-current documentation.

"**Documentation**" means the Supplier's user guides and technical specifications for the Services made generally available to its customers.

"**Security Incident**" means a confirmed breach of the Supplier's security leading to unauthorised access to, or disclosure of, Customer Data in the Supplier's possession or control.

"**Term**" means the Initial Term together with each Renewal Term.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. "Including" means "including without limitation".

2.1 Subject to this Agreement, the Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable right during the Term to access and use the Services for the Customer's internal business purposes.

2.2 The Supplier may modify, update or enhance the Services from time to time, provided that no such change materially reduces the core functionality of the Services during the then-current term.

2.3 The Customer shall not, and shall not permit any third party to: (a) copy, modify or create derivative works of the Services; (b) reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the Services except as permitted by non-excludable law; (c) resell, sublicense or provide the Services to third parties; (d) use the Services in breach of applicable law or to transmit unlawful, infringing or malicious content; or (e) interfere with or disrupt the integrity or performance of the Services.

2.4 The Customer is responsible for its users' compliance with this Agreement, the security of its credentials, and the accuracy, quality and legality of Customer Data and the means by which it is acquired.

2.5 The Supplier may suspend access to the Services where reasonably necessary to prevent harm to the Services or other customers, or where the Customer is in material breach, and shall use reasonable efforts to give prior notice and to restore access promptly once the cause is resolved.

## 3. TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until {{EXPIRY}} (the "**Initial Term**"), being a period of 12 months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

3.2 This Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of 12 months (each a "**Renewal Term**") unless either Party gives the other at least 45 days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term, in which case this Agreement expires at the end of that term.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier annual fees of USD 210,000 for the Services (the "**Fees**"). The total contract value for the Initial Term is USD 210,000.

4.2 The Supplier shall invoice the Fees in advance for each year of the Term. Undisputed invoices are payable within 60 days of the Customer's receipt of a valid invoice.

4.3 A valid invoice must identify the Customer, the Services and the amount due, and be addressed in accordance with clause 15.

4.4 The Customer shall notify the Supplier in writing of any good-faith dispute regarding an invoice promptly after receipt, giving reasonable detail, and shall pay the undisputed portion by the due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute.

4.5 Fees are exclusive of sales, use, value-added, withholding and similar taxes, which the Customer shall bear, other than taxes on the Supplier's net income. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the Fees for any Renewal Term shall be the Fees in effect at the end of the preceding term.

4.6 Overdue undisputed amounts may, at the Supplier's election, bear interest at the maximum rate permitted by applicable law from the due date until payment. The Supplier may suspend the Services under clause 2.5 for non-payment of undisputed amounts that remain unpaid after written notice.

## 5. SERVICE LEVELS

5.1 The Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Services available at least 99.0% of the time in each calendar month, excluding downtime resulting from: (a) scheduled or emergency maintenance; (b) the Customer's equipment, software, networks or acts or omissions; (c) third-party services or the public internet; (d) suspension permitted under this Agreement; and (e) force majeure events under clause 16.

5.2 The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any Security Incident within 96 hours after the Supplier confirms the Security Incident.

5.3 If availability falls below the level in clause 5.1 in a month, the Customer shall notify the Supplier, and the Supplier shall investigate and use reasonable efforts to remedy the cause. Except for the Customer's rights under clause 12, this clause 5 states the Customer's sole service-level remedy.

6.3 The Supplier shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the Customer Data and designed to protect it against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure.

6.4 The Supplier may engage subcontractors and hosting providers to support the Services and remains responsible for their performance of the obligations assumed by it under this clause to the extent required by applicable law.

6.5 Upon request made within 30 days after expiry or termination, the Supplier shall make Customer Data available for export in a standard format. Thereafter, the Supplier may delete Customer Data in accordance with its standard retention practices.
