This Statement of Work No. 1 (the "**SOW**") is entered into between **Arcadia Retail Inc.** (the "**Customer**") and **Helix Digital Consulting Inc** (the "**Supplier**") and is issued under the Master Services Agreement dated {{PARENT_DATE}} between the same parties (the "**Parent Agreement**"). Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Parent Agreement.

## 1. Background

1.1 The Customer is a retailer with its headquarters in the United States and wishes to consolidate its commercial, store, supply-chain and e-commerce data into a single governed data platform and to enable enterprise analytics on that platform.

1.2 The Supplier has represented that it has the skills, personnel and experience to design, build and transition such a platform, and the Customer has engaged the Supplier to do so on the terms of this SOW and the Parent Agreement.

1.3 This SOW forms part of, and is to be read together with, the Parent Agreement. If there is a conflict between this SOW and the Parent Agreement, the Parent Agreement prevails unless this SOW expressly states that it overrides a specified provision of the Parent Agreement.

## 2. Scope

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the following services (the "**Services**"):

(a) **Architecture.** Assess the Customer's existing data sources and systems, and design the target data platform architecture, including ingestion, storage, transformation, security, access control and data governance components.

(b) **Build.** Develop, configure, test and deploy the core data pipelines that bring in-scope data sources onto the platform, together with associated monitoring, logging and operational runbooks.

(c) **Analytics.** Establish the analytics layer, including curated data models, reporting datasets and dashboards agreed with the Customer, and support the Customer's use of them.

(d) **Handover.** Hand over the platform and analytics assets to the Customer and conduct knowledge transfer to the Customer's nominated personnel.

2.2 The following are outside the scope of this SOW unless agreed through a change under clause 5.4: replacement of the Customer's transactional or point-of-sale systems; procurement of third-party software licences or cloud infrastructure in the Customer's name; and ongoing production support after the handover under Milestone 3.

## 3. Deliverables and Milestones

3.1 The Supplier shall deliver the following Deliverables by the dates shown:

| No. | Milestone | Principal Deliverables | Due Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Milestone 1 | Data platform architecture approved | Architecture design document; security and data governance model; delivery plan | {{MS1}} |
| Milestone 2 | Core pipelines in production | Production-ready data pipelines for in-scope sources; test evidence; operational runbooks | {{MS2}} |
| Milestone 3 | Analytics handover and knowledge transfer | Analytics models and dashboards; platform documentation; completed knowledge-transfer sessions | {{MS3}} |

3.2 The Supplier shall notify the Customer promptly if it becomes aware that a Milestone is likely to be missed, stating the cause, the expected impact and its proposed recovery plan.

3.3 A due date is extended only to the extent that a delay is caused by the Customer's failure to perform its responsibilities under this SOW, provided the Supplier has notified the Customer of the delay in writing within a reasonable time after becoming aware of it.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

4.1 **Supplier.** The Supplier shall: (a) provide suitably skilled and experienced personnel; (b) appoint a delivery lead who is the Supplier's primary point of contact; (c) perform the Services with reasonable skill and care and in accordance with good industry practice; and (d) provide regular progress reports in the form and at the intervals reasonably requested by the Customer.

4.2 **Customer.** The Customer shall: (a) appoint a programme sponsor and a project manager with authority to make day-to-day decisions; (b) give the Supplier timely access to the systems, data, premises and personnel reasonably required to perform the Services; (c) provide timely feedback and approvals; and (d) make available the environments and third-party licences described in clause 6.

4.3 **Governance.** The parties' representatives shall meet at regular intervals to review progress, risks, issues and change requests. Decisions that alter scope, fees or Milestone dates take effect only in accordance with clause 5.4.

## 5. Assumptions and Change Control

5.1 The Services and the Fees are based on the following assumptions: (a) the Customer's source systems are accessible through supported interfaces; (b) the Customer will provide data of reasonable quality and the information necessary to interpret it; (c) the Customer's nominated personnel will be available for workshops, reviews and knowledge transfer; and (d) the Customer is responsible for the cloud infrastructure and third-party software used by the platform.

5.2 If an assumption proves incorrect, the Supplier shall promptly notify the Customer, and the parties shall discuss in good faith any resulting adjustment to scope, timetable or Fees through the change process.

5.3 The Supplier shall comply with the Customer's reasonable site, security and conduct policies notified to it in writing.

5.4 **Change control.** Either party may propose a change to this SOW. No change is binding unless recorded in a written change request signed by authorised representatives of both parties, describing the change and its effect on scope, Deliverables, timetable and Fees.

## 6. Fees and Invoicing

6.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier annual fees of USD 3,100,000 for each year of the initial term, giving a total contract value of USD 6,200,000 (the "**Fees**"). Any additional work is chargeable only if agreed under clause 5.4.

6.2 The Supplier shall invoice the Fees in accordance with the invoicing schedule agreed in writing between the parties' representatives. Each invoice must be valid, meaning it identifies this SOW, describes the Services or Milestone to which it relates, and states the amount due and any applicable taxes.

6.3 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 45 days of receipt of a valid invoice. If the Customer disputes an invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Supplier promptly, pay the undisputed portion, and the parties shall work to resolve the dispute under the dispute resolution provisions of the Parent Agreement.

6.4 Fees exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes, which shall be shown separately on the invoice and borne by the Customer, other than taxes on the Supplier's income, property or employees.

## 7. Acceptance

7.1 On completing each Milestone, the Supplier shall submit the relevant Deliverables to the Customer with written notice that they are ready for acceptance.

7.2 The Customer shall review the Deliverables against the descriptions in this SOW and the acceptance criteria agreed in the delivery plan, and shall, within a reasonable review period agreed between the parties' representatives, either accept the Deliverables in writing or give written notice identifying in reasonable detail how they fail to conform.

7.3 The Supplier shall correct any non-conformity at its own cost and resubmit the Deliverables promptly. The acceptance procedure then repeats. If Deliverables still fail to conform after reasonable resubmission, the Customer may exercise the remedies available under the Parent Agreement.

7.4 Acceptance of a Milestone confirms that the Deliverables conform to this SOW at the time of acceptance but does not waive the Supplier's warranty obligations or the Customer's rights in respect of latent defects.

## 8. Term, Renewal and Termination

8.1 This SOW commences on {{EFFECTIVE}} and, unless terminated earlier, continues until {{EXPIRY}}.

8.2 This SOW does not renew automatically. Any extension requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

8.3 Either party may terminate this SOW in the circumstances, and on the notice, set out in the termination provisions of the Parent Agreement. On expiry or termination the Supplier shall deliver to the Customer all work in progress and Deliverables completed to date, and the Customer shall pay undisputed Fees properly due for Services performed up to the date of termination.

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the purposes of this SOW, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations, in each case in accordance with the confidentiality provisions of the Parent Agreement, which apply to this SOW.

## 10. Intellectual Property

10.1 As between the parties, the Customer owns all right, title and interest in the Customer Data and in the Deliverables created specifically for the Customer under this SOW, on payment of the applicable Fees, as provided in the Parent Agreement.

10.2 The Supplier retains its pre-existing materials, tools, methodologies and know-how, and grants the Customer a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use any of them embedded in the Deliverables to the extent necessary to use the Deliverables for the Customer's business purposes.

## 11. Warranties

The Supplier warrants that (a) the Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care by suitably qualified personnel; (b) the Deliverables will conform in all material respects to this SOW when accepted; and (c) the Deliverables and Services will not knowingly infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party. These warranties are in addition to those in the Parent Agreement.

## 12. Data Protection and Security

12.1 The Supplier shall process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions and for the purposes of this SOW, and shall comply with applicable data protection laws and the data protection terms of the Parent Agreement.

12.2 The Supplier shall maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Customer Data against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure, and shall notify the Customer without undue delay on becoming aware of a security incident affecting Customer Data.

13.1 Each party's liability under or in connection with this SOW is governed by the liability provisions of the Parent Agreement.

13.2 Each party's indemnification obligations in connection with this SOW are as set out in the Parent Agreement, which apply to the Services and Deliverables as if set out in full in this SOW.
