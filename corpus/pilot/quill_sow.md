# Statement of Work No. 1

This Statement of Work ("**SOW**") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE}} (the "**Effective Date**") between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer headquartered in the United States ("**Customer**"), and **Quill Staffing Group Ltd** ("**Supplier**"). Each is a "**party**" and together the "**parties**".

## 1. Term, Fees and Payment

1.1 **Term.** This SOW commences on the Effective Date and continues until {{EXPIRY}} (the "**Initial Term**", being twelve (12) months), unless terminated earlier in accordance with this SOW.

1.2 **No automatic renewal.** This SOW does not renew automatically. Any extension of the term requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

1.3 **Fees.** The annual fees for the Services are USD 780,000 (the "**Fees**"). The total contract value of this SOW is USD 780,000. The Fees are inclusive of all Supplier costs and expenses unless the parties agree otherwise in writing in advance.

1.4 **Invoicing and payment.** Supplier shall invoice the Fees in accordance with the schedule agreed in writing by the parties. Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt of a valid invoice. A valid invoice must identify this SOW, the Services performed and the amount due. Customer shall notify Supplier promptly of any good-faith dispute and the parties shall work together to resolve it; Customer shall pay any undisputed portion in accordance with this clause.

1.5 **Taxes.** The Fees exclude applicable sales, use, value-added and similar taxes, which shall be separately stated on the invoice and borne by Customer, other than taxes on Supplier's income, property or employees.

## 2. Services

2.1 Supplier shall provide the staffing, recruitment and related resourcing services reasonably required by Customer in connection with its retail operations and technology functions, as requested by Customer in writing from time to time (the "**Services**"), and shall assign suitably qualified personnel ("**Personnel**").

2.2 Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with good industry practice and applicable law, and shall use reasonable efforts to meet any timescales agreed in writing.

2.3 Customer shall provide timely access to information, premises, systems and decision-makers reasonably required for the Services.

2.4 Personnel remain engaged by Supplier. Nothing in this SOW creates an employment relationship between Customer and any Personnel. Supplier is responsible for the pay, benefits, tax withholdings, insurance and statutory obligations relating to Personnel, and shall ensure that Personnel are legally entitled to work in the location of performance.

2.5 Supplier shall remove and replace any Personnel reasonably objected to by Customer, acting in good faith, promptly after notice.

2.6 Changes to the Services require a written change request signed by both parties.

## 3. Confidentiality

3.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other party that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential ("**Confidential Information**"), shall use it only to perform or receive the Services, and shall not disclose it except to its employees, contractors and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations.

3.2 These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no breach of this SOW, was lawfully known to or independently developed by the recipient, or is lawfully received from a third party without restriction. A party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, after giving the other party prompt notice where lawful.

3.3 This clause survives termination or expiry of this SOW.

## 4. Intellectual Property

4.1 Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property rights.

4.2 All work product, deliverables and materials created by Supplier or Personnel specifically for Customer in performing the Services ("**Deliverables**") are owned by Customer upon payment of the applicable Fees. Supplier assigns, and shall procure that Personnel assign, all rights in the Deliverables to Customer.

4.3 Supplier grants Customer a non-exclusive, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use any Supplier pre-existing materials incorporated in the Deliverables to the extent necessary to use the Deliverables.

## 5. Data Protection

5.1 Each party shall comply with all data protection and privacy laws applicable to its processing of personal information in connection with this SOW.

5.2 To the extent Supplier processes personal information on behalf of Customer, Supplier shall process it only on Customer's documented instructions, shall implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures, shall ensure that persons authorised to process it are bound by confidentiality, and shall assist Customer in responding to lawful requests from individuals and regulators.

5.3 Supplier shall notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of any unauthorised access to, or loss of, personal information relating to Customer, and shall, on termination or expiry, return or delete such information on Customer's request, except where retention is required by law.

## 6. Warranties

6.1 Each party warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this SOW.

6.2 Supplier warrants that (a) the Services will be performed in accordance with clause 2.2; (b) the Deliverables will not, to its knowledge, infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party; and (c) it and the Personnel will comply with Customer's reasonable site, security and conduct policies notified in advance.

6.3 Except as expressly stated in this SOW, all warranties and conditions, whether express or implied, including implied warranties of merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose, are excluded to the fullest extent permitted by law.

## 7. Indemnity

7.1 Supplier shall defend and indemnify Customer against all third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages and reasonable costs, arising from (a) any allegation that the Deliverables infringe a third party's intellectual property rights; (b) any claim by Personnel relating to their employment or engagement, or any failure by Supplier to meet its obligations under clause 2.4; or (c) Supplier's gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

7.2 The indemnified party shall give prompt notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying party sole control of the defence and settlement (provided no settlement imposes liability on the indemnified party without its consent, not to be unreasonably withheld), and give reasonable assistance at the indemnifying party's cost.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

8.1 Subject to clause 8.3, each party's aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this SOW, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, is limited to one (1) times the Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

8.2 Subject to clause 8.3, neither party is liable for any indirect, consequential or special loss, or for loss of profits, revenue or goodwill, however arising.

8.3 Nothing in this SOW limits or excludes liability for fraud, death or personal injury caused by negligence, wilful misconduct, or any liability that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded.

## 9. Termination

9.1 Customer may terminate this SOW without cause on fourteen (14) days' written notice to Supplier.

9.2 Either party may terminate this SOW immediately by written notice if the other party commits a material breach that is incapable of remedy or, if capable of remedy, is not remedied within a reasonable period stated in a written notice requiring remedy, or if the other party becomes insolvent, enters administration, liquidation or bankruptcy, or ceases to carry on business.

9.3 On termination or expiry, Customer shall pay all undisputed Fees for Services properly performed up to the effective date of termination, and each party shall return or delete the other's Confidential Information on request. Clauses 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10 and 11 and any accrued rights survive.

## 10. General

10.1 **Governing law.** This SOW and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York.

10.2 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign or subcontract its rights or obligations under this SOW without the other's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of any approved subcontractor.

10.3 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, recognised courier or email with confirmation of transmission to the address most recently notified by the receiving party, and are deemed received on delivery or, for email, on the next business day.

10.4 **Independent contractors.** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this SOW creates a partnership, agency or joint venture.

10.5 **Force majeure.** Neither party is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other promptly and uses reasonable efforts to mitigate the effects.

10.6 **Entire agreement.** This SOW is the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, representations and understandings. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.

10.7 **Severability and waiver.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of it.

10.8 **Counterparts.** This SOW may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
