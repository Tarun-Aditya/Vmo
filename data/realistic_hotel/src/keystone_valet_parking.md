**VALET PARKING AND GARAGE MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT**

**Seabright Harbor Hotel, Boston, Massachusetts**

THIS VALET PARKING AND GARAGE MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of December 15, 2025, and is effective as of January 1, 2026 (the "Commencement Date"), by and between **SEABRIGHT HOTELS & RESORTS INC**, a Delaware corporation ("Hotel Company"), as owner and operator of the Seabright Harbor Hotel located at 1 Harbor Way, Boston, Massachusetts 02210 (the "Hotel"), and **KEYSTONE VALET & PARKING LLC**, a Massachusetts limited liability company having its principal place of business at 310 Dorchester Avenue, South Boston, Massachusetts 02127 ("Operator").

## ARTICLE I. RECITALS

1.1 The Hotel includes a three-level below-grade parking garage of 140 spaces (the "Garage"), a covered porte-cochère and a valet drop-off lane on Harbor Way (together with the Garage, the "Parking Facilities").

1.2 Hotel Company wishes to engage Operator to provide valet parking services for hotel guests, restaurant patrons and event attendees, and to manage the Garage, and Operator has represented that it holds all licences required by the City of Boston to do so, including a valet parking permit issued by the Boston Transportation Department and an open-air parking licence issued by the Boston Licensing Board.

## ARTICLE II. SERVICES

2.1 **Valet services.** Operator shall provide valet parking at the porte-cochère twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, with staffing at the minimum levels in Exhibit 1, and additional staffing for banquets and events as requested by the Hotel's Director of Rooms Operations with at least seventy-two (72) hours' notice.

2.2 **Garage management.** Operator shall operate the Garage, including the parking access and revenue control system owned by Hotel Company, issue and validate tickets, manage monthly parking permits for Hotel employees, keep the Garage clean and free of debris and snow at its entrances, and report any defect in lighting, CCTV, gates or the structure to the Hotel's Director of Engineering within one (1) hour of discovery.

2.3 **Service standards.** Operator shall:

(a) retrieve a guest's vehicle within ten (10) minutes of request between 6:00 a.m. and 11:00 p.m., and within fifteen (15) minutes at other times, for at least ninety-five percent (95%) of retrievals each month, as recorded by the electronic valet ticketing system;

(b) greet arriving guests within two (2) minutes of arrival at the porte-cochère;

(c) perform a documented walk-around damage inspection of every vehicle on intake, with time-stamped photographs uploaded to the ticketing system;

(d) keep the drop-off lane clear so that it does not obstruct traffic on Harbor Way, in compliance with the conditions of the valet permit; and

(e) achieve an average score of at least 4.5 out of 5 for "valet and parking" in the Hotel's guest-satisfaction surveys in each calendar quarter.

2.4 **Electric vehicles.** Operator shall manage the twelve (12) electric vehicle charging spaces in the Garage, move vehicles off chargers once charged, and record charging sessions for billing to guests.

## ARTICLE III. PERSONNEL

3.1 **Employees.** All valet attendants, cashiers and supervisors (the "Personnel") shall be employees of Operator and not of Hotel Company. Operator is solely responsible for hiring, training, supervising, scheduling, paying and, where required, terminating the Personnel, and for all wages, overtime, payroll taxes, benefits and workers' compensation.

3.2 **Qualifications.** Each valet attendant shall be at least twenty-one (21) years of age, hold a valid driver's licence with no more than two (2) moving violations in the preceding three (3) years, and pass a background check and a motor vehicle record check before first assignment and annually thereafter. Operator shall train all Personnel in the Hotel's service standards, manual-transmission and electric-vehicle handling, and accident reporting.

3.3 **Manager.** Operator shall assign a full-time on-site manager, approved by Hotel Company, who shall attend the Hotel's daily operations meeting.

3.4 **Conduct; removal.** Personnel shall wear uniforms approved by Hotel Company and shall comply with the Hotel's policies on conduct, drugs and alcohol, and guest privacy. Hotel Company may require Operator to remove any member of Personnel from the Hotel for any lawful reason.

3.5 **Labour compliance.** Operator shall comply with the Massachusetts Wage Act, the Massachusetts Earned Sick Time Law, the Fair Labor Standards Act and all other employment laws. Operator shall distribute gratuities received by Personnel in accordance with the Massachusetts Tips Act, M.G.L. c. 149, § 152A.

## ARTICLE IV. REVENUE, RATES AND REPORTING

4.1 **Revenue belongs to Hotel Company.** All parking and valet charges collected from guests, patrons and monthly parkers (the "Gross Receipts") are the property of Hotel Company. Operator collects them as Hotel Company's agent.

4.2 **Rates.** Parking rates are set by Hotel Company. The rates in effect at the Commencement Date are: overnight valet US$62 per night (charged to the guest's room folio), daytime valet US$24 for up to three (3) hours and US$42 for up to twelve (12) hours, and restaurant validation US$15 for up to three (3) hours.

4.3 **Cash and card handling.** Operator shall deposit cash receipts daily into Hotel Company's designated bank account and shall process card payments only through Hotel Company's payment terminals. Operator shall not store or record payment card numbers.

4.4 **Reports.** Operator shall deliver a daily revenue and occupancy report by 9:00 a.m. the following day, and a monthly operating statement within ten (10) days after the end of each month, showing Gross Receipts, ticket counts, average retrieval times, damage claims and Reimbursable Expenses.

4.5 **Audit.** Hotel Company may audit Operator's records relating to the Parking Facilities at any time on forty-eight (48) hours' notice. Any shortfall in Gross Receipts identified by an audit shall be paid by Operator within ten (10) days, together with interest at one percent (1%) per month, and, if the shortfall exceeds one percent (1%) of Gross Receipts for the audited period, the cost of the audit.

## ARTICLE V. COMPENSATION

5.1 **Management fee.** Hotel Company shall pay Operator a fixed management fee of six thousand five hundred US dollars (US$6,500) per month (the "Management Fee").

5.2 **Reimbursable Expenses.** Hotel Company shall reimburse Operator monthly for the following costs actually incurred in operating the Parking Facilities, without mark-up, in accordance with the annual operating budget approved by Hotel Company (the "Reimbursable Expenses"): Personnel wages and payroll taxes and benefits at the rates in Exhibit 2; uniforms; ticketing system subscription; and the garage-keepers legal liability insurance premium. The approved budget for Reimbursable Expenses for calendar year 2026 is US$548,000. Operator shall not exceed any budget line by more than five percent (5%) without Hotel Company's prior written approval.

5.3 **Incentive fee.** Hotel Company shall pay Operator an annual incentive fee equal to three percent (3%) of the amount by which Gross Receipts for calendar year 2026 exceed US$1,450,000, provided that Operator has met the retrieval standard in Section 2.3(a) in at least ten (10) of the twelve (12) months.

5.4 **Invoicing and payment.** Operator shall invoice the Management Fee and Reimbursable Expenses monthly in arrears, with payroll registers and receipts attached. Hotel Company shall pay each correct invoice within fifteen (15) days after receipt.

5.5 **Set-off.** Hotel Company may set off against amounts due to Operator any shortfall in Gross Receipts and any damage claim payable by Operator under Article VII that Operator has not paid when due.

## ARTICLE VI. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 **Term.** The term of this Agreement begins on the Commencement Date and ends on December 31, 2026 (the "Term"), unless terminated earlier. This Agreement does not renew automatically. Any extension must be agreed in writing by both parties.

6.2 **Holdover.** If Operator continues to provide the services after the end of the Term at Hotel Company's request and without a written extension, it does so month to month on the terms of this Agreement, and either party may end the holdover on thirty (30) days' written notice.

6.3 **Termination without cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, without cause and without penalty, by giving the other party at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice.

6.4 **Termination for cause.** Hotel Company may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if: (a) Operator's valet permit or parking licence is suspended or revoked; (b) Operator fails to maintain the insurance required by Article VIII; (c) Operator or any of its Personnel commits theft, fraud or misappropriation of Gross Receipts or of a guest's property; or (d) Operator breaches any other provision of this Agreement and does not cure the breach within ten (10) days after written notice.

6.5 **Transition.** On expiry or termination, Operator shall return all tickets, keys, key-control cabinets, access cards, reports and records, and shall cooperate in transferring the services to Hotel Company or a successor operator without interruption, including allowing the successor to offer employment to Personnel. Operator shall pay all final wages and accrued benefits to its Personnel as required by law.

## ARTICLE VII. DAMAGE TO VEHICLES; CLAIMS

7.1 **Responsibility.** Operator is responsible for all loss of or damage to vehicles, and to their contents where listed on the valet ticket, while in the care, custody or control of Operator or its Personnel, from the time the vehicle is accepted at the porte-cochère until it is returned to the guest, including damage caused by theft, collision, fire or negligent handling.

7.2 **Claims process.** Operator shall acknowledge a guest's damage claim within twenty-four (24) hours, and shall resolve every claim of US$2,500 or less within fourteen (14) days. Operator shall not deny a claim without first consulting the Hotel's Director of Rooms Operations, and shall report all claims to Hotel Company monthly. Operator shall not require any guest to sign a release of the Hotel as a condition of payment.

7.3 **Ticket disclaimers.** No disclaimer or limitation of liability printed on a valet ticket, sign or receipt shall limit Operator's responsibility to Hotel Company or to any guest.

## ARTICLE VIII. INSURANCE

8.1 Operator shall obtain and keep in force throughout the Term, at its own cost, the following insurance, with insurers licensed in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts and rated A- VIII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) Commercial general liability insurance, including contractual liability, with limits of not less than US$1,000,000 per occurrence and US$2,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) Garage-keepers legal liability insurance on a direct primary basis, covering vehicles in Operator's care, custody and control, with limits of not less than US$1,000,000 per location and US$250,000 per vehicle, and a deductible of not more than US$2,500;

(c) Business automobile liability insurance, including hired and non-owned autos, with a combined single limit of not less than US$1,000,000;

(d) Umbrella liability insurance with limits of not less than US$5,000,000 per occurrence;

(e) Workers' compensation insurance as required by M.G.L. c. 152, and employer's liability insurance with limits of not less than US$500,000 per accident;

(f) Crime insurance, including employee dishonesty and money and securities, of not less than US$250,000; and

(g) Employment practices liability insurance of not less than US$1,000,000.

8.2 Hotel Company, its owners, lenders and managers shall be named as additional insureds under the policies in Sections 8.1(a), (c) and (d). All policies shall be primary to any insurance carried by Hotel Company and shall include a waiver of subrogation. Operator shall deliver certificates of insurance and endorsements before the Commencement Date and within ten (10) days of each policy renewal.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY

9.1 **Indemnity.** Operator shall indemnify, defend (with counsel reasonably acceptable to Hotel Company) and hold harmless Hotel Company, the owner of the Hotel, and their respective affiliates, officers, directors, employees and agents from and against any and all claims, demands, suits, losses, damages, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or related to: (a) the services or the use of the Parking Facilities by Operator or its Personnel; (b) loss of or damage to any vehicle or its contents as described in Article VII; (c) bodily injury or death of any person or damage to any property caused by the acts or omissions of Operator or its Personnel; (d) any claim by Personnel for wages, gratuities, benefits or discrimination, or any claim that Hotel Company is the employer or joint employer of any Personnel; (e) any violation of law or of the valet permit by Operator or its Personnel; or (f) any breach of this Agreement by Operator.

9.2 **No limitation.** Operator's obligations under this Article IX and Article VII are not limited by the amount or type of insurance required by Article VIII, and no limitation of liability applies to Operator under this Agreement.

9.3 **Hotel Company.** Hotel Company shall indemnify Operator against third-party claims for bodily injury to the extent caused by a structural defect in the Garage of which Hotel Company had written notice and failed to repair within a reasonable time. Hotel Company shall not be liable to Operator for lost profits or consequential damages.

## ARTICLE X. GUEST INFORMATION AND CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 Operator shall keep confidential all guest names, room numbers, licence plate numbers, vehicle descriptions and arrival and departure times ("Guest Information"), use it only to provide the services, and not disclose it to anyone, including law enforcement, except as required by law (in which case Operator shall first notify the Hotel's General Manager unless prohibited).

10.2 Operator shall retain valet ticket records and vehicle photographs for twelve (12) months and then delete them, unless they relate to an open claim.

10.3 Operator shall comply with Massachusetts data security regulations (201 CMR 17.00) with respect to any personal information it handles, and shall notify Hotel Company within twenty-four (24) hours of any unauthorised access to Guest Information.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

11.1 **Independent contractor.** Operator is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a lease, licence of real property, partnership or joint venture.

11.2 **Assignment.** Operator shall not assign this Agreement or subcontract any of the services without Hotel Company's prior written consent. A change of control of Operator is an assignment for this purpose. Hotel Company may assign this Agreement to any purchaser, lessee or manager of the Hotel.

11.3 **Permits and taxes.** Operator shall obtain and keep current, at its cost, all permits and licences required for the services, and shall collect and remit on Hotel Company's behalf the City of Boston parking excise where applicable.

11.4 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered personally, by nationally recognised overnight courier, or by certified mail, to: Hotel Company, at the Hotel, Attention: General Manager, with a copy to Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc, 200 Atlantic Avenue, Suite 1400, Boston, MA 02110, Attention: General Counsel; and Operator, at its address above, Attention: Managing Member.

11.5 **Governing law; jurisdiction.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts. Any action relating to this Agreement shall be brought in the Superior Court for Suffolk County or the United States District Court for the District of Massachusetts.

11.6 **Waiver of jury trial.** EACH PARTY KNOWINGLY AND VOLUNTARILY WAIVES ITS RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

11.7 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, with its Exhibits, is the entire agreement of the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes the Valet Parking Agreement between the parties dated December 1, 2022, which expired on December 31, 2025.

11.8 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
