## YIELDLINE SUBSCRIPTION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Subscription Services Agreement (the "Agreement") governs Customer's use of the Yieldline services ordered on an Order Form. The Agreement takes effect on the date of the first Order Form signed by both parties.

## 1. Definitions

"**Authorized Users**" means Customer's and its Covered Properties' employees and contractors whom Customer permits to use the Services. "**Customer Data**" means all data submitted to the Services by or for Customer, including reservation, rate, inventory and guest stay data imported from Customer's property management system. "**Documentation**" means Yieldline's then-current user guides and technical specifications. "**Services**" means the subscription services identified in an Order Form. "**Subscription Year**" means each twelve (12) month period beginning on the Subscription Start Date.

## 2. Services

2.1 **Provision.** Yieldline shall make the Services available to Customer during the Subscription Term in accordance with this Agreement, the Documentation and the Service Level Agreement in Exhibit B.

2.2 **Recommendations.** The Services generate pricing and inventory recommendations. Customer remains responsible for all rates it publishes. If Customer enables automated publishing, the Services shall respect the minimum and maximum rates and the rules Customer configures for each property, and Yieldline is responsible for errors in published rates to the extent caused by a defect in the Services.

2.3 **Restrictions.** Customer shall not (a) resell or sublicense the Services to anyone other than its Covered Properties; (b) reverse engineer the Services; or (c) use the Services to build a competing product.

2.4 **Changes.** Yieldline may update the Services from time to time, provided that it does not materially reduce the functionality of the subscribed Services during the Subscription Term.

## 3. Customer Responsibilities

Customer shall (a) be responsible for Authorized Users' compliance with this Agreement; (b) keep its login credentials secure and enable single sign-on where available; (c) provide the property management system access and data feeds described in the Documentation; and (d) obtain any notices and consents required by law for Yieldline to process Customer Data as described in this Agreement.

## 4. Fees and Payment

4.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay the fees set out in each Order Form. Except as expressly stated in this Agreement or an Order Form, fees are non-cancellable and non-refundable, and the quantities purchased cannot be decreased during the Subscription Term.

4.2 **Invoicing and payment.** Yieldline shall invoice fees in accordance with the Order Form. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after the invoice date. Invoices must quote Customer's purchase order number.

4.3 **Late payment; suspension.** Undisputed amounts more than thirty (30) days overdue bear interest at the lesser of 1.0% per month and the highest rate permitted by law. Yieldline may suspend the Services if undisputed amounts are more than sixty (60) days overdue and Yieldline has given Customer at least fifteen (15) days' written notice, sent to Customer's billing contact and to its Vice President, Procurement.

4.4 **Taxes.** Fees exclude taxes. Customer is responsible for sales, use and similar taxes, other than taxes on Yieldline's income.

## 5. Proprietary Rights and Customer Data

5.1 **Yieldline's rights.** Yieldline and its licensors own all rights in the Services, the Documentation and their underlying technology, including all improvements. No rights are granted except as stated in this Agreement.

5.2 **Customer Data.** As between the parties, Customer owns all Customer Data. Customer grants Yieldline a limited licence to host, copy and process Customer Data only as needed to provide the Services and as permitted by Exhibit C.

5.3 **Aggregated data.** Yieldline may use Customer Data in de-identified and aggregated form, combined with data of other customers so that neither Customer, any Covered Property nor any individual can be identified, to improve its forecasting models and to publish market benchmarks. Yieldline shall not disclose Customer's rates, occupancy or revenue for any individual property to any third party.

5.4 **Return of data.** For sixty (60) days after the end of the Subscription Term, Yieldline shall make Customer Data available for export in CSV format at no charge, and shall then delete it from its systems (including backups, within a further ninety (90) days) and certify the deletion on request.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care, use it only to perform or exercise rights under this Agreement, and disclose it only to employees, contractors and advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective. "Confidential Information" includes Customer Data, pricing and the terms of each Order Form. These obligations continue for five (5) years after termination, and indefinitely for trade secrets and Customer Data.

## 7. Security and Data Protection

7.1 **Security programme.** Yieldline shall maintain a written information security programme with administrative, physical and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature of Customer Data, including encryption of Customer Data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256), role-based access control, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, vulnerability scanning, and an annual third-party penetration test.

7.2 **Certification.** Yieldline shall maintain a SOC 2 Type II report covering the Services, and shall provide the current report to Customer each year under confidentiality.

7.3 **Personal data.** The Customer Data includes personal information about hotel guests (name, email address, loyalty number, stay dates, room type, rate paid and booking channel). Yieldline shall process that personal information only as Customer's service provider and processor in accordance with the Data Processing Addendum in Exhibit C, which forms part of this Agreement.

7.4 **Payment card data.** The Services do not require, and Customer shall not submit, payment card numbers. Yieldline shall configure the property management system interface to exclude payment card fields.

7.5 **Security incidents.** Yieldline shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident affecting Customer Data, and shall provide the information Customer reasonably needs to meet its own notification obligations.

## 8. Warranties

8.1 Yieldline warrants that (a) the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation; and (b) it will not materially decrease the security of the Services during the Subscription Term. Customer's remedy for breach of this warranty is for Yieldline to correct the non-conformity, and if Yieldline cannot do so within thirty (30) days, Customer may terminate the affected Order Form and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the remainder of the Subscription Term.

8.2 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY PROVIDED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED. YIELDLINE DOES NOT WARRANT THAT ANY PRICING RECOMMENDATION WILL ACHIEVE ANY PARTICULAR REVENUE OUTCOME.

## 9. Term and Termination

9.1 **Term.** This Agreement continues while any Order Form remains in effect.

9.2 **Renewal.** Each Subscription Term shall automatically renew for successive renewal terms of twelve (12) months unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

9.3 **Termination for cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement or an Order Form on written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving notice of it, or if the other party becomes the subject of a petition in bankruptcy or any proceeding relating to insolvency.

9.4 **Chronic SLA failure.** Customer may terminate the affected Order Form on thirty (30) days' notice if Monthly Availability falls below 99.0% in any three (3) months in a rolling twelve (12) month period.

9.5 **Refund on termination.** If Customer terminates for cause under Section 9.3 or 9.4, Yieldline shall refund any prepaid fees covering the remainder of the Subscription Term after the termination date. In no other case will termination relieve Customer of its obligation to pay the fees for the Subscription Term.

9.6 **Survival.** Sections 4, 5, 6, 10, 11, 12 and 13 survive termination.

## 10. Indemnification

10.1 **By Yieldline.** Yieldline shall defend Customer against any claim by a third party alleging that the Services infringe or misappropriate that third party's intellectual property rights, and shall pay any damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement. If the Services become subject to such a claim, Yieldline may procure the right for Customer to continue using them, modify them to be non-infringing, or terminate the affected subscription and refund prepaid fees for the remaining term.

10.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend Yieldline against any claim by a third party alleging that Customer Data, or Customer's use of the Services in breach of this Agreement, infringes that third party's rights or violates applicable law.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified party shall notify the indemnifying party promptly of the claim, give it sole control of the defence and settlement (provided no settlement imposes liability on the indemnified party without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 **Exclusion of damages.** IN NO EVENT WILL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR ANY LOST PROFITS, LOST REVENUE, LOSS OF GOODWILL, OR INDIRECT, SPECIAL, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, HOWEVER CAUSED, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THEIR POSSIBILITY.

11.2 **Cap.** EXCEPT AS SET OUT IN SECTION 11.3, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE FIRST EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY.

11.3 **Exceptions.** The cap in Section 11.2 does not apply to (a) a party's indemnification obligations under Section 10; (b) Customer's obligation to pay fees; or (c) liability for gross negligence, fraud or wilful misconduct. Yieldline's total aggregate liability for breach of Section 7 (Security and Data Protection) or Exhibit C, including the reasonable costs of notifying affected individuals, call-centre support, credit monitoring and regulatory fines to the extent permitted by law, shall not exceed two (2) times the total fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the incident, in place of the cap in Section 11.2.

## 12. Insurance

Yieldline shall maintain during the term: commercial general liability insurance of US$1,000,000 per occurrence; technology errors and omissions insurance of US$5,000,000 per claim; cyber liability (network security and privacy) insurance of US$5,000,000 per claim; and workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

Customer may also assign its rights for a Covered Property to the purchaser or new manager of that property.

**C.2 Instructions.** Yieldline shall process Personal Information only to provide the Services, on Customer's documented instructions, and shall not sell or share Personal Information, retain, use or disclose it for any other purpose, or combine it with personal information it receives from other sources, except as permitted for service providers under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CCPA) and other applicable U.S. state privacy laws.

**C.3 Sub-processors.** Yieldline's current sub-processors are its cloud hosting provider (data centres in the United States) and its customer-support ticketing provider. Yieldline shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' notice of any new sub-processor, and Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds. Yieldline remains liable for its sub-processors.

**C.4 Location.** Personal Information shall be stored and processed in the United States only.

**C.5 Assistance.** Yieldline shall assist Customer, at no additional charge, in responding to requests from individuals to access, correct or delete their Personal Information, and with data protection assessments.

**C.6 Retention.** Personal Information older than thirty-six (36) months shall be de-identified in the Services' production database, except where Customer instructs otherwise.

**C.7 Audit.** Customer may, once a year, review Yieldline's SOC 2 Type II report and request reasonable additional information to verify compliance with this Exhibit C; an on-site audit is permitted only following a Security Incident or where required by a regulator.

**C.8 Security Incidents.** Section 7.5 of the Agreement applies.
