**ADIRONDACK MECHANICAL SERVICES, INC.**
**MECHANICAL SYSTEMS PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT**

Agreement No. AMS-PM-24-0217 | Customer Account: SEA-LKP-01

This Mechanical Systems Preventive Maintenance Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made effective as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") between **Adirondack Mechanical Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation, 75 Industrial Park Road, Saranac Lake, New York 12983 ("**Contractor**"), and **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc**, a Delaware corporation, 200 Atlantic Avenue, Suite 1400, Boston, Massachusetts 02110, as owner and operator of the **Seabright Lakeside Resort**, 2600 Saranac Avenue, Lake Placid, New York 12946 (the "**Premises**") ("**Owner**").

## 1. Purpose

Owner wishes to engage Contractor to inspect, maintain and repair the heating, ventilation, air-conditioning, refrigeration and building automation equipment at the Premises listed in Schedule A (the "**Covered Equipment**"), so as to keep the Covered Equipment in safe and reliable operating condition, minimise unplanned downtime during guest occupancy, and preserve manufacturers' warranties. Contractor agrees to provide those services on the terms of this Agreement.

## 2. Scope of Services

2.1 **Preventive maintenance.** Contractor shall perform the scheduled preventive maintenance tasks in Schedule B at the frequencies stated there, following the equipment manufacturers' recommendations and ASHRAE/ACCA Standard 180 (Standard Practice for Inspection and Maintenance of Commercial Building HVAC Systems). This includes:

(a) two (2) seasonal start-ups and shut-downs per year of the two (2) 350-ton centrifugal chillers and the cooling tower, including eddy-current tube testing of each chiller once during the Initial Term;

(b) quarterly inspection of the three (3) gas-fired boilers, including combustion analysis and safety-control testing, and the annual internal inspection required for the New York State boiler certificate;

(c) monthly inspection, filter replacement and belt adjustment for the twelve (12) air-handling units and the two (2) make-up air units serving the kitchens;

(d) semi-annual service of the 226 guest-room packaged terminal air conditioners (PTACs) and fan coil units, with the work scheduled to take no more than twenty (20) rooms out of inventory on any one day;

(e) quarterly service of the walk-in coolers, walk-in freezers and ice machines in the main kitchen, banquet kitchen and the Boathouse restaurant;

(f) quarterly water treatment testing and chemical supply for the chilled water, condenser water and heating hot water loops; and

(g) software support, backups and alarm-point verification for the building automation system (BAS).

2.2 **Repairs.** Contractor shall repair or replace any Covered Equipment component that fails because of normal wear and tear. Labour for such repairs during Regular Hours is included in the Annual Contract Price. Parts and materials for covered repairs are included up to US$2,500 per occurrence; Owner shall pay for parts above that amount at Contractor's cost plus fifteen percent (15%), with Owner's prior written approval of any single repair estimated to exceed US$5,000.

2.3 **Exclusions.** The Annual Contract Price does not cover: replacement of a chiller compressor, boiler heat exchanger, cooling-tower fill, or any complete unit; ductwork, piping or electrical work beyond the unit disconnect; repairs needed because of misuse, vandalism, flood, freezing caused by Owner's failure to maintain heat, or work by others; refrigerant above ten (10) pounds per occurrence; and code upgrades required by a change in law. Contractor shall quote excluded work separately at the rates in Schedule C.

2.4 **Refrigerant management.** Contractor shall handle refrigerants in accordance with Section 608 of the Clean Air Act and 40 C.F.R. Part 82, using EPA-certified technicians, and shall keep the leak-rate records required for appliances with a charge of fifty (50) pounds or more.

## 3. Response Times and Service Levels

3.1 Contractor shall provide 24-hour, 7-day emergency service. Contractor shall respond to service calls placed through its dispatch line or customer portal as follows:

| Priority | Examples | On-site response |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency | Loss of heating or cooling to the building or a guest floor; loss of refrigeration in a walk-in; gas or refrigerant leak; boiler lockout | Technician on site within four (4) hours, 24/7 |
| Urgent | Single air-handling unit down; kitchen hood make-up air down; ice machine down | Technician on site within eight (8) hours |
| Routine | Individual guest-room PTAC or fan coil fault; noise; thermostat | Next Business Day |

3.2 **Guest-room units.** Contractor shall restore an individual guest-room unit to service, or replace it with a loaned unit, within twenty-four (24) hours of the call so that the room can be returned to inventory.

3.3 **Uptime.** Contractor shall keep the central chilled water plant available for at least 99.0% of the hours in each cooling season (May 15 to September 30), excluding scheduled maintenance agreed in advance with Owner's Director of Engineering and outages caused by utility failures.

3.4 **Credits.** For each Emergency call where Contractor's technician arrives more than two (2) hours after the response time, Contractor shall credit Owner US$500. If chiller plant availability falls below 99.0% in a cooling season, Contractor shall credit Owner five percent (5%) of the Annual Contract Price for that year. Credits are applied to the next monthly invoice and do not limit Owner's other remedies.

3.5 **Reporting.** Contractor shall record every visit, task, reading and repair in its computerised maintenance management system and give Owner online access to it. Within ten (10) days after each quarter, Contractor shall deliver a written report listing completed and missed preventive tasks, service calls and their response times, refrigerant added, and recommended capital replacements with budget estimates.

## 4. Personnel and Site Rules

4.1 Contractor shall assign a lead technician holding a New York State-recognised journeyman or equivalent licence and EPA Section 608 Universal certification, who shall be the primary technician for the Premises.

4.2 Contractor's personnel shall check in at the engineering office, wear Contractor's uniform and identification, follow Owner's lock-out/tag-out, hot-work permit and confined-space procedures, and avoid guest areas except as needed for the work. Work in guest rooms shall be coordinated with the Executive Housekeeper.

4.3 Owner may require Contractor to remove from the Premises any person whose conduct Owner reasonably considers unsafe or inappropriate for a hotel environment.

4.4 Contractor shall comply with the Occupational Safety and Health Act and all applicable codes, including the New York State Uniform Fire Prevention and Building Code.

## 5. Price and Payment

5.1 **Annual Contract Price.** Owner shall pay Contractor an annual contract price of one hundred forty-eight thousand eight hundred US dollars (US$148,800) (the "**Annual Contract Price**"), invoiced in twelve (12) equal monthly installments of US$12,400 in advance on the first day of each month.

5.2 **Price adjustment.** The Annual Contract Price is fixed for the Initial Term. For each Renewal Term, Contractor may increase the Annual Contract Price by giving Owner written notice at least ninety (90) days before the start of that Renewal Term, by no more than the lesser of (a) four percent (4%) and (b) the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U), Northeast Region, for the preceding twelve (12) months.

5.3 **Additional work.** Work outside the scope of Section 2 shall be performed only on Owner's written purchase order and charged at the rates in Schedule C.

5.4 **Payment terms.** Owner shall pay each correct invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt. Invoices for additional work must attach the signed service ticket. Amounts not paid when due bear interest at 1% per month. Owner may withhold amounts disputed in good faith on written notice.

5.5 **No other charges.** No fuel surcharge, trip charge, environmental fee or administrative fee shall be added to any invoice unless set out in this Agreement.

## 6. Term and Renewal

6.1 **Initial Term.** The initial term of this Agreement is thirty-six (36) months, beginning on the Effective Date and ending on January 31, 2027 (the "**Initial Term**").

6.2 **Automatic renewal.** This Agreement will automatically renew for successive terms of twelve (12) months each (each a "**Renewal Term**") unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the expiration of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term.

## 7. Termination

7.1 **Termination for convenience by Owner.** Owner may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason, by giving Contractor sixty (60) days' written notice. If Owner terminates under this Section 7.1 during the first twelve (12) months of the Initial Term, Owner shall reimburse Contractor for the unamortised cost of the eddy-current testing if it has been performed, not to exceed US$9,600.

7.2 **Termination for cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party fails to perform a material obligation and does not cure the failure within thirty (30) days after written notice. Without limiting the foregoing, Owner may terminate on fifteen (15) days' notice if Contractor misses the Emergency response time three (3) or more times in any rolling ninety (90) day period.

7.3 **Sale of the Premises.** If Owner sells or transfers the Premises, or engages a new hotel management company, Owner may assign this Agreement to the purchaser or manager under Section 13.2 or terminate it on thirty (30) days' written notice without charge.

7.4 **On termination.** Contractor shall promptly return Owner's keys, access cards, BAS passwords and equipment records, and Owner shall pay for services performed up to the termination date. Prepaid amounts for any period after the termination date shall be refunded within thirty (30) days.

## 8. Warranty

8.1 Contractor warrants that its services will be performed in a good and workmanlike manner by qualified personnel, in accordance with good industry practice. Labour for covered repairs is warranted for ninety (90) days; parts carry the manufacturer's warranty, which Contractor shall pass through to Owner.

8.2 EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION 8, CONTRACTOR MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 9. Insurance

9.1 Contractor shall carry, at its own expense, throughout the Term:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of US$1,000,000 per occurrence and US$2,000,000 aggregate, including products-completed operations and contractual liability;

(b) automobile liability insurance with a combined single limit of US$1,000,000;

(c) umbrella liability insurance of US$5,000,000;

(d) pollution liability insurance (including refrigerant release and Legionella from cooling towers) of US$2,000,000 per claim; and

(e) workers' compensation insurance at statutory limits under the New York Workers' Compensation Law and disability benefits insurance, and employer's liability insurance of US$1,000,000.

9.2 Owner, its lenders and its hotel manager shall be additional insureds on the policies in Sections 9.1(a), (c) and (d), on a primary and non-contributory basis, with a waiver of subrogation. Contractor shall deliver ACORD certificates before starting work and on each renewal.

## 10. Indemnity

10.1 To the fullest extent permitted by law, Contractor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Owner, its manager, lenders, and their officers, employees and agents from and against all claims, damages, losses and expenses, including attorneys' fees, for bodily injury, sickness, death, or damage to property (other than the Covered Equipment itself) to the extent caused by the negligent acts or omissions or wilful misconduct of Contractor, its subcontractors or anyone employed by them.

10.2 This indemnity is limited as required by New York General Obligations Law § 5-322.1 and does not apply to the extent a loss is caused by the negligence of an indemnified party.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 Neither party shall be liable to the other for any special, indirect, incidental or consequential damages, including loss of room revenue, loss of business or loss of use, arising out of this Agreement, except to the extent arising from that party's gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

11.2 Contractor's total liability to Owner for all claims arising out of or relating to this Agreement in any Contract Year, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the Annual Contract Price for that Contract Year. This limit does not apply to Contractor's obligations under Section 10 (Indemnity) for third-party claims, to the extent covered by the insurance required by Section 9.

## 12. Confidentiality and Security

12.1 Contractor shall keep confidential all information about the Premises, its security systems, building plans, guest information and Owner's business that it learns while performing this Agreement, and shall use that information only to perform this Agreement.

12.2 Remote access to the BAS shall be made only through Owner's approved secure remote-access gateway, using named accounts with multi-factor authentication. Contractor shall not install any remote-access device or software on Owner's network without Owner's written approval.

## 13. General Terms

13.1 **Independent contractor; subcontracting.** Contractor is an independent contractor. Contractor shall not subcontract any part of the services other than chiller eddy-current testing and water treatment laboratory analysis without Owner's prior written consent.

13.2 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's written consent, except that Owner may assign it without consent to an affiliate, a purchaser of the Premises, a lender, or a hotel manager engaged for the Premises.

13.3 **Liens.** Contractor shall keep the Premises free of mechanics' liens arising from its work and shall discharge or bond any lien within ten (10) days of notice.

13.4 **Force majeure.** Neither party is responsible for delays caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including severe weather that makes roads impassable, but Contractor shall use reasonable efforts to restore heating to the Premises during a winter storm.

13.5 **Notices.** Notices of non-renewal, termination or breach must be given in writing by courier or certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses in the preamble, and in Owner's case marked for the attention of the Director of Engineering, with a copy to Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc, Attention: Vice President, Procurement. Notices by email are effective only for operational matters.

13.6 **Governing law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York. The parties submit to the jurisdiction of the state courts sitting in Essex County, New York.

13.7 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, together with Schedules A through C, is the complete agreement between the parties regarding the services and supersedes all proposals and prior agreements. Pre-printed terms on Contractor's service tickets or invoices have no effect.

13.8 **Amendments.** This Agreement may be changed only by a written amendment signed by both parties.
