**STATEMENT OF WORK No. 1**

**Public-Area and Night Cleaning, Seabright Harbor Hotel**

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is issued under the Master Services Agreement dated March 1, 2025 between Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc ("**Seabright**") and Meridian Hospitality Cleaning Services LLC ("**Contractor**") (the "**Agreement**"), and is governed by it.

| | |
|---|---|
| Hotel | Seabright Harbor Hotel, 1 Harbor Way, Boston, MA 02210 |
| SOW start date | April 1, 2025 |
| SOW end date | March 31, 2027 |
| Hotel contact | Director of Housekeeping |
| Contractor site manager | Full-time, on site Monday to Friday |

## 1. Scope

Contractor shall clean the lobby, public restrooms, corridors, elevators and elevator lobbies, meeting and ballroom level, fitness centre, pool deck and back-of-house corridors, as follows:

| Area | Frequency | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Lobby, public restrooms, elevators | Continuous day coverage, restrooms checked every 60 minutes | 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. |
| Night cleaning: lobby floors, carpets, restaurant and bar | Nightly | 11:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m. |
| Meeting and ballroom level | Turnover after each event, within 2 hours of the event ending | As scheduled by the Events team |
| Guest corridors (vacuum, spot clean) | Daily | 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. |
| Carpet extraction, all public areas | Quarterly | Night |
| Fitness centre and pool deck | Three times daily | 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. |

## 2. Staffing

Minimum staffing is 6 cleaners on the day shift, 4 on the night shift and 1 supervisor on each shift, plus event staff as scheduled. Contractor shall not reduce staffing without the Hotel's written approval.

## 3. Service Levels

In addition to Section 2.3 of the Agreement: meeting rooms must be ready for the next event within 2 hours of the previous event ending; a public restroom complaint must be attended within 15 minutes; and the monthly inspection score for the lobby and public restrooms must be at least 92%.

## 4. Fees

4.1 The monthly fee is thirty-eight thousand five hundred US dollars (US$38,500), covering the scope and staffing in Sections 1 and 2. The annual fees under this SOW are US$462,000.

4.2 Event staffing beyond the minimum is charged at US$34.00 per person-hour on weekdays and US$41.00 on weekends and holidays.

4.3 Invoicing and payment are as set out in Section 4 of the Agreement.

## 5. Term

This SOW begins on the SOW start date and ends on March 31, 2027. It does not renew automatically; it may be extended by a written change order signed by both parties.

Signed for and on behalf of Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc by Marcus Bell, General Manager, Seabright Harbor Hotel, on March 18, 2025.

Signed for and on behalf of Meridian Hospitality Cleaning Services LLC by Carlos Ibarra, Chief Executive Officer, on March 14, 2025.
