## TERMS AND CONDITIONS

## 1. Services

1.1 Contractor shall provide the collection, transportation and disposal or processing services described in the Service Schedule (the "Services") for non-hazardous solid waste, recyclable materials and source-separated organic materials ("Acceptable Waste") generated at the service address.

1.2 Contractor shall provide the containers listed in the Service Schedule, other than customer-owned equipment, which remain the property of Contractor. Customer shall use them only for Acceptable Waste and is responsible for damage to them beyond normal wear and tear.

1.3 Collections shall take place between 5:00 a.m. and 9:00 a.m., Monday through Saturday, in accordance with the Village of Rosemont's noise ordinance, and shall not obstruct the hotel's guest entrance or shuttle bus lanes.

1.4 **Missed collections.** Contractor shall make up a missed scheduled collection within twenty-four (24) hours after Customer reports it, at no charge. Contractor shall service the compactor within twenty-four (24) hours of Customer's call, and within four (4) hours when the compactor is reported full during a period of high occupancy.

1.5 **Excluded Waste.** Acceptable Waste does not include hazardous waste, medical or infectious waste, radioactive waste, liquids, tyres, electronic waste, or any material regulated under the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act or the Illinois Environmental Protection Act other than as municipal waste ("Excluded Waste"). Title to and liability for Excluded Waste remain with Customer at all times.

## 2. Term and Automatic Renewal

2.1 The initial term of this Agreement is thirty-six (36) months, commencing on the Effective Date and ending on November 30, 2026 (the "Initial Term").

2.2 THIS AGREEMENT SHALL AUTOMATICALLY RENEW FOR SUCCESSIVE RENEWAL TERMS OF THIRTY-SIX (36) MONTHS EACH UNLESS CUSTOMER GIVES CONTRACTOR WRITTEN NOTICE OF TERMINATION BY CERTIFIED MAIL, RETURN RECEIPT REQUESTED, RECEIVED BY CONTRACTOR NOT MORE THAN ONE HUNDRED EIGHTY (180) DAYS AND NOT LESS THAN NINETY (90) DAYS BEFORE THE END OF THE INITIAL TERM OR THE THEN-CURRENT RENEWAL TERM. NOTICE RECEIVED OUTSIDE THAT PERIOD SHALL NOT BE EFFECTIVE.

2.3 Contractor shall send Customer a reminder of the renewal date not less than one hundred twenty (120) days before the end of each term, as required by the Illinois Automatic Contract Renewal Act (815 ILCS 601/10).

## 3. Exclusive Right

Customer grants Contractor the exclusive right to collect and dispose of all Acceptable Waste generated at the service address during the term, except materials Customer donates or sells for reuse (including used cooking oil collected by a rendering company and food donated to charitable organisations).

## 4. Customer Responsibilities

Customer shall provide safe, unobstructed access to the containers on each collection day, keep the compactor in good working order, and separate recyclable materials and organic materials from other waste in accordance with the Contractor's acceptance guidelines. Contractor shall not be liable for damage to pavement or curbs caused by its vehicles in the ordinary course of servicing containers at locations designated by Customer.

## 5. Rates and Charges

5.1 Customer shall pay the rates and charges in the Service Schedule, as adjusted under this Section 5.

5.2 **Annual increase.** Contractor may increase the rates on each anniversary of the Effective Date by the greater of four percent (4%) and the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for Water, Sewer and Trash Collection Services published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics for the preceding twelve (12) months.

5.3 **Pass-through increases.** In addition, Contractor may increase the rates at any time to reflect (a) increases in disposal or processing costs at the facilities Contractor uses; (b) increases in fuel costs (through the FERF, which Contractor may adjust monthly by reference to the U.S. Department of Energy diesel index); and (c) new or increased taxes, fees or surcharges imposed by any governmental authority. Contractor shall show such increases on Customer's invoice.

5.4 **Other increases.** Contractor may increase the rates for any other reason with Customer's consent. Customer's consent may be evidenced orally, in writing, or by Customer's payment of the increased amount.

5.5 **Service changes.** Customer may request changes in container size or collection frequency. Any change shall be priced at Contractor's then-current rates.

5.6 **Recycling rebate.** Contractor shall credit Customer for baled cardboard at fifty percent (50%) of the Chicago-area market price for old corrugated containers published in the month of collection, less a handling charge of US$20.00 per ton.

## 6. Payment

6.1 Contractor shall invoice Customer monthly in advance for fixed monthly services and in arrears for per-haul, per-ton and additional charges.

6.2 Customer shall pay each invoice in full within thirty (30) days after the invoice date.

6.3 Past-due balances are subject to a late charge of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, and an administrative fee of US$15.00 per invoice paid by paper check. If any amount remains unpaid for more than sixty (60) days, Contractor may suspend the Services after giving Customer seven (7) days' notice, and charges shall continue to accrue during suspension.

6.4 Customer must notify Contractor of any disputed charge within ninety (90) days of the invoice date; otherwise the charge is deemed accepted.

## 7. Compliance

7.1 Contractor shall comply with all laws applicable to the Services, including the Illinois Environmental Protection Act and the Village of Rosemont's waste hauler licensing ordinance, and shall deliver Acceptable Waste only to facilities that are permitted to receive it.

7.2 On request, Contractor shall provide Customer with monthly reports of the tonnage of each waste stream collected and the diversion rate achieved, in a form suitable for Customer's sustainability reporting.

## 8. Insurance and Indemnity

8.1 Contractor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of US$1,000,000 per occurrence, automobile liability insurance of US$5,000,000 combined single limit, pollution liability insurance of US$1,000,000, and workers' compensation insurance at statutory limits, and shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

8.2 Contractor shall indemnify and hold harmless Customer from and against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage to the extent caused by Contractor's negligence or wilful misconduct in performing the Services.

8.3 Customer shall indemnify and hold harmless Contractor from and against all claims, losses and expenses arising out of (a) Excluded Waste placed in any container; (b) Customer's breach of this Agreement; or (c) the negligence of Customer, its employees or guests, including damage to containers.

8.4 No limitation of the amount of either party's liability under this Agreement is agreed, except that neither party shall be liable for the other's lost profits.

## 9. Early Termination and Liquidated Damages

9.1 Customer may not terminate this Agreement before the end of the then-current term except for Contractor's material breach under Section 9.3.

9.2 If Customer terminates this Agreement before the end of the then-current term other than under Section 9.3, or if Contractor terminates it for Customer's breach, Customer shall pay Contractor, as liquidated damages and not as a penalty, an amount equal to Customer's average monthly charges over the six (6) months before termination multiplied by six (6), or, if fewer than six (6) months remain in the term, by the number of months remaining. The parties agree that Contractor's actual damages would be difficult to determine and that this amount is a reasonable estimate of them.

9.3 Customer may terminate this Agreement for Contractor's material failure to perform the Services, but only if Customer has notified Contractor in writing of the failure and Contractor has not cured it within fifteen (15) days after receiving the notice. Three (3) or more missed collections in a calendar month that are not made up within twenty-four (24) hours constitute a material failure.

## 10. Miscellaneous

10.1 **Assignment.** Contractor may assign this Agreement to any affiliate or to any purchaser of its business without Customer's consent. Customer may assign this Agreement to a purchaser or operator of the service location on written notice, provided the assignee assumes Customer's obligations in writing.

10.2 **Force majeure.** Contractor shall not be liable for failure to perform caused by weather, labour disputes, road closures, facility closures, acts of government or other causes beyond its reasonable control.

10.3 **Governing law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Illinois. Venue for any action shall lie in the Circuit Court of Cook County, Illinois.

10.4 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, including the Service Schedule, is the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes any prior agreement for the service location. Any change to this Agreement must be in writing, except rate adjustments made under Section 5.

10.5 **Notices.** Notices to Contractor must be sent to its address above, Attention: Contract Administration. Notices to Customer may be sent to its billing address.
