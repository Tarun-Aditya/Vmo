**MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT**

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of November 18, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") between **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc**, a Delaware corporation, 200 Atlantic Avenue, Suite 1400, Boston, Massachusetts 02110 ("**Seabright**"), and **Meridian Hospitality Cleaning Services LLC**, a New York limited liability company, 1180 Avenue of the Americas, 9th Floor, New York, New York 10036 ("**Meridian**").

## 1. Purpose

The parties wish to exchange information to evaluate a possible engagement of Meridian to provide contract cleaning and housekeeping support services at Seabright's hotels (the "**Purpose**").

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one party to the other in connection with the Purpose, in any form, including hotel floor plans, occupancy and staffing data, labour costs, pricing, security procedures and guest information. It does not include information that is or becomes public through no fault of the recipient, that the recipient already lawfully knew, that the recipient receives lawfully from a third party without restriction, or that the recipient develops independently.

## 3. Obligations

Each recipient shall use the other party's Confidential Information only for the Purpose, protect it with at least reasonable care, and disclose it only to its employees and advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by obligations at least as protective as these. A recipient may disclose Confidential Information when required by law, after giving the discloser prompt notice where lawful.

## 4. Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and ends two (2) years later, on November 17, 2026. Each party's obligations for Confidential Information received during the term continue for three (3) years after the end of the term. Either party may end this Agreement earlier on thirty (30) days' written notice.

## 5. Return of Information

On request, or when this Agreement ends, each recipient shall return or destroy the other party's Confidential Information, except copies kept under a routine backup or required by law, which remain subject to this Agreement.

## 6. General

No licence or other right is granted except as stated. Neither party is obliged to enter into any further agreement. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. It may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.

Signed for and on behalf of Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc by Andrea Whitfield, Vice President, Procurement, on November 18, 2024.

Signed for and on behalf of Meridian Hospitality Cleaning Services LLC by Carlos Ibarra, Chief Executive Officer, on November 18, 2024.
