**STATEMENT OF WORK No. 2**

**Supplemental Room Attendants, Seabright Midtown Suites**

This Statement of Work No. 2 (this "**SOW**") is issued under the Master Services Agreement dated March 1, 2025 between Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc ("**Seabright**") and Meridian Hospitality Cleaning Services LLC ("**Contractor**"), and is governed by it.

**Hotel:** Seabright Midtown Suites, 418 West 46th Street, New York, NY 10036
**Effective date:** June 1, 2025
**End date:** May 31, 2027

## 1. Scope

Contractor shall provide room attendants to clean guest suites to the Hotel's standards on days when occupancy exceeds the Hotel's own housekeeping capacity. The Hotel will request attendants by 2:00 p.m. on the previous day through Contractor's scheduling portal, stating the number of attendants and suites. Each attendant is expected to clean 13 suites per eight-hour shift (departures count as 1.5 suites).

## 2. Service Levels

Contractor shall fill at least 95% of requested shifts each month. Suites cleaned by Contractor's attendants must pass the Hotel's room inspection at the same rate as the Hotel's own attendants (at least 92% of inspected suites passing first time). A suite that fails inspection shall be re-cleaned within 30 minutes at no charge.

## 3. Fees

3.1 Room attendants are charged at US$31.50 per hour (US$47.25 per hour on the hotel's recognised holidays), with a minimum shift of six hours. Supervisors are charged at US$38.00 per hour when requested.

3.2 Based on the Hotel's forecast, the estimated annual fees under this SOW are US$318,000. This is an estimate, not a commitment.

3.3 Invoicing and payment are as set out in Section 4 of the Agreement.

## 4. Labour Terms

Contractor confirms that attendants assigned to the Hotel are paid not less than the wage and benefit rates required for hotel room attendants in New York City, and shall provide certified payroll records on request.

## 5. Term

This SOW runs from the effective date to May 31, 2027 and does not renew automatically. Either party may end it on sixty (60) days' written notice.
