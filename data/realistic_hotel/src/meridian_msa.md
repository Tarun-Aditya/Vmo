**MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT**

**Contract Cleaning and Housekeeping Support Services**

This Master Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into effective as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") between **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc**, a Delaware corporation, 200 Atlantic Avenue, Suite 1400, Boston, Massachusetts 02110 ("**Seabright**"), and **Meridian Hospitality Cleaning Services LLC**, a New York limited liability company, 1180 Avenue of the Americas, 9th Floor, New York, New York 10036 ("**Contractor**").

## 1. Structure of this Agreement

1.1 This Agreement sets out the terms on which Contractor will provide contract cleaning, public-area housekeeping, night cleaning and supplemental room-attendant services to the hotels owned or operated by Seabright (each a "**Hotel**").

1.2 Services for a Hotel are ordered by a Statement of Work signed by both parties that refers to this Agreement (a "**SOW**"). Each SOW states the Hotel, the scope, staffing, service levels, fees and term for that Hotel. Each SOW is governed by this Agreement; if a SOW conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement prevails unless the SOW expressly says which section it overrides.

1.3 Nothing in this Agreement obliges Seabright to order any minimum volume of services.

## 2. Services and Standards

2.1 Contractor shall perform the services with trained, uniformed and supervised personnel, to the Hotel's brand cleanliness standards and the cleaning checklists issued by each Hotel's Director of Housekeeping, as updated from time to time.

2.2 Contractor shall supply all cleaning chemicals, equipment and consumables needed for the services, except guest-room amenities and linen, which the Hotel provides. Chemicals must be EPA Safer Choice certified or approved in writing by the Hotel, and Contractor shall keep safety data sheets for every chemical on site.

2.3 **Inspections.** Each Hotel will inspect a sample of the areas cleaned each week using its standard inspection form. Contractor shall achieve an average inspection score of at least 90% in each calendar month. If the score falls below 90% in any month, Contractor shall submit a corrective action plan within five (5) business days; if it falls below 90% in two consecutive months, the Hotel may withhold five percent (5%) of that SOW's monthly fee for the second month and each further month below the standard.

2.4 **Response.** Contractor shall respond to a spill, biohazard or other urgent cleaning request at a Hotel within thirty (30) minutes during staffed hours.

## 3. Personnel

3.1 All personnel are employees of Contractor. Contractor is solely responsible for their recruitment, wages, overtime, benefits, payroll taxes, workers' compensation, immigration (Form I-9) verification and compliance with all employment laws, including the New York Hotel Trades Council agreements where they apply to a Hotel.

3.2 Contractor shall background-check every person assigned to a Hotel before first assignment and shall not assign anyone a Hotel has asked to be removed.

3.3 Contractor shall not solicit for employment any employee of a Hotel during the term and for twelve (12) months afterwards, other than through general advertisements.

## 4. Fees, Invoicing and Payment

4.1 Fees are set out in each SOW. Fees are fixed for the first twelve (12) months of each SOW; afterwards Contractor may increase them once in each twelve-month period by giving sixty (60) days' written notice, by no more than the increase in the applicable state or local minimum wage or three percent (3%), whichever is greater.

4.2 Contractor shall invoice each Hotel monthly in arrears, with time records by position.

4.3 Seabright shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt.

4.4 Seabright may dispute an invoice in good faith by written notice within thirty (30) days of receipt and pay the undisputed part.

## 5. Term and Termination

5.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until February 29, 2028 (the "**Initial Term**"). It then renews automatically for successive one-year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

5.2 Seabright may terminate this Agreement or any SOW, for any reason, on ninety (90) days' written notice to Contractor.

5.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement or a SOW if the other party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice.

5.4 If Seabright sells or ceases to operate a Hotel, Seabright may terminate the SOW for that Hotel on thirty (30) days' notice without charge, or assign it to the purchaser.

5.5 SOWs in force when this Agreement ends continue on the terms of this Agreement until they end.

## 6. Insurance

Contractor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of US$2,000,000 per occurrence and US$4,000,000 aggregate; umbrella liability of US$5,000,000; workers' compensation as required by law and employer's liability of US$1,000,000; crime (employee dishonesty, including third-party property) of US$500,000; and employment practices liability of US$1,000,000. Seabright and each Hotel's owner and manager shall be additional insureds on the general liability and umbrella policies.

## 7. Indemnity

Contractor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seabright, each Hotel and their owners, managers, officers and employees against all claims, losses and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from (a) bodily injury, death or property damage caused by Contractor's personnel; (b) any claim by Contractor's personnel for wages, benefits or discrimination, or that Seabright is their employer or joint employer; (c) theft by Contractor's personnel; or (d) Contractor's breach of law.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

8.1 Neither party is liable for indirect, special or consequential damages or lost profits, except for Contractor's obligations under Section 7 and either party's gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

8.2 Except for Contractor's obligations under Section 7, each party's total liability under this Agreement and all SOWs shall not exceed two (2) times the fees paid or payable under all SOWs in the twelve (12) months before the claim.

## 9. Confidentiality and Guest Privacy

Contractor shall keep confidential all information about the Hotels, their guests and their business, shall not photograph guest rooms or guests, and shall return any guest property found to the Hotel's lost-and-found the same day. The Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between the parties dated November 18, 2024 continues to apply to information exchanged before the Effective Date.

## 10. General

10.1 Contractor shall not subcontract any services without Seabright's prior written consent.

10.2 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. The courts in New York County, New York have exclusive jurisdiction.

10.3 Notices must be in writing and sent by courier or email with confirmation to the addresses above (for Seabright, Attention: Vice President, Procurement).

10.4 This Agreement may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.
