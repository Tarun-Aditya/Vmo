**PRIME VENDOR FOOD DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT**

Agreement No. SHR-FB-2025-004

This Prime Vendor Food Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc**, a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 200 Atlantic Avenue, Suite 1400, Boston, Massachusetts 02110, acting for itself and on behalf of the hotels listed in Schedule 1 (each a "**Hotel**" and together the "**Hotels**") ("**Seabright**"); and

**Northshore Foodservice Distributors LLC**, a Massachusetts limited liability company with its principal office at 41 Commerce Way, Woburn, Massachusetts 01801 ("**Distributor**").

Seabright and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## Recitals

A. Seabright owns and operates full-service, select-service and resort hotels in the United States and purchases food, beverages, disposables and kitchen supplies for its restaurants, banqueting, room service and employee dining operations.

B. Distributor is a broadline foodservice distributor operating temperature-controlled warehouses and a delivery fleet serving the northeastern and midwestern United States.

C. Following a competitive request for proposal issued by Seabright on August 12, 2024 (RFP No. SHR-2024-FB-01), Seabright selected Distributor as its primary ("prime vendor") supplier of the Products for the Hotels on the terms set out below.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Products**" means the food, beverage (excluding alcoholic beverages), chemical, paper, disposable and smallwares items listed in Distributor's product catalogue as updated from time to time, together with any items Distributor sources for a Hotel on a special-order basis.

1.2 "**Core List**" means the list of approximately 1,200 stock-keeping units agreed between the Parties for each Hotel, attached as Exhibit A and reviewed quarterly.

1.3 "**Landed Cost**" means the invoice price paid by Distributor to the manufacturer or grower for a Product, plus inbound freight actually paid by Distributor to bring the Product into Distributor's warehouse, less all off-invoice allowances, promotional allowances and early-payment discounts. Landed Cost excludes any marketing or "brand" allowances that Distributor earns under its national purchasing programmes, which are addressed in Section 5.4.

1.4 "**Fill Rate**" means, for a given period, the number of cases delivered complete and on time divided by the number of cases ordered for delivery on that day, excluding Products a manufacturer has placed on allocation and notified to Seabright in writing.

1.5 "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a federal holiday in the United States.

## 2. Appointment and Scope

2.1 **Appointment.** Seabright appoints Distributor as the prime vendor of Products to the Hotels during the Term. Seabright shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause the Hotels to purchase not less than eighty percent (80%) of their aggregate eligible purchases of Products from Distributor in each Contract Year ("**Volume Commitment**"). Eligible purchases exclude fresh seafood, artisan bakery products, local farm produce purchased under Seabright's sustainability programme, and Products Distributor cannot supply within the Fill Rate standard.

2.2 **No exclusivity.** Nothing in this Agreement prevents a Hotel from purchasing Products from another supplier where Distributor is out of stock, where a local sourcing commitment applies, or where Distributor's price for an item exceeds the price available from another reputable supplier by more than ten percent (10%) for comparable quality and Distributor declines to match that price within five (5) Business Days of written notice.

2.3 **Estimated volume.** The Parties estimate that the Hotels' purchases of Products under this Agreement will be approximately four million two hundred thousand US dollars (US$4,200,000) per Contract Year. This estimate is given for planning purposes only and is not a commitment by Seabright to purchase any minimum dollar amount.

2.4 **Hotels added or removed.** Seabright may add a hotel it acquires or manages to Schedule 1 on thirty (30) days' written notice, and that hotel shall be served at the prices in this Agreement. If Seabright sells, transfers or ceases to operate a Hotel, Seabright may remove that Hotel from Schedule 1 on thirty (30) days' written notice without liability, and the Volume Commitment shall be adjusted accordingly.

## 3. Ordering and Delivery

3.1 **Orders.** Each Hotel shall place orders through Distributor's online ordering platform or electronic data interchange no later than 4:00 p.m. local time on the Business Day before the scheduled delivery day.

3.2 **Delivery schedule.** Distributor shall deliver to each Hotel on the days set out in Schedule 1, between 6:00 a.m. and 10:00 a.m. local time, at the Hotel's receiving dock. Deliveries outside that window require the prior approval of the Hotel's Director of Food & Beverage or Executive Chef.

3.3 **Minimum drop.** The minimum order per scheduled delivery is US$750. Orders below the minimum drop incur a delivery charge of US$65 per delivery.

3.4 **Emergency deliveries.** Distributor shall make a will-call or emergency delivery within four (4) hours of a Hotel's request, at a charge of US$95 per delivery, waived where the emergency is caused by a short-shipment or error by Distributor.

3.5 **Temperature control.** Distributor shall deliver refrigerated Products at or below 41°F (5°C) and frozen Products at or below 0°F (-18°C), and shall provide on request the temperature logs for the delivery vehicle. A Hotel may refuse any Product that is delivered out of temperature, damaged, short-dated (less than seventy percent (70%) of the manufacturer's shelf life remaining) or not as ordered, and Distributor shall credit that Product in full.

3.6 **Title and risk.** Title and risk of loss pass to the Hotel when the Hotel's receiving clerk signs the delivery invoice.

## 4. Service Levels

4.1 Distributor shall meet the following service levels, measured monthly per Hotel:

| Measure | Standard |
|---|---|
| Fill Rate (Core List) | 98.0% or higher |
| On-time delivery (within the delivery window) | 95.0% or higher |
| Invoice accuracy (price and quantity) | 99.0% or higher |
| Credits for refused Products issued | within 5 Business Days |
| Emergency delivery | within 4 hours of request |

4.2 **Service credits.** If Distributor fails to meet the Fill Rate standard at a Hotel in any two (2) consecutive months, Distributor shall credit that Hotel one percent (1%) of the Hotel's purchases for the second month and each further month in which the failure continues. Service credits are a price adjustment and are not Seabright's sole remedy.

4.3 **Business reviews.** Distributor shall provide a monthly service report per Hotel and shall attend a quarterly business review with Seabright's Vice President of Procurement.

## 5. Pricing

5.1 **Cost-plus pricing.** The price of each Product is its Landed Cost plus the mark-up for its category set out below (the "**Mark-Up**"):

| Category | Mark-Up on Landed Cost |
|---|---|
| Dry grocery and canned goods | 8.5% |
| Frozen | 8.5% |
| Dairy and eggs | 7.0% |
| Fresh produce | 11.0% |
| Fresh meat and poultry | 6.5% |
| Chemicals, paper and disposables | 12.0% |
| Smallwares and equipment (special order) | 15.0% |

5.2 **Fixed delivery fee.** In addition to the Mark-Up, Distributor may charge a fuel and delivery fee of US$18.00 per delivery. Distributor may adjust this fee once per Contract Year by no more than the percentage change in the U.S. Energy Information Administration's on-highway diesel price index for New England over the preceding twelve (12) months.

5.3 **Mark-Up fixed.** The Mark-Ups are fixed for the Initial Term. Any change to the Mark-Ups for a Renewal Term must be agreed in writing at least one hundred twenty (120) days before the start of that Renewal Term.

5.4 **Manufacturer allowances.** Distributor shall disclose to Seabright, quarterly, all manufacturer rebates and allowances earned on purchases by the Hotels, and shall pass through to Seabright fifty percent (50%) of those amounts, paid by credit memo within forty-five (45) days after the end of each calendar quarter.

5.5 **Audit.** Seabright, or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality obligations, may audit Distributor's records relating to Landed Cost, Mark-Up and allowances once per Contract Year on fifteen (15) Business Days' notice. If an audit reveals an overcharge, Distributor shall refund the overcharge within thirty (30) days, and if the overcharge exceeds two percent (2%) of the amounts invoiced in the audited period, Distributor shall also bear the reasonable cost of the audit.

## 6. Invoicing and Payment

6.1 **Invoices.** Distributor shall issue an invoice per delivery, referencing the Hotel, the purchase order number and the delivery date, and shall send each Hotel a consolidated weekly statement.

6.2 **Payment terms.** Each Hotel shall pay undisputed amounts within twenty-one (21) days after the invoice date by ACH transfer. Payments made within ten (10) days of the invoice date earn a prompt-payment discount of one-half of one percent (0.5%).

6.3 **Late payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at one percent (1%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law if lower. Distributor may place a Hotel's account on credit hold only if undisputed amounts are more than forty-five (45) days past due and Distributor has given Seabright's Vice President of Procurement ten (10) days' written notice.

6.4 **Disputes.** A Hotel may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by notifying Distributor with reasonable detail before the due date. The Parties shall work to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

6.5 **Taxes.** Prices exclude applicable sales and use taxes, which Distributor shall state separately on each invoice.

## 7. Food Safety and Compliance

7.1 Distributor shall comply with the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, the Food Safety Modernization Act and their regulations, and all applicable state and local food safety laws.

7.2 Distributor shall maintain a current third-party food safety audit of each warehouse that serves the Hotels under a Global Food Safety Initiative benchmarked scheme with a score of "Excellent" or equivalent, and shall provide the certificate on request.

7.3 **Recalls.** Distributor shall notify each affected Hotel by telephone and email within four (4) hours of learning of any recall, market withdrawal or safety alert affecting a Product supplied to that Hotel, shall collect the affected Product, and shall credit or replace it at no cost.

7.4 **Allergens.** Distributor shall maintain accurate allergen, nutritional and country-of-origin information for every Product and make it available through its ordering platform.

## 8. Term and Renewal

8.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until December 31, 2027 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 9.

8.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive renewal periods of one (1) year each (each a "**Renewal Term**", and together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**"), unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

8.3 **Contract Year.** "Contract Year" means each twelve (12) month period beginning on January 1.

## 9. Termination

9.1 **For convenience.** Seabright may terminate this Agreement, in whole or for one or more Hotels, for any reason or no reason, by giving Distributor not less than one hundred twenty (120) days' written notice. Distributor may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving Seabright not less than one hundred eighty (180) days' written notice.

9.2 **For cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure that breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

9.3 **Service failure.** Seabright may terminate this Agreement for one or more Hotels on thirty (30) days' notice if Distributor fails to meet the Fill Rate standard at that Hotel in any three (3) months within a rolling six (6) month period.

9.4 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of a bankruptcy petition that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

9.5 **Effect of termination.** On expiry or termination, each Hotel shall pay for Products delivered up to the date of termination, and Distributor shall buy back, at Landed Cost plus Mark-Up, any special-order Products held in Distributor's warehouse for that Hotel. Distributor shall cooperate in an orderly transition to a successor distributor for up to sixty (60) days.

## 10. Insurance

10.1 Distributor shall maintain, at its own cost, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations, with limits of not less than US$2,000,000 per occurrence and US$4,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) commercial automobile liability insurance covering all owned, hired and non-owned vehicles with a combined single limit of not less than US$2,000,000;

(c) umbrella or excess liability insurance of not less than US$10,000,000;

(d) product contamination and recall insurance of not less than US$5,000,000; and

(e) workers' compensation insurance as required by law and employer's liability insurance of not less than US$1,000,000.

10.2 Seabright, each Hotel and their respective owners and managers shall be named as additional insureds on the policies in Sections 10.1(a) to (c). Distributor shall provide certificates of insurance before the first delivery and on each renewal of a policy, and shall give thirty (30) days' notice of any cancellation or material reduction in coverage.

## 11. Indemnification

11.1 Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Seabright, each Hotel and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all claims, losses, damages, liabilities, fines and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of (a) any Product that is adulterated, misbranded, contaminated or otherwise not in compliance with applicable law when delivered; (b) any recall of a Product; (c) bodily injury, death or damage to property caused by the negligence or wilful misconduct of Distributor or its drivers; or (d) Distributor's breach of Section 7.

11.2 Seabright shall indemnify Distributor against claims arising from the Hotels' improper storage, preparation or handling of Products after title has passed.

## 12. Limitation of Liability

12.1 EXCEPT FOR A PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 11, BREACH OF SECTION 13 (CONFIDENTIALITY), OR LIABILITY ARISING FROM GROSS NEGLIGENCE OR WILFUL MISCONDUCT, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS OR REVENUE, ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

12.2 SUBJECT TO SECTION 12.1, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL AMOUNTS PAID AND PAYABLE BY THE HOTELS TO DISTRIBUTOR UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM. THE EXCLUSIONS IN SECTION 12.1 ALSO APPLY TO THIS SECTION 12.2.

## 13. Confidentiality

13.1 Each Party shall keep confidential the terms of this Agreement, the Hotels' purchasing volumes and Distributor's Landed Cost information, and shall use them only to perform this Agreement. These obligations survive for three (3) years after the end of the Term.

13.2 Distributor shall not use Seabright's or any Hotel's name, logo or trademarks in any advertising or customer list without Seabright's prior written consent.

## 14. General

14.1 **Independent contractor.** Distributor is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship.

14.2 **Assignment.** Distributor shall not assign or subcontract this Agreement, in whole or in part, without Seabright's prior written consent. Seabright may assign this Agreement, or its rights for any Hotel, to an affiliate, to the purchaser of a Hotel or to a manager of a Hotel, on written notice to Distributor.

14.3 **Force majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, epidemics, war, terrorism, government orders, or labour disputes not involving the affected Party's own employees, provided the affected Party notifies the other promptly. If Distributor's performance at a Hotel is suspended for more than ten (10) consecutive days, the Hotel may buy from other suppliers without breaching the Volume Commitment.

14.4 **Supplier diversity and sustainability.** Distributor shall report each quarter the percentage of Products supplied to the Hotels sourced from certified minority-, women- or veteran-owned businesses and from producers within 250 miles of the relevant Hotel.

14.5 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses below, or to any other address a Party designates by notice:

To Seabright: Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc, 200 Atlantic Avenue, Suite 1400, Boston, MA 02110, Attention: Vice President, Procurement; with a copy to the General Counsel (legal@seabright-hotels.example).

To Distributor: Northshore Foodservice Distributors LLC, 41 Commerce Way, Woburn, MA 01801, Attention: President; email contracts@northshore-foods.example.

14.6 **Governing law and venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. The state and federal courts located in Suffolk County, Massachusetts have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of this Agreement.

14.7 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, including its Schedules and Exhibits, is the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior proposals, including Distributor's response to the RFP. In case of conflict, the body of this Agreement prevails over the Schedules and Exhibits.

14.8 **Amendment and waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written document signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing.

14.9 **Counterparts and electronic signature.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is an original.
