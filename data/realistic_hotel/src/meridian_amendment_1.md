**AMENDMENT No. 1 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT**

This Amendment No. 1 (this "**Amendment**") is made effective as of October 1, 2026 between Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc ("**Seabright**") and Meridian Hospitality Cleaning Services LLC ("**Contractor**"), and amends the Master Services Agreement dated March 1, 2025 between them (the "**Agreement**").

The parties agree as follows:

1. **Payment terms.** Section 4.3 of the Agreement is replaced with: "Seabright shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt."

2. **Term.** In Section 5.1 of the Agreement, the date "February 29, 2028" is replaced with "February 28, 2029". The Agreement therefore now expires on February 28, 2029, subject to renewal as set out in Section 5.1.

3. **Fees.** The fees under each SOW in force on January 1, 2027 increase by three and one-half percent (3.5%) from that date. No other increase under Section 4.1 of the Agreement may take effect before January 1, 2028.

4. **Sustainability reporting.** Contractor shall report to Seabright each quarter the volume of cleaning chemicals used at each Hotel and the share that is EPA Safer Choice certified.

5. Except as amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains in full force and effect. If this Amendment conflicts with the Agreement, this Amendment prevails.

Signed for and on behalf of Seabright Hotels & Resorts Inc by Andrea Whitfield, Vice President, Procurement, on September 24, 2026.

Signed for and on behalf of Meridian Hospitality Cleaning Services LLC by Carlos Ibarra, Chief Executive Officer, on September 22, 2026.
