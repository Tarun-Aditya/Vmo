# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between **Seabright Hotels & Resorts Ltd**, a hotel group that owns and operates six hotels (the "Customer", "we" or "us" when the context is the Customer's side), and **Stayflow Property Systems Inc** (the "Supplier"). In this Agreement, "we" and "us" mean both of us together unless we say otherwise, and "you" means the party receiving information.

It takes effect on January 25, 2026 (the "Effective Date").

## Why we are signing this

The Customer is considering buying property-management and related systems and services from the Supplier. Those services would be delivered at the Customer's individual hotels (the "Properties"). To talk about that openly, each of us will need to share information that we want kept private. This Agreement sets the ground rules.

## How long this Agreement lasts

This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and runs until January 25, 2028 (the "Initial Term", which is 24 months). We can extend it only in writing signed by both of us. Either of us may end it earlier by written notice, as explained in "Ending this Agreement" below.

## What counts as Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information that one of us (the "Discloser") gives to the other (the "Recipient"), or that the Recipient learns through the relationship, in any form, whether or not it is marked "confidential", if a reasonable person would understand it to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances. Examples include:

- pricing, quotes, contract terms and commercial plans;
- guest, loyalty-programme and booking data;
- technical documents, system designs, source code, security details and integration specifications;
- information about the Properties, their operations, staff, suppliers and financial performance;
- the fact and content of our discussions.

## What is not Confidential Information

Information is not Confidential Information if the Recipient can show that it:

1. is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient;
2. was already lawfully known to the Recipient before receiving it, free of any duty of confidence;
3. was lawfully received from a third party who had the right to share it; or
4. was developed independently without using or referring to the Discloser's information.

## What you must do

As Recipient, you agree to:

- use Confidential Information only to evaluate, negotiate and, if we proceed, perform a commercial relationship between us (the "Purpose");
- keep it confidential and protect it with at least the same care you use for your own sensitive information, and never less than reasonable care;
- share it only with your employees, officers, professional advisers and approved subcontractors who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality duties no less protective than these;
- not copy it except as needed for the Purpose, and keep any copies marked and secured;
- tell the Discloser promptly if you become aware of any unauthorised use or disclosure, and help to limit its effects.

You are responsible for any breach of this Agreement by people to whom you give access.

## When disclosure is required by law

If a court, regulator or other authority legally requires you to disclose Confidential Information, you may do so, provided that (where lawful) you give the Discloser prompt notice so it can seek protection, disclose only what is legally required, and cooperate reasonably with any effort to protect the information.

## Personal data and security

Some Confidential Information may include personal data, such as guest details. If so, the Recipient will process it only for the Purpose and on the Discloser's instructions, comply with applicable data protection laws, keep it secure with appropriate technical and organisational measures, and notify the Discloser without undue delay after discovering a security incident affecting it. We will not share personal data unless it is genuinely needed, and where possible we will use anonymised or sample data instead. If we later enter into a services agreement, it will contain the detailed data processing terms.

## Who owns what

All Confidential Information stays the property of the Discloser. Nothing in this Agreement gives the Recipient any licence or right in the Discloser's intellectual property, except the limited right to use the information for the Purpose. If you give us feedback or suggestions, we may use them freely, but this does not give us any right to your Confidential Information.

## No promises about the information, and no deal yet

Confidential Information is provided "as is". The Discloser makes no warranty about its accuracy or completeness, except that it confirms it has the right to share it. Neither of us is obliged to buy, sell or continue talks. Any commercial deal will exist only if and when we sign a separate written agreement. Each of us bears its own costs.

## Returning or destroying information

When the Discloser asks, or when this Agreement ends, the Recipient will promptly return or securely destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information and confirm in writing that it has done so. The Recipient may keep copies that it must retain by law or that sit in routine backups, but those copies remain subject to this Agreement until destroyed.

## If something goes wrong

If you breach this Agreement, the Discloser may suffer harm that money alone cannot fix. The Discloser may therefore ask a court for an injunction or other equitable relief without having to prove actual damage, in addition to any other remedy available.

Each of us is liable to the other for losses caused by its breach of this Agreement, subject to the following. Neither of us excludes liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, or anything else that cannot be limited by law. Otherwise, neither of us is liable for indirect or consequential losses, or loss of profit, except to the extent those arise from a breach of the confidentiality obligations above.

Each of us will indemnify the other against third-party claims, and the reasonable costs of defending them, to the extent they arise from the indemnifying party's unauthorised use or disclosure of the other's Confidential Information in breach of this Agreement.

## Ending this Agreement

Either of us may end this Agreement at any time by giving the other written notice. Ending it does not release either of us from obligations that arose before it ended.

**Your confidentiality duties survive for 3 years after this Agreement ends.** Obligations relating to personal data and trade secrets continue for as long as the information remains personal data or a trade secret, to the extent the law requires.

## Fees

No fees are payable under this Agreement. If we later agree a commercial relationship, fees and invoicing will be set out in a separate written agreement.

## The legal bits

**Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. Each of us submits to the courts located in Delaware for any dispute arising from it, subject to our right to seek urgent injunctive relief in any competent court.

**Assignment.** Neither of us may assign or transfer this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld. Either of us may, on written notice, assign it to a successor to all or substantially all of its business.

**Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognised courier, or by email with confirmation of sending, to the address each of us gives the other in writing. A notice is received when delivered or, for email, on the next business day after sending.

**Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the whole agreement between us about its subject matter and replaces all earlier discussions on it. Any change must be in writing and signed by both of us.

**General.** If any part is found unenforceable, the rest continues in effect. A delay in enforcing a right is not a waiver of it. We are independent parties, and nothing here creates a partnership, agency or joint venture. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including electronically, each of which is an original.
