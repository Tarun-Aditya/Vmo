# Draft DPA – Vaultline Data Services

**Parties:** Arcadia Retail Inc. (the "Customer") and Vaultline Data Services Inc (the "Supplier")
**Effective Date:** [to be agreed]
**Document Type:** DPA
**Reference:** VND-VAULTLINE_DPA
**Status:** DRAFT proposed by the Supplier – not executed

## 1. Scope
Services to be described by Vaultline Data Services Inc.

## 2. Term
This DPA commences on [Effective Date] and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, expires on [Expiry Date].

<!-- page: 2 -->

## 3. Data Protection
The Supplier shall process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions and only for the purposes stated in this DPA.

## 4. Sub-processors
The Supplier may appoint sub-processors at its discretion on notice to the Customer.

## 5. Breach Notification
The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach within 5 days of becoming aware.

<!-- page: 3 -->

## 6. Cross-Border Transfers
The Supplier may transfer personal data to any country where it or its sub-processors operate.

## 7. Audit Rights
The Customer has no right to audit the Supplier.

## 8. Return of Data
On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall return or securely delete all Customer Data within 30 days and certify the deletion in writing.

<!-- page: 4 -->

## 9. Governing Law
This DPA is governed by the laws of New York. The courts at New York County have exclusive jurisdiction.

Signature blocks: [Customer signatory: to be signed] [Supplier signatory: to be signed]. This draft has not been executed.
