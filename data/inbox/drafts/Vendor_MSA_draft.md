# Draft MSA – Uploadco

**Parties:** Arcadia Retail Inc. (the "Customer") and Uploadco Inc (the "Supplier")
**Effective Date:** 1 February 2027
**Document Type:** MSA

## 1. Payment
The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 10 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

## 2. Limitation of Liability
Each party's total aggregate liability arising under this Agreement shall not exceed 1 times the annual fees paid or payable.
