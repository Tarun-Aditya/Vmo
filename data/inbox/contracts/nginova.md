# Enterprise Subscription and Support Agreement – Nginova Systems

**Parties:** Arcadia Retail Inc. (the "Customer") and Nginova Systems Inc (the "Supplier")
**Effective Date:** 21 April 2026
**Document Type:** SaaS
**Reference:** ARC-SaaS-NGINOVA

## 1. Term
This Agreement commences on 21 April 2026 and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, expires on 10 April 2028.

## 2. Renewal
This Agreement does not renew automatically. Any extension requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## 3. Fees
The annual fees under this Agreement are USD 540,000 and the total contract value is USD 1,620,000, exclusive of applicable taxes.

## 4. Data Scope
The services do not involve the processing of personal data.

## 5. Payment
The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 45 days of receipt of a valid invoice. Late payment interest shall not exceed 1% per month.

<!-- page: 3 -->

## 6. Service Levels
The Supplier shall achieve at least 99.5% availability each month and shall issue service credits of 5% of monthly fees for each full 1% shortfall.

## 7. Information Security
The Supplier shall maintain security controls aligned to ISO 27001 and shall notify the Customer of any security incident within 48 hours of becoming aware.

## 8. Data Protection
The Supplier shall process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions and in accordance with applicable data protection law. A Data Processing Agreement must be executed before any personal data is processed.

<!-- page: 4 -->

## 9. Return of Data
On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall return or securely delete all Customer Data within 30 days and certify the deletion in writing.

## 10. Limitation of Liability
Each party's total aggregate liability arising under this Agreement shall not exceed 2 times the annual fees paid or payable.

## 11. Termination for Convenience
The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time by giving 30 days' written notice to the Supplier, without penalty.

<!-- page: 5 -->

## 12. Intellectual Property
The Customer owns all Customer Data and all deliverables created specifically for the Customer. The Supplier retains ownership of its pre-existing tools and grants the Customer a perpetual licence to use them within the deliverables.

## 13. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and continue to do so for 3 years after the Agreement ends.

## 14. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of New York. The courts at New York County have exclusive jurisdiction.

<!-- page: 6 -->

## 15. Execution
Signed for and on behalf of Arcadia Retail Inc. (the Customer) by Michael Brennan, Chief Procurement Officer, on 18 April 2026. Signed for and on behalf of Nginova Systems Inc (the Supplier) by Chris Dalton, Chief Revenue Officer, on 19 April 2026.
