# NDA – Skyguard Security

**Parties:** Arcadia Retail Inc. (the "Customer") and Skyguard Security Solutions Inc (the "Supplier")
**Effective Date:** 4 December 2025
**Document Type:** NDA
**Reference:** ARC-NDA-SKYGUARD_NDA

## 1. Term
This NDA commences on 4 December 2025 and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, expires on 4 December 2026.

## 2. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the purposes of this NDA, and continue to do so for 3 years after the NDA ends.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## 3. Return of Information
On request, each party shall promptly return or destroy the other party's Confidential Information and confirm destruction in writing.

## 4. Non-Solicitation
Neither party shall solicit for employment the other party's personnel involved in the services during the term and for 12 months afterwards.

## 5. Governing Law
This NDA is governed by the laws of England and Wales. The courts at London have exclusive jurisdiction.

<!-- page: 3 -->

## 6. Execution
Signed for and on behalf of Arcadia Retail Inc. (the Customer) by Dana Whitfield, General Counsel, on 1 December 2025. Signed for and on behalf of Skyguard Security Solutions Inc (the Supplier) by Imran Sheikh, Director, on 2 December 2025.
