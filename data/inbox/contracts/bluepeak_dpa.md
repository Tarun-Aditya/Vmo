# Data Protection Addendum

This Data Protection Addendum (the "**Addendum**") is made with effect from 07 March 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer with its headquarters in the United States (the "**Customer**"), and **Bluepeak Analytics Inc** (the "**Supplier**"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the Software-as-a-Service Agreement dated 02 March 2026 between the Customer and the Supplier (the "**Parent Agreement**").

1. **Term.** This Addendum begins on the Effective Date and continues until 07 March 2028 (the initial term of 24 months), unless terminated earlier under its terms. It will also continue after that date for as long as the Supplier processes Customer Personal Data under the Parent Agreement, and clauses 9, 10, 12 and 14 survive until that processing ends.

2. **Fees.** No separate fees are payable for this Addendum. The Supplier's performance of this Addendum is included in the fees payable under the Parent Agreement, which continue to be invoiced and paid in accordance with the Parent Agreement. Any additional work the Customer requests beyond the obligations in this Addendum will be agreed in writing in advance and invoiced under the Parent Agreement's invoicing provisions.

## Definitions and Scope

3. **Definitions.** Capitalised terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Parent Agreement. In this Addendum:
   - "**Applicable Data Protection Law**" means all laws and regulations relating to privacy, data protection, data security or the processing of personal data that apply to a party in connection with the Parent Agreement, including United States federal and state privacy and breach-notification laws.
   - "**Customer Personal Data**" means personal data of the Customer's customers and employees that the Supplier processes on the Customer's behalf in providing the services under the Parent Agreement.
   - "**Security Incident**" means any breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data in the Supplier's or a Subprocessor's possession or control.
   - "**Subprocessor**" means any third party engaged by the Supplier that processes Customer Personal Data.
   - The terms "**controller**", "**processor**", "**service provider**", "**processing**" and "**personal data**" have the meanings given in Applicable Data Protection Law, and "**business**" is read as including the Customer where that law uses it.

4. **Scope and roles.** The services involve processing personal data of the Customer's customers and employees. As between the parties, the Customer is the controller (or business) and the Supplier is the processor (or service provider) of Customer Personal Data. The subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, and the categories of data subjects and data, are those necessary to provide the services described in the Parent Agreement, and are further described in Schedule 1.

5. **Precedence.** If this Addendum conflicts with the Parent Agreement on the processing of Customer Personal Data, this Addendum prevails. In all other respects the Parent Agreement prevails.

## Processing Obligations

6. **Instructions.** The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions, which include the Parent Agreement and this Addendum, and only as needed to provide the services. If the Supplier believes an instruction infringes Applicable Data Protection Law, or if law requires processing otherwise, it shall promptly notify the Customer, unless the law prohibits notice.

7. **Restrictions.** The Supplier shall not:
   - (a) sell or share Customer Personal Data, as those terms are defined in Applicable Data Protection Law;
   - (b) retain, use or disclose Customer Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between the parties or for any purpose other than providing the services;
   - (c) combine Customer Personal Data with personal data obtained from other sources, except as Applicable Data Protection Law permits for a service provider; or
   - (d) attempt to re-identify any de-identified or aggregated data derived from Customer Personal Data.

   The Supplier certifies that it understands and will comply with these restrictions.

8. **Personnel.** The Supplier shall ensure that everyone authorised to process Customer Personal Data is bound by written or statutory confidentiality obligations, has received appropriate training, and has access only to the extent needed to perform the services.

9. **Security.** The Supplier shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Customer Personal Data against Security Incidents, taking into account the nature of the data and the risks of processing. These measures shall at least meet the standards described in Schedule 2 and shall include access controls, encryption of Customer Personal Data in transit and at rest, logging and monitoring, vulnerability management, and tested business-continuity arrangements. The Supplier shall not materially reduce the level of protection during the term.

10. **Security Incidents.** The Supplier shall notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a Security Incident, and shall provide the information the Customer reasonably needs to meet its legal notification duties, including the nature of the incident, the data and individuals affected, likely consequences and the measures taken or proposed. The Supplier shall promptly contain and investigate the incident, take reasonable steps to remediate it, and keep the Customer informed. The Supplier shall not notify any regulator or affected individual of an incident involving Customer Personal Data without the Customer's prior approval, unless the law requires it.

11. **Subprocessors.** The Customer gives general authorisation for the Supplier to use the Subprocessors listed in Schedule 1. The Supplier shall give the Customer reasonable prior notice of any intended addition or replacement of a Subprocessor, and the Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds. If the parties cannot resolve the objection in good faith, the Customer may terminate the affected services without penalty. The Supplier shall bind each Subprocessor to written obligations no less protective than this Addendum and remains liable for each Subprocessor's acts and omissions.

12. **Data subject requests and assistance.** The Supplier shall promptly forward to the Customer any request from an individual to exercise rights under Applicable Data Protection Law and shall not respond except as the Customer instructs. Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Supplier shall give the Customer reasonable assistance, through appropriate measures, in responding to such requests, and in carrying out data protection assessments and consulting regulators where required.

13. **Audit.** The Supplier shall make available to the Customer the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Addendum, including current third-party audit reports or certifications it holds. Where that information is not sufficient, or where a regulator requires it or a Security Incident has occurred, the Supplier shall allow the Customer, or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality, to audit its relevant processing facilities, systems and records on reasonable notice, during normal business hours and in a manner that does not unreasonably disrupt the Supplier's operations. Each party bears its own costs of an audit, unless the audit reveals a material breach by the Supplier, in which case the Supplier shall bear the Customer's reasonable costs.

14. **Location and transfers.** The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data only in the locations disclosed in Schedule 1 and shall not transfer it to any other country without the Customer's prior written consent. Where a transfer is permitted, the Supplier shall put in place any safeguards that Applicable Data Protection Law requires.

15. **Return and deletion.** On expiry or termination of the Parent Agreement or this Addendum, or earlier on the Customer's written request, the Supplier shall, at the Customer's choice, return Customer Personal Data in a commonly used format or securely delete it, and shall delete existing copies, including those held by Subprocessors, unless law requires retention. In that case the Supplier shall continue to protect the retained data under this Addendum and use it only for the legally required purpose. On request, the Supplier shall certify deletion in writing.

## General Terms

16. **Confidentiality.** Customer Personal Data is the Customer's Confidential Information under the Parent Agreement, and the confidentiality obligations of the Parent Agreement apply to it without limit in time.

17. **Intellectual property.** As between the parties, the Customer retains all rights, title and interest in Customer Personal Data. Nothing in this Addendum transfers any intellectual property right of either party to the other.

18. **Warranties.** Each party warrants that it has authority to enter into this Addendum and shall comply with Applicable Data Protection Law in performing it. The Supplier further warrants that it has, and will maintain, the capability to meet the obligations in clauses 6 to 15. The Customer warrants that it has a lawful basis to instruct the Supplier to process Customer Personal Data and has given any notices required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

19. **Indemnity.** The Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless the Customer against losses, damages, fines, penalties, costs and reasonable expenses arising from third-party claims or regulatory actions to the extent caused by the Supplier's or its Subprocessors' breach of this Addendum or of Applicable Data Protection Law. The Customer shall give prompt notice of the claim, reasonable cooperation, and control of the defence and settlement to the Supplier, provided the Supplier does not settle any claim in a way that admits fault on the Customer's behalf without the Customer's consent. The Customer shall indemnify the Supplier on the same basis to the extent a claim arises from the Customer's breach of clause 18.

20. **Liability.** The limitations and exclusions of liability in the Parent Agreement apply to this Addendum, and any liability under this Addendum counts towards, and is not additional to, the limits in the Parent Agreement. Nothing in this Addendum limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

21. **Termination.** The Customer may suspend the transfer of Customer Personal Data, or terminate this Addendum and the affected services under the Parent Agreement, if the Supplier materially breaches this Addendum and does not cure the breach within a reasonable period after written notice, or immediately if the breach cannot be cured. This Addendum terminates automatically when the Parent Agreement ends, subject to the survival provisions in clause 1.

22. **Governing law and jurisdiction.** This Addendum, and any dispute arising from it, is governed by the laws of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The parties submit to the jurisdiction specified in the Parent Agreement for disputes arising from it, which applies equally to this Addendum.

23. **Assignment.** Neither party may assign or transfer this Addendum, in whole or in part, without the other party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, except that either party may assign it to a successor to the whole of its business that also takes over the Parent Agreement. Any purported assignment in breach of this clause is void.

24. **Notices.** Notices under this Addendum must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognised courier or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the address or contact designated for notices in the Parent Agreement. Security Incident notices shall also be sent to the Customer's designated security or privacy contact. A notice takes effect on receipt.

25. **Entire agreement and amendment.** This Addendum, with its Schedules and the Parent Agreement, is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject matter and replaces all prior discussions and understandings on it. It may be amended only in writing signed by both parties. If a change in Applicable Data Protection Law requires a change to this Addendum, the parties shall negotiate the necessary amendment in good faith.

26. **Severability and counterparts.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect and the provision shall be replaced by a valid one closest to the parties' intent. This Addendum may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is an original.

## Schedule 1: Processing Details

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Subject matter | Provision of the software-as-a-service analytics services under the Parent Agreement |
| Nature and purpose | Hosting, storage, analysis, reporting and support of the services for the Customer's retail business |
| Data subjects | Customers of the Customer; employees of the Customer |
| Categories of data | Contact details, account and identification data, purchase and transaction history, loyalty and preference data, and employee business and workforce data, each as supplied by or for the Customer |
| Sensitive data | None, unless the Customer expressly agrees otherwise in writing |
| Duration | The term of this Addendum and any period during which the Supplier retains Customer Personal Data under clause 15 |
| Processing locations | United States, unless the Customer consents in writing under clause 14 |
| Approved Subprocessors | Those identified in writing by the Supplier to the Customer before the Effective Date, as updated under clause 11 |

## Schedule 2: Minimum Security Measures

| Area | Measure |
|---|---|
| Access control | Role-based access on a least-privilege basis; multi-factor authentication for administrative access; prompt removal of access when no longer needed |
| Encryption | Industry-standard encryption of Customer Personal Data in transit and at rest, with managed keys |
| Logging and monitoring | Logging of access to Customer Personal Data and monitoring for anomalous activity |
| Vulnerability management | Regular vulnerability scanning, timely patching and periodic independent penetration testing |
| Segregation | Logical separation of Customer Personal Data from the data of the Supplier's other customers |
| Resilience | Backups, tested disaster-recovery and business-continuity plans |
| Personnel | Background screening where lawful, confidentiality undertakings and regular security and privacy training |
| Incident response | A documented and tested incident response plan consistent with clause 10 |
