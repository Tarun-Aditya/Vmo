# Data Processing Agreement – Ledgerline Finance Cloud

**Parties:** Arcadia Retail Inc. (the "Customer") and Ledgerline Systems Inc (the "Supplier")
**Effective Date:** 21 January 2026
**Document Type:** DPA
**Reference:** ARC-DPA-LEDGERLINE_DPA

## 1. Scope
This DPA supplements the SaaS Subscription Agreement – Ledgerline Finance Cloud and applies to all personal data the Supplier processes on behalf of the Customer.

## 2. Term
This DPA commences on 21 January 2026 and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, expires on 15 January 2029.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## 3. Data Protection
The Supplier shall process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions and only for the purposes stated in this DPA.

## 4. Sub-processors
The Supplier shall not appoint sub-processors without the Customer's prior written authorisation and remains liable for their acts and omissions.

## 5. Breach Notification
The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach within 72 hours of becoming aware.

<!-- page: 3 -->

## 6. Cross-Border Transfers
The Supplier shall not transfer personal data outside the United States without the Customer's prior written consent.

## 7. Audit Rights
The Customer may audit the Supplier's records and facilities relevant to this DPA once per year on 14 days' notice.

## 8. Return of Data
On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall return or securely delete all Customer Data within 30 days and certify the deletion in writing.

<!-- page: 4 -->

## 9. Governing Law
This DPA is governed by the laws of New York. The courts at New York County have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 10. Execution
Signed for and on behalf of Arcadia Retail Inc. (the Customer) by Dana Whitfield, General Counsel, on 18 January 2026. Signed for and on behalf of Ledgerline Systems Inc (the Supplier) by Farhan Qureshi, Director, on 19 January 2026.
