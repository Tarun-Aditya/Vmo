# Amendment No. 1 to MSA – Accentia Technology Services

**Parties:** Arcadia Retail Inc. (the "Customer") and Accentia Technology Services Inc (the "Supplier")
**Effective Date:** 7 September 2026
**Document Type:** Amendment
**Reference:** ARC-AMD-ACCENTIA_AMD1
**Amends:** Master Services Agreement – Accentia Technology Services
This Amendment overrides the clauses listed below; all other terms of the Agreement continue unchanged.

## 1. Term
The expiry date of the Agreement is extended so that the Agreement expires on 6 October 2028.

## 2. Payment
The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice. Late payment interest shall not exceed 1% per month.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## 3. Limitation of Liability
Each party's total aggregate liability arising under this Agreement shall not exceed 2 times the annual fees paid or payable.

## 4. Execution
Signed for and on behalf of Arcadia Retail Inc. (the Customer) by Michael Brennan, Chief Procurement Officer, on 4 September 2026. Signed for and on behalf of Accentia Technology Services Inc (the Supplier) by Laura Stephens, Managing Director, on 5 September 2026.
