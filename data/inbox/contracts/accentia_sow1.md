# SOW 1 – Application Managed Services – Accentia Technology Services

**Parties:** Arcadia Retail Inc. (the "Customer") and Accentia Technology Services Inc (the "Supplier")
**Effective Date:** 2 February 2026
**Document Type:** SOW
**Reference:** ARC-SOW-ACCENTIA_SOW1

## 1. Master Agreement Reference
This SOW is issued under the Master Services Agreement dated 11 December 2025 between the parties (Master Services Agreement – Accentia Technology Services). Capitalised terms have the meaning given in that agreement.

## 2. Scope
24x7 application managed services (L2/L3) for Arcadia's e-commerce, merchandising and ERP platforms.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## 3. Deliverables and Milestones
Milestone 1: mobilisation and delivery plan by 16 February 2026. Milestone 2: core delivery by 3 May 2026. Milestone 3: handover and written acceptance by 1 August 2026.

## 4. Term
This SOW commences on 2 February 2026 and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, expires on 2 February 2027.

## 5. Fees
The annual fees under this SOW are USD 4,800,000 and the total contract value is USD 9,600,000, exclusive of applicable taxes.

<!-- page: 3 -->

## 6. Payment
The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 45 days of receipt of a valid invoice. Late payment interest shall not exceed 1% per month.

## 7. Key Personnel
The Supplier may replace any personnel at any time and without notice to the Customer.

## 8. Acceptance
A deliverable is deemed accepted 3 days after delivery unless the Customer objects in writing.

<!-- page: 4 -->

## 9. Service Levels
The Supplier shall meet the service levels stated in this SOW and shall credit 5% of monthly fees for each service level missed.

## 10. Governance and Reporting
The parties shall hold a monthly service review meeting, and the Supplier shall provide a written monthly report of progress against milestones and service levels.

## 11. Change Control
Any change to the scope, timeline or fees requires a written change request signed by both parties.

<!-- page: 5 -->

## 12. Order of Precedence
This SOW is issued under, and governed by, the Master Services Agreement. If there is a conflict, the Master Services Agreement prevails over this SOW. The liability, indemnity, intellectual property, confidentiality, data protection, audit and governing law terms of the Master Services Agreement apply to this SOW.

## 13. Termination for Convenience
The Customer may terminate this SOW for convenience at any time by giving 30 days' written notice to the Supplier, without penalty.

## 14. Limitation of Liability
Liability under this SOW is governed by the liability provisions of the MSA.

<!-- page: 6 -->

## 15. Execution
Signed for and on behalf of Arcadia Retail Inc. (the Customer) by Michael Brennan, Chief Procurement Officer, on 30 January 2026. Signed for and on behalf of Accentia Technology Services Inc (the Supplier) by Laura Stephens, Managing Director, on 31 January 2026.
