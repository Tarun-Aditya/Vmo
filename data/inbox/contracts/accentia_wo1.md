# Work Order 1 – Data Center Exit Wave 2 – Accentia Technology Services

**Parties:** Arcadia Retail Inc. (the "Customer") and Accentia Technology Services Inc (the "Supplier")
**Effective Date:** 7 September 2026
**Document Type:** Work Order
**Reference:** ARC-WO-ACCENTIA_WO1

## 1. Statement of Work Reference
This Work Order is issued under SOW 2 – AWS Cloud Migration and Platform Engineering – Accentia Technology Services (effective 29 July 2026) and the Master Services Agreement dated 11 December 2025.

## 2. Scope
Decommissioning of the secondary data center and migration of the remaining 38 servers to AWS.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## 3. Deliverables and Milestones
Milestone 1: mobilisation and delivery plan by 21 September 2026. Milestone 2: core delivery by 6 December 2026. Milestone 3: handover and written acceptance by 6 March 2027.

## 4. Term
This Work Order commences on 7 September 2026 and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, expires on 21 November 2027.

## 5. Fees
The fixed fee for this Work Order is USD 1,200,000, exclusive of applicable taxes.

<!-- page: 3 -->

## 6. Payment
The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 45 days of receipt of a valid invoice. Late payment interest shall not exceed 1% per month.

## 7. Acceptance
A deliverable is accepted only when the Customer confirms acceptance in writing after not less than 10 business days of testing.

## 8. Change Control
Any change to the scope, timeline or fees requires a written change request signed by both parties.

<!-- page: 4 -->

## 9. Order of Precedence
This Work Order is issued under the Statement of Work and the Master Services Agreement named above. If there is a conflict, the Master Services Agreement prevails, then the Statement of Work, then this Work Order.

## 10. Execution
Signed for and on behalf of Arcadia Retail Inc. (the Customer) by Michael Brennan, Chief Procurement Officer, on 4 September 2026. Signed for and on behalf of Accentia Technology Services Inc (the Supplier) by Laura Stephens, Managing Director, on 5 September 2026.
