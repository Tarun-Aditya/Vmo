# NDA – Accentia Technology Services

**Parties:** Arcadia Retail Inc. (the "Customer") and Accentia Technology Services Inc (the "Supplier")
**Effective Date:** 1 December 2025
**Document Type:** NDA
**Reference:** ARC-NDA-ACCENTIA_NDA

## 1. Term
This NDA commences on 1 December 2025 and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, expires on 11 December 2027.

## 2. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the purposes of this NDA, and continue to do so for 1 year after the NDA ends.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## 3. Return of Information
On request, each party shall promptly return or destroy the other party's Confidential Information and confirm destruction in writing.

## 4. Non-Solicitation
Neither party shall solicit for employment the other party's personnel involved in the services during the term and for 12 months afterwards.

## 5. Governing Law
This NDA is governed by the laws of New York. The courts at New York County have exclusive jurisdiction.

<!-- page: 3 -->

## 6. Execution
Signed for and on behalf of Arcadia Retail Inc. (the Customer) by Dana Whitfield, General Counsel, on 28 November 2025. Signed for and on behalf of Accentia Technology Services Inc (the Supplier) by Laura Stephens, Managing Director, on 29 November 2025.
