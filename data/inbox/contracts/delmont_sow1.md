# SOW 1 – SAP S/4HANA Transformation, Phase 1 – Delmont Consulting

**Parties:** Arcadia Retail Inc. (the "Customer") and Delmont Consulting LLP (the "Supplier")
**Effective Date:** 7 January 2026
**Document Type:** SOW
**Reference:** ARC-SOW-DELMONT_SOW1

## 1. Master Agreement Reference
This SOW is issued under the Master Services Agreement dated 2 October 2025 between the parties (Master Services Agreement – Delmont Consulting). Capitalised terms have the meaning given in that agreement.

## 2. Scope
Phase 1 of the SAP S/4HANA finance and supply-chain transformation: design, build and data migration.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## 3. Deliverables and Milestones
Milestone 1: mobilisation and delivery plan by 21 January 2026. Milestone 2: core delivery by 7 April 2026. Milestone 3: handover and written acceptance by 6 July 2026.

## 4. Term
This SOW commences on 7 January 2026 and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, expires on 7 January 2027.

## 5. Fees
The annual fees under this SOW are USD 7,200,000 and the total contract value is USD 14,400,000, exclusive of applicable taxes.

<!-- page: 3 -->

## 6. Payment
The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice. Late payment interest shall not exceed 1% per month.

## 7. Key Personnel
The Supplier shall assign the key personnel named in this SOW and shall not replace them without the Customer's prior written consent, and shall provide a replacement of equivalent skill within 15 days of any departure.

## 8. Acceptance
A deliverable is accepted only when the Customer confirms acceptance in writing after not less than 10 business days of testing.

<!-- page: 4 -->

## 9. Service Levels
The Supplier shall meet the service levels stated in this SOW and shall credit 5% of monthly fees for each service level missed.

## 10. Governance and Reporting
The parties shall hold a monthly service review meeting, and the Supplier shall provide a written monthly report of progress against milestones and service levels.

## 11. Change Control
Any change to the scope, timeline or fees requires a written change request signed by both parties.

<!-- page: 5 -->

## 12. Order of Precedence
In case of conflict, this SOW prevails over the Master Services Agreement.

## 13. Termination for Convenience
The Customer may terminate this SOW for convenience at any time by giving 30 days' written notice to the Supplier, without penalty.

## 14. Limitation of Liability
Liability under this SOW is governed by the liability provisions of the MSA.

<!-- page: 6 -->

## 15. Execution
Signed for and on behalf of Arcadia Retail Inc. (the Customer) by Michael Brennan, Chief Procurement Officer, on 4 January 2026. Signed for and on behalf of Delmont Consulting LLP (the Supplier) by Richard Hale, Partner, on 5 January 2026.
