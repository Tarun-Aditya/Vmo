# NDA – Taraka Consultancy Services

**Parties:** Arcadia Retail Inc. (the "Customer") and Taraka Consultancy Services Ltd (the "Supplier")
**Effective Date:** 19 February 2026
**Document Type:** NDA
**Reference:** ARC-NDA-TARAKA_NDA

## 1. Term
This NDA commences on 19 February 2026 and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, expires on 16 December 2027.

## 2. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the purposes of this NDA, and continue to do so for 3 years after the NDA ends.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## 3. Return of Information
On request, each party shall promptly return or destroy the other party's Confidential Information and confirm destruction in writing.

## 4. Non-Solicitation
Neither party shall solicit for employment the other party's personnel involved in the services during the term and for 12 months afterwards.

## 5. Governing Law
This NDA is governed by the laws of New York. The courts at New York County have exclusive jurisdiction.

Signed for and on behalf of Arcadia Retail Inc. (the Customer) by Dana Whitfield, General Counsel, on 16 February 2026. Signed for and on behalf of Taraka Consultancy Services Ltd (the Supplier) by Venkatesh Iyer, VP Delivery, on 17 February 2026.
