# Work Order 1 – Incident Response Retainer – Delmont Consulting

**Parties:** Arcadia Retail Inc. (the "Customer") and Delmont Consulting LLP (the "Supplier")
**Effective Date:** 13 October 2026
**Document Type:** Work Order
**Reference:** ARC-WO-DELMONT_WO1

## 1. Statement of Work Reference
This Work Order is issued under SOW 2 – Cybersecurity Posture Assessment and Roadmap – Delmont Consulting (effective 8 October 2026) and the Master Services Agreement dated 3 October 2025.

## 2. Scope
Prepaid 800-hour incident response and forensics retainer.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## 3. Deliverables and Milestones
Milestone 1: mobilisation and delivery plan by 27 October 2026. Milestone 2: core delivery by 11 January 2027. Milestone 3: handover and written acceptance by 11 April 2027.

## 4. Term
This Work Order commences on 13 October 2026 and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, expires on 12 November 2027.

## 5. Fees
The fixed fee for this Work Order is USD 600,000, exclusive of applicable taxes.

<!-- page: 3 -->

## 6. Payment
The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 45 days of receipt of a valid invoice. Late payment interest shall not exceed 1% per month.

## 7. Acceptance
A deliverable is accepted only when the Customer confirms acceptance in writing after not less than 10 business days of testing.

## 8. Change Control
Any change to the scope, timeline or fees requires a written change request signed by both parties.

## 9. Order of Precedence
This Work Order is issued under the Statement of Work and the Master Services Agreement named above. If there is a conflict, the Master Services Agreement prevails, then the Statement of Work, then this Work Order.

## 10. Execution
Signed for and on behalf of Arcadia Retail Inc. (the Customer) by Michael Brennan, Chief Procurement Officer, on 10 October 2026. Signed for and on behalf of Delmont Consulting LLP (the Supplier) by Richard Hale, Partner, on 11 October 2026.
