# Master Services Agreement – Taraka Consultancy Services

**Parties:** Arcadia Retail Inc. (the "Customer") and Taraka Consultancy Services Ltd (the "Supplier")
**Effective Date:** 2 March 2026
**Document Type:** MSA
**Reference:** ARC-MSA-TARAKA_MSA

## 1. Term
This Agreement commences on 2 March 2026 and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, expires on 25 April 2029.

## 2. Renewal
This Agreement does not renew automatically. Any extension requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## 3. Services
The Supplier shall provide the services described in each Statement of Work signed under this Agreement with reasonable skill and care, using suitably qualified personnel.

## 4. Order of Precedence
If there is a conflict, the terms of this Agreement prevail over any Statement of Work, and a Statement of Work prevails over any Work Order, unless the later document expressly and specifically overrides a named clause of this Agreement.

## 5. Payment
The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice. Late payment interest shall not exceed 1% per month.

<!-- page: 3 -->

## 6. Termination for Convenience
The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time by giving 30 days' written notice to the Supplier, without penalty.

## 7. Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party commits a material breach that is not remedied within 30 days of notice.

## 8. Warranties
The Supplier warrants that the services will be performed with reasonable skill and care and will conform to the applicable Statement of Work, and shall correct any non-conforming services free of charge for 90 days after acceptance.

<!-- page: 4 -->

## 9. Limitation of Liability
Each party's total aggregate liability arising under this Agreement shall not exceed 1 times the annual fees paid or payable.

## 10. Indemnity
The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against third-party claims arising from the Supplier's breach of law, infringement of intellectual property rights, or gross negligence.

## 11. Insurance
The Supplier shall maintain professional indemnity and commercial general liability insurance of at least USD 5,000,000 per claim throughout the term and provide certificates on request.

<!-- page: 5 -->

## 12. Intellectual Property
The Customer owns all Customer Data and all deliverables created specifically for the Customer. The Supplier retains ownership of its pre-existing tools and grants the Customer a perpetual licence to use them within the deliverables.

## 13. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and continue to do so for 3 years after the Agreement ends.

## 14. Data Protection
The Supplier shall process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions and in accordance with applicable data protection law. A Data Processing Agreement must be executed before any personal data is processed.

<!-- page: 6 -->

## 15. Audit Rights
The Customer may audit the Supplier's records and facilities relevant to this Agreement once per year on 14 days' notice.

## 16. Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 17. Force Majeure
Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other promptly and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. Either party may terminate the affected services if the event continues for more than 60 days.

<!-- page: 7 -->

## 18. Dispute Resolution
Disputes shall first be escalated to the parties' senior management for resolution within 15 days, and then referred to mediation before either party commences court proceedings.

## 19. Non-Solicitation
Neither party shall solicit for employment the other party's personnel involved in the services during the term and for 12 months afterwards.

## 20. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Mumbai have exclusive jurisdiction.

Signed for and on behalf of Arcadia Retail Inc. (the Customer) by Michael Brennan, Chief Procurement Officer, on 27 February 2026. Signed for and on behalf of Taraka Consultancy Services Ltd (the Supplier) by Venkatesh Iyer, VP Delivery, on 28 February 2026.
