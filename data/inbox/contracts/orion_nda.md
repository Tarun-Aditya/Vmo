# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Agreement is made with effect from 31 January 2026 (the "Effective Date") between **Arcadia Retail Inc.**, a retailer with its headquarters in the United States ("Arcadia"), and **Orion Cybersecurity Partners LLP** ("Orion"). Each is a "Party" and together the "Parties".

1. **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term ending on 31 January 2029 (36 months), unless terminated earlier under clause 14. The Parties may extend the term only by written agreement signed by both Parties.

2. **Fees.** No fees, charges or other consideration are payable by either Party to the other under this Agreement. Each Party bears its own costs of performing it. If the Parties later agree to any paid services, those services and the related invoicing terms shall be set out in a separate written agreement, and nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to enter into one.

3. **Purpose.** The Parties wish to discuss and evaluate a possible cybersecurity services relationship, including assessment, monitoring and advisory services (the "Purpose"). Each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other solely for the Purpose. This Agreement does not oblige either Party to disclose any information or to enter into any further transaction.

4. **Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means all non-public information, in any form, disclosed by or on behalf of a Party (the "Discloser") to the other Party (the "Recipient") in connection with the Purpose, whether before or after the Effective Date, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature or the circumstances of disclosure. It includes, without limitation, security architectures, vulnerability and penetration-testing findings, system configurations, credentials, incident data, customer and personnel data, pricing, business plans and the existence and content of the Parties' discussions.

5. **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show: (a) is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient without restriction before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction and without breach of any duty owed to the Discloser; or (d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of or reference to the Confidential Information.

6. **Obligations.** The Recipient shall: (a) keep the Confidential Information confidential and protect it with at least the degree of care it applies to its own confidential information of a similar nature, and no less than reasonable care; (b) use it only for the Purpose; (c) not disclose it to any person except those of its and its affiliates' directors, partners, employees, professional advisers and approved subcontractors who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement; and (d) remain liable for any act or omission of such persons that would breach this Agreement if done by the Recipient.

7. **Compelled Disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, court order or a competent regulatory authority, provided that (where lawful) it gives the Discloser prompt prior notice, cooperates with any effort to seek protective treatment, and discloses only the portion legally required.

8. **Security Incidents.** The Recipient shall notify the Discloser without undue delay on becoming aware of any unauthorised access to, or use or disclosure of, the Discloser's Confidential Information, and shall take reasonable steps to contain the incident, mitigate its effects and prevent recurrence.

9. **Return and Destruction.** On the Discloser's written request, or on expiry or termination of this Agreement, the Recipient shall promptly return or securely destroy all Confidential Information in its possession or control and, on request, confirm in writing that it has done so. The Recipient may retain copies (a) required by law or regulation, or (b) held in routine electronic back-ups that are not readily accessible, in each case subject to continued confidentiality under this Agreement and not used for any other purpose.

10. **Intellectual Property.** All Confidential Information and all intellectual property rights in it remain the property of the Discloser or its licensors. No licence or right is granted to the Recipient except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose. Feedback given on a Party's products or services may be used freely by the receiving Party, provided it does not include or reveal the giving Party's Confidential Information.

11. **Warranties and Disclaimer.** Each Party warrants that it has authority to enter into this Agreement. Save as stated in this clause, Confidential Information is provided "as is" and neither Party gives any warranty as to its accuracy, completeness or fitness for any purpose. Neither Party shall have liability for use of Confidential Information in accordance with this Agreement.

12. **Data Protection.** Each Party shall comply with data protection laws applicable to it, including, where relevant, the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and applicable United States state privacy laws. The Parties do not intend to exchange personal data under this Agreement beyond business contact details and shall avoid doing so wherever practicable. If personal data must be shared, the Parties shall first agree appropriate data protection terms and any required transfer mechanism.

13. **Liability and Remedies.** Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for fraud, death or personal injury caused by negligence, or any liability that cannot lawfully be limited. Subject to that, neither Party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss arising under this Agreement, except in respect of loss caused by its breach of clauses 4 to 9. Each Party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims, and related reasonable costs, to the extent arising from its breach of clauses 6 to 8, provided the indemnified Party gives prompt notice, reasonable assistance and conduct of the claim. The Parties acknowledge that damages alone may be an inadequate remedy for breach of this Agreement and that the Discloser may seek injunctive or other equitable relief without proof of special damage.

14. **Termination.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time on written notice to the other. Termination or expiry does not affect accrued rights or the obligations that survive under clause 15.

15. **Survival.** The Recipient's obligations of confidentiality and restricted use under this Agreement survive expiry or termination and continue for 7 years after the Agreement ends. Clauses 9, 10, 13, 16 and 17 also survive in accordance with their terms.

16. **Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** This Agreement and any dispute or claim (including non-contractual disputes or claims) arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of England and Wales. The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction, save that either Party may seek injunctive relief to protect its Confidential Information in any court of competent jurisdiction.

17. **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign, transfer or subcontract any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld. Any purported assignment in breach of this clause is void.

18. **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognised courier or by email to the address that each Party has notified to the other for this purpose. Notices are deemed received on delivery if by hand or courier, or at the time of transmission if by email, provided that any notice received outside business hours is deemed received at the start of the next business day.

19. **General.** This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties relating to its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, understandings and agreements on it. It may be varied only in writing signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of it. If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect. No third party has any right to enforce this Agreement. Nothing in it creates a partnership, agency or joint venture between the Parties. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
