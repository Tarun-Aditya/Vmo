# SaaS Subscription Agreement – AdSpark Analytics

**Parties:** Arcadia Retail Inc. (the "Customer") and AdSpark Analytics LLP (the "Supplier")
**Effective Date:** 28 November 2025
**Document Type:** SaaS
**Reference:** ARC-SaaS-ADSPARK

## 1. Term
This Agreement commences on 28 November 2025 and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, expires on 28 November 2026.

## 2. Renewal
This Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of 12 months unless either party gives at least 30 days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## 3. Fees
The annual fees under this Agreement are USD 220,000 and the total contract value is USD 220,000, exclusive of applicable taxes.

## 4. Data Scope
The services do not involve the processing of personal data.

## 5. Payment
The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 45 days of receipt of a valid invoice. Late payment interest shall not exceed 1% per month.

<!-- page: 3 -->

## 6. Service Levels
The Supplier shall achieve at least 99.9% availability each month and shall issue service credits of 5% of monthly fees for each full 1% shortfall.

## 7. Information Security
The Supplier shall maintain security controls aligned to ISO 27001 and shall notify the Customer of any security incident within 48 hours of becoming aware.

## 8. Data Protection
The Supplier shall process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions and in accordance with applicable data protection law. A Data Processing Agreement must be executed before any personal data is processed.

<!-- page: 4 -->

## 9. Return of Data
On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall return or securely delete all Customer Data within 30 days and certify the deletion in writing.

## 10. Limitation of Liability
Each party's liability under this Agreement is unlimited, including liability for indirect and consequential losses.

## 11. Termination for Convenience
The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time by giving 30 days' written notice to the Supplier, without penalty.

<!-- page: 5 -->

## 12. Intellectual Property
The Supplier owns all deliverables and work product and grants the Customer a limited, non-transferable licence to use them during the term.

## 13. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and continue to do so for 3 years after the Agreement ends.

## 14. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore. The courts at Singapore have exclusive jurisdiction.

<!-- page: 6 -->

## 15. Execution
Signed for and on behalf of Arcadia Retail Inc. (the Customer) by Michael Brennan, Chief Procurement Officer, on 25 November 2025. Signed for and on behalf of AdSpark Analytics LLP (the Supplier) by Tanvi Joshi, Partner, on 26 November 2025.
