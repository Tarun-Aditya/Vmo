# Amendment No. 1 to MSA – Delmont Consulting

**Parties:** Arcadia Retail Inc. (the "Customer") and Delmont Consulting LLP (the "Supplier")
**Effective Date:** 22 September 2026
**Document Type:** Amendment
**Reference:** ARC-AMD-DELMONT_AMD1
**Amends:** Master Services Agreement – Delmont Consulting
This Amendment overrides the clauses listed below; all other terms of the Agreement continue unchanged.

## 1. Term
The expiry date of the Agreement is extended so that the Agreement expires on 14 January 2029.

## 2. Payment
The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 60 days of receipt of a valid invoice. Late payment interest shall not exceed 1% per month.
