WITHOUT AND WITH STRUCTURED DATA

Every contract operation changes once the terms are data

Operation

Today: the hotel team, per document

With data: every contract, every hotel

Renewals

Read each contract, work out the notice date, set a reminder

Last day to act computed; alerts before it passes

Supplier service

Complaints with no measure; promises nobody remembers

Each call timed against the promised response

Certificates

Chase insurance and food-safety papers by email

Required by the contract, tracked, flagged on expiry

Invoices

Paid as billed

Checked against the contracted rate

Drafts and redlines

Copy last year’s; compare the supplier’s draft by eye

From approved clauses; checked against our standards

Walk the rows in hotel terms. Renewals: today someone reads each contract and sets a reminder; with data, the last day to give notice is computed for every contract at every hotel and the team is alerted before it passes. Supplier service: today a late elevator or HVAC call is a complaint with no measure; with data, each call is timed against the response time the contract promises. Certificates: today the team chases insurance and food-safety certificates by email; with data, the contract says which are required, they are tracked and flagged on expiry. Invoices: paid as billed today; checked against the contracted rate with data. Drafts and redlines: copied from last year and compared by eye today; drafted from approved clauses and checked against our standards with data. Same pattern every row: structure first, then the operation runs on it.
