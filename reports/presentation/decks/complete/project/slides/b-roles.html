WHO IT HELPS

Less manual work at the hotel, and for the owner

THE PROPERTY MANAGER

Runs the hotel’s contracts without the paperwork

  • One list to act on: renewals, certificates, obligations
  • When something breaks: who to call and how fast they must come
  • Late suppliers measured, not argued about
  • Questions answered from the contract, with the clause

THE OWNER

Runs the same operations across every hotel

  • Every hotel’s renewals and risks in one place
  • The same supplier’s rates compared across hotels
  • Group agreements shared fairly between hotels
  • On a sale: what ends, what needs notice, what transfers

One set of data; the manager operates the hotel, the owner operates the portfolio.

The same structured data eases the work of two people. The property manager runs the hotel's contracts without the paperwork: one list of what to act on, who to call and how fast they promised to come when something breaks, suppliers measured against their promises, and answers that quote the clause. A manager sees only their own hotel. The owner runs the same operations across all their hotels: every hotel's renewals and risks in one place, the same supplier's rates side by side, group agreements shared fairly, and on a sale, which contracts end, need notice or transfer to the buyer. Both views exist in the proof of concept.
