Be precise here; this is where credibility is won or lost. All figures were measured on 15 November 2026 with the offline rule engine, which runs without any AI model; the Claude engine is built in but was not measured in this round. 99%: 668 of 672 values on a 32-document test portfolio generated for the proof of concept. 91%: 49 of 54 labelled values on 12 real, public hotel-related contracts from public records (hotel management, housekeeping, linen, elevator, pest control); this is the closest we have to real paper, and the sample is small. 17 of 17: all deliberately planted risky deviations in four vendor drafts were found, with nothing high or critical raised on a clean control draft. About 2 minutes: a folder of 11 contracts, including an image-only scan read by OCR, on a single machine. Zero duplicates: a byte-identical copy under a different name is recognised and not stored twice. What is not proven: our own contracts. The realistic test contracts were written for the pilot and the rules were improved on them, so they are not an independent measure. The pilot's first result should replace the placeholder.
