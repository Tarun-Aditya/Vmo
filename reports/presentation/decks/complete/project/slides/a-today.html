WITHOUT AND WITH STRUCTURED DATA

Every contract process changes once the terms are data

Process

Today: a person, per document

With data: for every contract at once

Renewals

Read each contract, work out the notice date, set a reminder

Last day to act computed for all; alerts before it passes

Obligations

Known to whoever negotiated them

Extracted with owners and due dates, tracked

Redlines

New contracts

Copy the last one and edit

Risk and spend

Found after something goes wrong

Flagged when the document arrives; invoices checked

Walk the rows; do not read them all. The left column is how it works today: each process is a person reading one document. The right column is the same process once the contract is data: it runs for every contract at once and it is repeatable. Renewals: instead of someone reading and setting a reminder, the last day to give notice is computed for every contract and people are alerted before it passes. Obligations: instead of living in the negotiator's memory, they are extracted with owners and due dates. Redlines: a vendor draft is compared clause by clause against our standard positions, with suggested wording. New contracts: drafted from approved clauses. Risk and spend: flagged on arrival, and invoices can be checked against the contracted rates. The pattern is the same in every row: structure first, then automation.
