Why now: every workflow, alert and dashboard needs structured input, and hotel contracts never were structured, so the hotel team did the structuring by hand and it never kept up. AI can now read any supplier's form the way a trained person does, and show the sentence each answer came from, which is what makes it safe to rely on. The model is simple: AI does the reading; the hotel team and the owner's team make the decisions and correct anything wrong, and those corrections are kept as the measure of accuracy on our own contracts.
