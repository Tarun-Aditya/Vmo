This is the "we can do anything with it" slide. The foundation at the bottom is the structured data. The roof is the outcome we care about: business outcomes driven by AI. The pillars are examples, not the limit: renewal alerts, obligation tracking, risk flags, invoice checks against contract rates, plain-English questions across all contracts, and drafting and redlining from our own standards. Each pillar is a small application once the foundation exists; without it, each one is a manual project. Stress that the pillars are interchangeable: other teams will have their own, and they all use the same foundation.
