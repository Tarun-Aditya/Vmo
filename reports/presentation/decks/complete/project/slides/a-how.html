HOW IT WORKS

A continuous pipeline: every document that arrives becomes data

1

Read

Text and pages from PDF, Word, scans (OCR)

2

Structure

Clauses found and recognised; documents linked

3

Extract

Terms with source sentence and confidence

4

Index

Every clause searchable by meaning

5

Check

Against our standards; deadlines and risks raised

Continuous: runs on a schedule or as files land in drives, mailboxes and procurement systems

Incremental: new and changed files are read; duplicates and unchanged files are skipped

Human sign-off: people review flagged values; corrections are kept

This is the engine behind the idea, and it is the part I will show live. Five steps for every document: read it, including scans; find and recognise its clauses and link it to related documents, so a schedule sits under its master agreement and an amendment updates the terms it changes; extract the terms with their source and confidence; index every clause so it can be searched by meaning; and check it against our own standards. It is not a one-off upload: it runs continuously against wherever contracts live, reads only what is new or changed, and skips duplicates. People stay in the loop for anything flagged.
