Why this was not possible before: every automation, every workflow tool, every dashboard needs structured input. Our contracts never were structured, so we had people do the structuring by hand, and it never kept up. What changed is that AI can read documents in any wording, the way a trained analyst does, and it can show where each answer came from, which is what makes it trustworthy enough for contracts. The operating model is simple: AI does the reading; people make the decisions and correct anything that is wrong, and those corrections are kept, so accuracy is measured on our own documents over time.
