About 20 contract fields (dates, renewal, notice, value, payment, cap, exit, law, personal data, signers) plus 19 configurable clause measures (response time, insurance cover, rates)

Extraction produces the terms, each one checkable. With Claude on, extraction is a forced tool call against a fixed JSON schema: for every field Claude returns a value, a confidence and the clause it came from; the prompt lives in a file, not in code. The offline rules extract the same fields without any AI call; they are the engine in Demo mode and they fill any field Claude leaves empty where a rule can read it exactly, such as dates and signatures. The record on the right is a real example from the waste contract: notice period 90 days, confidence 0.90, from the Term and Automatic Renewal clause on page 3. There are about 20 contract fields and 19 clause measures that an administrator can configure, such as response time, insurance cover or a rate per kilogram. When a person corrects a value, the correction is stored, survives every later re-read and becomes a label against which accuracy is measured.
