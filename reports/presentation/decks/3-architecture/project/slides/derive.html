Every rule is configuration, not code.

Admin edits the clause library, checklist, measures, obligation and alert rules, thresholds and risk weights.

Once the terms are stored, everything operational is derived from them by rules. The checklist has 49 rules: a clause that must be present or must not be, a measure that must be within a threshold, wording that is not allowed, each with a severity. Redline compares a supplier's draft clause by clause with our standard templates and playbook positions and proposes replacement wording. Obligations are derived from clauses by rules, including the certificates a contract requires. Renewals compute the last day to act, and decisions are recorded. Alert rules raise alerts and route them by hotel to channels. Spend compares invoices with contracted rates, and supplier risk is scored from weights. The point: every one of these is configuration an administrator can change, not code a developer has to.
