VIEWS

v_effective_terms amendments applied, with the source of each term · v_renewals last day to act · v_property_contracts what applies at each hotel

The database is Postgres with the pgvector extension, so relational terms and clause vectors live together. At the centre, contracts: one row per document with its supplier, type, hotel, dates, renewal and notice terms, value, payment terms, liability cap, exit right, governing law, personal-data flag, signers and the file's fingerprint; parent_id links a schedule to its master agreement and an amendment to what it amends. Around it: clauses with their type, text, page and vector; field_extractions, one row per extracted value with its confidence, source clause and page; field_corrections, what people fixed; extracted_values for the configurable clause measures; check results and redline deviations with severity; obligations and certificates; and operational data such as invoices and service-level measurements. Three views carry the business logic: v_effective_terms applies amendments over the original and records which document each current term comes from; v_renewals computes the last day to act; v_property_contracts says which contracts apply at each hotel, including shares of group agreements. In total 57 tables and 17 views, built through 33 versioned migrations.
