The controls that make it safe to rely on. Access: three roles, single sign-on through the customer's identity provider, multi-factor authentication, and hotel accounts limited to one hotel with every other screen denied by default. People decide: corrections are kept and approvals and sign-off sit where judgement matters. Audit: an audit log of actions, and every AI call is logged with its model, tokens, time and cost, shown on an admin page. Demo or live: one switch between the offline engine with recorded results and Claude; if Claude is unavailable, the system falls back to the offline engine and says so. Data stays put: the search index is built on our own server, questions run on a read-only database role, and connector secrets are stored encrypted. Measured: there are accuracy evaluations for extraction, Ask and redline, plus about 200 automated tests. Be explicit about what leaves our servers: in live mode, document text and questions go to Claude; in demo mode nothing does. The data-processing terms and hosting region need confirming with IT before a pilot.
