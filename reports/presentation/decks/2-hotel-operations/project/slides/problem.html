THE PROBLEM

Every hotel runs on supplier contracts, and running them is manual

Locked in files

Laundry, food, elevators, HVAC, security, waste, valet, hotel systems: each a PDF, a scan or a supplier’s form in a drawer or drive.

Manual, one at a time

For each one the hotel team drafts, checks, keys terms into a sheet, sets reminders, chases certificates and argues about service.

Repeated at every hotel

A manager can just about cope with one hotel. An owner with many repeats the same work at each, with no way to compare.

Misses cost money

A waste contract auto-renews for three years, an insurance certificate lapses, a supplier misses its response time unnoticed.

The information exists. It just isn’t data.

So no one can track, alert on or compare it, at one hotel or across many.
