THE IDEA

AI turns hotel contracts into structured, related data

UNSTRUCTURED

Supplier documents

Group agreements, hotel schedules, amendments, certificates, scans, email attachments

AI READS

Like a person, at scale

Finds the clauses, extracts the terms, links each value to its sentence and to the hotels it covers

STRUCTURED

Related data

Suppliers, hotels, dates, rates, response times, obligations, certificates, and which amendment changed what

Once it is data, the operations can run on it.

Track it, alert on it, measure suppliers against it, compare hotels with it, draft from it.

The idea in one picture, in hotel terms. Left: what the hotel and the owner have today: group agreements, each hotel's schedule, amendments, insurance and food-safety certificates, scans and email attachments. Middle: AI reads each document like an analyst, finds the clauses, pulls out the terms, keeps a link from every value to the sentence it came from, and records which hotels it applies to. Right: data, not another document: suppliers, hotels, dates, rates, promised response times, obligations, certificates, and which amendment changed which term. The line to land: once it is data, the operations can run on it.
