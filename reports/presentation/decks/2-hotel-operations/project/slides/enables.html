THE FOUNDATION

Structured, related contract data: suppliers, hotels, clauses, terms, rates, obligations and amendments, each value traced to its source

The “we can do anything with it” slide in hotel terms. The foundation is the structured data. On top of it, each operation the hotel team does by hand today becomes a small application: renewal alerts; supplier service measured against the promised response time; compliance certificates tracked; spend checked against contracted rates and compared with other hotels; plain-English questions that quote the clause; and drafting and redlining from our own standards. The pillars are examples, not the limit; all of them use the same foundation.
