Teal steps run on the data automatically. Rust steps keep a person’s sign-off. Each renewal feeds the next draft: what we agreed, what we paid, how the supplier performed.

Not only reading old contracts: the same data serves the whole life of a supplier contract at a hotel. Drafted from approved clauses; the supplier's draft redlined against our standards; approved by the hotel or the owner depending on value and risk; the signed copy read back into data and tagged to the hotel; then operated: service calls timed, certificates tracked, invoices checked, deadlines alerted; and renewed or exited before the notice date with the supplier's record in hand. Teal steps are automated; rust steps keep a person's sign-off.
