The engine, the part I will show live. Five steps for every contract from every hotel: read it, including scans; find its clauses and link it, so a hotel's schedule sits under the group agreement and an amendment updates what it changes; extract the terms, rates and promised response times with their source; index every clause so it can be searched by meaning; and check it against our standards. It runs continuously wherever the contracts arrive, reads only what is new or changed, skips duplicates, and keeps the hotel team in the loop for anything flagged.
