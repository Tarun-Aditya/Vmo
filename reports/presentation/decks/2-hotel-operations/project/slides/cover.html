From hotel contracts to hotel operations

Turning the supplier contracts every hotel runs on into structured data that AI keeps current, so the work around them stops being manual, for the property manager and the owner.

Same idea as the general deck, told through a hotel. Every hotel runs on supplier contracts: laundry, food, elevators, HVAC, security, waste, valet, the hotel systems. Operating those contracts, drafting, checking, tracking, renewing, chasing certificates, measuring suppliers, is manual work done one document at a time by the property team, and repeated at every hotel the owner has. AI can now read those documents and turn them into structured data. Once the terms are data, the operations can run on it. The product at the end is only the proof.
