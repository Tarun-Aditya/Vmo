# Opening the documents: the manual way

Use this before switching to *From paper to rows*. Open each file, point at the passage named, and say what a person
has to work out by hand. The point to land: every one of these is a different format, layout and wording, and the
answers that matter are buried on different pages. One contract is fine. Hundreds, across every hotel, is not.

All companies, people and documents are fictional. The eleven files are the folder the page reads, in the same order.
Pages are PDF page numbers; Word files have no fixed pages, so their passages are given by section.

**Suggested pace (about 4 minutes):** spend a minute on the waste contract (10), a minute on the cleaning contractor's
family (1 to 5), and a few seconds on each of the rest. Finish on the duplicate (11).

---

## The cleaning contractor's family (5 files, 3 formats)

**01 · `01_meridian_nda.docx` · Mutual NDA (Word)**
- Section 4, Term: it expires on **17 November 2026**.
- Manual work: nothing in the file tells you it belongs with the master agreement. Someone has to know.

**02 · `02_meridian_msa.pdf` · Master Services Agreement (PDF, 4 pages)**
- Page 2, Section 4.3: pay within **45 days**.
- Page 2, Section 5.1: ends **29 February 2028**, renews yearly unless notice is given **90 days** before.
- Page 3, Section 8: liability capped at **2 times annual fees**.
- Manual work: copy these into a spreadsheet, and calculate the last day to give notice yourself.

**03 · `03_meridian_sow_harbor.pdf` · Schedule No. 1, Harbor Hotel (PDF, 2 pages)**
- Page 2, Section 4: **$462,000 a year**. Section 5: ends **31 March 2027**.
- Manual work: link it to the master agreement and to the hotel it serves.

**04 · `04_meridian_sow_midtown.docx` · Schedule No. 2, Midtown Suites (Word)**
- Section 3: **$318,000 a year**. Section 5: ends **31 May 2027**.
- Manual work: same again, in a different format.

**05 · `05_meridian_amendment_1_scan.pdf` · Amendment No. 1 (scanned image, 1 page)**
- Try to select or search the text: you can't. It's a picture.
- Section 1: payment changes from 45 to **30 days**. Section 2: the end date moves to **28 February 2029**.
- Manual work: someone has to read it, realise the spreadsheet row for the master agreement is now wrong, and fix it.
  If they don't, finance keeps paying on 45 days.

## Five unrelated suppliers (5 files, every one laid out differently)

**06 · `06_northshore_food_supply.pdf` · Food distribution, all hotels (PDF, 8 pages)**
- Page 2, Section 2.3: estimated purchases of about **$4.2 million a year**, the biggest contract in the folder.
- Page 4, Section 6: pay within **21 days**. Page 5, Section 8: ends **31 December 2027**, renews automatically,
  **90 days** notice. Page 6, Section 12: cap of **1 times annual fees**.
- Manual work: the terms are spread across eight pages and four sections.

**07 · `07_adirondack_hvac_maintenance.docx` · HVAC maintenance, Lakeside Resort (Word)**
- Section 5.1: **$148,800 a year**. Section 6.2: renews for 12 months unless notice is given **60 days** before the end
  (**31 January 2027**): the last day to act is **2 December 2026**.
- Section 3: response times and service credits that someone should be checking against actual service.
- Manual work: this deadline is two weeks away. Does anyone have it in a calendar?

**08 · `08_yieldline_rms_subscription.pdf` · Revenue management software (PDF, 9 pages)**
- Pages 1 to 2, order form: **$82,800 a year**.
- Page 6, Section 9.2: renews for 12 months, **60 days** notice. Section 11: cap of 1 times annual fees.
- Exhibit C: a data processing addendum, because it handles guest data. There is no right to end it early for convenience.
- Manual work: the order form and the legal terms say different things in different places.

**09 · `09_keystone_valet_parking.docx` · Valet and garage, Harbor Hotel (Word)**
- Section 5.1: a fee of $6,500 a month (**$78,000 a year**). Section 5.4: pay within 15 days.
  Section 6.1: ends **31 December 2026**.
- Section 9.2: **no limitation** on the operator's liability, so liability is uncapped.
- Manual work: "uncapped" is never written as a word. You have to read the clause and understand it.

**10 · `10_prairie_waste_services.pdf` · Waste and recycling, Airport Inn (PDF, 6 pages)** *(spend the most time here)*
- Page 1: a rate table, not one annual value. Someone has to add it up (about $5,550 a month).
- Page 3, Section 2.2, in capitals: it renews for **another 36 months** unless notice is sent by certified mail
  **between 180 and 90 days** before **30 November 2026**. The last day was **1 September 2026**. It has passed.
- Page 3, Section 5.2: rates can rise **at least 4% a year**.
- Page 5, Section 8.4: **no limitation** of liability. Page 5, Section 9.1: we **cannot end it early**, and
  leaving costs six months of charges (Section 9.2).
- Manual work: the most important sentence in the contract is in the fine print, and nobody calendared it.

## The copy

**11 · `11_Northshore food supply - signed copy.pdf`**

- Manual work: would anyone notice? Or would the $4.2 million contract be counted twice?

## The switch

> "That's eleven files, three formats, and one scan you can't even search. To answer the basic questions (when must we
> act, what can we leave, where are we exposed), someone has to read every page and keep a spreadsheet up to date,
> at every hotel, forever. Here is the same folder, done by the pipeline."

Then open *From paper to rows* (https://claude.ai/artifact/KNfuZFnyyYj5gUV8Mboqci, or `paper-to-rows.local.html`
in the folder above) and start with **One document**: it is the same waste contract you just showed.
