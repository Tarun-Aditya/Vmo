# Opening the documents: the manual way

Use this before switching to *From paper to rows*. Open each file, point at the passage named, and say what a person
has to work out by hand. The point to land: every file is a different format, layout and wording, and the answers
that matter are buried on different pages. One is fine. Hundreds, everywhere, is not.

[All companies, people and documents are fictional.] The files are the folder the page reads, in the same order.
Pages are PDF page numbers; Word files have no fixed pages, so their passages are given by section.

**Suggested pace (about 4 minutes):** a minute on [the "aha" document], a minute on [the related family], a few
seconds on each of the rest. Finish on the duplicate.
