THE IDEA

AI turns documents into structured, related data

UNSTRUCTURED

Documents

PDF and Word files, scans, email attachments, shared drives, procurement systems

AI READS

Like a person, at scale

Finds the clauses, extracts the terms, links each value to the sentence it came from

STRUCTURED

Related data

Contracts, parties, clauses, dates, values, obligations, and how documents relate: master, schedule, amendment

Once it is data, we can do anything with it.

Search it, join it, count it, track it, alert on it, and build new processes on top of it.

This is the whole idea in one picture. Left: what we have today, documents in every format and place. Middle: AI reads each document the way an analyst would: it finds the clauses, pulls out the terms, and keeps a link from every value back to the sentence and page it came from, so a person can always check it. Right: the result is not another document or a summary, it is data in a relational form: one record per contract, its parties, its clauses, its dates and values, its obligations, and its relationships, such as which schedules sit under which master agreement and which amendment changed which term. The sentence to land: once it is data, we can do anything with it. Everything after this slide is just examples of that.
